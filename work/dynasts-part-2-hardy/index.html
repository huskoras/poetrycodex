The Dynasts: Part Second

An Epic-Drama of the War with Napoleon, 1807-1812 (Part Second)

by Thomas Hardy

The second part of Hardy's epic-drama, in six acts, follows Napoleon and Britain through the Peninsular War, the Walcheren expedition, Napoleon's second marriage and the birth of the King of Rome, watched throughout by the Spirits.
