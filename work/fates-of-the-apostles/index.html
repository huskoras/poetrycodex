The Fates of the Apostles

by Cynewulf

Translated by Charles W. Kennedy (1910)

A brief martyrology recounting, in turn, the missionary travels and deaths of Christ's twelve apostles, closing the Vercelli Book poem with Cynewulf's runic signature.
