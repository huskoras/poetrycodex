Romance (masnavi) · 1197

هفت پیکر (Haft Peykar)

The Persian original of Nizami's Haft Peykar

by Nizami Ganjavi

Nizami's Persian romance of the Sasanian king Bahram Gur and the seven princesses in seven pavilions, completed in 1197: the original text, in Ganjoor's section order.
