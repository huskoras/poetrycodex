Uta monogatari (poem-tale) · 10th c.

伊勢物語 (Ise monogatari)

Nurigome-bon text (朱雀院塗籠御本)

by Anonymous (traditionally associated with Ariwara no Narihira)

The best-known Heian poem-tale: a series of short episodes in kana prose and waka about an unnamed 'man of old', whom tradition has identified with the poet Ariwara no Narihira.
