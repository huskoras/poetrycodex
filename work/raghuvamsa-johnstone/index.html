The Raghuvamsa (The Story of Raghu's Line)

Raghuvamsa

by Kalidasa

Translated by P. de Lacy Johnstone (1902)

Kalidasa's epic of the solar dynasty of Raghu, from King Dilipa's vow at the hermitage of Vasishtha through Rama's life and banishment of Sita to the decline of the line under the last king, Agnivarna.
