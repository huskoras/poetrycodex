Fables · c. 400

Fabulae

Forty-two Latin verse fables

by Avianus

Avianus's forty-two fables in elegiac couplets, a late Roman verse version of the Aesopic tale (the tortoise and the eagle, the wind and the sun, the crow and the urn) that served as a school text throughout the Middle Ages.
