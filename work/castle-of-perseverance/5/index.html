The Castle of Perseverance · c. 1425

Scene V: Before World's Scaffold

by Anonymous (Middle English) c. 13th–16th century

(On the Scaffold, World, Pleasure, Liking, Vain-Glory.)

Mundus. Now I sytte in my semly sale;
I trotte & tremle in my trew trone;
as a hawke, I hoppe in my hende hale;
Kyng, knyth & kayser, to me makyn mone.
of God ne of good man, ȝyf I neuere tale;
as a lykynge lord, I leyke here a-lone;
wo-so brawle any boste, be downe or be dale,
þo gadlyngis schal be gastyd, & gryslych grone,
I-wys.
Lust, Foly & Veynglory,
all þese arn in myn memory:
þer be-gynnyth þe nobyl story
of þis werldis blys.

Lust, Lykyng & Foly,
comly knytis of renoun,
be-lyue þorwe þis londe do crye
al a-bowtyn in toure & toun.
If any man be fer or nye,
þat to my seruyse wyl buske hym boun,
if he wyl be trost & trye,
he schal be kyng, & were þe croun,
With rycches[t] robys in res.
wo-so to þe Werld wyl drawe,
Of God ne of good man ȝeuyt he not a hawe:
Syche a man, be londys lawe,
schal syttyn on my dees.

Voluptas. lo, me, here! redy, lord, to faryn & to fle,
to sekyn þee a seruaunt dynge & dere.
Who-so wyl with foly rewlyd be,
he is worthy to be a seruaunt here,
þat drawyth to synnys seuene.
Who-so wyl be fals & covetouse,
With þis werld he schal haue lond & house;
þis werldys wysdom ȝeuyth no[t] a louse
of God, nyn of hye heuene.
[tunc descendat in placea parita.]

Pes, pepyl! of pes we ȝou pray.
syth & sethe wel to my sawe!
Who-so wyl be ryche & in gret aray,
to-ward þe werld he schal drawe.
Who-so wyl be fals, al þat he may,
of God hym-self he hath non awe,
& lyuyn in lustis, nyth & day,
þe werld of hym wyl be ryth fawe,
do dwelle in his howse.
who-so wyl with þe werld haue his dwellynge,
& ben a lord of his clothynge,
he muste nedys, ouyr al þynge,
euere-more be couetowse:
Non est in mundo diues, qui dicit 'habundo.'

Stulticia. ȝa! couetouse he muste be,
& me, Foly, must haue in mende;
for who-so wyl alwey foly fle,
In þis werld schal ben vnthende.
þorwe werldys wysdom of gret degre,
Schal neuere man in werld moun wende,
but he haue help of me
þat am Foly fer & hende;
he muste hangyn on my hoke.
werldly wyt was neuere nout,
but with foly it were frawt;
þus þe wysman hath tawt
a-botyn in his boke:
Sapiencia penes Domini.
Voluptas. Now, all þe men þat in þis werld wold thryue,
for to rydyn on hors ful hye,
cum speke with Lust & Lykynge belyue,
& his felaw, ȝonge Foly!
late se who-so wyl vs knowe.
Who-so wyl drawe to Lykynge & Luste,
& as a fole, in foly ruste,
On vs to he may truste,
& leuyn louely, I trowe.

Malus Angelus (re-entering). How, Lust, Lykyng, & Folye!
take to me good entent!
I haue browth, be downys drye,
to þe Werld a gret present;
I haue gylyd hym ful qweyntly,
For, syn he was born, I haue hym blent;
he schal be serwaunt good & try;
a-monge ȝou his wyl is lent,
to þe Werld he wyl hym take;
For, syn he cowde wyt, I vndirstonde,
I haue hym tysyd in euery londe.
hys Goode Aungel, be strete & st[r]onde,
I haue don hym forsake.

þerfor, Lust, my trewe fere,
þou art redy al-wey I-wys;
of worldly lawys þou hym lere,
þat he were browth in werldly blys;
Loke he be ryche, þe soþe to tell;
help hym, fast he gunne to thrywe;
& whanne he wenyth best to lywe,
þanne schal he deye, & not be schrywe,
& goo with vs to hell.

Voluptas. be Satan, þou art a nobyl knawe
to techyn men fyrst fro goode!
Lust & Lykynge he schal haue;
Lechery schal ben hys fode;
Metis & drynkis he schal haue trye.
With a lykynge lady of lofte,
he schal syttyn in sendel softe,
to cachen hym to helle crofte
þat day þat he schal deye.

Stulticia. With ryche rentys I schal hym blynde,
Wyth þe werld tyl he be pytte;
& þanne schal I, longe or his ende,
make þat caytyfe to be knytte
On þe werld whanne he is set s[ore]. [Re-enter Mankind.]
Cum on, man! þou schalt not rewe,
for þou wylt be to vs trewe;
þou schalt be clad in clothis newe,
& be ryche euere-more.

Humanum Genus. Mary, felaw, gramercy!
I wolde be ryche & of gret renoun.
[Of God] I ȝeue no tale trewly,
So þat I be lord of toure & toun,
be buskys & bankys broun.
Syn þat þou wylt make me
boþe ryche of gold & fee,
goo forthe! for I wyl folow þee
be dale & euery towne.

[Trumpe vp. tunc ibunt Voluptas & Stulticia, Malus Angelus & Humanum Genus, ad Mundum, & dicat

Voluptas. How, lord! loke owt! for we haue browth
a serwant of nobyl fame; [Presents Mankind.]
of worldly good is al his þouth;
of lust & folye he hath no schame;
he wolde be gret of name,
he wolde be at gret honour,
for to rewle town & toure;
he wolde haue to his paramoure
Sum louely dynge dame.

Mundus [to Mankind]. welcum, syr, semly in syth!
þou art welcum to worthy wede,
for þou wylt be my seruaunt, day & nyth.
With my seruyse I schal þee foster & fede;
þi bak schal be betyn with besawntis bryth;
þou schalt haue byggyngys be bankis brede;
to þi cors schal knele kayser & knyth,
& ladys louely on lere,
Where þat þou walke, be sty or be strete.
but Goddys seruyse þou must forsake,
& holy to þe werld þee take,
& þanne a man I schal þee make,
þat non schal be þi pere.

Humanum Genus. ȝys, Werld, & þer-to here myn honde,
to forsake God & hys seruyse.
to medys, þou ȝeue me howse & londe,
þat I regne rychely at myn enprise
so þat I fare wel be strete & stronde
Whil I dwelle here in werldly wyse,
I reeke neuere of heuene wonde,
nor of Jhesu, þat jentyl justyse;
of my sowle I haue non rewthe.
what schulde I reckenen of domysday,
so þat I be ryche & of gret a-ray?
I schal make mery whyl I may,
& þer-to here my trewthe.

Mundus. Now sertis, syr, þou seyst wel:
I holde þee trewe ffro top to þe too.
but þou were ryche, it were gret del,
& all men þat wyl fare soo.
cum up, my serwaunt trew as stel! [tunc ascendit Humanum Genus ad Mundum.
þou schalt be ryche, where so þou goo;
men schul seruyn þee at mel
with mynstralsye & bemys blo,
with metis & drynkis trye.
lust & lykynge schal be þin ese;
louely ladys þee schal plese:
who-so do þee any disesse,
he schal ben hangyn hye.

Lykynge! be-lyue
late clothe hym swythe
In robys ryve
With ryche a-ray.
Folye! þou fonde,
be strete & stronde,
serue hym at honde
bothe nyth & day.

Voluptas. trostyly,
lord, redy,
Je vous pry,
Syr, I say.
in lyckynge & lust
he schal rust,
tyl dethys dust
do hym to day.

Stulti[cia]. & I, Folye,
schal hyen hym hye,
tyl sum enmye
hym ouer-goo.
In worldis wyt
þat in folye syt,
I þynke ȝyt
his sowle to sloo. [trumpe vp! [Exeunt.]
