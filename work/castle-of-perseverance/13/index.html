The Castle of Perseverance · c. 1425

Scene VIII: The Four Daughters of God

by Anonymous (Middle English) c. 13th–16th century

[Enter Mercy, Righteousness, Truth, Justice, and Peace.]

Misericordia. A mone I herd of mercy meve,
& to me, Mercy, gan crye & call;
but if it haue Mercy, sore it schal me greve,
For ellis it schal to helle fall.
Rythwysnes, my syster cheve,
þys ȝe herde: so dyde we all;
For we were mad frendis leve
whanne þe Jevys proferyd Criste eysyl & gall
on þe good Fryday.
God graunted þat remission,
Mercy & absolicion,
þorwe vertu of his passion,
to no man schuld be seyd 'nay.'

þerfore, my systers Rytwysnes,
Pes, & Trewthe, to ȝou I tell,
whanne man crieth mercy, & wyl not ses,
Mercy schal be his waschynge well:
wytnesse of holy kyrke.
For þe leste drope of blode
þat God bledde on þe rode,
it hadde ben satysfaccion goode
For al Mankyndys werke.
Justicia. Systyr, ȝe sey me a good skyl,
þat mercy pasyt mannys mysdede;
but take mercy, who so wyl,
he muste it aske with love & drede;
& euery man þat wyl Fulfyll
þe dedly synnys, & folw mysdede,
to graunte hem mercy, me þynkyth it no skyl;
& þerfore, systyr, ȝou I rede,
lete hym a-bye his mysdede;
For, þou he lye in hell & stynke,
it schal me neuere ouer-þynke:
as he hath browyn, lete hym drynke;
þe devyl schal qwyte hym his mede.
Vnus-quisque suum honus portabit.

trowe ȝe, þat whanne a man schal deye,
þanne, þow þat he mercy craue,
þat a-non he schal haue mercye?
nay, nay, so Crist me saue!
for, schuld no man do no good,
alle þe dayes of hys lyve,
but hope of mercy be þe rode,
schulde make boþe werre & stryve,
& torne to gret grewaunse.
who-so in hope dothe any dedly synne
to his lyvys ende, & wyl not blynne,
Rytfully þanne schal he wynne
Crystis gret vengaunse.

Veritas. Rytwysnes, my syster fre,
ȝour jugement is good & trewe;
In good fayth so þynkit me;
late hym his owyn dedis rewe.
I am 'Veritas,' & trew wyl be,
in word & werke, to olde & newe.
was neuere man, in fawte of me,
dampnyd nor savyd, but it were dew:
I am euere at mans ende.
whanne body & sowle partyn a-twynne,
þanne wey I his goode dedys & his synne;
& weyder of hem be more or mynne,
he schal it ryth sone fynde.

For I am Trewþe, & trewþe wyl bere,
as grete God hymself vs byd.
þer schal no þynge þe sowle dere,
but synne þat þe body dyd.
syth þat he deyed in þat Coueytous synne,
I, Trewþe, wyl þat he goo to pyne.
of þat synne cowde he not blynne;
þerfore he schal his sowle tyne
to þe pytte of hell.
Ellys schulde we, boþe Trewþe & Rytwysnes,
he pud to ouer mekyl dystresse,
& euery man schuld be þe wers
þat þer-of myth here tell.

Pax. Pes, my syster Verite!
I preye ȝou, Rytwysnes, be stylle;
lete no man be ȝou dampnyd be,
nor deme ȝe no man to helle.
he is on kyn tyl vs thre,
þow he haue now not al his wylle;
For his loue þat deyed on tre,
late saue Mankynde fro al peryle,
& schelde hym fro myschaunsse.
if ȝe tweyne putte hym to dystresse,
it schuld make gret hevynesse
be-twene vs tweyne, Mercy & Pes;
& þat were gret grevaunce.
Rytwysnes & Trewthe, do be my red!
& Mercy, go we to ȝone hey place! [Points to God's scaffold on the East.]
we schal enforme þe hey Godhed,
& pray hym to deme þis case.
ȝe schal tell hym ȝoure entent
of Trewthe & of Rytwysnesse;
& we schal pray þat his Jugement
May pase be vs, Mercy & Pes.
all Foure, now go we hens
wytly to þe Trinite;
&, þer schal we sone se
what þat his Jugement schal be,
with-owtyn any deffens.
tunc ascende[n]t ad Patrem omnes paritores; & dixit Verita[s:]

Veritas. heyl, God al-myth!
we cum, þi dowteris in syth,
Trewth, Mercy, & Ryth,
& Pes, pesyble in Fyth.

Misericordia. We cum to preve,
if Man, þat was þee ful leve,
If he schal cheve
to hell or heuene, be þi leve.

Justicia. I, Rytwysnes,
þi dowtyr, as I ges,
let me, neuere-þe-lesse,
at þi dom putte me in pres.

Pax. Pesyble kynge!
I, Pes, þi dowtyr ȝynge,
here my preyinge
whanne I pray þee, Lord, of a thynge.
Deus. welcum, in Fere,
bryther þanne blossum on brere!
My dowteris dere,
cum forth, & stande ȝe me nere!

Veritas. Lord, as þou art Kyng of kyngis, crownyd with crowne,
as þou lovyste me, Trewthe, þi dowtyr dere,
lete neuere me, Trewþe, to fall a-downe,
my feythfful Fadyr, saunȝ pere!
Quia veritatem dilexisti.
For in all trewthe standit þi renowne,
þi feyth, þi hope, & þi powere.
lete it be sene, Lord, now, at þi dome,
þat I may haue my trewe prayere
to do trewþe to Mankynde.
for if Mankynde be dempte be ryth,
& not be Mercy, most of myth,
here my threwthe, Lord, I þee plyth,
in presun man schal be pynyde.

Lord! whov schuld Mankynde be savyde,
Syn he dyed in dedly synne,
& all þi comaundementis he depravyde,
& of fals covetyse he wolde neuere blyne?
Aurum sitisti; Aurum bibisti.
þe more he hadde, þe more he cravyd,
whyl þe lyf lefte hym with-Inne.
but he be dampnyd, I am a-bavyd,
þat Trewthe schuld com of rytwys kynne,
& I am þi dowter Trewþe.
þou he cried mercy, moriendo,
Nimis tarde penitendo,
talem mortem reprehendo:
lete hym drynke as he brewyth!

late repentaunce, if man saue scholde,
wheyþer he wrouth wel or wyckydnesse;
þanne euery man wolde be bolde
to trespas, in trost of Forȝevenesse;
For synne in hope is dampnyd, I holde;
For-gevyn is neuere hys trespase.
He synnyth in þe Holy Gost many folde:
þat synne, Lord, þou wylt not reles,
in þis werld nor in þe toþer,
Quia veritas manet in eternum,
tendit homo ad infernum;
Nunquam venit ad supernum,
þou he were my broþer.

for man on molde halt welthe & wele,
lust & lykynge in al his lyfe,
techynge, prechynge, in euery sele;
but he forgetyth þe Lord be-lyve,
hye of hert, happe & hele,
gold & syluyr, chyld & wyf;
denteth drynke at mete & mele;
vnnethe, þee to þanke, he can not kyth
In any maner thynge.
whanne mans welþe gynnyth a-wake,
Ful sone, Lord, þou art forsake.
As he hathe browne & bake,
Trewthe wyl þat he drynke.

For if Man haue mercy & grace,
þanne I, þi dowtyr Sothfastnesse,
at þi dom schal haue no place,
but be putte a-bak be wronge dures.
Lord! lete me neuere fle þi fayr face,
to make my power any lesse!
I pray þee, Lord, as I haue space,
late Mankynde haue dew dystresse,
In helle fere to be brent.
In peyne, loke he be stylle,
Lord, if it be þi wylle,
or ellys I haue no skylle
be þi trew Jugement.
Misericordia. O pater maxime, & Deus tocius consolacionis,
qui consolatur nos in omni tribulacione nostra!
O þou Fadyr, of mytys moste,
Mercyful God in Trinite!
I am þi dowter, wel þou woste,
& mercy fro heuene þou browtyst fre.
schew me þi grace in euery coste!
In þis cas my counforte be!
lete me, Lord, neuere be loste
at þi Jugement, whov-so it be,
of Mankynde.
Ne had mans synne neuere cum in cas,
I, Mercy, schuld neuere in erthe had plas;
þerfore graunte me, Lord, þi grace,
þat Mankynde may me fynde;

& mercy, Lord, haue on þis man,
aftyr þi mercy, þat mekyl is;
vn-to þi grace þat he be tan;
of þi mercy þat he not mys!
as þou descendyst fro þi trone,
& lyth in a maydyns wombe I-wys,
In-carnat was in blod & bone,
lat Mankynde cum to þi blys,
as þou art Kynge of Heuene!
For werldly veyn-glory
he hath ben ful sory,
Punchyd in purgatory
for all þe synnys seuene.

Si pro Peccato vetus Adam non cecidisset,
Mater pro nato nunquam grauidada fuisset.
Ne had Adam synnyd here be-fore,
& þi hestis in paradys had offent,
Neuere of þi moder þou schuldyst a be bore,
Fro heuene to erthe to haue be sent.
but xxxti wynter here, & more,
bowndyn, & betyn, & al to-schent,
Scornyd & scovrgyd, sadde & sore,
& on þe rode rewly rent,
Passus sub Pilato Poncio.
as þou henge on þe croys,
on hye þou madyste a voys,
mans helthe, þe gospel seys,
whanne þou seydyst 'scicio.' scilicet, salutem animarum.

þane þe Jeves þat were vnquert
dressyd þee drynke, Eysyl & galle:
it to taste, þou myth nowth styrt,
but sayd, 'consummatum est' was alle.
a knyt, with a spere so smert,
whanne þou forgafe þi fomen þrall,
he stonge þe Lord vn-to þe hert.
þanne watyr & blod gan ovte wall,
Aqua bap[t]ismatis & sanguis redempcionis.
þe watyr of Baptoum,
þe blod of redempcioun,
þat fro þin herte ran doun,
est causa saluacionis.

Lord, þou þat man hathe don more mysse þanne good,
if he dey in very contricioun,
Lord, þe lest drope of þi blod,
For hys synne makyth satisfaccioun.
as þou deydyst, Lord, on þe Rode,
graunt me my peticioun!
lete me, Mercy, be hys Fode,
& graunte hym þi saluacion,
quia dixisti 'misericordiam amabo.'
Mercy schal I synge & say,
& 'miserere' schal I pray
For Mankynde euere & ay;
Misericordias domini in eternum cantabo.
Justicia. Rythwys kynge, Lord God almyth!
I am þi dowter Rythwysnesse.
þou hast louyd me euere, day & nyth,
as wel as oþer, as I gesse:
Justicias Dominus justicia dilexit.
Iff þou mans kynde Fro peyne a-quite,
þou dost a-geyns þyne owyn processe.
lete hym in prison to be pyth
For his synne & wyckydnesse,
of a bone I þee pray.
Ful oftyn he hathe þee, Lord, for-sake,
& to þe devyl he hathe hym take;
lete hym lyn in helle lake,
dampnyd for euere & ay.
Quia Deum, qui se genuit, dereliquit.

For whanne man to þe werld was born,
he was browth to holy kyrke,
Feythly followd in þe funte ston,
& wesch fro orygynal synne so dyrke;
Satanas, he forsok as his fone;
all his pompe & al his werke,
& hyth to serue þee a-lone;
to kepe þi commandementis he schuld not irke,
Sicut Iusti tui.
but whanne he was com to mans a-state,
all his behestis he þanne for-gate:
he is worþi be dampnyd for þat,
quia oblitus est Deum creatoris sui.

for he hathe for-getyn þee þat hym wrout,
& formydiste hym lyke þyne owyn face,
& with þi precyous blod hym bowth,
& in þis world þou ȝeue hym space:
all þi benefetis he set at nowth,
but toke hym to þe deuelys trase,
þe fl[e]sch, þe world, was most in Is þowth,
& purpose to plese hem in euery plase,
so grymly on grounde.
I pray þee, Lord lovely,
of man haue no mercy!
but, dere Lord, lete hym ly!
In hell lete hym be bounde!

man hathe forsake þe Kynge of Heuene
& his Good Aungels gouernaunce,
& solwyd hes sovle with synnys seuene
be his Badde Aungels comberaunce.
Vertuis, he putte ful evyn a-way
whanne Coveytyse gan hym a-vaunce;
he wende þat he schulde a levyd ay,
tyl dethe trypte hym on his daunce,
he loste his wyttis fyve.
ouyr late he callyd confescion;
ouer lyt was his contricion;
he made neuere satisfaccion;
dampne hym to helle be-lyve!

For if þou take mans sowle to þee
a-geyns þi rythwysnesse,
þou dost wronge, Lorde, to Trewth & me,
& puttis us fro oure devnesse.
Lord! lete vs neuere fro þee fle,
Ner streyne vs neuere in stresse,
but late þi dom be by vs thre,
Mankynde in hell to presse,
Lord, I þee be-seche!
For Rythwynes dwellis euere sure,
to deme man aftyr his deseruiture;
for, to be dampnyd, it is his vre;
on man I crie wreche.
letabitur justus cum viderit vindictam.
Misericordia. Mercy, my syster Rythwysnes!
þou schape Mankynde no schonde!
leve syster, lete be þi dresse!
to saue man, lete vs fonde!
For if man be dampnyd to hell dyrknes,
þanne myth I wryngyn myn honde,
þat euere my state schulde be les,
my fredam to make bonde;
Mankynd is of oure kyn.
For I, Mercy, pase al[le] thynge
þat God made at þe begynnynge,
& I am his dowter ȝynge:
dere syster, lete be þi dyn!
Et misericordia eius super omnia opera eius.

of Mankynde aske þou neuere wreche
be day ner be nyth,
for God hym-self hath ben his leche,
of his mercyful myth;
to me he gan hym be-teche,
be-syde al his ryth.
for hym wyl I prey & preche,
to gete hym fre respyth,
& my syster Pese.
for his mercy is with-out be-gynnynge,
& schal be with-outyn endynge,
as David seyth, þat worthy kynge:
in scripture is no les.
Et misericordia eius a progenie in progenies, & cetera.

Veritas. Mercy is Mankynd non worthy,
Dauid þou þou recorde & rede;
For he wolde neuere þe hungry
neyþer clothe nor fede,
Ner drynke gyf to þe þrysty,
nyn pore men helpe at nede;
For if he dyd non of þese, for-þy
In heuene he getyth no mede;
so seyth þe gospel.
for he hathe be vnkynde
to lame & to blynde,
In helle he schal be pynde:
so is resun & skyl.

Pax. Pesible Kyng in maieste!
I, Pes, þi dowter, aske þee a bone
of man, whou-so it be.
Lord, graunte me myn askynge sone,
þat I may euermore dwelle with þee,
as I haue euere ȝyt done,
& lat me neuere fro þee fle,
specialy at þi dome
of man, þi creature.
þou my sysers, Ryth & Trewthe,
of Mankynde haue non rewthe,
Mercy & I ful sore vs mewythe
to cacche hym to our cure.

For whanne þou madyst erthe & hevyn,
Ten orderis of aungelys to ben in blys,
Lucyfer, lyter þanne þe leuyn,
tyl whanne he synnyd, he fel I-wys.
to restore þat place Ful evyn,
þou madyst Mankynde with þys,
to Fylle þat place þat I dyd nevene,
if þy wyl be resun it is,
In pes & rest,
amonge þyne aungels bryth,
to worchep þee In syth,
graunt, Lord God al-myth!
& so I holde it best.

For þou Truthe, þat is my syster dere,
arguyth þat man schuld dwell in wo
& Rytwysnes, with hyr powere,
wolde fayn & fast þat it were so,
but Mercy & I, Pes, bothe in fere,
schal neuere in feyth a-corde þer-to;
þanne schuld we euere dyscorde here,
& stande at bate for frend or foo,
& euere at dystaunce.
þerfore my counseyl is,
lete vs foure systeris kys,
& restore Man to blys,
as was Godis ordenaunce.
Misericordia & Veritas obuiauerunt, sed Justicia & Pax osculate sunt.

For if ȝe, Ryth & Truthe, schuld haue ȝour wylle,
I, Pes, & Mercy, schuld euere haue trauest;
þanne vs be-twene had bene a gret perylle,
þat oure joyes in heuene schuld a ben lest;
þerfore, gentyl Systeris, consentyth me tyll,
ellys betwene oure self schuld neuere be rest.
where schuld be luf & charite, late þer cum non ille!
loke oure joyes be parfyth, & þat I holde þe best
In heuene-ryche blys,
For þer is pes with-owtyn were;
þere is rest with-owtyn Fere;
þer is charite with-owtyn dere;
our Faderis wyll so is:
'hic pax, hic bonitas, hic laus, hic semper honestas.'

þerfore, jentyl systeris, at on word,
Truth, Ryth & Mercy hende,
lete us stonde at on a-cord,
at pes with-owtyn ende!
late loue & charyte be at oure bord,
alle veniauns awey wende,
to heuene þat man may be restoryd;
lete us be all hys frende
be-fore oure Faders face!
we schal deuoutly pray,
at dredful domysday,
& I schal for vs say
þat Mankynde schal haue grace.
'Et tuam, Deus, deposamus pietatem, ut ei tribuere digneris lucidas & qui[etas ? MS.] mansiones.'

Lord, for þi pyte, & þat pes
þou sufferyst in þi pascioun,
boundyn & betyn, with-out les,
fro þe fote to þe croun,
'tanquam ouis ductus es.'
whanne 'gutte sangu[in]is' ran adoun,
ȝyt þe Jves wolde not ses,
but on þyn hed þei þryst a croun,
& on þe cros þee naylyd.
as petously as þou were pynyd,
haue mercy of Mankynde,
so þat he may Fynde
oure prayer may hym a-vayle!

Pater sedens in trono. Ego cogito cogitaciones pacis, non afflicionis.
Fayre falle þee, Pes, my dowter dere!
on þee I þynke, & on Mercy.
Syn ȝe a-cordyd beth all in fere,
my Jugement I wyl ȝeue ȝou by,
not aftyr deseruynge, to do reddere,
to dampne Mankynde to turmentry,
but brynge hym to my blysse ful clere,
In heuene to dwelle endelesly,
at ȝour prayere for þi.
to make my blysse perfyth,
I menge with my most myth,
alle pes, sum treuthe, & sum Ryth,
& most of my mercy.
Misericordia Domini plena est terra. Amen! Dicat filiabus:
My dowters hende,
lufly & lusti to lende,
goo to ȝone fende,
& fro hym take Mankynde!
brynge hym to me!
& set hym here be my kne,
In heuene to be,
in blysse with gamyn & gle.

Veritas. We schal fulfylle
þin hestis, as resun & skylle,
Fro ȝone gost grylle,
Mankynde to brynge þee tylle.
[tunc ascendent ad Malum Angelum omnes paritores; & dicat

Pax. A, þou foule wyth!
lete go þat soule so tyth!
In he[ue]ne lyth,
Mankynde sone schal be pyth.

Justicia. Go þou to helle,
þou devyl bold as a belle,
þer-In to dwelle,
In bras & brimston to welle!
tunc ascendant ad tronum.

Misericordia. lo here Mankynde,
lyter þanne lef is on lynde!
þat hath ben pynyd,
þi mercy, Lord, lete hym fynde!

Pater sedens in Judi[cio]. Sicut sintille in medio maris. . . .
My mercy, Mankynde, ȝeue I þee.
Cum, syt at my ryth honde!
Ful wel haue I louyd þee,
Vnkynde þow I þee fonde.
as a sparke of fyre in þe se,
My mercy is synne quenchande:
þou hast cause to love me
a-bovyn al thynge in lande,
& kepe my comaundement.
If þou me loue & drede,
heuene schal be þi mede;
my face þee schal fede:
þis is myn Jugement.
'Ego occidam & viuificabo, percuciam & sanabo; & nemo est qui de manu mea possit eruere.'

kyng, kayser, knyt & kampyoun,
Pope, patriark, prest, & prelat in pes,
duke dowtyest in dede be dale & be doun,
lytyl & mekyl, þe more & þe les,
all þe statis of þe world, is at myn renoun;
to me schal þei ȝeue a-compt at my dygne des.
whanne Myhel his horn blowith at my dred dom,
þe count of here conscience schal putten hem in pres,
& ȝelde a reknynge
of here space whou þey han spent;
& of here trew talent,
at my gret Jugement,
an answere schal me brynge.

'Ecce! requiram gregem meum de manu pastorum.'
& I schal Inquire of my flok & of here pasture,
whou þey haue leuyd, & led here peple soiet.
þe goode, on þe ryde syd schal stond ful sure;
þe badde on þe lyfte syd, þer schal I set.
þe vij dedis of mercy, who-so hadde vre
to Fylle,—þe hungry for to geue mete,
or drynke to þrysty; þe nakyd, vesture;
þe pore or þe pylgrym, hom for to fette
þi neybour þat hath nede.
who-so doth mercy to his myth,
to þe seke, or in presun pyth,
he doth to me: I schal hym qvyth:
heuene blys schal be his mede.

'Et qui bona egerunt, ibunt in vitam eternam: qui vero mala, in ignem eternum.'
& þei þat wel do in þis werld here, welthe schal a-wake;
In heuene þei schal heynyd [be] in bounte & [in] blys;
& þei þat evyl do, þei schul to helle lake,
In bytter balys to be brent: my jugement it is.
My vertus in heuene þanne schal þei qwake:
þer is no wyth in þis werld þat may skape þis.
all men example here-at may take,
to mayntein þe goode, & mendyn here mys;
þus endyth oure gamys!
To saue ȝou fro synnynge,
Evyr at þe begynnynge
Thynke on ȝoure last endynge!
Te, Deum, laudamus! [Exeunt.]
