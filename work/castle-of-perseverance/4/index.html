The Castle of Perseverance · c. 1425

Scene IV: On the Castle-Green

by Anonymous (Middle English) c. 13th–16th century

(Enter Mankind, with a Good Angel on his right, and a Bad Angel on his left.)

Humanum Genus. aftyr oure forme faderis kende,
þis nyth I was of my moder born.
Fro my moder I walke, I wende;
Ful feynt & febyl, I fare ȝou be-forn;
I am nakyd of lym & lende,
as mankynde is schapyn & schorn;
I not wedyr to gon ne to lende,
to helpe my-self mydday nyn morn:
for schame I stonde & schende.
I was born þis nyth in blody ble,
& nakyd I am, as ȝe may se.
a! Lord God in trinite!
Whow Mankende is vnthende!

Where-to I was to þis werld browth,
I ne wot; but to woo & wepynge
I am born, & haue ryth nowth
to helpe my self in no doynge.
I stonde & stodye, al ful of þowth;
bare & pore is my clothynge;
a sely crysme, myn hed hath cawth,
þat I tok at myn crystenynge:
certis, I haue no more.
of erthe I cam, I wot ryth wele;
&, as erthe, I stande þis sele;
of mankende it is gret dele.
Lord God, I crye þyne ore!

ij aungels bene a-synyd to me;
þe ton techyth me to goode:
on my ryth syde ȝe may hym se;
he cam fro Criste þat deyed on rode.
a-noþer is ordeynyd her to be,
þat is my foo, be fen & flode;
he is a-bout, in euery degre,
to drawe me to þo dewylys wode,
þat in helle ben thycke.
swyche to, hath euery man on lyue,
to rewlyn hym & hys wyttis fyue:
whanne man doth ewyl, þe ton wolde schryue;
þe tother drawyth to wycke.

but syn þese aungelys be to me falle,
Lord Jhesu! to ȝou I bydde a bone,
þat I may folwe, be strete & stalle,
þe aungyl þat cam fro heuene trone.
now, Lord Jhesu! in heuene halle,
here, whane I make my mone!
Coryows Criste, to ȝou I calle;
as a grysly gost, I grucche & grone,
I wene, ryth ful of thowth.
a! Lord Jhesu! wedyr may I goo?
a crysyme I haue, & no moo.
alas! men may be wondyr woo
Whanne þei be fyrst forth browth.

Bonus Angelus. ȝa, forsothe; & þat is wel sene:
of woful wo, man may synge,
for iche creature helpith hym-self be-dene,
Saue only man at hys comynge.
neuyr-þe-lesse, turne þee fro tene,
& serue Jhesu, heuene kynge,
& þou schalt, be greuys grene,
fare wel in alle thynge
þat Lord þi lyfe hath lante.
haue hym alway in þi mynde,
þat deyed on rode for mankynde,
& serue hym to þi lyfes ende,
& sertis þou schalt not wante!

Malus Angelus. Pes, aungel! þi wordis are not wyse!
þou counselyst hym not a-ryth;
he schal hym drawyn to þe werldis seruyse,
to dwelle with caysere, kynge, & knyth,
þat in londe be hym non lyche.
Cum on with me, stylle as ston!
þou & I, to þe werd schul goon,
& þanne þou schalt sen a-non
whow sone þou schalt be ryche.

Bonus Angelus. A, pes, aungel! þou spekyst folye!
Why schuld he coueyt werldis goode,
syn Criste in erthe, & hys meynye,
all in pouert here þei stode?
werldis wele, be strete & stye,
Faylyth & fadyth, as fysch in flode;
but he[ue]ne-ryche is good & trye,
þer Criste syttyht, bryth as blode,
With-outyn any dystresse.
to þe world, wolde he not flyt,
but forsok it euery whytt:
example I fynde in holy wryt,
he wyl bere me wytnesse:
diuicias & paupertates ne dederis mihi, Domine.

Malus Angelus. ȝa, ȝa, man! leue hym nowth,
but cum with me, be stye & strete!
haue þou a gobet of þe werld cawth,
þou schalt fynde it good & swete.
a fayre lady, þee schal be tawth,
þat in bowre þi bale schal bete;
with ryche rentis þou schalt be frawth;
with sylke sendel to syttyn in sete.
I rede, late Bedys be!
If þou wylt haue wel þyn hele,
& faryn wel at mete & mele,
with Goddis seruyse may þou not dele,
but cum & folwe me.

Humanum Genus. Whom to folwe, wetyn I ne may:
I stonde in stodye, & gynne to raue;
I wolde be ryche in gret a-ray,
& fayn I wolde my sowle saue:
as wynde in watyr I wave. [To the Bad Angel.]
þou woldyst, to þe werld I me toke;
& he wolde þat I it for-soke.
now, so God me helpe, & þe holy boke,
I not wyche I may haue.

Malus Angelus. Cum on, man! where-of hast þou care?
go we to þe werld, I rede þee blyue;
for þer þou schalt mow ryth wel fare,
In case if þou þynke for to thryue;
no lord schal be þee lyche.
take þe werld to þine entent,
& late þi loue be þer-on lent;
With gold & syluyr, & ryche rent,
a-none þou schalt be ryche.

Humanum Genus. now, syn þou hast be-hetyn me so,
I wyl go with þee & a-say;
I ne lette, for frende i.e fo,
but with þe Werld I wyl go play,
certis, a lytyl þrowe.
In þis World is al my trust,
to lyuyn in lykyng & in lust:
haue he & I onys cust,
we schal not part, I trowe.

Bonus Angelus. a! nay! man! for Cristis blod,
cum a-gayn be strete & style!
þe Werld is wyckyd, & ful wod,
& þou schalt leuyn but a whyle.
What coueytyst þou to wynne?
man! þynke on þyn endynge day
Whanne þou schalt be closyd vnder clay!
& if þou thenke of þat a-ray,
certis þou schalt not synne.
homo, memento finis! & in eternum non peccabis.

Malus Angelus. ȝa, on þi sowle þou schalt þynke al be tyme.
Cum forth, man, & take non hede!
Cum on, & þou schalt holdyn hym Inne;
þi flesch þou schalt foster & fede
with lofly lyuys fode.
With þe World þou mayst be bold
tyl þou be sexty wynter hold.
wanne þi nose waxit cold,
þanne mayst þou drawe to goode.
Humanum Genus. I vow to God, & so I may
Make mery a ful gret throwe;
I may leuyn many a day;
I am but ȝonge, as I trowe,
for to do þat I schulde.
Myth I ryde be sompe & syke,
& be ryche, & lord [i-]lyke,
certis þanne schulde I be Fryke,
& a mery man on molde.

Malus Angelus. ȝys, be my feyth, þou schalt be a lord,
& ellys hange me be þe hals!
but þou muste be at myn a-cord;
oþer whyle þou muste be fals
a-monge kythe & kynne.
Now go we forth, swythe a-non!
to þe Werld us must gon;
& bere þee manly euere a-mong,
Whanne þou comyst out or Inne.

Humanum Genus. ȝys, & ellys haue þou my necke.
but I be manly be downe & dyche;
& þou I be fals, I ne recke,
with so þat I be lord [i-]lyche,
I folwe þee as I can.
þou schalt be my bote of bale;
for, were I ryche of holt & hale,
þanne wolde I ȝeue neuere tale
of God ne of good man. [Exeunt Mal. Ang. and Hum. Gen.]

Bonus Angelus. I weyle, & wrynge & make mone!
þis man, with woo schal be pylt.
I sye sore, & grysly grone,
for hys folye schal make hym spylt;
I not weder to gone.
Mankynde hath forsakyn me!
alas, man, for loue of the!
ȝa, for þis gamyn & þis gle,
þou schalt grocchyn & grone. [Exit.] [pipe vp, mu[sic].]
