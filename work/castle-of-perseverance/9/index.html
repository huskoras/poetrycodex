The Castle of Perseverance · c. 1425

Scene VI: The Seven Deadly Sins and the Castle (part 4 of 6)

by Anonymous (Middle English) c. 13th–16th century

Caro. Roy Bakbytynge,
ful redy in robys to rynge!
ful glad tydynge,
be Belyalys bonys, I trow þow brynge.

Detraccio. ȝa, for God, owt I crye
on þi too sonys & þi dowtyr ȝynge:
Glotoun, Slawthe, & Lechery,
hath put me in gret mornynge.
þey let Mankynde gon up hye
In-to ȝene castel at hys lykynge,
þer-in for to leue & dye,
with þo ladys to make endynge,
þe flouris fayre & fresche.
he is in þe Castel of Perseuerauns,
& put hys body to penauns;
of hard happe is now þi chauns,
Syr kynge, Mankyndys flesche. [tunc Caro clamabit ad Gulam, Accidiam, & Luxuriam.

Luxuria. Sey now þi wylle!
Syr Flesch, why cryest þou so schylle?

Caro. A, Leechery, þou skallyd mare!
& þou Gloton, God ȝeue þee wo!
& vyle Slawth, euyl mote þou fare!
Why lete ȝe Mankynde fro ȝou go
In ȝone Castel so hye?
euele grace com on þi snowte!
now I am dressyd in gret dowte.
Why nad ȝe lokyd betyr a-bowte?
be Belyalys bonys, ȝe shul a-bye! [tunc uerberant eos in placeam.

Detraccio. Now, be God, þis is good game!
I, Bakbyter, now bere me wel.
if I had lost my name,
I vow to God it were gret del.
I schape þese schrewys to mekyl schame:
iche rappyth on oþer with rowtynge rele;
I, Bakbyter, with fals fame
Do brekyn & brestyn hodys of stele;
þorwe þis cuntre I am knowe.
now wyl I gynne forth to goo,
& make Coueytyse haue a knoke or too;
& þanne I-wys I haue doo
my deuer, as I trowe. [ad Mundum.

heyl, styf in stounde!
heyl, gayly gyrt up on grounde!
heyl, fayre flowr I founde!
heyl, syr Werld, worþi in wodis wonde!

Mundus. Bakbyter in rowte!
þou tellyst talys of dowte,
So styf & so stowte.
what tydyngis bryngyst þou a-bowte?

Detraccio. No-þynge goode: þat schalt þou wete.
Mankynde, syr Werld, hath þee for-sake;
with Schryfte & Penauns he is smete,
& to ȝene Castel he hath hym take,
a-monge ȝene ladys, whyt as lak[e].
lo, syr Werld! ȝe mown a-gryse
þat ȝe be seruyd on þis wyse.
go pley ȝou with syr Coueytyse
tyl his crowne crake! [tunc buccinabunt cornuo ad Auariciam.

Auaricia (entering). Syr bolnynge bowde,
tell me why blowe ȝe so lowde!

Mundus. lowde losel! þe deuel þee brenne!
I prey God ȝeue þee a fowl hap!
sey, why letyst þou Mankynde
In-to ȝene castel for to skape!
I trow þou gynnyst to raue.
now, for Mankynde is went,
al oure game is schent:
þerfore, a sore dryuynge dent,
harlot, þou shalt haue! [tunc uerberant eum.

Auaricia. Mercy! mercy! I wyl no more;
þou hast me rappyd with rewly rowtis!
I snowre, I sobbe, I sye sore!
myn hed is clateryd al to clowtis!
In al ȝoure state I schal ȝou store,
if ȝe abate ȝoure dyntis dowtis.
Mankynde, þat ȝe haue for-lore,
I schal do com owt fro ȝone skowtis
to ȝoure hende hall.
if ȝe wyl no more betyn me,
I schal do Mankynde com out fre;
he schal for-sake, as þou schalt se,
þe fayre vertus all.

Mundus. Haue do þanne! þe deuyl þee tere!
þou schalt ben hangyn in hell herne.
by-lyue, my baner up þou bere,
& be-sege we þe castel ȝerne,
Mankynde for to stele.
whanne Mankynde growyth good,
I, þe Werld, am wylde & wod;
þo bycchys schul bleryn in here blood,
with Flappys felle & fele.

ȝerne lete flapyr up my fane,
& schape we schance & schonde!
I schal brynge with me þo bycchys bane;
þer schal no vertus dwellyn in my londe.
Mekenes is þat modyr þat I mene:
to hyre I brewe a bytter bonde;
Sche schal dey up-on þis grene,
if þat sche com al in myn honde,
ȝeue rappokis with here rumpys.
I am þe Werld! it is my wyll,
þe Castel of Vertu for to spyll.
Howtyth hye up-on ȝene hyll,
ȝe traytours in ȝoure trumpys! [tunc Mundus, Cupiditas, & Stulticia ibunt ad castellum cum vexillo & domino Demon.

Belyal. I here trumpys trebelen al of tene
þe worþi Werld walkyth to werre,
for to clyuyn ȝone Castel clene,
þe maydnys meyndys for to merre.
sprede my penon up on a prene,
& stryke we forthe now vndyr sterre!
schapyth now ȝoure scheldys schene,
ȝene skallyd skoutis for to skerre
up-on ȝone grene grese!
buske ȝou now, boyes, be-lyue! tunc mutabit.
for euere I stonde in mekyl stryue;
whyl Mankynde is in clene lyue,
I am neuere wel at ese.

make ȝou redy, alle þre,
bolde battyl for to bede!
to ȝone feld[e] lete us fle,
& bere my baner forthe on brede!
to ȝone castel wyl I te;
þo mameryng modrys schul haue here mede.
but þei ȝeld[yn] up to me,
with byttyr balys þei schul blede;
of here reste I schal hem reue.
In woful watrys I schal hem wasche.
haue don, felaus! & take ȝoure trasche,
& wende we þedyr on a rasche,
þat castel for to cleue.

Superbia. Now, now! now, go now!
on hye hyllys lete us howte—
for in pride is al my prow—
þi bolde baner to bere a-bowte.
to Golyas I make a vow
for to schetyn ȝone Iche skowte.
on hyr ars, raggyd & row,
I schal boþe clatyr & clowte,
& ȝeue Meknesse myschanse.
Belyal bryth! it is þyn hest
þat I, Pryde, goo þee nest,
& bere þi baner beforn my brest
with a comly contenaunce.

Caro. I here an hydowse whwtynge on hyt:
be-lyue byd my baner forth for to blase!
wahanne I syt in my sadyl, it is a selkowth syt;
I gape as a Gogmagog whanne I gynne to gase:
þis worthy, wylde werld, I wagge with a wyt;
ȝone rappokis I ruble, & al to-rase;
boþe with schot & with slynge I caste with a sleyt,
with care to ȝone castel to crachen & to crase
In Flode.
I am mans flesch: where I go
I am mans most fo;
I-wys I am euere wo
whane he drawyth to goode.

þerfor, ȝe bolde boyes, buske ȝou a-bowte!
scharply on scheldis, ȝour schaftys ȝe scheuere!
& Lechery ledron, schete þou a skoute!
help we, Mankynde fro ȝone castel to keuere!
helpe! we mon hym wynne.
schete we all at a schote,
with gere þat we cunne best note,
to chache Mankynde fro ȝene cote
In-to dedly synne.

Gula. Lo, syr Flesch, whov I fare to þe felde,
with a faget on myn hond, for to settyn on a fyre!
with a wrethe of þe wode, wel I can me welde;
with a longe launce, þe loselys I schal lere.
go we with oure gere!
þe bycchys schul bleykyn & blodyr,
I schal makyn swyche a powdyr,
boþe with smoke & with somodyr,
þei schul schytyn for fere. [tunc descendunt in placea.

Malus Angelus. 'as armys,' as an heyward, hey now I howte.
Deuyl dyth þee as a duke, to do þe damyselys dote! [dicat ad Belyal.
Belyal, as a bolde boy, þi brodde I bere a-bowte:
helpe to cache Mankynde fro caytyfys cote!
Pryd! put out þi penon of raggys & of rowte!
Do þis modyr Mekenes meltyn to mote!
Wrethe! prefe Paciens, þe skallyd skowte!
Envye, to Charyte, schape þou a schote
Ful ȝare!
With Pryde, Wrethe & Envye,
þese deuelys, be downys drye,
as comly kynge, I dyscrye,
Mankynde to kachyn to care. [ad Carnem.

Flesch, frele & fresche, frely fed!
with Gloton, Slawthe & Lechery, mans sowle þou slo!
as a duke dowty, do þee to be dred;
gere þee with geris fro toppe to þe too!
kyth þis day þou art a kynge frely fedde! [to Glutton]
Gloton! sle þou Abstyne[n]sce with wyckyd woo!
with Chastyte, þou Lechour, be not ouyr-ledde!
Slawthe! bete þou Besynes on buttokys bloo!
do now þi crafte, in coste to be knowe! [ad Mundum.
worthy, wytty, & wys, wondyn in wede!
lete Coueytyse karpyn, cryen & grede!
here ben bolde bacheleris, batyl to bede,
Mankynde to tene, as I trowe.
Humanum Genus. þat dynge duke þat deyed on rode,
þis day, my sowle kepe & safe!
whanne Mankynde drawyth to goode,
be-holde what enmys he schal haue!
þe Werld, þe Deuyl, þe Flesche, arn wode;
to men ben casten a careful kaue;
byttyr balys þei brekyn on brode,
Mankynde in wo to weltyr & waue,
lordyngis, sothe to sey.
þerfore Iche man be-war of þis!
for, whyl Mankynde clene is,
his enmys schul temptyn hym to don a-mys,
if þei mown, be any wey.

Omne gaudium existimate, cum variis temtacionibus insideritis.
þerfor, lordys, beth now glad,
with elmes dede & orysoun
for to don as oure Lord bad!
styfly with-stonde ȝoure temptacyoun!
with þis foul fende I am ner mad.
to batayle þei buskyn hem bown.
certis, I schuld ben ouer-lad,—
but þat I am in þis castel town,—
with synnys sore & smerte.
who so wyl leuyn oute of dystresse,
& ledyn hys lyf in clennesse,
In þis castel of vertu & of goodnesse
hym muste haue hole his hert.
delectari in domino, & dabit tibi peticiones cordis tui.

Bonus Angelus. A! Mekenesse, Charyte & Pacyens,—
prymrose pleyeth parlasent,—
Chastyte, Besynes, & Abstynens,
myn hope, ladys, in ȝou is lent!
so come paramourys, swetter þanne sens,
Rode as rose on rys I-rent!
þis day ȝe dyth a good defens!
whyl Mankynde is in good entent,
his þoutis arn vn-hende.
Mankynde is browt in-to þis walle,
In freelte to fadyn & falle;
þerfore, ladys, I pray ȝou alle,
helpe þis day Mankynde!

Humilitas. God, þat syttyth in heuene on hy,
saue al Mankynde be se & sonde!
lete hym dwellyn here, & ben vs by,
& we schul puttyn to hym helpynge honde.
ȝyt forsoþe neuere I sy
þat any fawte in vs he fonde,
but þat we sauyd hym fro synne sly,
if he wolde be us styfly stonde
In þis castel of ston.
þerfor drede þee not, mans aungel dere!
if he wyl dwellyn with vs here,
Fro seuene synnys we schul hym were,
& his enmys Ichon.

now, my seuene systerys swete, [To the Seven Virtues.]
þis day fallyth on us þe lot,
Mankynde for to schylde & schete
fro dedly synne & schamely schot.
hys enmys strayen in þe strete,
to spylle man with spetows spot;
þerfor oure flouris, lete now flete,
& kepe we hym, as we haue het,
amonge vs in þis halle.
þerfor, vij systeris swote,
lete oure vertus reyne on rote!
þis day we wyl be mans bote
a-geyns þese deuelys alle.

Belyal. þis day, þe vaward wyl I holde.
a-vaunt my baner, precyous Pride,
Mankynde to cache to karis colde!
bold batayl now wyl I byde.
buske ȝou, boyes, on brede!
alle men þat be with me wytholde,
boþe þe ȝonge & þe olde,
Envye, Wrathe, ȝe boyes bolde,
to rounde rappys ȝe rape, I rede!

Superbia. As armys, Mekenes! I brynge þi bane,
al with pride, peyntyd & pyth.
what seyst þou, faytour? be myn fayr fane,
with robys rounde, rayed ful ryth,
grete gounse, I schal þee gane!
to marre þee, Mekenes, with my myth.
no werldly wyttys here ar wane;
lo! þi castel is al be-set!
moderis! whov schul ȝe do?
Mekenes! ȝelde þee to me, I rede.
myn name in londe is precyous Prede;
myn bolde baner to þee I bede:
modyr! what seyste þer-to?

Humilitas. a-geyns þi baner of pride & bost,
a baner of meknes & mercy
I putte ageyns pride, wel þou wost,
þat schal schende þi careful cry.
þis meke Kyng is knowyn in euery cost,
þat was croysyd on Caluary.
whanne he cam down fro heuene ost,
& lytyd with mekenes in Mary,
þis Lord þus lytyd lowe.
whanne he cam fro þe Trynyte,
In-to a maydyn lytyd he,
& al was for to dystroye þee:
Pride, þis schalt þou knowe.
Deposuit potentes de sede, & cetera.
for, whanne Lucyfer to helle fyl,
Pride, þer-of þou were chesun;
& þou, deuyl, with wyckyd wyl,
In paradys trappyd us with tresun,
so þou us bond in balys Ille:
þis may I preue be ryth resun.
tyl þis Duke þat dyed on hylle,
in heuene man myth neuere han sesun;
þe gospel þus declaryt.
for who-so lowe hym, schal ben hy;
þerfore þou schalt not comen us ny;
& þou þou be neuere so sly,
I schal felle al þi fare.
qui se exaltat, humiliabitur, & cetera.

Ira. Dame Pacyens! what seyst þou to Wrathe & Ire?
putte Mankynde fro þi castel clere,
or I schal tappyn at þi tyre
with styffe stonys þat I haue here.
I schal slynge at þee many a vyre,
& ben a-vengyd hastely here:
þus Belsabub, oure gret syre,
bad me brenne þee with wyld fere,
þou bycche blak as kole.
þerfor, fast, fowle skowte,
putte Mankynde to us owte,
or of me þou schalt haue dowte,
þou modyr, þou motyhole!

Paciencia. fro þi dowte, Crist me schelde
þis Iche day, & al mankynde!
þou wrecchyd wreche, wood & wylde,
Pacyens schal þee schende!
quia ira viri, justicia Dei non operatur.
for Marys sone, meke & mylde,
rent þee up, rote & rynde,
whanne he stod meker þanne a chylde,
& lete boyes hym betyn & bynde:
þerfor, wrecche, be stylle!
for þo pelouris þat gan hym pose,
he myth a dreuyn hem to dros;
& ȝyt, to casten hym on þe cros,
he sufferyd al here wylle.

þowsentis of aungellis he myth han had,
to a wrokyn hym þer ful ȝerne;
& ȝyt, to deyen he was glad,
us, pacyens to techyn & lerne.
þerfor, boy, with þi boystous blad,
fare a-wey be feldys ferne!
for I wyl do as Jhesu bad,
wrecchys, fro my wonys werne
with a dyngne defens.
if þou fonde to comyn a-lofte,
I schal þee cache fro þis crofte
with þese rolys swete & softe,
peyntyd with pacyens.

Inuidia. Out! myn herte gynnyth to breke,
for Charyte þat stondyth so stowte.
alas! myn herte gynnyth to wreke.
ȝelde up þis castel, þou hore clowte!
it is myn offyce, fowle to speke,
fals sklaundrys to bere a-bowte.
Charyte! þe deuyl mote þee cheke,
but I þee rappe with rewly rowte,
þi targe for to tere.
let Mankynde cum to us doun,
or I schal schetyn to þis castel town
a ful fowle defamacyoun;
þerfore þis bowe I bere.
Caritas. þou þou speke wycke & fals[e] fame,
þe wers schal I neuere do my dede.
who-so peyryth falsly a-noþer mans name,
Cristys curs he schal haue to mede:
ve homini illi per quem scandalum ven[it].
who-so wyl not hys tunge tame,—
take it sothe, as mes crede,—
wo, wo, to hym, & mekyl schame!
In holy wrytte þis I rede;
for euere þou art a schrewe.
þou þou speke euyl, I ne ȝeue a gres;
I schal do neuere þe wers;
At þe last, þe sothe vers
certis hym-self schal schewe.
