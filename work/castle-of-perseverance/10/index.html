The Castle of Perseverance · c. 1425

Scene VI: The Seven Deadly Sins and the Castle (part 5 of 6)

by Anonymous (Middle English) c. 13th–16th century

Oure louely Lord, with-owtyn lak,
ȝaf example to charyte:
whanne he was betyn blo & blak
for trespas þat neuere dyd he,—
In sory synne had he no tak,
& ȝyt for synne he bled blody ble,—
he toke his cros up on his bak,
synful man, & al for þee:
þus he mad defens.
Envye, with þi slaundrys þycke,
I am putte at my Lordys prycke;
I wyl do good a-ȝeyns þe wycke,
& kepe in sylens.

Belyal. What, for Belyalys bonys,
where a-bowtyn chyde ȝe?
haue don, ȝe boyes, al at onys!
lasche don þese moderys, alle þre!
werke wrake to þis wonys!
þe vaunward is grauntyd me.
do þese moderys to makyn monys!
ȝoure dowty dedys, now lete se!
dasche hem al to daggys!
haue do, boyes blo & blake!
wirke þese wenchys wo & wrake!
Claryouns, cryith up at a krake,
& blowe ȝour brode baggys! [tunc pugnabunt domini.

Superbia. Out! my prowde bak is bent!
Mekenes hath me al for-bete;
Pride with Mekenes is for-schent.
I weyle & wepe, with wondys wete;
I am betyn in þe hed.
my prowde pride a-doun is dreuyn,
so scharpely Mekenes hath me schreuyn,
þat I may no lenger leuyn;
my lyf is me be-reuyd.

Invidia. Al myn Enmyte is not worth a fart;
I schyte & schake al in my schete;
Charyte, þat sowre swart,
with fayre rosys myn hed gan breke:
I brede þe malaundyr.
with worthi wordis & flouris swete,
Charyte makyth me so meke,
I dare neyþer crye nore crepe,
not a schote of sklaundyr.

Ira. I, Wrethe, may syngyn wele-a-wo.
Pacyens me ȝaf a sory dynt;
I am al betyn blak & blo
with a rose þat on rode was rent;
my speche is almost spent.
hyr rosys fel on me so scharpe,
þat myn hed hangyth as an harpe:
I dar neyþer crye nor carpe;
sche is so pacyent.
Malus Angelus. go hens! ȝe do not worthe a tord!
foule falle ȝou, alle foure!
ȝerne, ȝerne, let fall on bord!
Syr Flesch, with þyn ey[e]n soure,
for care I cukke & koure.
syr Flesch, with þyn company,
ȝerne, ȝerne, make a crye!
helpe we haue no velony,
þat þis day may be oure!

Caro. war, war! late mans flesche go to!
I com with a company.
haue do, my chyldryn! now haue do,
Gloton, Slawth, & Lechery!
Iche of ȝou wynnyth a scho.
lete not Mankynde with maystry!
lete slynge hem in a fowl[e] slo,
& fonde to feffe hym with foly!
dothe now wel ȝoure dede!
ȝerne lete se whov ȝe schul gynne,
Mankynde to temptyn to dedly synne.
if ȝe muste þis castele wynne,
hell schal be ȝour mede.

Gula. war! syr Gloton schal makyn a smeke
a-ȝeyns þis castel, I vowe.
Abstynens! þou þou bleyke,
I loke on þee with bytter browe.
I haue a faget in myn necke,
to settyn Mankynde on a lowe;
my foul leye schalt þou not let,
I wou to God, as I trowe;
þerfor putte hym out here!
In meselynge glotonye,
with goode metis & drynkys trye,
I norche my syster Lecherye
tyl man rennyth on fere.
Abstinencia. þi metis & drynkys arn vnthende:
whanne þei are out of mesure take,
þei makyn men mad & out of mende,
& werkyn hem bothe wo & wrake.
þat, for þi fere þou þou here kyndyl,
certis I schal þi wele a-slake
with bred þat browth us out of hell,
& on þe croys sufferyd wrake:
I mene þe sacrament;
þat Iche blysful bred
þat hounge on hyl tyl he was ded,
schal tempere so myn maydynhed,
þat þi purpos schal be spent.

In abstynens þis bred was browth,
certys, Mankynde, & al for þee.
of fourty dayes ete he nowth,
& þanne was naylyd to a tre;
example us was be-tawth:
In sobyrnesse he bad us be.
þerfor Mankynde schal not be cawth,
Glotony, with þy degre:
þe sothe þou schalt se.
to norysch fayre, þou þou be fawe,
abstynens, it schal with-drawe
tyl þou be schet vnder schawe,
& fayn for to fle.

Luxuria. lo, Chastyte, þou fowle skowte!
þis ilke day here þou schalt deye.
I make a fer in mans towte,
þat launcyth up as any leye.
þese cursyd colys I bere abowte,
Mankynde in tene for to teye.
men & wommen hathe no dowte,
with pyssynge pokys for to pleye;
I bynde hem in my bondys.
I haue no reste, so I rowe,
with men & wommen, as I trowe,
tyl I, Lechery, be set on a lowe,
In al Mankyndis londys.

Casti[t]as. I, Chastyte, haue power in þis place,
þee, Lechery, to bynd & bete.
Madyn Marye, well of grace,
schal qwenche þat fowle hete.
oure Lord God mad þee no space
whanne his blod strayed in þe strete.
fro þis castel he dyd þee chase
whanne he was crounyd with þornys grete
& grene.
to drery deth whanne he was dyth,
& boyes dyd hym gret dyspyth,
In lechery had he no delyth,
& þat was ryth wel sene.

at oure Lady I lere my iessun,
to haue chaste lyf tyl I be ded.
sche is qwene, & beryth þe croun;
& al was for hyr maydynhed.
þerfor go fro þis castel toun,
Lechery, now I þee rede;
for Mankynde getyst þou nowth doun,
to sowen hym synful sede:
In care þou woldys hym cast.
& if þou com up to me,
trewly þou schalt betyn be
with þe ȝerde of Chastyte
whyl my lyf may last.

Accidia. Ware, war! I delue with a spade;
men calle me þe 'lord syr Slowe.'
gostly grace I spylle & schade;
fro þe watyr of grace, þe dyche I fowe;
ȝe schulyn com ryth I-nowe
be þis dyche drye, be bankys brede.
xxxti thousende þat I wel knowe,
In my lyf louely I lede,
þat had leuere syttyn at þe ale,
iij mens songys to syngyn lowde,
þanne to-ward þe chyrche for to crowde.
þou Besynesse, þou bolnyd bowde!
I brewe to þee þyne bale.

Solicitudo. a, good men! be-war now all
of Slugge & Slawthe, þe fowl[e] þefe!
to þe sowle he is byttyrer þanne gall;
rote he is of mekyl myschefe;
Goddys seruyse, þat ledyth us to heuene hall,
þis lordeyn, for to lettyn us, is lefe.
who-so wyl schryuyn hym of his synnys all,
he puttyth þis brethel to mykyl myschefe,
Mankynde, he þat myskaryed.
men moun don no penauns for hym þis,
nere schryue hem whanne þey don a-mys,
but euer he wold, in synne I-wys,
þat Mankynde were taryed.

þerfor he makyth þis dyke drye,
to puttyn Mankynde to dystresse;
he makyth dedly synne a redy weye
In-to þe Castel of Goodnesse;
but with tene I schal hym teye,
þorwe þe helpe of heuene emperesse;
with my bedys he schal a-beye;
& oþer ocupacyons more & lesse
I schal schape, hym to schonde,
for whoso wyle Slawth putte doun
with bedys & with orysoun
or sum oneste ocupacyoun,
as boke to haue in honde.
nec lege, nec hora, nec disce, neque labora.
Caro. Ey, for B[e]lyalys bonys, þe kynge,
where-a-bowte stonde ȝe al day?
Caytyuys! lete be ȝour kakelynge,
& rappe at rowtis of a-ray!
Glotony, þou fowle gadlynge,
sle Abstynens, if þou may!
Lechery, with þi werkynge,
to Chastyte make a wyckyd a-ray
a lytyl þrowe.
& whyl we fyth
for owre ryth,
In bemys bryth
late blastis blowe! [tunc pugnabunt domini.

Gula. Out, Gloton! a-down I dryue.
Abstyne[n]s hathe lost my myth.
Syr Flesch, I schal neuere thryue;
I do not worthe þe deuelys dyrt;
I may not leuyn longe.
I am al betyn, toppe & tayl;
with Abstynens wyl I no more dayl;
I wyl gon cowche [&] qwayl
at hom in ȝour gonge.

Luxuria. Out on Chastyte, be þe rode!
Sche hathe dayschyd & so drenchyd.
ȝyt haue sche þe curs of God,
for al my fere þe qwene hath qwenchyd;
for ferd I fall & feynt.
In harde ropys mote sche ryde!
here dare I not longe a-byde;
sum-where myn hed I wolde hyde,
As an Irchoun þat were schent.

Accidia. Out! I deye! ley on watyr!
I swone, I swete, I feynt, I drulle!
ȝene qwene, with hyr pytyr-patyr,
hath al to-dayschyd my skallyd skulle!
it is as softe a[s] wulle.
or I haue here more skathe,
I schal lepe a-wey, be lurkynge lathe,
þere I may my ballokys bathe,
& leykyn at þe fulle.

Malus Angelus. ȝa! þe deuyl spede ȝou, al þe packe!
For sorwe, I morne on þe mowle;
I carpe, I crye, I coure, I kacke,
I frete, I fart, I fesyl fowle!
I loke lyke an howle. [Ad Mundum.
Now, syr World! what so it cost,
helpe now, or þis we haue lost;
al oure fare is not worth a thost;
þat makyth me to mowle.

Mundus. how, Coveytyse! banyour a-vaunt!
here comyth a batayl, nobyl & newe;
for, syth þou were a lytyl faunt,
Coveytyse, þou hast ben trewe.
Haue do þat damysel! do hyr dawnt!
bytter balys þou hyr brewe!
þe medys, boy, I þee graunt,
þe galows of Canwyke, to hangyn on newe,
þat wolde þee wel be-falle.
haue don, syr Coueytyse!
Wyrke on þe best wyse!
Do Mankynde com & aryse
fro ȝone vertuse all.

Auaricia. how, Mankynde! I am a-tenyde
for þou art þere so in þat holde.
Cum & speke with þi best frende,
Syr Coueytyse! þou knowyst me of olde.
what, deuyl, schalt þou þer lenger lende
with grete penaunce in þat castel colde?
In-to þe werld if þou wylt wende,
a-monge men to bere þee bolde,
I rede, be seynt Gyle.
how, Mankynde! I þee say,
com to Coueytyse, I þee prey;
we to schul to-gedyr pley,
if þou wylt, a whyle.

Largitas. a! God helpe! I am dysmayed,
I curse þee, Covetyse, as I can;
for certys, treytour, þou hast be-trayed
nerhand now Iche erthely man.
so myche were men neuere a-frayed
with Coueytyse, syn þe werld be-gan:
God almythy is not payed.
syn þou, fende, bare þe werldys bane,
ful wyde þou gynnyst wende.
now arn men waxyn ner woode;
þey wold gon to helle for werldys goode;
þat Lord þat restyd on þe rode
is maker of an ende.
Maledicti sunt auariciosi huius temporis!

þer is no dysese nor debate
þorwe þis wyde werld so rounde,
tyde nor tyme, erly nor late,
but þat Coueytyse is þe grounde.
þou norchyst pride, Envye & hate,
þou Coueytyse, þou cursyd hounde!
Criste þee schelde fro oure gate,
& kepe us fro þee saf & sounde,
þat þou no good here wynne!
swete Jhesu, jentyl justyce,
kepe Mankynde fro Coueytyse!
for I-wys he is, in al wyse,
rote of sorwe & synne.

Auaricia. what eylyth þee, lady Largyte,
Damysel dyngne up-on þi des?
& I spak ryth not to þee,
þerfore I prey þee holde þi pes.
how, Mankynde! cum speke with me!
Cum ley þi loue here in my les!
Coueytyse is a frend ryth fre,
þi sorwe, man, to slake & ses.
Coueytyse hathe many a ȝyfte.
Mankynde! þyne hande heder þou reche!
Coueytyse schal be þi leche;
þe ryth wey I schal þee teche,
to thedom & to þryfte.

Humanum Genus. Coueytyse! whedyr schuld I wende?
what wey woldyst þat I sulde holde?
to what place woldyst þou me sende?
I gynne to waxyn hory & colde;
my bake gynnyth to bowe & bende;
I crulle & crepe, & wax al colde;
age makyth man ful vnthende,
body & bonys, & al vnwolde.
my bonys are febyl & sore.
I am arayed in a sloppe;
as a ȝonge man, I may not hoppe;
my nose is colde, & gynnyth to droppe;
myn her waxit al hore.

Auaricia. Petyr! þou hast þe more nede
to haue sum good in þyn age:
markys, poundys, londys & lede,
howsys & homys, castell & cage;
þerfor do as I þee rede!
to Coueytyse cast þi parage!
Cum, & I schal þyne erdyn bede;
þe werthi Werld schal ȝeue þee wage,
certis not a lyth.
Com on, olde man! it is no reprefe
þat Coueytyse be þee lefe:
if þou deye at any myschefe,
it is þi selfe to wyth.

Humanum Genus. nay, nay! þese ladys of goodnesse
wyl not lete me fare a-mys;
& þou I be a whyle in dystresse,
whanne I deye, I schal to blysse.
it is but foly, as I gesse,
al þis werldys wele I-wys;
þese louely ladys, more & lesse,
In wyse wordys þei telle me þys.
þus seyth þe bok of kendis
I wyl not do þese ladys dyspyt,
to forsakyn hem for so lyt;
to dwellyn here is my delyt;
here arn my best frendis.

Auaricia. ȝa! up & down þou take þe wey,
þorwe þis werld to walkyn & wende,
& þou schalt fynde, soth to sey,
þi purs schal be þi best[e] frende.
þou þou syt al day, & prey,
no man schal com to þee, nor sende;
but if þou haue a peny to pey,
men schul to þee þanne lystyn & lende,
& kelyn al þi care.
þerfore to me þou hange & helde,
& be coueytous whylys þou may þee welde.
if þou be pore, & nedy & elde,
þou schalt oftyn euyl fare.

Humanum Genus. Coueytyse, þou seyst a good skyl.
so grete God me [wyl] a-vaunce,
al þi byddynge don I wyl.
I forsake þe Castel of Perseueraunce;
In Coueytyse I wyl me hyle,
for to gete sum sustynaunce.
a-forn mele, men mete schul tyle;
it is good, for al chaunce,
sum good owhere to hyde.
Certys, þis ȝe wel knowe,
it is good, whon-so þe wynde blowe,
a man to haue sum-what of his owe,
what happe so-euere be-tyde.

Bonus Angelus. A, ladyse! I prey ȝou of grace,
helpyth to kepe here Mankynne!
he wyl for-sake þis precyous place,
& drawe a-ȝeyn to dedly synne.
helpe, ladys, louely in lace!
he goth fro þis worthi wonnynge.
Coueytyse, a-wey ȝe chace;
& schyttyth Mankynde sum-where here-Inne,
in ȝoure worþi wyse!
ow, wrechyd man! þou schalt be wroth,
þat synne schal be þee ful loth.
a, swete ladys, helpe! he goth
a-wey with Coueytyse. [tunc descendit ad Auariciam [Humanum Genus].
