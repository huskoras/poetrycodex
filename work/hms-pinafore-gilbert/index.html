H.M.S. Pinafore

Or, The Lass That Loved a Sailor

by W. S. Gilbert

Aboard H.M.S. Pinafore off Portsmouth, Captain Corcoran's daughter Josephine loves the humble sailor Ralph Rakestraw, while the Captain wants her to marry Sir Joseph Porter, First Lord of the Admiralty, who rose from office boy to rule the Queen's Navee. The lovers plan to elope, the villainous Dick Deadeye betrays them, and Little Buttercup's confession of a baby-swap long ago puts every rank in its right place.
