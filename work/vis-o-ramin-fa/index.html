Romance (masnavi) · c. 1054

ویس و رامین (Vis o Ramin)

Gorgani's Persian romance of Vis and Ramin, in the original

by Fakhr al-Din As'ad Gorgani

Gorgani's Persian romance of the Parthian-era lovers Vis and Ramin, composed about 1054 at the Seljuk court in Isfahan: among the oldest surviving Persian romances, here in Ganjoor's section order.
