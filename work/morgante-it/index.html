Epic poem · 1483

Morgante

Poema in ventotto cantari, in ottava rima

by Luigi Pulci

Pulci's comic epic of Charlemagne's paladins, in which Orlando converts the giant Morgante and the two set out together, ending with the treachery of Gano and the defeat at Roncesvalles, in the original Italian ottava rima. Lord Byron's English version of the first canto is held separately among his poems (The Morgante Maggiore of Pulci).
