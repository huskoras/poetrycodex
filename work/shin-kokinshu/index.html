新古今和歌集 (Shin kokin wakashū)

New Collection of Ancient and Modern Japanese Poems

by Fujiwara no Teika

The eighth imperial anthology of waka, in twenty books of seasons, celebration, grief, travel, love and Buddhist verse, with its 1,978 poems taken in order; book 4 holds the three autumn-evening poems (sanseki no uta) by Jakuren, Saigyō and Teika.
