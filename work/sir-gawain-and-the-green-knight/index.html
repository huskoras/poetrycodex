Sir Gawain and the Green Knight

A Middle-English Arthurian Romance

by Gawain Poet

Translated by Jessie L. Weston (1898)

A green-skinned knight rides into Arthur's Christmas feast with an impossible challenge; Sir Gawain's answer sends him on a year-long quest that tests his courage, his courtesy, and his honesty alike.
