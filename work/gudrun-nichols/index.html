Epic poem · c. 1230–1240

Gudrun

A Mediaeval Epic

by Anonymous (Middle High German)

Translated by Mary Pickering Nichols (1889)

The Middle High German epic of Kudrun, the Hegeling princess abducted by Hartmut and held captive for thirteen years until her kin come to win her back, in thirty-two tales of four-line stanzas, rendered into English verse by Mary Pickering Nichols.
