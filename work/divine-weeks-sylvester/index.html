The Divine Weeks

Du Bartas his Divine Weekes and Workes: La Sepmaine, ou Creation du monde, and La Seconde Sepmaine

by Guillaume de Salluste Du Bartas

Translated by Josuah Sylvester (1605–1611)

Du Bartas's Huguenot epic of the Creation and of sacred history, in Sylvester's rhyming English couplets: the seven Days of the First Week, and four Days of the Second Week from Adam to David.
