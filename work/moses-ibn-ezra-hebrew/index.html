Twenty-nine Hebrew piyyutim and the Sefer ha-Anaq (Book of Tarshish)

by Moses ibn Ezra

Moses ibn Ezra's Hebrew verse from Muslim Spain: penitential hymns and prayers for the Days of Awe, Hanukkah and the fast-days, and the twenty-two chapters of the Sefer ha-Anaq, a book of moral quatrains.
