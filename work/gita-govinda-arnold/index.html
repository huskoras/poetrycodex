The Indian Song of Songs (Gita Govinda)

by Jayadeva

Translated by Sir Edwin Arnold (1875)

Jayadeva's twelfth-century Sanskrit song cycle of Krishna and Radha, in Sir Edwin Arnold's English verse: a hymn to Vishnu and eleven sargas of longing, quarrel and union.
