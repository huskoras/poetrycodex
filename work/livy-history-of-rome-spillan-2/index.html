The History of Rome, Books IX–XXVI

Ab Urbe Condita, from the Samnite wars to the fall of Capua

by Livy

Translated by D. Spillan and Cyrus R. Edmonds (1849-50)

Also in this archive

D. Spillan’s translation (1849) →

Livy's narrative from the Caudine Forks and the later Samnite wars, through the Pyrrhic and First Punic wars (known for Books XI–XX only from short epitomes), to Hannibal's invasion and the fall of Capua in the Second Punic War, in the English translation of D. Spillan and Cyrus R. Edmonds.
