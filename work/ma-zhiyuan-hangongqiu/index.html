Zaju play · c. 1250

破幽夢孤雁漢宮秋 (Han gong qiu) — Autumn in the Han Palace

by Ma Chih-yüan

Ma Zhiyuan's Yuan zaju in a prologue and four acts: the Han emperor Yuan sends his beloved consort Wang Zhaojun to the Xiongnu khan, and mourns her after she drowns herself in the Amur.
