Lyric collection · c. 1st c. BC - 6th c. AD

Ἀνακρεόντεια (Anacreontea)

Greek text, in Bergk's order

by Anacreontea (Anonymous)

A collection of some sixty short Greek songs of wine, love, spring and old age, written in the manner of the sixth-century BC poet Anacreon but composed by anonymous imitators between the Hellenistic period and late antiquity. Transmitted together in a Byzantine manuscript, they were mistaken for Anacreon's own work and shaped European love lyric from the Renaissance onward.
