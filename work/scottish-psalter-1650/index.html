Psalter (metrical version) · 1650

The Psalms of David in Metre

The Scottish Metrical Psalter of 1650

by Church of Scotland (Francis Rous et al.)

The metrical psalter that the Church of Scotland approved in 1650, a revision of Francis Rous's version, sung in Scottish worship for centuries; it includes 'The Lord's my shepherd, I'll not want' (Psalm 23).
