Ludus Coventriae (N-Town Plays)

The Coventry Mysteries, ed. J. O. Halliwell (1841)

by Anonymous (Middle English)

A cycle of forty-two mystery plays in East Anglian Middle English, written down in the fifteenth century (British Library, Cotton MS Vespasian D. VIII) and long known as the Ludus Coventriae because Halliwell and earlier antiquaries took it for the Corpus Christi plays of Coventry. It is now called the N-Town cycle, from the Banns' call for players to appear 'in N. towne'. A Proclamation by three banner-bearers announces the pageants, which run from the Creation and the Fall through the life of Mary, the birth, ministry, trial and Crucifixion of Christ, to the Resurrection, the Assumption of the Virgin and Doomsday.
