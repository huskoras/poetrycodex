Epic poem · traditional, c. 5th century BCE - 3rd century CE

रामायणम् (Ramayana)

Bala Kanda and Ayodhya Kanda

by Valmiki

The original Sanskrit of the epic of Rama, attributed to the sage Valmiki: the birth and youth of Rama in the Bala Kanda, and his exile from Ayodhya in the Ayodhya Kanda.
