Verse drama · c. 1300

西廂記 (Xi xiang ji) — The Story of the Western Wing

by Wang Shifu

The student Zhang Jun-rui meets Cui Ying-ying, the daughter of a dead prime minister, at the Temple of Universal Salvation, and a rebel siege, a clever maid named Hongniang and a stolen night of love bring them together against her mother's wishes. Written in Yuan-dynasty zaju form across five books of four acts each, with long sung arias in rhymed tunes, it is the most loved Chinese love drama, famous for its farewell scene at the Pavilion of Parting and for its happy reunion.
