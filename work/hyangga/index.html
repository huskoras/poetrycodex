향가 (Hyangga)

Songs of Silla and early Goryeo, in the original hyangchal script

by Hyangga (Silla and early Goryeo songs)

The earliest surviving Korean poems: fourteen songs from the Samguk yusa, Gyunyeo's eleven Bohyeon vows, and the 1120 elegy for two generals.
