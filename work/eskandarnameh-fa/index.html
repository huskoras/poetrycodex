Epic romance (masnavi) · 1202

اسکندرنامه (Eskandarnameh)

The Persian original of Nizami's Iskandarnama: Sharafnama and Iqbalnama (Khiradnama)

by Nizami Ganjavi

Nizami's Persian epic romance of Alexander the Great, composed between about 1194 and 1202: the Sharafnameh, the hero's conquests and travels, and the Khardnameh (Iqbalnameh), his wisdom and the sages who attend him. The original text, in Ganjoor's section order.
