Epic poem · 1447

용비어천가 (Yongbi eocheon ga, Songs of the Dragons Flying to Heaven)

125 cantos in Middle Korean, 1447

by Jeong In-ji, An Ji and Gwon Je

The earliest large work printed in the Korean alphabet (1447): 125 cantos celebrating the Joseon founders and their forebears, composed in 1445 by Jeong In-ji, An Ji and Gwon Je. The text is the Middle Korean song text only, with old Hangul letters and tone dots, from Korean Wikisource.
