Epic poem · 3rd–4th century AD

Τὰ μεθ' Ὅμηρον (Ta meth' Homeron)

by Quintus Smyrnaeus

The Greek text of the Posthomerica, Quintus Smyrnaeus's fourteen-book hexameter continuation of the Iliad, from the funeral of Hector through the deaths of Akhilleus, Paris and Penthesileia, the wooden horse and the sack of Troy, to the Greeks' storm-wrecked voyage home. Each book pairs with the same-numbered book of Way's English translation, The Fall of Troy.
