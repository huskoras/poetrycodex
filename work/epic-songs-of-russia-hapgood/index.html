The Epic Songs of Russia

The Byliny of the Kiev and Novgorod Cycles

by Anonymous (Russian Byliny)

Translated by Isabel Florence Hapgood (1886)

The great heroic songs of the Russian north: Ilya of Murom and the Nightingale the Robber, Dobrynya the Dragon-slayer, Sadko beneath the sea, and the champions who defend Prince Vladimir's Kiev.
