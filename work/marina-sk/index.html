Epic poem · 1846

Marína

Ľúbostná básnická skladba

by Andrej Sládkovič

The great Slovak Romantic love poem of the Štúr generation: a hundred ten-line stanzas in praise of Marína, the poet's beloved, in whom Tatra landscape, nation and ideal beauty are joined.
