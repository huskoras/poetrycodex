Allegorical poem · 1480

سلامان و ابسال (Salaman o Absal)

Jami's allegorical masnavi (c. 1480), the first poem of the Haft Awrang, in the original Persian

by Jami

Jami's allegory of Salaman, the king's son, and Absal, his nurse: their flight, the pyre, and the soul's freeing from bodily love, in rhymed couplets.
