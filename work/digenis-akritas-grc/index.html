Epic poem · 1875

Βασίλειος Διγενὴς Ἀκρίτης

Digenis Akritas, the Trebizond version (ed. Sathas and Legrand, 1875)

by Anonymous (Byzantine Greek)

The Byzantine frontier epic of Basil Digenes Akrites, whose Arab father abjured his faith for a Greek bride, in the Trebizond manuscript: the lament of the five brothers, the emir's journey into Romania, the hero's boyhood hunts, his duels with the apelatai and Maximo, his palace on the Euphrates and his death.
