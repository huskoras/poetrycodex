Tragedy · 401 BC

Οἰδίπους ἐπὶ Κολωνῷ (Oidipous epi Kolono)

by Sophocles

The blind, exiled Oidipous comes to the grove of the Eumenides at Kolonos near Athens, where Theseus shelters him and he is to die. Creon and Polyneikes try to claim him, and he passes mysteriously from the world. The Greek text is given in the order of the Storr edition, so that it pairs with the English prose translation of the same play.
