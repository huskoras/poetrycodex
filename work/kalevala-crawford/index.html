Epic poem · 1888

The Kalevala

The Epic Poem of Finland

by Elias Lönnrot

Translated by John Martin Crawford (1888)

Also in this archive

W. F. Kirby’s translation (1907) →

Fifty runes of Finnish folk song gathered into one epic: the birth of the sage Wainamoinen, the rivalry of Finns and the people of Pohjola for the Sampo, the doomed Kullervo, and the hero's departure as a new age arrives.
