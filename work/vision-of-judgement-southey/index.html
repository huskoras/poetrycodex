Narrative poem (English hexameters) · 1821

A Vision of Judgement

In twelve parts (1821)

by Robert Southey

Southey's dream-vision of the Last Judgment of King George III, written in English hexameters for the death of the king in 1820: the trial before the gate of Heaven, the accusers and absolvers, and the king's welcome among the worthies of the Georgian age. Byron's parody of the same title is a different poem.
