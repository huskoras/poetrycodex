Lyric cycle · c. 1240

Strofische gedichten

The Strophic Poems (Middle Dutch)

by Hadewijch

The forty-five strophic songs of Hadewijch, the Brabantine beguine and mystic of the thirteenth century: courtly love-lyric turned to the service of Minne, divine Love, with its seasons, its longing and its suffering.
