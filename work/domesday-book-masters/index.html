Narrative poem · 1920

Domesday Book

by Edgar Lee Masters

A verse narrative in blank verse that opens with a coroner's inquest into the death of Elenor Murray, a nurse found dead by the Illinois River. Witness after witness is called, and each account lays bare the hidden lives of a Midwestern town and of America.
