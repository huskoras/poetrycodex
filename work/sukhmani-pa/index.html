ਸੁਖਮਨੀ ਸਾਹਿਬ (Sukhmani Sahib)

ਗਉੜੀ ਸੁਖਮਨੀ ਮਃ ੫

by Guru Arjan

The 'Jewel of Peace', Guru Arjan's long meditation on the divine Name in twenty-four ashtapadis of eight stanzas each, each opening with a salok; included in the Adi Granth (1604) in the Gauri measure.
