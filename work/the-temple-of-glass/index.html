Dream vision · c. 1403–1408 (printed by Caxton, 1477)

The Temple of Glass

by John Lydgate

A Chaucerian dream vision in which the poet, carried in sleep to a temple of glass, overhears a lady and her knightly lover lament and console each other in unrequited love — modelled closely on Chaucer's House of Fame and Parliament of Fowls.
