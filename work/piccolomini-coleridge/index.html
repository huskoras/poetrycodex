The Piccolomini

by Friedrich Schiller

Translated by Samuel Taylor Coleridge (1800)

At Pilsen, in the Thirty Years' War, the imperial generals gather round their commander Wallenstein while a plot against him is already being laid by Octavio Piccolomini, whose son Max loves Wallenstein's daughter Thekla. Octavio holds a secret commission to take the generalship, and father and son are drawn toward a collision of loyalties.
