The Dance to Death

A Historical Tragedy in Five Acts

by Emma Lazarus

Nordhausen, May 1349: as the Black Death spreads, the Jews of the free city are accused of poisoning the wells. Susskind von Orb, a respected Jew, sees his daughter Liebhaid loved by Prince William of Meissen, while the vengeful Governor Henry Schnetzen, whose wife was once burned through Susskind, secures the Landgrave's order for their destruction. Lazarus's verse tragedy, dedicated to George Eliot, ends with the community choosing a martyr's dance of death.
