The Martyrdom of Polycarp

A letter of the Church of Smyrna

by The Apostolic Fathers

Translated by Kirsopp Lake (1912)

The earliest surviving account of a Christian martyrdom, written by the Church of Smyrna to the Church of Philomelium: the arrest, trial and burning of the aged bishop Polycarp.
