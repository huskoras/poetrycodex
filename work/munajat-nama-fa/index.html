Munajat (prayers in rhymed prose and verse) · 11th century

مناجات نامه (Munajat-nama)

262 prayers in rhymed prose and quatrains

by Khwaja 'Abdullah Ansari of Herat

The Munajat-nama of Khwaja 'Abdullah Ansari, the Pir of Herat: 262 intimate prayers addressed to God in rhymed prose, studded with Persian quatrains. A landmark of Sufi devotional writing.
