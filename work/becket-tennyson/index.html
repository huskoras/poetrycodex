Becket

by Alfred, Lord Tennyson

Henry II raises his friend and Chancellor Thomas Becket to the see of Canterbury, hoping for an ally in the Church; Becket instead defends the Church's rights against the Crown. Through the quarrel at Northampton, exile in France, a patched-up reconciliation and the King's hidden love for Rosamund, the two old friends move toward the murder of the Archbishop in his own cathedral.
