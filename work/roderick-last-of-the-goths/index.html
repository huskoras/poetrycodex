Narrative poem · 1814

Roderick, the Last of the Goths

A Tragic Poem

by Robert Southey

A blank-verse epic in twenty-five sections on the last Visigothic king of Spain, who after his defeat by the Moors lives on as a penitent and fights again beside Pelayo.
