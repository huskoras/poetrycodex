Didactic poem · 1481

تحفة الاحرار (Tuhfat al-Ahrar)

Jami's didactic masnavi (1481), the second poem of the Haft Awrang, in the original Persian

by Jami

Jami's 'Gift of the Free', a mystical-ethical masnavi in twenty maqalas, each a discourse on a moral or spiritual theme with illustrative tales, written for his friend and shaykh's circle in 1481.
