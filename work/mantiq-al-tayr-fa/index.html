Allegorical poem

منطق‌الطیر (Mantiq al-Tayr)

by Farid al-Din Attar

Attar's Sufi allegory in rhymed couplets, in which the birds of the world set out under the hoopoe to seek their king, the Simorgh, crossing seven valleys and learning, with their guide's tales along the way, what the search demands.
