Tragedy · c. 430 BC

Τραχίνιαι (Trachiniai)

by Sophocles

Deianeira, waiting at Trachis for Herakles, sends him a robe smeared with what she believes is a love-charm from the dying centaur Nessos. It is poison, and Herakles' agony and her suicide follow. The Greek text is given in the order of the Storr edition, so that it pairs with the English prose translation of the same play.
