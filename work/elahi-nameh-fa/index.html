Allegorical poem

الهی‌نامه (Elahi-nameh)

by Farid al-Din Attar

Attar's Book of God, a Sufi mathnavi in rhymed couplets framed as a story: a king questions his six sons about their dearest wish, and the father answers each in turn with a sermon and illustrative tales on the vanity of desire, drawing the princes toward the one true longing, the knowledge of God.
