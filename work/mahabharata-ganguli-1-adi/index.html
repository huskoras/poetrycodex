Epic poem (prose translation) · 1883–1884

The Mahabharata, Book 1: Adi Parva

The Book of the Beginning

by Vyasa

Translated by Kisari Mohan Ganguli (published by Pratap Chandra Roy, 1883–1896)

Also in this archive

Romesh Chunder Dutt’s translation (1899) →

Book 1 of Kisari Mohan Ganguli's complete English prose translation of the Mahabharata, published in parts by Pratap Chandra Roy, Calcutta, from 1883. The Adi Parva tells how the epic came to be recited, the stories of the Bharata line, the birth and upbringing of the Kauravas and Pandavas, and the winning of Draupadi, down to the burning of the Khandava forest.
