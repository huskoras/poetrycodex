The Sacred Formulas of the Cherokees · 1891

This Tells About Going into the Water

by Anonymous (Cherokee) traditional; manuscripts recorded 1887–1888

Translated by James Mooney (1891)

Listen! O, now instantly, you have drawn near to hearken, O Agĕ́‘yagúga.
You have come to put your red spittle upon my body.
My name is (Gatigwanasti.) The blue had affected me.
You have come and clothed me with a red dress.
She is of the (Deer) clan.
She has become blue.
You have directed her paths straight to where I have my feet, and I shall feel exultant.
Listen!
