Works and Days

by Hesiod

Translated by Hugh G. Evelyn-White (1914)

Hesiod’s poem of advice to his idle brother Perses, mixing the myths of Pandora and the Five Ages with farming lore, moral counsel and a calendar of lucky and unlucky days.
