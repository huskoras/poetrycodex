Epic poem (prose translation) · 1883–1896

The Mahabharata, Book 12: Santi Parva

The Book of Peace

by Vyasa

Translated by Kisari Mohan Ganguli (published by Pratap Chandra Roy, 1883–1896)

Also in this archive

Romesh Chunder Dutt’s translation (1899) →

Book 12 of Kisari Mohan Ganguli's complete English prose translation of the Mahabharata, published in parts by Pratap Chandra Roy, Calcutta. The Santi Parva, the longest book of the epic, opens with Bhishma lying on his bed of arrows after the war, instructing the grieving Yudhishthira on the duties of kings, on conduct in times of distress, and on the paths to liberation.
