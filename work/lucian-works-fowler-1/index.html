Satires and dialogues · 1905

The Works of Lucian of Samosata, Volume I

Satires and dialogues, from The Vision to the Voyage to the Lower World

by Lucian

Translated by H. W. and F. G. Fowler (1905)

Fifteen pieces by the Syrian satirist of the Antonine age: his autobiographical Vision, the dialogues of gods, sea-gods and the dead, and the comic fantasies in which Charon, Hermes and Zeus show up the follies of philosophers, priests and the rich.
