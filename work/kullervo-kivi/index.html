Verse tragedy · 1864

Kullervo

Näytelmä viidessä näytöksessä

by Aleksis Kivi

Kullervo (1864) is Aleksis Kivi's verse tragedy in five acts, drawn from the Kullervo cycle of the Kalevala. It won the Finnish state literary prize and is among the foundational works of Finnish drama. This is the original Finnish text, with no English version in the archive.
