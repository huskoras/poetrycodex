The Chester Plays

A Collection of Mysteries Founded upon Scriptural Subjects, Formerly Represented by the Trades of Chester at Whitsuntide

by Anonymous (Middle English)

The twenty-five pageants of the Chester cycle, the Whitsun mystery plays acted by the craft guilds of Chester, run from the fall of Lucifer and the Creation through Noah's Flood, Abraham, the Nativity and the Passion to the Harrowing of Hell, the Resurrection and Doomsday. Each play was staged on a wagon by a different guild, in rhymed stanzas full of alliteration, with stage directions in Latin.
