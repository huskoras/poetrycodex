Free-verse poem · 1883–1902

Towards Democracy

Complete Edition in Four Parts

by Edward Carpenter

Carpenter's long free-verse chant in the manner of Whitman, begun in 1881: a single continuing poem of freedom, simplicity and comradeship, followed by three further parts of shorter poems on the city, nature, love and the coming of a new social life.
