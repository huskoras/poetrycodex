Narrative poem · c.1330-1343 (ed. Tomás Antonio Sánchez, Poesías del Arcipreste de Hita, Madrid: Antonio de Sancha, 1790)

Libro de buen amor

Old Spanish text, edition of Tomás Antonio Sánchez (1790)

by Juan Ruiz, Arcipreste de Hita

The Archpriest of Hita's great medieval Castilian poem: a fictional autobiography of failed loves, with beast-fables, the battle of Don Carnal and Doña Cuaresma, the go-between Trotaconventos, mountain-girl songs and hymns to the Virgin.
