Verse play · 1804

Wilhelm Tell

Schauspiel

by Friedrich Schiller

Schiller's last completed play, in blank verse: the Swiss cantons of Schwytz, Uri and Unterwalden rise against their Austrian bailiffs. Wilhelm Tell, forced by the tyrant Gessler to shoot an apple from his son's head, kills him in the hollow way near Küssnacht, and the three cantons swear their league of freedom.
