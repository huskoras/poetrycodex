Saint's life · c. 9th century

Juliana

by Cynewulf

Translated by Charles W. Kennedy (1910)

One of Cynewulf's four signed poems: the passion of the virgin martyr Juliana of Nicomedia, who resists both an unwanted marriage to a pagan prefect and torture by a demon sent to break her faith.
