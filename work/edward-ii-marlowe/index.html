History play · c. 1592 (published 1594)

The Troublesome Reign and Lamentable Death of Edward the Second

by Christopher Marlowe

Edward II, newly king, recalls his favourite Gaveston from exile and showers him with titles, driving the barons under the younger Mortimer into open revolt. After Gaveston is killed and the king turns to the Spensers, Queen Isabella and Mortimer invade, depose Edward and have him murdered in Berkeley Castle. His son, the young Edward III, then brings Mortimer to the block.
