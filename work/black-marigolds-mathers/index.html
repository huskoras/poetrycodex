Black Marigolds (Chaurapanchasika)

A free interpretation of the Chaurapanchasika

by Bilhana

Translated by E. Powys Mathers (Black Marigolds, 1919)

Fifty stanzas, each beginning "Even now", in which a condemned lover remembers his princess, in E. Powys Mathers's free-verse rendering of the poem attributed to the Kashmiri poet Bilhana.
