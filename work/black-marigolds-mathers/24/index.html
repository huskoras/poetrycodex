Black Marigolds (Chaurapanchasika) · 1919

25

by Bilhana 11th century

Translated by E. Powys Mathers (Black Marigolds, 1919)

Even now
I call to mind her weariness in the morning
Close lying in my arms, and tiredly smiling
At my disjointed prayer for her small sake.
Now in my morning the weariness of death
Sends me to sleep. Had I made coffins
I might have lived singing to three score.

Source Bilhana, Black Marigolds, being a rendering into English of the "Panchasika of Chauras", trans. E. Powys Mathers (Oxford: B. H. Blackwell, 1919). Digital text: sacred-texts hin/bilhana.
