Epic poem · 1483–1495

Orlando innamorato

Poema in tre libri, in ottava rima

by Matteo Maria Boiardo

Boiardo's romance epic of Orlando in love with Angelica, the siege of Paris, and the love of Ruggiero and Bradamante, in the original Italian ottava rima. Three books, 69 cantos; the third book breaks off unfinished in its ninth canto.
