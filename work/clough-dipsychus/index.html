Verse drama · 1850

Dipsychus

A verse drama in two parts

by Arthur Hugh Clough

In Venice a young man, scrupulous and unsure of his faith, is shadowed by a cynical Spirit who urges him to enjoy the world, take a profession and make terms with the way things are. Dipsychus answers in blank verse, lyric and song, and at last yields in part, with reservations.
