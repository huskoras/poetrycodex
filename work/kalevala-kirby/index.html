Epic poem · 1907

Kalevala

The Land of the Heroes

by Elias Lönnrot

Translated by W. F. Kirby (1907)

Also in this archive

John Martin Crawford’s translation (1888) →

Kirby's 1907 English version of the Finnish national epic in fifty runos, from the birth of the sage Väinämöinen through the forging and theft of the Sampo and the doomed Kullervo to the hero's departure as Marjatta's child is made King of Carelia.
