Cain

A Mystery

by George Gordon, Lord Byron

Byron's biblical drama in three acts, first published in 1821. Cain, resentful of the fate bequeathed by his parents' fall, is led by Lucifer through the abyss of space and the realm of the dead, where he is shown the vastness of creation and the meaning of death. Returning to earth, he kills his brother Abel in a rage at God's favour and is marked by an angel and driven eastward from Eden.
