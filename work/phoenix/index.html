Allegorical poem · c. 9th century

The Phoenix

by Anonymous (Old English)

Translated by Cosette Faust Newton & Stith Thompson (1918)

A richly decorative description of the mythical phoenix and its cyclical death and rebirth in a paradisal eastern land, allegorized in the poem's second half as a figure of Christian resurrection.
