The Fragments of Empedocles · 1908

Last Echoes of a Song Half Lost

by Empedocles c. 494–434 BC

Translated by William Ellery Leonard (1908)

148.
Man-enfolding Earth.

149.
The cloud-collecting.

150.
The blood-full liver.

151.
Life-giving.

152.
Evening, the day's old age.

153.
The belly.

153a.
In seven times seven days.
