Pre-Islamic odes · 1782

The Moallakát

The Seven Arabian Poems which were suspended on the Temple at Mecca

by The Mu'allaqat Poets

Translated by Sir William Jones (1782)

Seven great odes of pre-Islamic Arabia, said to have been hung on the Kaaba at Mecca, by Imru' al-Qais, Tarafa, Zuhayr, Labid, Antara, Amr ibn Kulthum and al-Harith ibn Hilliza.

Contents · 7 parts

  • The Poem of Amriolkais (Imru' al-Qais)
  • The Poem of Tarafa
  • The Poem of Zohair (Zuhayr)
  • The Poem of Lebeid (Labid)
  • The Poem of Antara
  • The Poem of Amru (Amr ibn Kulthum)
  • The Poem of Hareth (al-Harith ibn Hilliza)
