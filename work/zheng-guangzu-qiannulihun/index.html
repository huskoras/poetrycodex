Verse drama · early 14th century

迷青瑣倩女離魂 (Mi qing suo Qiannü li hun) — The Soul of Qiannü Leaves Her Body

by Zheng Guangzu

Qiannü, the daughter of the widow Madam Li, has been promised since birth to the young scholar Wang Wenju, but when he arrives to visit the family her mother makes the two swear brotherhood instead of betrothal and tells him to win a degree first. Wang sets off for the capital, and Qiannü, left behind and wasting with love-sickness, sees her soul leave her body and race after his boat. The spirit travels with him to the capital while the body lies ill at home, until after he wins the top place in the examination he comes back with his wife, and the two Qiannüs meet and become one. The best-known play of Zheng Guangzu, one of the four great masters of Yuan drama, it is a wedge and four acts of sung arias and spoken dialogue.
