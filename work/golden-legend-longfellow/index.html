Verse drama · 1851

The Golden Legend

Christus: A Mystery, Part II

by Henry Wadsworth Longfellow

A dramatic poem set in medieval Germany, drawn from Hartmann von Aue's tale of Poor Henry. Prince Henry of Hoheneck, wasting with a mysterious sickness, is told that only a maiden's willing death can cure him. Elsie, a peasant girl, offers hers, and the two travel through Strasburg, the Black Forest convent of Hirschau, the Alps and Italy to the medical school at Salerno, where Henry prevents the sacrifice. Lucifer in disguise tempts the travellers throughout, and a Miracle Play of the Nativity is performed inside the Strasburg scenes. Longfellow made the poem the second part of his trilogy Christus: A Mystery.
