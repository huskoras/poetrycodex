The Nibelungenlied

by Anonymous (Middle High German)

Translated by William Nanson Lettsom (1850; revised edition 1901)

The Middle High German epic of Siegfried, Kriemhild and the fall of the Burgundians, composed in Austria around 1200, in thirty-nine adventures, rendered into English rhymed verse by William Nanson Lettsom in 1850.
