रामचरितमानस — बालकाण्ड (Ramcharitmanas, Balkand)

The first sopan (book), in part: from the opening shlokas to Doha 35

by Tulsidas

Tulsidas's Awadhi retelling of the Rama story, in the first book (Balkand) only, and only as far as the transcription goes: the opening shlokas and sorathas, then the chaupai groups and dohas through Doha 35.
