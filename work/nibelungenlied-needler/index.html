The Nibelungenlied

by Anonymous (Middle High German)

Translated by George Henry Needler (1904)

The Middle High German epic of Siegfried, Kriemhild and the fall of the Burgundians, composed in Austria around 1200, in thirty-nine adventures and 2,379 four-line stanzas, rendered into English verse by George Henry Needler.
