Epic poem · 5th century AD

Dionysiaca

Διονυσιακά

by Nonnus of Panopolis

The Greek text of the Dionysiaca, Nonnus of Panopolis's forty-eight-book hexameter epic of Dionysus, the longest surviving poem of antiquity: from the abduction of Europa and the battle of Zeus with Typhoeus, through the wanderings of Cadmus, the births of the god and his Indian campaign, to his triumph and ascent to Olympus. Books 1–16 are given here so far.
