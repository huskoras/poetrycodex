Italy

A Poem

by Samuel Rogers

Samuel Rogers's travel poem in blank verse, published in two parts in 1822 and 1828, in which a pilgrim from the north passes through the Alps, Lombardy, Venice, Florence, Rome and Naples, telling the local legends as he goes.
