Letters · c. AD 65

Moral Letters to Lucilius, Volume II (Letters 66–92)

Epistulae Morales ad Lucilium

by Seneca

Translated by Richard M. Gummere (1920)

Letters 66 to 92 of the series Seneca wrote in his last years to his friend Lucilius: Stoic essays on virtue and the supreme good, illness, old age and suicide, benefits, drunkenness, the philosophy of liberal studies, and the happy life.
