یوسف و زلیخا (Yusuf o Zulaikha)

Jami's romance of Joseph and Zulaikha (1483), fifth poem of the Haft Awrang, in the original Persian

by Jami

Jami's Persian romance of the Qur'anic story of Yusuf (Joseph) and Zulaikha, from her dreams and long pining to her repentance and the restoring of her youth.
