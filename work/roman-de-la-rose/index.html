Allegorical dream vision · c. 1230 and c. 1275

Le Roman de la Rose

The Old French original, in four tomes (Marteau, 1878)

by Guillaume de Lorris and Jean de Meun

A young dreamer enters the walled Garden of Love and sets his heart on a rosebud, helped and hindered by allegorical figures such as Fair Welcome, Danger, Reason and Nature, in the most influential French poem of the Middle Ages.
