The Gododin · 1888

LXXXVII. To Gereint

by Aneirin fl. late 6th century

Thomas Stephens (1888)

Gereint raised the war-shout before the south.
And at the White Lake, the shield was pierced ;
Old is the spear, and gentle was the ancient chief
Who is praised on mountain and sea.
The offspring of Gereint, who was a generous ruler,
Grew up together, and were equally maintained.
