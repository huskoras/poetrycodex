Epic poem (prose translation) · 1883–1896

The Mahabharata, Book 6: Bhishma Parva

The Book of Bhishma

by Vyasa

Translated by Kisari Mohan Ganguli (published by Pratap Chandra Roy, 1883–1896)

Also in this archive

Romesh Chunder Dutt’s translation (1899) →

Book 6 of Kisari Mohan Ganguli's complete English prose translation of the Mahabharata, published in parts by Pratap Chandra Roy, Calcutta. The Bhishma Parva opens the great war at Kurukshetra and holds the Bhagavad Gita, Krishna's teaching to Arjuna on the eve of battle; it follows the first ten days of fighting under Bhishma's command until he falls on a bed of arrows.
