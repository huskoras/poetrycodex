Faust: The Second Part

Translated in the original metres by Bayard Taylor

by Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

Translated by Bayard Taylor (1871)

The Second Part of Goethe's Faust in Bayard Taylor's translation in the original metres: Faust serves the Emperor's court, follows Helen of Troy through the Classical Walpurgis-Night to a marriage that ends with the death of their son Euphorion, wins land from the sea, and dies at the hour his own labour is complete, as the angels carry off what is immortal in him.
