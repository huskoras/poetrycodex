The Kasîdah of Hâjî Abdû El-Yezdî

by Sir Richard Francis Burton

A philosophical quatrain-sequence in which a desert traveller, Haji Abdu, reflects on faith, fate and the vanity of creeds. Burton's own composition, offered as a rendering from the Persian.
