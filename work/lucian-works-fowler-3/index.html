The Works of Lucian of Samosata, Volume III

The Life of Demonax to The Fly: biography, dialogues, essays and lectures

by Lucian

Translated by H. W. and F. G. Fowler (1905)

Nineteen pieces by the Syrian satirist of the Antonine age: the Life of Demonax, the two Portrait-Study essays, Toxaris on friendship, the Zeus dialogues, Icaromenippus and its flight to heaven, The Parasite, Anacharsis, and the comic lectures on Dionysus, Heracles, the Fly and the book-collector.
