Narrative poem · 1841

Гайдамаки

Поема

by Taras Shevchenko

Shevchenko's narrative poem of the 1768 Koliivshchyna uprising, in which the haidamaks, led by Zalizniak and Honta, rise against Polish landlords in Right-Bank Ukraine.
