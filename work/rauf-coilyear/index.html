Verse romance (Charlemagne cycle) · c. 1475

The Taill of Rauf Coilyear

A Scots romance of Charlemagne and the collier

by Anonymous (Middle English)

A Scots comic romance in thirteen-line stanzas. Caught in a storm on the way to Paris, Charlemagne takes shelter in the house of the collier Rauf, who knocks him down for his bad manners and makes him sit at his table; next day, at court, Rauf finds his surly guest is the king and is knighted for his pains.
