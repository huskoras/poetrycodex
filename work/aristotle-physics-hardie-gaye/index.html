Treatise · 1930

Physics

Eight books on nature, change, place, time and the first mover

by Aristotle

Translated by R. P. Hardie and R. K. Gaye (1930)

Aristotle's lectures on nature: the principles of natural things, the four causes, chance and purpose, the analysis of motion, the infinite, place, void and time, the continuity of change, and the argument for an eternal first mover.
