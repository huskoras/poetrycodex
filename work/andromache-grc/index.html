Verse play · c. 425 BC

Ἀνδρομάχη (Andromache)

by Euripides

Andromache, the Trojan widow of Hector, lives at Phthia as the captive concubine of Achilles' son Neoptolemos and has borne him a son, Molossos. While Neoptolemos is away at Delphi, his childless Spartan wife Hermione and her father Menelaos plot to kill the captive and her child, who has taken refuge at the altar of the sea-goddess Thetis, until old Peleus arrives and rescues them. Hermione, terrified of what her husband will do on his return, is carried off by Orestes, who has already arranged that Neoptolemos shall be murdered at Delphi. Peleus receives his grandson's body, and Thetis appears above the house to ordain the future of the survivors and to promise Peleus a divine life beside her in the sea.
