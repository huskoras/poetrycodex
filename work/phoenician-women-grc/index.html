Verse play · c. 410 BC

Φοίνισσαι (Phoinissai)

by Euripides

Thebes is under siege: Eteokles and Polyneikes, the sons of Oedipus, are at war over the city, and their mother Iokaste tries in vain to reconcile them at a meeting before the seven gates. The seer Teiresias then declares that Thebes can be saved only if Kreon's son Menoikeus is sacrificed, and the young man goes to his death for the city. The brothers kill each other in single combat and Iokaste takes her own life over their bodies. The blinded Oedipus is led into exile by his daughter Antigone, and the Greek text is given here in the order and bracketing of Nauck's Teubner edition, so that it pairs with Arthur Way's English verse translation.
