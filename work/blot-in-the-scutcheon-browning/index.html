A Blot in the 'Scutcheon: A Tragedy

by Robert Browning

Mildred Tresham, sister of the proud Earl Tresham, has secretly taken Lord Mertoun as her lover, and when he comes openly to ask for her hand the family honour seems about to be satisfied. Thorold Tresham learns of the earlier nights from his old retainer Gerard and, believing his house disgraced, confronts them both. Mertoun is killed in a duel at the foot of the yew-tree avenue, Mildred dies of grief, and Thorold dies after forgiving them, leaving the blot he sought to wipe away.
