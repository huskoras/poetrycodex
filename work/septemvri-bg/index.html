Epic poem · 1924

Септември

September (1924)

by Geo Milev

The long poem on the September Uprising of 1923, a landmark of Bulgarian modernism in free verse, with its one-word lines and spaced stanzas. The poem was banned, and Milev was murdered in 1925.
