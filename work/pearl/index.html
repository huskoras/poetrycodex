Dream vision, elegy · late 14th century (this translation 1908)

Pearl

by Gawain Poet

Translated by Sophie Jewett (1908)

A grieving father falls asleep on the grave of his infant daughter and dreams her transfigured into a maiden of Heaven, radiant among the elect — an elegy of loss answered by consolation, in interlocking rhymed stanzas.
