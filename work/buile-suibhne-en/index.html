Romance in prose and verse · 1913

Buile Suibhne

The Frenzy of Suibhne

by Anonymous (Old Irish)

Translated by J. G. O'Keeffe (1913)

O'Keeffe's English version of the twelfth-century Irish romance of King Suibhne of Dal Araidhe, cursed by St Ronan, driven mad at the battle of Magh Rath, and wandering Ireland as a bird-like madman who speaks his sorrow and his love of the wild in verse, until St Moling receives him and he dies at his church.
