Autobiographical poem · 1850

The Prelude

or Growth of a Poet's Mind

by William Wordsworth

Wordsworth's blank-verse autobiography traces the growth of his imagination from boyhood in the Lake District through Cambridge, London and revolutionary France to the recovery of his poetic vocation.
