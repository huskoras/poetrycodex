後撰和歌集 (Gosen wakashū)

Later Collection of Japanese Poems

by Nashitsubo no gonin (Minamoto no Shitagō et al.)

The second imperial anthology of waka, a successor to the Kokinshū: 1,425 poems in twenty books of the seasons, love, miscellany, parting and travel, congratulation and grief.
