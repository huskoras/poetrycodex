The Homeric Hymns · 1914

Hymn XIII — To Demeter

by Homer fl. c. 8th century BC

Translated by Hugh G. Evelyn-White (1914)

Also in this archive

George Chapman’s translation (c. 1624) · Hymn XIII — To Demeter Compare side by side

(ll. 1-2) I begin to sing of rich-haired Demeter, awful goddess, of her
and of her daughter lovely Persephone.
(l. 3) Hail, goddess! Keep this city safe, and govern my song.

Source Hesiod and Homer, Hesiod, The Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, with an English translation by Hugh G. Evelyn-White, trans. Hugh G. Evelyn-White (Loeb Classical Library) (London: William Heinemann; Macmillan (New York), 1914). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 348.
