Hymn XVI — To Asclepius

by Homer fl. c. 8th century BC

Translated by Hugh G. Evelyn-White (1914)

Also in this archive

George Chapman’s translation (c. 1624) · Hymn XVI — To Asclepius Compare side by side

Andrew Lang’s translation (1899) · XVI. To the Dioscouri Compare side by side

(ll. 1-4) I begin to sing of Asclepius, son of Apollo and healer of
sicknesses. In the Dotian plain fair Coronis, daughter of King
Phlegyas, bare him, a great joy to men, a soother of cruel pangs.
(l. 5) And so hail to you, lord: in my song I make my prayer to thee!
