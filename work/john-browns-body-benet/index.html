John Brown’s Body

by Stephen Vincent Benét

Benét’s narrative poem of the American Civil War opens with an invocation to the American muse and follows the war from John Brown’s raid on Harpers Ferry to Appomattox and its aftermath, through eight books. It weaves the fortunes of imagined families, Jack Ellyat of Connecticut, Clay Wingate of Georgia and the mountain boy Luke Breckinridge, together with Lincoln, Davis, Lee, Grant and the battles from Bull Run to Gettysburg, and carries inset songs and lyrics within its books.
