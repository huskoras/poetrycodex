Philosophical poem · 1915

اسرار خودی (Asrar-e Khudi)

Iqbal's Persian masnavi of 1915, in the original Persian

by Muhammad Iqbal

Iqbal's Persian masnavi of 1915, the poem that made his name: a prologue and eighteen chapters on the strengthening of the Self through love and desire, with tales of a bird, a diamond and a coal, and a Sheikh and a Brahmin.
