The Shepheard’s Calender

Twelve Æglogues Proportionable to the Twelve Monethes

by Edmund Spenser

Spenser’s first major work: twelve eclogues, one for each month, mixing pastoral love-complaint, satire of the clergy, and elegy in a deliberately archaic English.
