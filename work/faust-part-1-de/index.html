Faust. Der Tragödie erster Teil

by Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

The German text of Faust I as first printed in 1808. Dr. Faust, a scholar sick of the limits of human knowledge, is wagered over in Heaven and then bargains his soul to Mephistopheles in return for a moment of fulfilment; the devil draws him through the tavern, the witches' kitchen and the Walpurgis Night, and into a love affair with the young Gretchen, who is destroyed in its wake. Each scene pairs with the same scene of Bayard Taylor's English translation.
