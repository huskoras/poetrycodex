Epic poem · 1913

The Fall of Troy

Posthomerica

by Quintus Smyrnaeus

Translated by Arthur S. Way (1913)

Quintus Smyrnaeus continues the Iliad from the funeral of Hector through the deaths of Achilles, Paris and the Amazon queen Penthesileia, the wooden horse and the sack of Troy, to the Greeks' storm-wrecked voyage home.
