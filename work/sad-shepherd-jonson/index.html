Pastoral drama · c. 1637

The Sad Shepherd; or, A Tale of Robin Hood

by Ben Jonson

Robin Hood invites the shepherds and shepherdesses of the Vale of Belvoir to a feast in Sherwood, where the shepherd Aeglamour mourns his lost love Earine, believed drowned in the Trent. The witch Maudlin of Papplewicke, who has in fact shut Earine up in a tree, takes Maid Marian's shape to quarrel with Robin and spoil the feast, until Robin seizes her enchanted girdle. Jonson's last play, it ends unfinished in Act III.
