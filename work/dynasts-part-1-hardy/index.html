The Dynasts: Part First

A Drama of the Napoleonic Wars

by Thomas Hardy

Hardy's vast panorama of the Napoleonic Wars, from the threat of invasion in 1805 to Austerlitz. Above the earthly action the Spirits of the Years and the Pities watch the workings of the unconscious Immanent Will, in choruses of lyric verse.
