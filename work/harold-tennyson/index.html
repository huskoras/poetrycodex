Harold: A Drama

by Alfred, Lord Tennyson

Tennyson's verse drama of the last Saxon king. Harold, Earl of Wessex, is shipwrecked on the Norman coast and forced to swear an oath to Duke William, a vow that haunts him when the dying Edward the Confessor names him heir. Crowned king, he defeats his brother Tostig and the Norwegians at Stamford Bridge, then marches south to meet the Normans at Hastings, where he falls and Edith finds his body on the field of the dead.
