Mystical poem · 1898

The Masnavi (abridged), Books I–VI

The Spiritual Couplets, abridged and translated by E. H. Whinfield

by Jalal al-Din Rumi

Translated by E. H. Whinfield (abridged; 1898)

Also in this archive

James W. Redhouse’s translation (1881) →

Rumi's great Persian Sufi teaching poem in six books, here in E. H. Whinfield's abridged English version, opening with the lament of the reed-flute torn from its reed-bed and moving through tales of the prince and the handmaid, the lion and the beasts, the harper and 'Omar, and the elephant in the dark room.
