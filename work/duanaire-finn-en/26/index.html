Duanaire Finn: The Book of the Lays of Fionn, Part I · 1908

XXVII. Oisin's Sorrow

by Anonymous (Old Irish) c. 8th–12th century

Translated by Eoin MacNeill (1908)

Sad is that, O dear Caoilte, by whom sea-roving crews were thinned, our parting from each other after the Fian is a cause of sorrow.

To fall in the east in the battle of Gabhair happily befell Mac Lughach, rather than that the youth of great deeds should be tearful and gloomy in our company.

All but myself, an ancient, despicable after all the battles, the race of Baoiscne is gone — sad is that, dear Caoilte.
