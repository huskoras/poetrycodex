মেঘনাদবধ কাব্য (Meghnadbadh Kavya)

Michael Madhusudan Dutt's Bengali epic in blank verse, in nine sargas, in the original Bengali

by Michael Madhusudan Dutt

Madhusudan's blank-verse epic of the death of Meghnad (Indrajit), son of Ravana, in the war of Lanka, told from the Rakshasa side in nine sargas; the first great epic of modern Bengali literature.
