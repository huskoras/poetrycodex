Epic poem · 1810

The Curse of Kehama

by Robert Southey

A Hindu mythological epic in twenty-four sections, in irregular rhymed stanzas: the Rajah Kehama curses the peasant Ladurlad, who is drawn into a pursuit that reaches the Swerga and the hell of Padalon.
