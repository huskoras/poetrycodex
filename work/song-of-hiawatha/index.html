Epic poem · 1855

The Song of Hiawatha

by Henry Wadsworth Longfellow

A trochaic epic drawing on Ojibwe legend, following the life of the hero Hiawatha — his childhood, wooing, feats, and eventual departure — in a rhythm modeled on the Finnish Kalevala.
