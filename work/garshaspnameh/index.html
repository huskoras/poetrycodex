گرشاسپ‌نامه (Garshaspnameh)

Asadi of Tus's Persian epic of the hero Garshasp (Garshasp-nama), completed in 1066

by Asadi of Tus

Asadi of Tus's Persian epic of Garshasp, the dragon-slaying hero of the Sam dynasty and ancestor of Rustam, told in some nine thousand couplets and completed in 1066, a generation after Firdausi's Shahnameh. The original Persian text.
