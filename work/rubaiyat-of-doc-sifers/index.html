Rubáiyát of Doc Sifers

by James Whitcomb Riley

A Hoosier country doctor, Doc Sifers, is sketched in numbered quatrain-stanzas in Indiana dialect, from his rounds on horseback and his fire-fighting to his plain-spoken kindness.
