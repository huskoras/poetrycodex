Tragedy · 1603

Sejanus His Fall

by Ben Jonson

Jonson's Roman tragedy follows the rise and ruin of Aelius Sejanus, favourite of the emperor Tiberius. Sejanus poisons Drusus, the emperor's son, and schemes against the house of Germanicus, while the honest Romans Arruntius, Silius and Sabinus watch an age of flatterers and informers close over them. Tiberius, withdrawing to Capri, secretly raises Macro against his favourite and brings him down in the senate with a long, doubtful letter, and the mob tears Sejanus to pieces.
