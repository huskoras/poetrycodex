Hymn cycle · 7th–6th century BC

Ὕμνοι Ὁμηρικοί (Homeric Hymns, Greek text)

by Homer

Thirty-three anonymous hexameter hymns of invocation to the Greek gods, transmitted alongside the Homeric epics since antiquity and traditionally, though almost certainly wrongly, attributed to Homer. The Greek text is given in the order of the Evelyn-White edition, so that each hymn pairs with the English prose translation of the same number. Hymn 1 survives only in part.
