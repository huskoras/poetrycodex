Beowulf

by Beowulf Poet

Translated by Francis Barton Gummere (1910)

The Geatish hero Beowulf crosses the sea to rid the Danish hall of Heorot of the monster Grendel, then Grendel's vengeful mother, and in old age faces a dragon guarding a cursed hoard — the oldest surviving epic in English.
