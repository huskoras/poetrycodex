Comedy · 1852

Aulularia; or, The Concealed Treasure

The Pot of Gold

by Plautus

Translated by Henry Thomas Riley (1852)

A comedy of Euclio, a miser who has found a pot of gold hidden by his grandfather and cannot rest for fear of thieves, while a rich neighbour asks for his daughter's hand, a slave steals the pot, and the girl's secret comes out. The ending is lost. Molière drew on it for L'Avare.
