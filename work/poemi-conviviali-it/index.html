Poem cycle · 1905

Poemi conviviali

by Giovanni Pascoli

Pascoli's cycle of Hellenic and ancient-world poems, written as if recited at a symposium: Solon, the blind Homer, Achilles' lyre, the last voyage of Odysseus, Alexander, Tiberius and the Nativity.
