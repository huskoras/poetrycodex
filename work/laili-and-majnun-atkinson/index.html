Laili and Majnun

A poem from the original Persian of Nizami, translated by James Atkinson

by Nizami Ganjavi

Translated by James Atkinson (1836)

The Arab tale of Qays, who is driven mad by love and called Majnun, and of Laili, who is married to another and dies of longing, as Nizami told it in the twelfth century and Atkinson first rendered it into English verse.
