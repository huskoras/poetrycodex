Romance (verse) · 1412-20

Troy Book

by John Lydgate

Lydgate's immense retelling of the Trojan War, made at the request of Prince Henry (later Henry V) and finished about 1420. Working from Guido delle Colonne's Latin Historia destructionis Troiae, he tells the whole story in rhyming couplets: Jason and Medea and the first destruction of Troy (Book I), the rebuilding of the city and the abduction of Helen (II), the war and the death of Hector (III-IV), and the treason that ends Troy, followed by the Greeks' disastrous homecomings (V). Chaucer is honoured throughout as Lydgate's master, and the poem closes with an Envoy to the prince.
