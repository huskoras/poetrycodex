Satirical poem · 1509

The Ship of Fools

Translated out of Latin, French and Dutch by Alexander Barclay, 1509

by Alexander Barclay

Translated by Alexander Barclay (1509)

A shipload of fools of every kind, from the owner of unread books to the dishonest judge and the ill-matched bride, is rowed through a long series of rime-royal chapters, each closing with Barclay’s own warning, in the first English version of Brant’s great German satire.
