長生殿 (Changsheng dian)

The Palace of Eternal Youth

by Hong Sheng 洪昇

A play of fifty scenes on the love of the Tang emperor Xuanzong and his consort Yang Guifei, from their vow in the Palace of Eternal Youth through the rebellion and her death to their reunion in heaven.
