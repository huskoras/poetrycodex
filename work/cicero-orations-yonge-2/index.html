Oration · 1852

The Orations of Cicero, Volume II

For Tullius, Fonteius, Caecina, Cluentius, Rabirius, Murena, Sulla, Archias, Flaccus and Scaurus; the Manilian Law, the Agrarian Law, the Catilinarians, and the speeches after his return

by Cicero

Translated by C. D. Yonge (1852)

The second volume of Yonge's Bohn translation of Cicero's speeches: the pleadings of his consular years and after, among them the Manilian Law, the three speeches on the Agrarian Law, the four Catilinarians, and the defences of Murena, Archias, Flaccus and Sulla, with the two speeches after his return from exile.
