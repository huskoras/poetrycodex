Sylvia; or, The May Queen · 1827

Persons of the Play

by George Darley 1795–1846

ROMANZO
ANDREA, his Servant
GERONYMO
SYLVIA
AGATHA, her Mother
STEPHANIA, ROSELLE, JACINTHA, Peasant Girls
Peasants, &c.
Spirits
MORGANA, Queen of the Fairies
NEPHON, OSME, FLORETTA, Fairies
ARARACH, King of the Fiends
GRUMIEL, MOMIEL, Demons

The Scene lies in Italy, amongst the Apennines.
