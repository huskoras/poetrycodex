Epic poem · 1825

Fridthjof's Saga

A Norse Romance

by Esaias Tegnér

Translated by Thomas A. E. Holcomb and Martha A. Lyon Holcomb (1876)

Tegnér's Swedish romantic epic in twenty-four cantos, drawn from an Icelandic saga: the farmer's son Fridthjof loves Ingeborg, daughter of King Bele, and is parted from her by her brothers, outlawed, and finally reconciled, here in English verse by the Holcombs.
