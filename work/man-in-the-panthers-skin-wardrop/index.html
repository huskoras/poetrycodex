The Man in the Panther's Skin

A Georgian epic, translated in prose

by Shota Rustaveli

Translated by Marjory Scott Wardrop (1912)

Rustaveli's Georgian national epic of c. 1200: the knight Tariel, clad in a panther's skin, and Avt'handil, who sets out to find him, search for the lost Nestan-Daredjan, in a tale of love, loyalty and chivalry.
