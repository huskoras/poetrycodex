Epic poem · 1925

Pan Balcer w Brazylji

Poemat w sześciu księgach

by Maria Konopnicka

Konopnicka's great narrative poem of Polish peasant emigrants who sail to Brazil in 1890s hope, are cheated and decimated in the emigrant barracks and the forest, and finally turn home. Told in ottava rima by the old peasant Balcer, it is one of the major works of Polish Positivist poetry.
