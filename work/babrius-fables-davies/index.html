Fable collection · 1860

The Fables of Babrius

Translated into English verse by the Rev. James Davies, from the text of Sir G. C. Lewis

by Babrius

Translated by Rev. James Davies (1860)

The Greek fables of Babrius, which put the tales of Aesop into choliambic verse, turned into English rhymed couplets: the two proems and 126 fables of Part I, with a closing fragment, then the proem and 91 fables of Part II.
