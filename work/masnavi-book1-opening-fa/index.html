Didactic poem · 13th century

مثنوی معنوی، دفتر اول: سرآغاز (Masnavi, Book 1: opening)

by Jalal al-Din Rumi

The opening of Rumi's Masnavi in the original Persian: the lament of the reed-flute, the story of the king and the slave girl, and the tale of the goldsmith, each section titled as on Ganjoor.
