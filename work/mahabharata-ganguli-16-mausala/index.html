Book 16: Mausala Parva

The Book of the Clubs

by Vyasa

Translated by Kisari Mohan Ganguli (published by Pratap Chandra Roy, 1883–1896)

Book 16 of Kisari Mohan Ganguli's complete English prose translation of the Mahabharata, published in parts by Pratap Chandra Roy, Calcutta. The Mausala Parva records the destruction of the Vrishni clan, who slaughter one another with iron clubs thirty-six years after the war, and the departure of Krishna and Balarama from the world.
