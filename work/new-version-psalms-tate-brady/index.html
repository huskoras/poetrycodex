Psalter (metrical version) · 1696

A New Version of the Psalms of David

Fitted to the Tunes Used in Churches (Tate and Brady)

by Nahum Tate and Nicholas Brady

The metrical psalter of 1696 by Nahum Tate and Nicholas Brady, the standard version sung in the Church of England for more than a century; it contains 'As pants the hart for cooling streams' (Psalm 42) and 'Through all the changing scenes of life' (Psalm 34).
