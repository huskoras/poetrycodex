Epic poem (prose translation) · 1883–1896

The Mahabharata, Book 3: Vana Parva

The Book of the Forest

by Vyasa

Translated by Kisari Mohan Ganguli (published by Pratap Chandra Roy, 1883–1896)

Also in this archive

Romesh Chunder Dutt’s translation (1899) →

Book 3 of Kisari Mohan Ganguli's complete English prose translation of the Mahabharata, published in parts by Pratap Chandra Roy, Calcutta. The Vana Parva tells of the twelve years of the Pandavas' exile in the forest after the game of dice, with the tale of Nala and Damayanti, the pilgrimages, Arjuna's quest for celestial weapons, and the Yaksha's riddling questions.
