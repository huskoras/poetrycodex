The Pastime of Pleasure

An Allegorical Poem

by Stephen Hawes

Hawes's allegorical romance in rhyme royal: the young Graunde Amoure learns the seven liberal arts in the Tower of Doctrine, wins the hand of La Bell Pucell by slaying giants and monsters, and is brought through age and death to the judgement of Fame, Time and Eternity.
