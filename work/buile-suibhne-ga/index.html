Romance in prose and verse · c. 12th century

Buile Suibhne

Middle Irish text

by Anonymous (Old Irish)

The twelfth-century Irish romance of King Suibhne of Dal Araidhe, cursed by St Ronan for hurling a spear at him and his psalter, who is driven mad at the battle of Magh Rath and wanders Ireland as a bird-like madman, speaking his sorrow and his love of the wild in some fifty poems, until he is received by St Moling and dies at his church.
