桃花扇 (Taohua shan) — The Peach Blossom Fan

by Kong Shangren

In Nanjing in 1643 the scholar Hou Fangyu, a member of the reformist Restoration Society, falls in love with the courtesan Li Xiangjun and gives her a painted fan as a pledge. The powerful minister Ma Shiying and the eunuch-party schemer Ruan Dacheng, enemies of the Society, force her into marriage with another; she refuses and strikes her head on the floor, and her blood spatters the fan, which a painter turns into peach blossoms. As the Ming court is lost to rebels and Manchus, the lovers are parted and meet again only at the end, when a Daoist priest tears up the fan and sends them both away to the religious life. Kong Shangren's 1699 play, in forty-four scenes of sung verse and speech, sets a love story against the fall of the Southern Ming.
