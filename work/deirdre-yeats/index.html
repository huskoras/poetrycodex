Deirdre

by W. B. Yeats

Fergus brings Naisi and Deirdre back to Ireland from exile, on the promise of King Conchubar's pardon. Waiting in a guest-house in a wood, while musicians sing and hint at treachery, the lovers learn that Conchubar means to take Deirdre for himself. The tragedy of the old Irish tale ends in a trap, a death, and a queen's last choice.
