Verse psalter · 1579

Psałterz Dawidów

Jan Kochanowski's verse Psalter, in Polish (Turowski edition, 1857)

by Jan Kochanowski

Jan Kochanowski's Polish verse paraphrase of all 150 Psalms, printed at Kraków in 1579. Each psalm is recast in its own metre and rhyme, and the work is counted among the high points of Polish Renaissance poetry.
