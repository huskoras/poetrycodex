Philosophical poem · c. 450 BC

Περὶ φύσεως · Καθαρμοί (Fragments)

The verse fragments, Greek text

by Empedocles

The surviving hexameter fragments of Empedocles of Acragas, in Greek: On Nature (the four roots, Love and Strife, the Sphere, the cycles of the cosmos) and Purifications (the daimon's exile, the golden age of Cypris, the ban on bloodshed), in the numbering of Diels.
