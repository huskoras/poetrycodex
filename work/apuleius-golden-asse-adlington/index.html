The Golden Asse

The Metamorphoses of Apuleius, in Adlington's Tudor translation

by Apuleius

Translated by William Adlington (1566)

Lucius, a curious young man of Thessaly, is turned into an ass by a witch's ointment and endures many masters and adventures, including the tale of Cupid and Psyche, until the goddess Isis restores him.
