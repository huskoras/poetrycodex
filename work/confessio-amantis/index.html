Confessio Amantis

Tales of the Seven Deadly Sins

by John Gower

A lover confesses to Venus's priest Genius, who works through the Seven Deadly Sins with a great gathering of exemplary tales — classical, biblical, and romance — in eight books of octosyllabic couplets.
