The Psalms of David (Psalms 44–150)

Metrical paraphrase from the Sidney Psalter, in the 1823 printed text

by Mary Sidney Herbert, Countess of Pembroke

The Countess of Pembroke's verse paraphrase of Psalms 44–150, each psalm in a different stanza form, completing the psalter begun by her brother Sir Philip Sidney.
