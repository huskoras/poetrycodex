Epic poem · 1928

The Epic of Gilgamish

Verse translation from the Assyrian and Babylonian tablets

by Anonymous Babylonian Poets

Translated by R. Campbell Thompson (1928)

Also in this archive

Morris Jastrow Jr. and Albert T. Clay’s translation (1920) →

The Babylonian epic of Gilgamish, king of Erech, and his friend Enkidu, who together slay the Ogre Humbaba and the Bull of Heaven, until Enkidu's death sends the hero in vain search of everlasting life.
