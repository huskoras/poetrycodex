Epic poem (prose translation) · 1884

The Mahabharata, Book 2: Sabha Parva

The Book of the Assembly Hall

by Vyasa

Translated by Kisari Mohan Ganguli (published by Pratap Chandra Roy, 1883–1896)

Also in this archive

Romesh Chunder Dutt’s translation (1899) →

Book 2 of Kisari Mohan Ganguli's complete English prose translation of the Mahabharata, published in parts by Pratap Chandra Roy, Calcutta. The Sabha Parva tells of the building of Yudhishthira's great assembly hall, the Rajasuya sacrifice, the death of Sisupala, and the fateful game of dice.
