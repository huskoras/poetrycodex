Epic poem · 1915

The Tale of the Armament of Igor

A Russian historical epic, A.D. 1185 (the Slovo o polku Igoreve)

by Anonymous (The Lay of Igor's Campaign)

Translated by Leonard A. Magnus (1915)

The only surviving epic of medieval Kievan Rus', telling of Prince Igor Svyatoslavic's ill-fated raid on the Polovtsy in 1185, his defeat and capture, the lament of his wife Yaroslavna, and his escape home.
