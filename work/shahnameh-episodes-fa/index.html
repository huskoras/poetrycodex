شاهنامه — داستان‌ها (Shahnameh)

The great episodes of the Shahnameh, in Persian

by Firdausi

The best-loved stories of the Persian national epic in Firdausi's own words: the tyrant Zahhak and the smith Kaveh, Zal and Rudabeh, Rostam's seven labours, the tragedies of Sohrab and Siyavash, Bizhan and Manizheh, and the deaths of Esfandiyar and of Rostam.
