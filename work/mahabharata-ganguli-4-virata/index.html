Epic poem (prose translation) · 1883–1896

The Mahabharata, Book 4: Virata Parva

The Book of Virata

by Vyasa

Translated by Kisari Mohan Ganguli (published by Pratap Chandra Roy, 1883–1896)

Also in this archive

Romesh Chunder Dutt’s translation (1899) →

Book 4 of Kisari Mohan Ganguli's complete English prose translation of the Mahabharata, published in parts by Pratap Chandra Roy, Calcutta. The Virata Parva tells of the thirteenth year of exile, when the Pandavas live in disguise at the court of King Virata, the slaying of Kichaka, and the great cattle raid and battle against the Kauravas.
