The Vespers of Palermo

A Tragedy, in Five Acts

by Felicia Hemans

Sicily, 1282, under the yoke of Charles of Anjou. The exiled Count di Procida returns in disguise and plots a rising with the nobleman Montalba. His son Raimond, who loves Constance, the sister of the French viceroy Eribert, is drawn into the conspiracy, while Eribert presses his suit on Vittoria, widow-in-grief of the murdered Conradin. At the signal of the Vesper-bell the Sicilians rise and massacre their oppressors. Raimond, bound by an oath, must choose between his love and his country, and the play ends in tragedy.
