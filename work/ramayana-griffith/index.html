by Valmiki

Translated by Ralph T. H. Griffith (1870–1874)

The Sanskrit epic of Rama, prince of Ayodhya: his exile, the abduction of Sita by the demon Ravana, the alliance with the monkey host and the war in Lanka, rendered into English verse by Ralph T. H. Griffith.
