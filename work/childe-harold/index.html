Narrative poem · 1812–1818

Childe Harold's Pilgrimage

by Lord Byron

Byron's Spenserian travelogue in four cantos follows a world-weary young pilgrim through Portugal, Spain, Greece, Belgium, the Rhine and Italy, turning from personal gloom to meditations on liberty, ruin and the sublime.
