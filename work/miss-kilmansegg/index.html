Miss Kilmansegg and Her Precious Leg

A Golden Legend

by Thomas Hood

Hood's comic narrative of an heiress whose golden leg, bought after a riding accident, makes her fortune and her ruin: a satire on money, marriage and the worship of gold, told in eighteen sections.
