Poem · c. 1050

כתר מלכות

Keter Malkhut (The Royal Crown)

by Solomon ibn Gabirol

A long devotional hymn on the greatness of God, the order of the heavens and the soul's penitence, sung in the Sephardi rite on the eve of the Day of Atonement.
