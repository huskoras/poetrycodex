Epic episode · 1835

Nala and Damayanti

An episode of the Mahabharata (Vana Parva)

by Vyasa

Translated by Henry Hart Milman (1835)

The story of King Nala of Nishadha and Damayanti, princess of Vidarbha: the swan messenger, the choice among the gods, the possession of Nala by the demon Kali, the dice, the desertion in the forest and the long wanderings, until husband and wife are reunited and his kingdom restored.
