Narrative poem · 1882

Yusuf and Zulaikha

Verse translation

by Jami

Translated by Ralph T. H. Griffith (1882)

Jami's Persian romance of the Qur'anic story of Yusuf (Joseph) and Zulaikha, the wife of the Egyptian vizier who falls in love with him, followed from her dreams and long pining, through his temptation, imprisonment and rise, to her repentance and the restoring of her youth.
