Genesis (Old English)

Old English text

by Anonymous (Old English)

The Old English verse of the Junius Manuscript in the Krapp and Dobbie edition: a verse paraphrase of Genesis running from the Creation and the fall of the rebel angels through Abraham and the near-sacrifice of Isaac, opening the Junius Manuscript.
