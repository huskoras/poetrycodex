Christus: A Mystery. Part I: The Divine Tragedy

by Henry Wadsworth Longfellow

The first part of Longfellow's trilogy Christus: A Mystery, a dramatic poem on the life of Christ. After an Introitus in which an Angel carries the prophet Habakkuk to Babylon, three Passovers follow the Gospel story from John the Baptist's preaching and the temptation in the wilderness through the miracles, the raising of Lazarus, the Last Supper, trial and Crucifixion, to the appearances after the Resurrection. The Epilogue gives the Apostles' Creed, spoken line by line by the Twelve. Part II is The Golden Legend.
