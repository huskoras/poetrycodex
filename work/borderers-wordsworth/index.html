Tragedy · 1796-97 (published 1842)

The Borderers

by William Wordsworth

Oswald, a hardened freebooter, persuades his young captain Marmaduke that the blind old Baron Herbert, who is travelling with his daughter Idonea, is an impostor who has bought the girl as a child to sell her to Lord Clifford. Marmaduke abandons the old man on a barren moor to the judgment of Heaven, and Herbert dies. When the truth comes out Oswald is killed by his own men, and Marmaduke condemns himself to a life of wandering penance.
