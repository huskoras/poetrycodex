Verse play · 1800

The Death of Wallenstein

by Friedrich Schiller

Translated by Samuel Taylor Coleridge (1800)

Wallenstein, dismissed by the Emperor and urged by his officers toward revolt, hesitates between the stars and his own ambition and opens talks with the Swedes. Max Piccolomini chooses duty over love, and dies in battle; Thekla follows him in grief; at Egra Wallenstein is murdered by his own officers.
