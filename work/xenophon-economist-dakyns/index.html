Philosophical dialogue · 1897

The Economist

Oeconomicus: a Treatise on the Science of the Household, in the Form of a Dialogue

by Xenophon

Translated by Henry Graham Dakyns (1897)

Xenophon's dialogue on the management of a household and estate: Socrates and Critobulus discuss wealth and farming, and Socrates recalls the model gentleman Ischomachus, his methods and his instruction of his young wife.
