Leylâ vü Mecnûn

by Fuzuli

Fuzuli's mesnevi of the lovers Leylâ and Mecnûn, composed in 1535: the classic Ottoman Turkish telling of the Arab legend of the poet driven mad by love for a woman he cannot marry.
