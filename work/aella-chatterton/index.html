Verse play · 1777

Ælla: A Tragycal Enterlude

or Discoorseynge Tragedie, wrotenn bie Thomas Rowleie

by Thomas Chatterton

Chatterton's most admired Rowley forgery, a tragedy supposedly written by the fifteenth-century priest Thomas Rowley for William Canynge of Bristol. Ælla, lord of Bristol castle, marries Birtha, then is called away to repel a Danish raid. His friend Celmonde, secretly in love with Birtha, abducts her in the woods, and the Danes who capture them lead to a deadly end for all three. The minstrels' songs, including 'O! synge untoe mie roundelaie', sit within the play.
