The Shorter Play-Fragments · 1921

The Hero: Other Fragments

by Menander 4th century BC (Paton’s date)

Translated by Francis G. Allinson (1921)

DAVUS (perhaps resuming after his request for faggots).
. . . Oh, Getas, have you never loved?

I am not one of these (malignant?) ‘Heroes.’

DAVUS.
Than Love, my mistress, there exists no greater force.
Nay, even Zeus himself, who over heaven’s gods
Holds sway, to Love’s compulsion yields in everything.

’T were right that Honour’s beauty stamp the nobly-bred
And that the free-born everywhere think lofty thoughts.

. . . . of well-mixed wine
A pitcher-full. Now take and drink it down. . .

Scarce from the spell set free, dear sir, you're drugged again.

Now round our pear-trees I will guide
These hunters from the city who have just come up.

Be well assured, I too will yield in this.

. . . . . To some one of the slave-maids giving (it).

O luckless you, unless you'll go. . . . . . . .

. . . you'll make yourself a city-man again.

(LACHES)
Good Heracles! Let me talk without anyone to overhear.

LACHES.
Explain who in the world is her mother.

MYRRHINA.
They say it is the Thracian woman.

LACHES.
But it isn’t.

MYRRHINA.
Well, who is it then?

LACHES.
’Tis you, unfortunate!

MYRRHINA.
What!

LACHES.
Yes, clearly, you my wife, by Zeus. Crows pick you!
You're astonished?

MYRRHINA.
Why—I—what things you say!

LACHES.
That which I shall also make good. I’ve settled that long since.

MYRRHINA.
Sweat and confusion!

LACHES.
Yes, good, by Zeus, my Myrrhina, I took unto myself a shepherd who (. . . adopted?) a bleating (baby?).

(Lacuna of some 14 lines.)

LACHES.
What is the matter with you? Nay! You stand there like a statue!

MYRRHINA.
How pitiable! I who all alone have such misfortunes as have no believable climax beyond them!

LACHES.
Your resolve will bring efficient healing. But did someone by violence once wrong you?

LACHES.
Do you guess who in the world it was . . .

. . . She was thus brought to shame (at the festival of?) Alea Athena.

(MYRRHINA?)
She is eighteen years old.

(LACHES?)
She is not the only one . . . But granted, if you agree . . .

MYRRHINA.
. . . this thing happens.
