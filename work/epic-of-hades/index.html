Narrative poem · 1876–1877

The Epic of Hades

In Three Books

by Sir Lewis Morris

A Victorian dream-vision in three books: the poet walks through Tartarus, Hades and Olympus, hearing the shades of Greek myth tell their own stories and the gods reveal what they stand for.
