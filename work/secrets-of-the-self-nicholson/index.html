Philosophical poem · 1920

The Secrets of the Self (Asrar-i Khudi)

A philosophical poem, translated from the Persian

by Muhammad Iqbal

Translated by Reynold A. Nicholson (1920)

Iqbal's Persian masnavi of 1915, the poem that made his name, in Nicholson's English verse: a Prologue and eighteen chapters on the strengthening of the Self through love and desire, with tales of a tiger and sheep, a diamond and a coal, and a Brahmin and a Sheikh.
