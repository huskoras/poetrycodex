Funerary texts · 1904

The Egyptian Book of the Dead

Translation and Commentary

by Anonymous Egyptian Poets

Translated by P. Le Page Renouf and Édouard Naville (1904)

The funerary spells of ancient Egypt, known as the Chapters of Coming Forth by Day, from hymns to the sun and the Negative Confession to charms for the heart and the tomb, in the English translation of Renouf and Naville (1904).
