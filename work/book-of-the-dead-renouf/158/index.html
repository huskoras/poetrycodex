The Egyptian Book of the Dead · 1904

Chapter CLXVII. Chapter of bringing an Eye.

by Anonymous Egyptian Poets c. 2000–1000 BC

Translated by P. Le Page Renouf and Édouard Naville (1904)

When Thoth had brought the Eye, he appeased the Eye, After Rā had wounded her, she was raging furiously and then Thoth calmed her after she had gone away raging. As I am sound, she is sound and N. is sound.

This translation is printed as prose in its source edition.
