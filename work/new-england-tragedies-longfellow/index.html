The New England Tragedies

Christus: A Mystery, Part III

by Henry Wadsworth Longfellow

The third part of Longfellow's trilogy Christus: A Mystery, two verse plays on the religious intolerance of colonial New England. John Endicott shows the Puritan governor of Boston persecuting Quakers in 1665, whipping and hanging those who will not conform, while his own son sides with them. Giles Corey of the Salem Farms dramatises the witchcraft delusion of 1692, in which an old farmer, pressed to death for refusing to plead, becomes its martyr. The part closes with the Finale, Saint John wandering the earth, and the Interludes between the parts of the trilogy are printed after the plays.
