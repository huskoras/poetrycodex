Narrative poem · 1836

Krst pri Savici

Povest v verzih

by France Prešeren

Prešeren's verse tale of the pagan Slovene chieftain Črtomir, defeated in war against the Christian Valjhun, and of his love for the priestess Bogomila, who has become a Christian. It opens with a sonnet of dedication to the poet's friend Matija Čop, an introduction in terza rima and the narrative proper in ottava rima.
