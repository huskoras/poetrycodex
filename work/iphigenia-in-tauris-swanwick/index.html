Iphigenia in Tauris

by Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

Translated by Anna Swanwick

Goethe's verse drama in five acts. Iphigenia, saved from sacrifice at Aulis and made priestess of Diana among the Taurians, longs for Greece. When her brother Orestes, pursued by the Furies, arrives with his friend Pylades as captives destined for the altar, she must choose between truth and escape, and between loyalty to the king who sheltered her and the call of her family.
