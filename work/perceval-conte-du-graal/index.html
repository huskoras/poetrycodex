Perceval ou le Conte du Graal

Le Roman de Perceval; Li Contes del Graal

by Chrétien de Troyes

Chrétien's last, unfinished Arthurian romance and the first story of the Grail, in which the naive young Perceval is knighted and fails to ask the question at the Fisher King's castle.
