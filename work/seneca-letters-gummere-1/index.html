Letters · c. AD 65

Moral Letters to Lucilius, Volume I (Letters 1–65)

Epistulae Morales ad Lucilium

by Lucius Annaeus Seneca

Translated by Richard M. Gummere (1917)

The first sixty-five of the letters Seneca wrote in his last years to his friend Lucilius, then procurator in Sicily: short essays in Stoic practice on time, friendship, death, anger, wealth, retirement and the philosopher's daily discipline.
