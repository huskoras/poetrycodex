Epic poem · 1845

Childe John

János vitéz

by Sándor Petőfi

Translated by William N. Loew (1912)

Petőfi's Hungarian folk epic in twenty-seven cantos: the shepherd boy Jancsi (Childe John) is parted from his sweetheart Helen and goes out into the world through war, giants and witches, until he wins her back and a fairy kingdom, here in English verse by William N. Loew.
