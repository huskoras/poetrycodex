The Mahabharata, Book 11: Stri Parva

The Book of the Women

by Vyasa

Translated by Kisari Mohan Ganguli (published by Pratap Chandra Roy, 1883–1896)

Also in this archive

Romesh Chunder Dutt’s translation (1899) →

Book 11 of Kisari Mohan Ganguli's complete English prose translation of the Mahabharata, published in parts by Pratap Chandra Roy, Calcutta. The Stri Parva is the lament of the women: Dhritarashtra's grief and anger, Gandhari's curse, the widows moving over the field of Kurukshetra among the dead, and the funeral rites for the fallen.
