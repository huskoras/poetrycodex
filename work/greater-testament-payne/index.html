Poem (mock testament with inset ballads) · 1878

The Greater Testament

Le Testament, translated by John Payne

by François Villon

Translated by John Payne (1878; Mosher edition, 1909)

Begun about 1461, after Villon's release from prison at Meung, this long mock will mingles confession, satire and lament, and holds the ballads on which his fame mostly rests, among them the Ballad of Old-Time Ladies.
