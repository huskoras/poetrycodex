Parallel Lives: Themistocles and Camillus

by Plutarch

Translated by Bernadotte Perrin (1914)

Plutarch pairs the Athenian Themistocles, architect of the fleet that won Salamis, with Camillus, the Roman who saved the city from the Gauls. Each statesman is driven into exile by the ingratitude of his own people, and each is shown in the hour of the country's peril. The comparison of the pair is lost.
