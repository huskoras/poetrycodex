Epic poem · 1807

The Columbiad

A Poem in Ten Books

by Joel Barlow

Barlow's patriotic epic on the American Republic: the imprisoned Columbus is shown, in vision, the history and future of the New World by the genius Hesper, in ten books of heroic couplets.
