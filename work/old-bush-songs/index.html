Anthology of folk songs · 1905

The Old Bush Songs

Comprising Bush Ballads, Songs of the Bullock Dray and the Overland Routes, Digger’s Ditties, and other Songs of Australian Life

by A. B. Paterson (ed.)

Paterson’s collection of anonymous and half-forgotten songs of the Australian bush: shearers, stockmen, swagmen, diggers, immigrants and bushrangers.
