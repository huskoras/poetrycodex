Poetic diary (nikki) · c. 1008

和泉式部日記

Izumi Shikibu nikki

by Izumi Shikibu

The diary of Izumi Shikibu (c. 976 to after 1033), probably written around 1007 to 1008: the story of her affair with Prince Atsumichi, told in prose and in the poems the two exchange from the fourth month until she is taken into his palace at the end of the year.
