The Homeric Hymns · c. 1624

Hymn XXIV — To Hestia

by Homer fl. c. 8th century BC

Translated by George Chapman (c. 1624)

Also in this archive

Andrew Lang’s translation (1899) →

Vesta, that as a servant oversees
King Phœbus’ hallow’d house, in all degrees
Of guide about it, on the sacred shore
Of heavenly Pythos, and hast evermore
Rich balms distilling from thy odorous hair,
Grace this house with thy housewifely repair!
Enter, and bring a mind that most may move,
Conferring even, the great in counsels, Jove;
And let my verse taste of your either’s love.

Source Homer, The Odysseys of Homer, together with the Shorter Poems (the Hymns), translated according…, trans. George Chapman (London and New York: George Newnes, Limited; Charles Scribner's Sons, 1904). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 48895.
