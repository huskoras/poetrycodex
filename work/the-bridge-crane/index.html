The Bridge

A Poem

by Hart Crane

Hart Crane’s long poem of America, taking the Brooklyn Bridge as its emblem: from Columbus’s landfall in Ave Maria through the Pocahontas section, Cutty Sark, the Whitmanic Cape Hatteras, the Three Songs, Quaker Hill and The Tunnel, to the closing hymn of Atlantis.
