Essays and Miscellanies (Moralia), Volume III

Translated from the Greek by Several Hands, corrected and revised by William W. Goodwin

by Plutarch

Translated by Several hands, revised by William W. Goodwin (1870)

Ten of Plutarch's essays in the old English version of 1684-94, revised by William Goodwin: on living unnoticed, exile, brotherly love, Aristophanes and Menander, the verse of the Delphic oracles, the opinions of the philosophers on nature, the Stoics, moral virtue, and the long table-talk of the Symposiacs and the Natural Questions.
