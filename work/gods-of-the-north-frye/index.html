The Gods of the North

An Epic Poem

by Adam Oehlenschläger

Translated by William Edward Frye (1845)

Oehlenschläger's cycle of thirty cantos retelling the Norse myths, chiefly the adventures of Thor and the mischief of Lok, among the Aesir and the giants, in Frye's English rhymed and blank verse.
