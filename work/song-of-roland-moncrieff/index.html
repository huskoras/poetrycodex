Epic poem · 1919

The Song of Roland

La Chanson de Roland

by Anonymous (Old French)

Translated by C. K. Scott Moncrieff (1919)

Betrayed by his stepfather Ganelon, Charlemagne's nephew Roland holds the pass at Roncevaux against the Saracens, refuses to sound his horn until too late, and dies a martyr; the Emperor then avenges him.
