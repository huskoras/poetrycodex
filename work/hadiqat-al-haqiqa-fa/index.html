حدیقة الحقیقه، الباب الاوّل (Hadiqat al-Haqiqa, Book I: On the Unity of God)

Sana'i's Garden of Truth, Book I of ten, in the original Persian

by Sana'i

Book I of Sana'i's Garden of Truth, the first great Persian mystical masnavi: the unity of God, the limits of reason, and the tale of the blind men and the elephant. Book I of ten.
