The Prophet

by Kahlil Gibran

Gibran's best-known work, written in English: the prophet Almustafa, about to sail home after twelve years in the city of Orphalese, answers the townspeople's questions on love, work, joy and sorrow, death and other matters of life, in twenty-six poetic discourses framed by his arrival and his farewell.
