Tragedy · c. 441 BC

Ἀντιγόνη (Antigone)

by Sophocles

Creon forbids burial of the traitor Polyneikes, and his niece Antigone buries him in defiance, appealing to the unwritten laws of the gods. Sentenced to a living tomb, she dies, and Creon loses son and wife. The Greek text is given in the order of the Storr edition, so that it pairs with the English prose translation of the same play.
