Fables (Ysopet)

Old French text, ed. B. de Roquefort (1820)

by Marie de France

One hundred and three fables in Old French octosyllabic couplets, with a prologue and epilogue, in which Marie de France says she turned the tales of 'Ysopet' from English into French for Count William.
