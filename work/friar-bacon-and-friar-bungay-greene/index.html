Friar Bacon and Friar Bungay

The Honourable History of Friar Bacon and Friar Bungay

by Robert Greene

Prince Edward, hunting near Fressingfield, falls in love with Margaret, the Keeper's daughter, and sends Lacy, Earl of Lincoln, to woo her on his behalf; Lacy falls for her himself. Meanwhile Friar Bacon of Oxford, a scholar of magic, builds a brazen head to wall England in brass, defeats the German conjurer Vandermast in a contest of spirits, and shows Edward the distant courtship in a glass. The play ends with Edward's renunciation of Margaret, her marriage to Lacy, and Bacon's prophecy at a royal wedding.
