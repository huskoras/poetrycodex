Epic poem · 1569, 1578, 1589

La Araucana

Epic of the conquest of Chile, in three parts (Cantos I–XXXVII)

by Alonso de Ercilla

Ercilla's epic in 37 cantos of ottava rima on the Spanish war against the Mapuche of Arauco, written by a soldier who took part in it, and the first great epic to give its enemies heroic stature.
