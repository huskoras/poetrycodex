Poetic prose · 1869

Petits Poèmes en prose (Le Spleen de Paris)

in the original French

by Charles Baudelaire

Fifty short prose poems of Paris, written from the 1850s until the poet’s death: wandering, boredom, charity, vice and tenderness in the modern city. Baudelaire called them a serpent that may be cut anywhere.
