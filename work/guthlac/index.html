Saint's life · c. 8th-9th century (poet unknown)

Guthlac

Guthlac A and Guthlac B

by Anonymous (Old English)

Translated by Charles W. Kennedy (1910)

The life of St. Guthlac of Crowland, a former warrior turned hermit who does battle with demons in the Lincolnshire fens (Guthlac A), and an account, closely based on Felix's Latin Life, of his final illness and death (Guthlac B).
