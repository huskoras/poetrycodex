மணிமேகலை (Manimekalai)

The Jewelled Girdle: the prologue and the thirty cantos, in the original Tamil

by Sittalai Sattanar

The story of Manimekalai, daughter of Kovalan and Madhavi, who renounces the world to become a Buddhist nun: the Buddhist twin of the Silappatikaram, in thirty cantos with a prologue.
