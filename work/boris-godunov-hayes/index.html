Boris Godunov

A Drama in Verse

by Alexander Pushkin

Translated by Alfred Hayes (1918)

Pushkin's historical tragedy of the Time of Troubles. After the death of the young Tsarevich Dimitry, the boyar Boris Godunov is begged to take the crown, but a novice monk, Gregory Otrepiev, learns that he may pass himself off as the murdered prince and escapes to Poland. Backed by Polish nobles and the love of Marina, the Pretender invades Russia, while Boris, haunted by his crime, sees his power fail. Boris dies, and on the Pretender's approach Moscow turns against the family of Godunov.
