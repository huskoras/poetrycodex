Allegorical poem · 1856

Salaman and Absal

An Allegory

by Jami

Translated by Edward FitzGerald (1856)

Jami's allegory of a king of Greece whose son Salaman, nursed and then seduced by the beautiful Absal, flees with her, is saved from a funeral pyre, and is finally freed from earthly love by the heavenly Venus, with the closing section explaining the story as the soul's journey.
