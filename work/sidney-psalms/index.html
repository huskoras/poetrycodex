Psalter (verse paraphrase) · c. 1580s (first printed 1823)

The Psalms of David (Psalms 1–43)

Metrical paraphrase from the Sidney Psalter, in the 1823 printed text

by Sir Philip Sidney

Sir Philip Sidney's verse paraphrase of the first forty-three Psalms, each in a different metre and stanza form, begun in the early 1580s and completed by his sister Mary, Countess of Pembroke.
