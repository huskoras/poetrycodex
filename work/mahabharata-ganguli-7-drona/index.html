Epic poem (prose translation) · 1883–1896

The Mahabharata, Book 7: Drona Parva

The Book of Drona

by Vyasa

Translated by Kisari Mohan Ganguli (published by Pratap Chandra Roy, 1883–1896)

Also in this archive

Romesh Chunder Dutt’s translation (1899) →

Book 7 of Kisari Mohan Ganguli's complete English prose translation of the Mahabharata, published in parts by Pratap Chandra Roy, Calcutta. The Drona Parva narrates the five days of battle under Drona's command: the death of Abhimanyu in the wheel formation, the slaying of Jayadratha, the night battle in which Ghatotkacha falls, and the stratagem by which Drona himself is killed.
