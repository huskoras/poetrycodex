Epic poem · 1917

Who Can Be Happy and Free in Russia?

Verse translation

by Nikolay Nekrasov

Translated by Juliet M. Soskice (1917)

Seven peasants, freed from serfdom in 1861, wander Russia asking who lives happily and at ease: the priest, the landowner, the merchant or the Tsar.
