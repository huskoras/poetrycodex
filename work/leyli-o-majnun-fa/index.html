Narrative poem · 1188

لیلی و مجنون (Layli o Majnun)

The Persian original of Nizami's romance

by Nizami Ganjavi

Nizami's Persian masterpiece of 1188, the tale of Qays, called Majnun, mad for love of Layli, as he wrote it for the Shirvanshah Akhsitan: the original of which Atkinson's English version is a rendering.
