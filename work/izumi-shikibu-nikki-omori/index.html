Poetic diary (nikki) · 1920

The Diary of Izumi Shikibu

Translated by Annie Shepley Omori and Kochi Doi

by Izumi Shikibu

Annie Shepley Omori and Kochi Doi (1920)

Omori and Doi's 1920 English version of the Heian poetic diary in which Izumi Shikibu records her love affair with Prince Atsumichi in letters and waka, from the fourth month to her removal to his palace at the year's end.
