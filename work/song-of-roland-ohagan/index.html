The Song of Roland

La Chanson de Roland

by Anonymous (Old French)

Translated by John O'Hagan (1880)

Also in this archive

C. K. Scott Moncrieff’s translation (1919) →

John O'Hagan's version renders the epic of Roland's last stand at Roncevaux in rhymed octosyllabic couplets, divided into three Parts and numbered laisses, with scene headings of his own.
