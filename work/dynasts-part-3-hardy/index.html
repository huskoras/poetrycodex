The Dynasts: Part Third

An Epic-Drama of the War with Napoleon, 1812-1815

by Thomas Hardy

Part Third follows Napoleon from the crossing of the Niemen and the retreat from Moscow through Vitoria, Leipzig, abdication and Elba to the Hundred Days and Waterloo. The Spirits of the Years, the Pities and the Ironic watch from the Overworld, and an After Scene closes the whole epic-drama with a chorus on the Immanent Will.
