Epic poem · 1905–1909 (vols I–IV of 9)

The Shahnama of Firdausi

Done into English by Arthur George Warner and Edmond Warner (vols I–IV)

by Firdausi

Translated by Arthur George Warner and Edmond Warner (1905–1925)

Also in this archive

James Atkinson’s translation (1832) →

The Warners' complete blank-verse English translation of Firdausi's Persian national epic; volumes I–IV, from the creation of kingship under Gaiumart to the war with Mazandaran, Suhrab, Siyawush and the coming of Kai Khusrau, then Kai Khusrau's long wars with Turan (Piran, Kamus, the Khan of Chin, Bizhan and Manizha, the Twelve Rukhs, the fall of Afrasiyab) and the reign of Luhrasp.
