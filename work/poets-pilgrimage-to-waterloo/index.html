The Poet's Pilgrimage to Waterloo

Proem and two parts (1816)

by Robert Southey

Southey's account in six-line stanzas of his journey in 1815 through Flanders and Brussels to the field of Waterloo, followed by a dream-vision on the Mountain of the Muse in which he is shown the hopes of mankind after the victory.
