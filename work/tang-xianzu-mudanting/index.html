牡丹亭 (Mudan ting) — The Peony Pavilion

by Tang Xianzu

The Nanan prefect's daughter Du Liniang falls asleep in her garden, dreams of a young scholar, Liu Mengmei, and wastes away of longing for him. She paints her own portrait, dies, and is buried under a plum tree. Three years later Liu finds the portrait, her ghost visits him, and with the help of the underworld judge and his own devotion he opens her tomb and she lives again. They marry in the face of her father's disbelief, a rebel siege and Liu's imprisonment, until the emperor sets all right. Written in sung verse in fifty-five scenes, it is the great romance of the Chinese stage; the dream scene, 驚夢, is its most famous passage.
