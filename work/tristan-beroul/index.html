Romance · 1922

Le Roman de Tristan

Old French text (the surviving fragment)

by Béroul

The older, rougher of the two great twelfth-century Tristan poems in French: the lovers' flight to the forest of Morrois, the leper episode, the oath on the relics, and the lovers' parting, in octosyllabic couplets.
