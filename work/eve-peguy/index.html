Long poem · 1913

Ève

by Charles Péguy

Péguy's vast poem of some nineteen hundred rhymed quatrains, spoken from the mother of mankind to her children and through her to the fallen of France, among them the celebrated run beginning 'Heureux ceux qui sont morts pour la terre charnelle'.
