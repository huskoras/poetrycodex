Anthology of sijo · 1728

청구영언 (Cheonggu yeongeon, Jinbon)

The 1728 manuscript anthology of sijo compiled by Kim Cheon-taek

by Kim Cheon-taek (comp.)

The oldest surviving anthology of Korean sijo, compiled in 1728 by the singer-poet Kim Cheon-taek: about six hundred short songs, from the poets of late Goryeo and Joseon to anonymous bawdy and rambling verses, arranged by musical mode.
