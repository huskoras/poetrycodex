Piers Plowman

The Vision of Piers Ploughman

by William Langland

Falling asleep on the Malvern Hills, the dreamer Will sees a field full of folk and sets out through a sequence of visions to find Do-well, Do-bet, and Do-best — a vast alliterative satire of church, court, and commons.
