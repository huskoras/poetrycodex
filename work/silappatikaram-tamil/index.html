சிலப்பதிகாரம் (Silappatikaram)

The Tale of the Anklet: the prologue and the thirty cantos, in the original Tamil

by Ilango Adigal

The story of Kovalan, Kannagi and Madhavi, and of the anklet that brought down the city of Madurai, told in song, verse and rhythmic prose by the Chera prince turned ascetic Ilango Adigal.
