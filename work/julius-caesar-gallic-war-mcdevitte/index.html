The Gallic War

Commentaries on the war in Gaul, 58–52 BC, in eight books (De Bello Gallico)

by Julius Caesar

Translated by W. A. McDevitte and W. S. Bohn (1869)

Caesar's own account of his campaigns against the Gauls, Germans and Britons, from the Helvetian migration to the siege of Alesia and the final pacification of Gaul, with Hirtius's continuation.
