Verse play · c. 450 BC

Ajax

Aias

by Sophocles

Translated by Lewis Campbell (1906)

Denied the armour of the dead Achilles, which is awarded to Odysseus, Ajax plans to murder the Greek chiefs; Athena drives him mad and he slaughters the army’s flocks instead. Waking to his shame, he deceives his captive-wife Tecmessa and the sailors of Salamis and falls on his sword, and his half-brother Teucer must then defend his body against Menelaus and Agamemnon until Odysseus secures his burial.
