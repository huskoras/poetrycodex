Fragments · 1914

The Catalogues of Women and Other Fragments

Fragments of the Hesiodic poems, in prose translation

by Hesiod

Translated by Hugh G. Evelyn-White (Loeb, 1914)

The surviving fragments of the poems ancient readers ascribed to Hesiod beyond the Theogony and Works and Days: the genealogical Catalogue of Women, the Great Eoiae, the Melampodia, the Aegimius and shorter pieces, mostly preserved as quotations by later writers.
