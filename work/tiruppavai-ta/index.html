Lyric sequence · 9th century

திருப்பாவை (Tiruppavai)

The Maiden’s Vow, in Tamil

by Andal

Thirty pasurams in which the young Andal, as a cowherd girl, calls her friends from sleep to bathe in the month of Margali and win the favour of Krishna: the best-known poem of the Tamil Alvars, sung every winter in Vaishnava temples.
