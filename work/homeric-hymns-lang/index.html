The Homeric Hymns

by Homer

Translated by Andrew Lang (1899)

Also in this archive

Andrew Lang’s prose version of the thirty-three Homeric Hymns, the anonymous invocations to the Greek gods that antiquity ascribed to Homer. Lang numbers them in his own order, so his Hymn IV is the long Hymn to Demeter. Square brackets in the text are Lang’s own, marking passages of doubtful authenticity; his essays, notes and illustrations are omitted.
