Judith (Old English)

Old English text

by Anonymous (Old English)

A retelling, in the Nowell Codex alongside Beowulf, of the apocryphal Book of Judith, in the original Old English: the widow Judith beheads the drunken Assyrian general Holofernes and delivers her people, recast in the heroic idiom of Old English verse.
