The Story of Sigurd the Volsung and the Fall of the Niblungs

In four books: Sigmund, Regin, Brynhild, Gudrun

by William Morris

Morris's epic retelling of the Volsunga Saga in long anapaestic lines: the Volsung kings, Sigurd's slaying of Fafnir, his waking of Brynhild, the Niblungs' treachery, and the vengeance of Gudrun, in four books.
