Epic poem · 1825

The Tale of Frithiof

Frithiof's Saga

by Esaias Tegnér

Translated by Captain H. Spalding (1872)

Also in this archive

Thomas A. E. Holcomb and Martha A. Lyon Holcomb’s translation (1876) →

Tegnér's Swedish romantic epic in twenty-four cantos, drawn from an Icelandic saga: the farmer's son Frithiof loves Ingeborg, daughter of King Bele, and is parted from her by her brothers, outlawed, and finally reconciled, here in the English verse of Captain Spalding, a second translation, in the original measures, of 1872.
