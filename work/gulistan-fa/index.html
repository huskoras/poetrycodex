گلستان (Golestan)

Sa'di's Rose Garden (1258): the preface and eight chapters, prose and verse together, in the original Persian

by Sa'di of Shiraz

Sa'di's Gulistan (The Rose Garden, 1258): a preface and eight chapters of moral tales and maxims, in rhymed prose studded with verse, on kings, dervishes, contentment, silence, love and youth, old age, education and companionship.
