XXIII

by Jean Ingelow

From Poems by Jean Ingelow, In Two Volumes, Volume II.

History & Politics

'Or the night shall rest my fee
Or the day shall nought of me,'
Quoth the witch. 'An't thee beseem,
Sell thy kingdom for a dream.'
