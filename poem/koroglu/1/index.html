My Prince Has Issued an Order

by Köroğlu

Translated by Alexander Chodzko (1842) · From Specimens of the Popular Poetry of Persia (1842)

History & PoliticsSocial Commentaries

My prince has issued an order that I should be punished. But, by Allah, I know how to defend myself, I shall escape. In vain wouldst thou offer me thy riches and favours, as food is offered to the gluttonous and famished eagle; I will reject them all.

This translation is printed as prose in its source edition.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Roushan, facing arrest by the prince whose order blinded his father, defies him in this improvised song while the prince's men wait.
