O My Gardens!

by Köroğlu

Translated by Alexander Chodzko (1842) · From Specimens of the Popular Poetry of Persia (1842)

LivingNature

O my gardens! I used to gather your flowers, make nosegays of them, and send them as presents to my sweethearts. My friends standing on the road, cried and remained alone. Kurroglou, put thy confidence in God! He will be a shield against the misfortune that may befal thee unawares. Foe! do not step out before mine eyes, and do not conceal from me the last sight of Chamly-bill, left so far behind me.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

A farewell song in which Kurroglou remembers his gardens and the road he has left behind him at Chamly-bill.
