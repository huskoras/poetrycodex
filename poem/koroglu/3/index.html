It Is Thyself That Thou Hast Built and Ruined

by Köroğlu

History & Politics

It is thyself that thou hast built and ruined, when thou hast lent thy ear to base calumniators. I shall take life. I shall overturn thy throne.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Roushan warns the prince that his trust in base advisers will bring about his own ruin.
