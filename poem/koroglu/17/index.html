O Thou Whom I Got First into My Hands

by Köroğlu

Translated by Alexander Chodzko (1842) · From Specimens of the Popular Poetry of Persia (1842)

Relationships

O thou whom I got first into my hands in Turkestan, come Kyrat, come, darling of my life! Thou hast fallen into the hands of a villain; come Kyrat, thou dearest to me of all things in life, come! I had a bit made for thee of fifteen pounds of iron. When thou art angry, thou wilt not touch food for three days. Thou wilt not stumble once in a race of forty farsakhs. O! Kyrat, thou dearest of all things to me in life, come!

This translation is printed as prose in its source edition.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Kurroglou's call to his horse Kyrat, whom he says he first won in Turkestan.
