The Mind

Kurroglou Says, I Came into This World

by Köroğlu

Translated by Alexander Chodzko (1842) · From Specimens of the Popular Poetry of Persia (1842)

The MindLiving

Kurroglou says, 'I came into this world. I learned the price of the days gone by. I died the very day my mother brought me forth. I have only two brothers, my sword and my horse. Ye mountains, ye are my sisters.'

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

A self-description in which Kurroglou says he has only his sword and his horse as brothers, and calls the mountains his sisters.
