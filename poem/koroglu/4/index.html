History & Politics

Enough of This Boasting

by Köroğlu

Translated by Alexander Chodzko (1842) · From Specimens of the Popular Poetry of Persia (1842)

History & PoliticsThe Mind

Enough of this boasting. What are in my eyes thirty, sixty, or a hundred of your warriors? What are your rocks, your precipices, and deserts, under the hoof of my steed? In me you behold the leopard of the mountains and of the valleys.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Roushan replies to the prince's offer of a command by answering his threats with a boast of his own power.
