Without Kyrat

by Köroğlu

Relationships

Without Kyrat, life and the world is but a sin to me. Poor Kurroglou! Now, when Kyrat has left thy hands, thou must strike with sorrow thy head, Kurroglou!

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Kurroglou's lament after he has parted with his horse Kyrat.
