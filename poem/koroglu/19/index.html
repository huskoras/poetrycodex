Nature

The Pregnant Sun Brings Forth the Day

by Köroğlu

Translated by Alexander Chodzko (1842) · From Specimens of the Popular Poetry of Persia (1842)

NatureTime & Brevity

The pregnant sun brings forth the day. It gathers a host of rays on its head. From its face comes snow — the frost. What does it signify? Is it a hurricane, or a winter?

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

A riddling song about the sun and the frost, sung in the narrative just before Ayvaz's angry frown sets up a quarrel.
