Death After War

by Helen Gray Cone 1859–1934

From Soldiers of the Light (1911)

History & Politics

Gone the Red Harvester, with heaped-up wain
Darkening against the blood-bright sky; yet lingers
The lone, gaunt Gleaner on the twilight plain,
Blind-gathering with the clutch of hungry fingers.

Source Helen Gray Cone, Soldiers of the Light (Boston: Richard G. Badger (The Gorham Press), 1911). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 71380.
