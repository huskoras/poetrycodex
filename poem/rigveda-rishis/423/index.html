Rig Veda 5.71: O Varuna and Mitra, ye who slay the foemen, come with might

by The Rishis of the Rig Veda c. 1500–1000 BCE (traditional; uncertain)

Translated by Ralph T. H. Griffith (1896) · From The Hymns of the Rigveda (1896)

Religion

O Varuna and Mitra, ye who slay the foemen, come with might
To this our goodly sacrifice.

For, Varuna and Mitra, ye Sages are Rulers over all.
Fill full our songs, for this ye can.

Come to the juice that we have pressed. Varuna, Mitra, come to drink
This Soma of the worshipper.
