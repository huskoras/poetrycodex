Relationships

崔九欲往南山馬上口號與別 (Cui jiu yu wang nan shan ma shang kou hao yu bie)

by P'ei Ti 9th century AD

From 唐詩三百首 (Three Hundred Tang Poems)

Relationships

歸山深淺去，須盡丘壑美。
莫學武陵人，暫遊桃源裏。

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

五言絕句. Also titled 留別王維; 送崔九.
