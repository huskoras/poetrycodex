The Rose of Youth

by Dionysius the Sophist

Translated by Rennell Rodd (1916) · From Love, Worship and Death (1916)

Time & Brevity

Girl with the roses and the grace
Of all the roses in your face,
Are you, or are the blooms you bear,
Or haply both your market ware?

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Rodd's notes give no Anthology number for this epigram; it is placed here under Dionysius the Sophist at the editor's direction.
