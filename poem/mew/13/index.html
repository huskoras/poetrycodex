Religion

Exspecto Resurrectionem

by Charlotte Mew

From The Farmer's Bride (1921 edition)

ReligionDeath & Dying

Oh! King who hast the key
Of that dark room,
The last which prisons us but held not Thee,
Thou know’st its gloom.
Dost Thou a little love this one
Shut in to-night,
Young and so piteously alone,
Cold--out of sight?
Thou know’st how hard and bare
The pillow of that new-made narrow bed,
Then leave not there
So dear a head!

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

A short prayer to the King who holds the key of the dark room, asking Him not to leave 'so dear a head' on the hard pillow of a narrow new-made bed. The title is from the Creed: 'I look for the resurrection'.
