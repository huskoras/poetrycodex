The Shade-Catchers

by Charlotte Mew 1869–1928

From The Farmer's Bride (1921 edition)

LivingNature

I think they were about as high
As haycocks are. They went running by
Catching bits of shade in the sunny street:
“I’ve got one,” cried sister to brother.
“I’ve got two.” “Now I’ve got another.”
But scudding away on their little bare feet,
They left the shade in the sunny street.

Source Charlotte Mew, The Farmer's Bride (a new edition with eleven new poems), A new edition with eleven new poems (London: The Poetry Bookshop (35 Devonshire Street, Theobald's Road, W.C.), 1921). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 71305.

Codex Note

Eight lines in which two children run through a sunny street catching bits of shade. They leave the shade in the street.
