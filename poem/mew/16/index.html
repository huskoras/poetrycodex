Love

Song

by Charlotte Mew

From The Farmer's Bride (1921 edition)

LoveTime & Brevity

Love, Love to-day, my dear,
Love is not always here;
Wise maids know how soon grows sere
The greenest leaf of Spring;
But no man knoweth
Whither it goeth
When the wind bloweth
So frail a thing.

Love, Love, my dear, to-day,
If the ship’s in the bay,
If the bird has come your way
That sings on summer trees;
When his song faileth
And the ship saileth
No voice availeth
To call back these.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

'Love, Love to-day, my dear, / Love is not always here': two stanzas urging love now, before the wind or the ship takes it away.
