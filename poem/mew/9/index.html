Time & Brevity

À quoi bon dire

by Charlotte Mew

From The Farmer's Bride (1921 edition)

Time & BrevityLove

Seventeen years ago you said
Something that sounded like Good-bye;
And everybody thinks that you are dead,
But I.

So I, as I grow stiff and cold
To this and that say Good-bye too;
And everybody sees that I am old
But you.

And one fine morning in a sunny lane
Some boy and girl will meet and kiss and swear
That nobody can love their way again
While over there
You will have smiled, I shall have tossed your hair.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Seventeen years after a good-bye, everyone thinks the beloved dead 'but I', and the speaker ages while the other does not. A fifteen-line lyric whose title means 'what is the use of saying'.
