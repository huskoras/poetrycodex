Nature

The Valley Wind

by Lu Yün 4th century AD

Translated by Arthur Waley (1918) · From A Hundred and Seventy Chinese Poems (1918)

Nature

Living in retirement beyond the World,
Silently enjoying isolation,
I pull the rope of my door tighter
And stuff my window with roots and ferns.
My spirit is tuned to the Spring-season:
At the fall of the year there is autumn in my heart.
Thus imitating cosmic changes
My cottage becomes a Universe.
