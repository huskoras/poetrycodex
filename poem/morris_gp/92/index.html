Nature

Song of the Reapers

by George P. Morris 1802–1864

From Poems (Project Gutenberg eText #2558)

Nature

Joyous the carol that rings in the mountains,
While the cleared vales are refreshed by the fountains--
After the harvest the cheerful notes fall,
And all the glad reapers re-echo the call!
La ra la la, &c.

Oh, how the heart bounds at that simple refrain!
Dear haunts of my childhood, I'm with you again!
Green be your valleys, enriched by the rills,
And long may that carol be sung on your hills!
La ra la la, &c.

Source George P. Morris, Poems of George P. Morris; with a Memoir of the Author, Fourth edition (the Internet Archive scan; see note) (New York: Charles Scribner, 1860). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 2558. (details from catalogue records, not yet checked against the title page)
