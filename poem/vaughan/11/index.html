Religion

The Pursuite.

by Henry Vaughan 1621–1695

Religion

LOrd! what a busie, restles thing
Hast thou made man?
Each day, and houre he is on wing,
Rests not a span;
Then having lost the Sunne, and light
By clouds surpriz'd
He keepes a Commerce in the night
With aire disguis'd;
Hadst thou given to this active dust
A state untir'd,
The lost Sonne had not left the huske
Nor home desir'd;
That was thy secret, and it is
Thy mercy too,
For when all failes to bring to blisse,
Then, this must doe.
Ah! Lord! and what a Purchase will that be
To take us sick, that sound would not take thee?

Source Henry Vaughan, Silex Scintillans: or Sacred Poems and Private Ejaculations (London: Printed by T. W. for H. Blunden at the Castle in Cornhill, 1650). Digital text: EEBO-TCP A64747.
