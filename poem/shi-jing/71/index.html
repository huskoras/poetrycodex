Love

The K‘ew Chung Yéw Ma

by Shi Jing (The Book of Songs) poems c. 1000–600 BC; compilation traditionally ascribed to Confucius

Translated by James Legge (1876) · From The She King (1876), Part I, Book VI

Love

Where the hemp grows on the mound,
There some one keeps Tsze-tseay ;
There some one keeps Tsze-tseay :—
Why comes not he to me with a bound ?

On the mound where grows the wheat,
There some one keeps Tsze-kwoh ;
There some one keeps Tsze-kwoh :—
Why comes not he with me here to eat?

On the mound plum trees have place.
There some one keeps those youths ;
There some one keeps those youths :—
O for their kew-stones girdle to grace!

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

A woman longs for the presence of her lovers, who, she thinks, are detained from her by another woman.
