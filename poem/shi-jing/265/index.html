Religion

The T‘éen Tsoh

by Shi Jing (The Book of Songs) poems c. 1000–600 BC; compilation traditionally ascribed to Confucius

Translated by James Legge (1876) · From The She King (1876), Part IV, Book I

Religion

’Twas Heaven that made the lofty hill,
And there king Tae his work began,
Labouring the land all round to till.
Then in tranquillity king Wan
Pursued the work, till that mount K‘e,
Rugged and craggy though it frowned,
Was reached by the smooth roads we see.
Long be it by their offspring owned !

Source Shi Jing, The She King; or, The Book of Ancient Poetry, Translated in English Verse, with Essays and Notes, trans. James Legge (London: Trübner & Co, 1876). Digital text: Internet Archive shekingorbookofa0000unse.
