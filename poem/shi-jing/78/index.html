Living

The Tsun Ta Loo

by Shi Jing (The Book of Songs)

Translated by James Legge (1876) · From The She King (1876), Part I, Book VII

Living

Along the great highway,
I hold you by the cuff.
O spurn me not, I pray,
Nor break old friendship off.

Along the highway worn,
I hold your hand in mine.
Do not as vile me scorn;
Your love I can’t resign.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Old friendship should not be hastily broken off.
