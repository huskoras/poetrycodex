The Kéuen Yu

by Shi Jing (The Book of Songs) poems c. 1000–600 BC; compilation traditionally ascribed to Confucius

Translated by James Legge (1876) · From The She King (1876), Part I, Book XI

Living

He lodged us in a spacious house,
And plenteous was our fare.
But now at every frugal meal
There’s not a scrap to spare.
Alas! alas that this good man
Could not go on as he began!

Four dishes on the mat the grain
For every meal supplied.
The change is great, from every meal
We rise unsatisfied.
Alas! alas that this good man :
Could not go on as he began!

Source Shi Jing, The She King; or, The Book of Ancient Poetry, Translated in English Verse, with Essays and Notes, trans. James Legge (London: Trübner & Co, 1876). Digital text: Internet Archive shekingorbookofa0000unse.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Some parties, probably refugees, complain of the diminished respect and attention paid to them.
