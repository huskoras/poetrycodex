Relationships

Sent to Li Po as a Gift

by Tu Fu 712–770

Translated by Florence Ayscough and Amy Lowell (1921) · From Fir-Flower Tablets (1921)

Relationships

Autumn comes,
We meet each other.
You still whirl about as a thistledown in the wind.
Your Elixir of Immortality is not yet perfected
And, remembering Ko Hung, you are ashamed.
You drink a great deal,
You sing wild songs,
Your days pass in emptiness.
Your nature is a spreading fire,
It is swift and strenuous.
But what does all this bravery amount to?

Source Florence Ayscough and Amy Lowell, trans., Fir-Flower Tablets: Poems Translated from the Chinese (Boston and New York: Houghton Mifflin Company, 1921). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 48222.
