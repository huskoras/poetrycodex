Living

XXIII

by Dante Gabriel Rossetti

From The Blessed Damozel

Living

"Yea, verily; when he is come
We will do thus and thus:
Till this my vigil seem quite strange
And almost fabulous;
We two will live at once, one life;
And peace will be with us."
