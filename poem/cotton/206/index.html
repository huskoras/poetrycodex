Id. Lib. 4. Ep. 86 — In Ponticum

by Charles Cotton 1630–1687

Translated by Charles Cotton (1689) · From Poems on Several Occasions (1689)

Social Commentaries

We drink in Glass, thou Myrrh, Ponticus; why?
Lest Glass of two Wines make discovery.

Source Charles Cotton, Poems on Several Occasions (London: Printed for Tho. Bassett, Will. Hinsman and Tho. Fox, 1689). Digital text: EEBO-TCP A34643.
