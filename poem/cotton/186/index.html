Martial, Epig. Lib. I. Ep. XX

by Charles Cotton

Translated by Charles Cotton (1689) · From Poems on Several Occasions (1689)

Social Commentaries

As I remember, Aelia cought full sore;
She cought out twice two Teeth, she had but four.
Now she may safely cough for over: Why?
Her Mouth's not charg'd to let such Bullets fly.
