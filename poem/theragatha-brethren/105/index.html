Religion

Psalms of the Brethren CVI: Suhemanta

by The Brethren of the Theragatha

Translated by Mrs. C. A. F. Rhys Davids (1913) · From Psalms of the Early Buddhists II: Psalms of the Brethren (1913)

Religion

A hundred tokens show, a hundred marks
Betray wherein the hidden meaning lies.
Whoso hath eyes to see but one, a dullard is,
Who can discern the hundred, he is wise.
