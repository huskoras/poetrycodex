Religion

Psalms of the Brethren CXVII: Yasa

by The Brethren of the Theragatha

Translated by Mrs. C. A. F. Rhys Davids (1913) · From Psalms of the Early Buddhists II: Psalms of the Brethren (1913)

Religion

With perfumed skin and delicately clad
And head ablaze with gems, natheless my way
I found and made the Threefold Lore my own;
And now the Buddha-ordinance is done.
