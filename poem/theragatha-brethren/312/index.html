Theragāthā 48: Sañjaya

by The Brethren of the Theragatha traditionally 6th–3rd century BCE; composition uncertain

From Theragāthā (Pali, Mahāsaṅgīti text)

Religion

“Yato ahaṁ pabbajito,
Agārasmānagāriyaṁ;
Nābhijānāmi saṅkappaṁ,
Anariyaṁ dosasaṁhitan”ti.
