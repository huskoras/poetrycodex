Living

…: Arist.

by Thomas Randolph 1605–1635

Living

FRom witty men and mad
All Poetry conception had.
No sires but these will Poetry admit
Madnesse or wit.
This definition Poetry doth fit,
It is a witty madnesse, or mad wit.
Only these two Poetique heat admits,
A witty man, or one that's out of's wits.

Source Thomas Randolph, Poems with the Muses Looking-Glasse: and Amyntas (Oxford: Leonard Lichfield, printer to the University, for Francis Bowman, 1638). Digital text: EEBO-TCP A10411.
