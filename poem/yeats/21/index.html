Love

The Arrow

by W. B. Yeats

From In the Seven Woods (1903)

Love

I thought of your beauty and this arrow
Made out of a wild thought is in my marrow.
There's no man may look upon her, no man,
As when newly grown to be a woman,
Blossom pale, she pulled down the pale blossom
At the moth hour and hid it in her bosom.
This beauty's kinder yet for a reason
I could weep that the old is out of season.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Eight lines: the poet thinks of the beloved's beauty and an arrow of wild thought lodges in his marrow.
