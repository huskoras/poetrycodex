A Coat

by W. B. Yeats

Arts & Sciences

I made my song a coat
Covered with embroideries
Out of old mythologies
From heel to throat;
But the fools caught it,
Wore it in the world's eye
As though they'd wrought it.
Song, let them take it
For there's more enterprise
In walking naked.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

The poet had made his songs a coat 'covered with embroideries / Out of old mythologies'. Fools took it and wore it in the world's eyes, so now he will walk naked.
