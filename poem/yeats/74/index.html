The Three Monuments

by W. B. Yeats

From The Tower (1928)

History & PoliticsSocial Commentaries

They hold their public meetings where
Our most renowned patriots stand,
One among the birds of the air,
A stumpier on either hand;
And all the popular statesmen say
That purity built up the state
And after kept it from decay;
Admonish us to cling to that
And let all base ambition be,
For intellect would make us proud
And pride bring in impurity:
The three old rascals laugh aloud.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Statesmen hold public meetings beneath three monuments of renowned patriots and preach that purity built the state, while 'the three old rascals laugh aloud'.
