To Be Carved on a Stone at Ballylee

by W. B. Yeats

From Michael Robartes and the Dancer (1921)

History & PoliticsArts & Sciences

I, the poet William Yeats,
With old mill boards and sea-green slates,
And smithy work from the Gort forge,
Restored this tower for my wife George;
And may these characters remain
When all is ruin once again.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

An inscription for Thoor Ballylee, the tower Yeats restored in Galway: 'I, the poet William Yeats... restored this tower for my wife George; / And may these characters remain / When all is ruin once again.'
