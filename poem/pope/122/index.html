A Dialogue

by Alexander Pope 1688–1744

From Miscellanies

Social Commentaries

Pope.
Since my old friend is grown so great,
As to be Minister of State,
I'm told, but 'tis not true, I hope,
That Craggs will be ashamed of Pope.

Craggs.
Alas! if I am such a creature,
To grow the worse for growing greater;
Why, faith, in spite of all my brags,
'Tis Pope must be ashamed of Craggs.

Source Alexander Pope, The Poetical Works of Alexander Pope, with Memoir, Critical Dissertation, and Explanatory Notes, I-II, ed. George Gilfillan (Edinburgh: James Nichol, 1856). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 9413.
