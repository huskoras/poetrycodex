Death & Dying

On Mr Elijah Fenton, at Easthamstead, in Berks, 1730

by Alexander Pope 1688–1744

From Epitaphs

Death & Dying

This modest stone, what few vain marbles can,
May truly say, Here lies an honest man:
A poet, blest beyond the poet's fate,
Whom Heaven kept sacred from the proud and great:
Foe to loud praise, and friend to learnèd ease,
Content with science in the vale of peace.
Calmly he look'd on either life, and here
Saw nothing to regret, or there to fear;
From Nature's temperate feast rose satisfied,
Thank'd Heaven that he had lived, and that he died.

Source Alexander Pope, The Poetical Works of Alexander Pope, with Memoir, Critical Dissertation, and Explanatory Notes, Vol. I, ed. George Gilfillan (Edinburgh: James Nichol, 1856). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 9413.
