Death & Dying

Epitaph (Stop, Christian Passer-by)

by Samuel Taylor Coleridge 1772–1834

From Poems of Coleridge, selected by Arthur Symons (1905)

Death & Dying

Stop, Christian passer-by!--Stop, child of God,
And read with gentle breast. Beneath this sod
A poet lies, or that which once seem'd he.--
O, lift one thought in prayer for S. T. C.;
That he who many a year with toil of breath
Found death in life, may here find life in death!
Mercy for praise--to be forgiven for fame
He ask'd, and hoped, through Christ. Do thou the same!

Source Samuel Taylor Coleridge, ed. Arthur Symons, Poems of Coleridge, Selected and Arranged with an Introduction and Notes by Arthur Symons (London: Methuen & Co, 1905). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 8208.
