The Bridge Street Committee

by Samuel Taylor Coleridge 1772–1834

From Complete Poetical Works, ed. E. H. Coleridge (1912)

Social Commentaries

Jack Snipe
Eats Tripe:
It is therefore credible
That tripe is edible.
And therefore, perforce,
It follows, of course,
That the Devil will gripe
All who do not eat Tripe.

And as Nic is too slow
To fetch 'em below:
And Gifford, the attorney,
Won't quicken their journey;
The Bridge-Street Committee
That colleague without pity,
To imprison and hang
Carlile and his gang,
Is the pride of the City,
And 'tis Association
That, alone, saves the Nation
From Death and Damnation.

Source Samuel Taylor Coleridge, The Complete Poetical Works of Samuel Taylor Coleridge, including poems and versions of…, Vol. I: Poems (with appendix poems from Vol. II), ed. Ernest Hartley Coleridge (Oxford: At the Clarendon Press, 1912). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 29091.
