Social Commentaries

On the Most Veracious Anecdotist, and Small-Talk Man, Thomas Hill, Esq

by Samuel Taylor Coleridge 1772–1834

From Complete Poetical Works, ed. E. H. Coleridge (1912)

Social Commentaries

Tom Hill, who laughs at Cares and Woes,
As nauci--nili--pili--
What is he like, as I suppose?
Why, to be sure, a Rose--a Rose.
At least, no soul that Tom Hill knows
Could e'er recall a Li-ly.
