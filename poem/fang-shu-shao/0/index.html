Death & Dying

To His Coffin

by Fang Shu-shao

Translated by Herbert A. Giles (1898) · From Chinese Poetry in English Verse (1898)

Death & Dying

An eternal home awaits me, shall I hesitate to go?
Or struggle for a few more hours of fleeting life below?
A home, wherein the clash of arms
I can never hear again!
And shall I strive to linger in this thorny world of pain?
The breeze will soon blow cool o'er me, and the bright moon shine o'erhead,
When blended with the gems of earth
I lie in my last bed.
My pen and ink shall go with me inside my funeral hearse,
So that if I've leisure "over there"
I may soothe my soul with verse.
