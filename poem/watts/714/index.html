Religion

Hymn III.39

by Isaac Watts 1674–1748

From Hymns and Spiritual Songs, Book III (1709)

Religion

To him that chose us first,
Before the world began;
To him that bore the curse
To save rebellious man;
To him that formed
Our hearts anew
Is endless praise
And glory due.

The Father's love shall run
Through our immortal songs;
We bring to God the Son
Hosannahs on our tongues:
Our lips address
The Spirit's name
With equal praise,
And zeal the same.

Let every saint above,
And angel round the throne,
For ever bless and love
The sacred Three in One;
Thus heav'n shall raise
His honors high,
When earth and time
Grow old and die.

Source Isaac Watts, The Psalms and Hymns of Isaac Watts, with All the Additional Hymns and Complete Indexes…, Hymns and Spiritual Songs, Book III. Digital text: CCEL watts/psalmshymns. (details from catalogue records, not yet checked against the title page)
