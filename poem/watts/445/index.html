Religion

Hymn I.90: Youth and judgment

by Isaac Watts 1674–1748

From Hymns and Spiritual Songs, Book I (1707)

Religion

Lo! the young tribes of Adam rise,
And through all nature rove
Fulfil the wishes of their eyes,
And taste the joys they love.

They give a loose to wild desires;
But let the sinners know
The strict account that God requires
Of all the works they do.

The Judge prepares his throne on high,
The frighted earth and seas
Avoid the fury of his eye,
And flee before his face.

How shall I bear that dreadful day,
And stand the fiery test?
I give all mortal joys away,
To be for ever blessed.

Source Isaac Watts, Hymns and Spiritual Songs, in Three Books (Book I: Collected from the Holy Scriptures) (London, 1707). Digital text: Christian Classics Ethereal Library psalmshymns. (details from catalogue records, not yet checked against the title page)
