Religion

Hymn I.12: Free grace in revealing Christ

by Isaac Watts 1674–1748

From Hymns and Spiritual Songs, Book I (1707)

Religion

Jesus, the man of constant grief,
A mourner all his days;
His spirit once rejoiced aloud,
And tuned his joy to praise:

"Father, I thank thy wondrous love,
That hath revealed thy Son
To men unlearned, and to babes
Has made thy gospel known.

"The mysteries of redeeming grace
Are hidden from the wise,
While pride and carnal reasonings join
To swell and blind their eyes."

Thus doth the Lord of heav'n and earth
His great decrees fulfil,
And orders all his works of grace
By his own sovereign will.

Source Isaac Watts, Hymns and Spiritual Songs, in Three Books (Book I: Collected from the Holy Scriptures) (London, 1707). Digital text: Christian Classics Ethereal Library psalmshymns. (details from catalogue records, not yet checked against the title page)
