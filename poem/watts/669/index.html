Hymn II.164: The end of the world

by Isaac Watts 1674–1748

From Hymns and Spiritual Songs, Book II (1707)

Religion

Why should this earth delight us so?
Why should we fix our eyes
On these low grounds where sorrows grow,
And every pleasure dies ?

While time his sharpest teeth prepares
Our comforts to devour,
There is a land above the stars,
And joys above his power.

Nature shall be dissolved and die,
The sun must end his race,
The earth and sea for ever fly
Before my Savior's face.

When will that glorious morning rise?
When the last trumpet sound,
And call the nations to the skies,
From underneath the ground?

Source Isaac Watts, Hymns and Spiritual Songs, Book II (London, 1707). Digital text: Christian Classics Ethereal Library psalmshymns. (details from catalogue records, not yet checked against the title page)
