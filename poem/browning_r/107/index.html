Living

You like us for a glance, you know--

by Robert Browning

From Dramatic Lyrics (1842)

Living

For a word's sake
Or a sword's sake,
All's the same, whate'er the chance, you know.

And in turn we make you ours, we say--
You and youth too,
Eyes and mouth too,
All the face composed of flowers, we say.
