A CHILD'S STORY

by Robert Browning

From Dramatic Romances (1845)

Living

(_Written for, and inscribed to, W. M. the Younger_)

Macready's eldest son when a child was confined to the house by
illness, and Browning wrote this _jeu d'esprit_ to amuse the child and
give him a subject for illustrative drawings.

I

Hamelin Town's in Brunswick,
By famous Hanover city;
The river Weser, deep and wide,
Washes its wall on the southern side;
A pleasanter spot you never spied;
But, when begins my ditty,
Almost five hundred years ago,
To see the townsfolk suffer so
From vermin, was a pity.
