PERSONS

by Robert Browning

Living

COLOMBE OF RAVESTEIN, _Duchess of Juliers and Cleves_.
SABYNE, ADOLF, her Attendants.
GUIBERT, GAUCELME, MAUFROY, CLUGNET, Courtiers.
VALENCE, _Advocate of Cleves_.
PRINCE BERTHOLD, _Claimant of the Duchy_.
MELCHIOR, his Confidant.

PLACE, _The Palace at Juliers_.

TIME, 16--.
