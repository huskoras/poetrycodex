Living

Teach me to flirt a fan

by Robert Browning

From Dramatic Lyrics (1842)

Living

As the Spanish ladies can,
Or I tint your lip
With a burnt stick's tip
And you turn into such a man!
Just the two spots that span
Half the bill of the young male swan.
