Living

Past we glide, and past, and past! (2)

by Robert Browning

From Dramatic Romances (1845)

Living

Why's the Pucci Palace flaring
Like a beacon to the blast?
Guests by hundreds, not one caring
If the dear host's neck were wried:
Past we glide!

_She sings._
