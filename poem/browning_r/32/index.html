Living

See the creature stalking

by Robert Browning

From Dramatic Lyrics (1842)

Living

While we speak!
Hush and hide the talking,
Cheek on cheek!

What so false as truth is,
False to thee?
Where the serpent's tooth is
Shun the tree--
