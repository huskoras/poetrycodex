XIX (2)

by Robert Browning

From Dramatic Romances (1845)

Living

Oh, their Rafael of the dear Madonnas,
Oh, their Dante of the dread Inferno,
Wrote one song--and in my brain I sing it,
Drew one angel--borne, see, on my bosom!

R. B.
