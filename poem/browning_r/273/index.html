A MIDDLE-AGE INTERLUDE

by Robert Browning

From Dramatic Romances (1845)

Living

_Rosa Mundi; seu, fulcite me Floribus. A Conceit of Master
Gysbrecht, Canon-Regular of Saint Jodocus-by-the-Bar, Ypres City._
_Cantuque_, Virgilius. _And hath often been sung at Hock-tide and
Festivals._ _Gavisus eram_, Jessides.

(It would seem to be a glimpse from the burning of Jacques du
Bourg-Molay, at Paris, A. D. 1314; as distorted by the refraction from
Flemish brain to brain, during the course of a couple of centuries. R.
B.)
