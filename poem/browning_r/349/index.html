Living

XXI

by Robert Browning

From Dramatic Romances (1845)

Living

"How? Is mine,
The world?" (I cried, while my soul broke
Out in a transport.) "Hast thou spoke
Plainly in that? Earth's exquisite
Treasures of wonder and delight
For me?"
