by Robert Browning

From Dramatic Romances (1845)

Living

TO E. B. B.

_London, September, 1855_

Originally appended to the collection of Poems called _Men and Women_,
the greater portion of which has now been, more correctly, distributed
under the other titles of this edition. R. B.

I

There they are, my fifty men and women
Naming me the fifty poems finished!
Take them, Love, the book and me together;
Where the heart lies, let the brain lie also.
