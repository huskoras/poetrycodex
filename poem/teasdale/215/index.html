Time & Brevity

The Coin

by Sara Teasdale

From Flame and Shadow (1920)

Time & Brevity

Into my heart's treasury
I slipped a coin
That time cannot take
Nor a thief purloin,--
Oh better than the minting
Of a gold-crowned king
Is the safe-kept memory
Of a lovely thing.
