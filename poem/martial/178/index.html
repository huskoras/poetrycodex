Book VIII, Epigram 1: Ad Librum suum

by Martial

Translated by R. Fletcher (1656) · From Ex Otio Negotium (1656)

Social Commentaries

Thou that art entring the tryumphant Court,
Learn with a blushing grace more chast to sport.
Strip'd
Wenus hence: this is not thy book,
Great Caesar's goddess come, and on me look.
