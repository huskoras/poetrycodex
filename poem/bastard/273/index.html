Epigram 33 (Martiall in Rome full thirtie yeares had spent,)

by Thomas Bastard

From Chrestoleros (1598), Book 7

Social Commentaries

Martiall in Rome full thirtie yeares had spent,
Then went he home, was not that banishment?
