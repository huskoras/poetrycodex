Epigram 8 (Phylo is richly rayde, and beareth hye)

by Thomas Bastard

From Chrestoleros (1598), Book 3

Social Commentaries

Phylo is richly rayde, and beareth hye
His great reuenues dated in his coate,
Coyne, iewels, plate and land: loa heere they lie.
This is their last which lately so did floate.
First in his bely shipt they suffred wracke.
Now they are landed all vpon his backe.
