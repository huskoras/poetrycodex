Nature

LXXI

by Elizabeth Barrett Browning 1806–1861

From The Poetical Works of Elizabeth Barrett Browning, Volume 2

Nature

Oh, she fluttered like a tame bird, in among its forest-brothers
Far too strong for it; then drooping, bowed her face upon her hands;
And I spake out wildly, fiercely, brutal truths of her and others:
I, she planted in the desert, swathed her, windlike, with my sands.

Source Elizabeth Barrett Browning, The Poetical Works of Elizabeth Barrett Browning, in Six Volumes, 2 (London: Smith, Elder, & Co., 15 Waterloo Place, 1890). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 33363.
