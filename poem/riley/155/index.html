The Tree-Toad

by James Whitcomb Riley 1849–1916

From Pipes O' Pan at Zekesbury (1895)

Nature

"'Scurious-like," said the tree-toad,
"I've twittered far rain all day;
And I got up soon,
And I hollered till noon--
But the sun, hit blazed away,
Till I jest clumb down in a crawfish-hole,
Weary at heart, and sick at soul!

Source James Whitcomb Riley, Pipes O' Pan at Zekesbury (Indianapolis: Bowen-Merrill Co, 1895). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 13908.
