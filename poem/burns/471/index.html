Living

On A Suicide

by Robert Burns

From Poems and Songs, 1794

Living

Earth’d up, here lies an imp o’ hell,
Planted by Satan’s dibble;
Poor silly wretch, he’s damned himsel’,
To save the Lord the trouble.
