Anna, Thy Charms

by Robert Burns 1759–1796

From Poems and Songs, 1788

Living

Anna, thy charms my bosom fire,
And waste my soul with care;
But ah! how bootless to admire,
When fated to despair!

Yet in thy presence, lovely Fair,
To hope may be forgiven;
For sure ’twere impious to despair
So much in sight of heaven.

Source Robert Burns, The Poems and Songs of Robert Burns, with Introduction, Notes, and Glossary, Vol. 6, ed. Charles W. Eliot (The Harvard Classics, ed. Charles W. Eliot) (New York: P. F. Collier & Son Company, 1909). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 1279.
