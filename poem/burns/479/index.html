Love

Inconstancy In Love

by Robert Burns 1759–1796

From Poems and Songs, 1794

Love

Let not Woman e’er complain
Of inconstancy in love;
Let not Woman e’er complain
Fickle Man is apt to rove:
Look abroad thro’ Nature’s range,
Nature’s mighty Law is change,
Ladies, would it not seem strange
Man should then a monster prove!

Mark the winds, and mark the skies,
Ocean’s ebb, and ocean’s flow,
Sun and moon but set to rise,
Round and round the seasons go.
Why then ask of silly Man
To oppose great Nature’s plan?
We’ll be constant while we can—
You can be no more, you know.

Source Robert Burns, The Poems and Songs of Robert Burns, with Introduction, Notes and Glossary (edited by Charles W, Vol. 6 (The Harvard Classics) (New York: P. F. Collier and Son, 1909). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 1279.
