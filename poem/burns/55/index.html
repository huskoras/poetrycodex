Death & Dying

Epitaph On A Henpecked Country Squire

by Robert Burns

From Poems and Songs, 1784

Death & Dying

As father Adam first was fool’d,
(A case that’s still too common,)
Here lies man a woman ruled,
The devil ruled the woman.
