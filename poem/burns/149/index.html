Love

Rattlin’, Roarin’ Willie

by Robert Burns

From Poems and Songs, 1787

Love

As I cam by Crochallan,
I cannilie keekit ben;
Rattlin’, roarin’ Willie
Was sittin at yon boord-en’;
Sittin at yon boord-en,
And amang gude companie;
Rattlin’, roarin’ Willie,
You’re welcome hame to me!
