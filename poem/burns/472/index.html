Living

On A Swearing Coxcomb

by Robert Burns

From Poems and Songs, 1794

Living

Here cursing, swearing Burton lies,
A buck, a beau, or “Dem my eyes!”
Who in his life did little good,
And his last words were “Dem my blood!”
