Relationships

IX

by Francis Thompson

From Poems

Relationships

Baby smiled, mother wailed,
Earthward while the sweetling sailed;
Mother smiled, baby wailed,
When to earth came Viola.

And her elders shall say:—

So soon have we taught you a
Way to weep, poor Viola!
