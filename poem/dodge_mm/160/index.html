Lemons for Molly

by Mary Mapes Dodge

From Rhymes and Jingles (1875)

Living

Lemons for Molly;
Molly is sour.
Roses for Polly;
Polly's a flower.
Ginger for Willie;
Willie is quick.
Powders for Tillie;
Tillie is sick.
