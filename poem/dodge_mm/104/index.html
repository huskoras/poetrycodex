Have You Apples, Good Grocer?

by Mary Mapes Dodge

From Rhymes and Jingles (1875)

Living

"Have you apples, good grocer?"
"O yes, ma'am, how many?"
"How do you sell them, Sir?"
"Two for a penny."

"I'll have two, Mr. Grocer,
They're good for my baby;
Send 'em home in an hour, Sir."
"That I will, lady."
