Find Me a Stem of the Tiger Lily

by Mary Mapes Dodge

From Rhymes and Jingles (1875)

Living

Sunlight or starlight,
Tilly, my nilly,
Find me a stem
Of the tiger-lily;
I'll fill it full
From the fountain there
And spirt the water
Over your hair!
