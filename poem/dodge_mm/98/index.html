Rut-a-Tut-Tuts

by Mary Mapes Dodge 1831–1905

From Rhymes and Jingles (1875)

Living

Rut-a-tut-tuts!
Who can crack nuts?
Squirrels, can you?
"That we can, true--
Rut-a-tut-tuts,
We can crack nuts!"

Chicketty-chack,
Cracketty-crack.
"Pooh!" said the hammer,
"Silence your clamor,
Rut-a-tut-tuts--
Who can't crack nuts?"
