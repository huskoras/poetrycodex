Religion

"Unto Me?"

by Emily Dickinson

From Further Poems of Emily Dickinson (1929)

Religion

"Unto Me?"
"I do not know you—
Where may be your house?"

"I am Jesus—late of
Judea,
Now of Paradise."

"Wagons have you, to
Convey me?
This is far from thence"—

"Arms of mine sufficient
Phaeton,
Trust Omnipotence."

"I am spotted."
"I am Pardon."
"I am small."
"The least
Is esteemed in Heaven
The chiefest.
Occupy my house."
