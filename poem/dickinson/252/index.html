Nature

I went to heaven, --

by Emily Dickinson 1830–1886

Nature

I went to heaven, --
'T was a small town,
Lit with a ruby,
Lathed with down.
Stiller than the fields
At the full dew,
Beautiful as pictures
No man drew.
People like the moth,
Of mechlin, frames,
Duties of gossamer,
And eider names.
Almost contented
I could be
'Mong such unique
Society.

Source Emily Dickinson, Poems (First, Second and Third Series), ed. Mabel Loomis Todd and T. W. Higginson (Three series in one Gutenberg e-text) (Boston: Roberts Brothers, 1890-1896). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 12242.
