The Mind

I cannot be ashamed

by Emily Dickinson

From Further Poems of Emily Dickinson (1929)

The Mind

I cannot be ashamed
Because I cannot see
The love you offer.
Magnitude
Reverses modesty.

And I cannot be proud
Because a height so high
Involves Alpine
Requirements,
And services of snow.
