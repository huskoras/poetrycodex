The Mind

No romance sold unto

by Emily Dickinson 1830–1886

From The Single Hound (1914)

The Mind

No romance sold unto,
Could so enthrall a man
As the perusal of
His individual one.
'Tis fiction's, to dilute
To plausibility
Our novel, when 'tis small enough
To credit,—'tis n't true!

Source Emily Dickinson, The Single Hound: Poems of a Lifetime, with an introduction by her niece Martha Dickinson Bianchi (Boston: Little, Brown, and Company, 1914). Digital text: Wikisource The Single Hound. (details from catalogue records, not yet checked against the title page)
