Death & Dying

From the "Divan"

by Moses ibn Ezra c. 1055–after 1138

Translated by Emma Lazarus (Poems, 1889) · From The Poems of Emma Lazarus, vol. II (1889)

Death & Dying

My thoughts impelled me to the resting-place
Where sleep my parents, many a friend and brother.
I asked them (no one heard and none replied):
"Do ye forsake me, too, oh father, mother?"
Then from the grave, without a tongue, these cried,
And showed my own place waiting by their side.

Source Emma Lazarus, trans., The Poems of Emma Lazarus, in Two Volumes, Vol. II: Jewish Poems; Translations (Boston and New York: Houghton, Mifflin and Company, 1889). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 3473.
