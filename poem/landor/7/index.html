Love

Tell Me Not Things Past All Belief

by Walter Savage Landor

From Imaginary Conversations and Poems: A Selection

Love

Tell me not things past all belief;
One truth in you I prove;
The flame of anger, bright and brief,
Sharpens the barb of Love.
