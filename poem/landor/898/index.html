Upon His Death-Bed Lay a Pagan Priest

by Walter Savage Landor 1775–1864

From Works and Life, ed. Forster, vol. VIII (1876)

Death & Dying

Upon his death-bed lay a pagan priest;
A pious brother when the worst had ceast
Consoled him thus.
" Think now what pleasure yields
The nearer prospect of Elysian fields."
" Ah! " said he, " all about those fields we know
But mushrooms, are good mushrooms there below? "
