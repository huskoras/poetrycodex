Religion

Rune of the 'Muthairn'

by Anonymous (Scottish Gaelic)

Translated by Alexander Carmichael (1900) · From Carmina Gadelica, vol. I (1900): Invocations

Religion

Thou King of the moon,
Thou King of the sun,
Thou King of the planets,
Thou King of the stars,
Thou King of the globe,
Thou King of the sky,
Oh! lovely Thy countenance,
Thou beauteous Beam.

Two loops of silk
Down by thy limbs,
Smooth-skinned;
Yellow jewels
And a handful
Out of every stock of them.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Gaelic: Duan Na Muthairn. Carmina Gadelica I, no. 9, Invocations.
