Madam, If You Please

by Christopher Smart 1722–1771

LivingLove

Madam if you please
To hear such things as these.
Madam, I have a rival sad,
And if you don’t take my part it will made me mad.
He says he well send his son;
But if he does I will get me a gun.
Madam if you please to pity,
O poor Kitty, O poor Kitty!

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

A comic eight-line rhyme that is reported to be Smart's first verse, written in about 1726 when he was four years old.
