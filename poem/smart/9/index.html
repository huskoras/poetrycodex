Nature

For Saturday

by Christopher Smart 1722–1771

From Hymns for the Amusement of Children (1771)

NatureReligion

Now's the time for mirth and play,
Saturday's an holyday;
Praise to heav'n unceasing yield,
I've found a lark's nest in the field.

A lark's nest, then your play-mate begs
You'd spare herself and speckled eggs;
Soon she shall ascend and sing
Your praises to th'eternal King.

Source Christopher Smart, Hymns for the Amusement of Children, Philadelphia edition, 'to which are added Watts's Divine Songs for Children' (Philadelphia: William Spotswood, 1791). Digital text: Wikisource Hymns for the Amusement of Children (1791).

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

A hymn in two stanzas in which a child finds a lark's nest and a playmate begs it be spared.
