Doxology (7.7.7.7)

by James Montgomery

From Sacred Poems and Hymns (1854)

Religion

Glory to the Father be,
Equal glory to the Son;
And the Holy Spirit,--Three,
In eternal Godhead One.
As it was ere time begun,
As it now is, and shall be,
While unending ages run
Onward to eternity.
