See, see there The Tulips!

by Hafiz

Translated by Edna Worthley Underwood (1917) · From Songs of Hafiz (1917)

Love

See, see there The Tulips!
Merry drinkers, they,
Holding straight their cups up,
Colored oh! so gay,
That when heavenly nectar
From the clouds rains down
They may gayly banquet
All of Tulip Town!
