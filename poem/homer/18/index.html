Death & Dying

Book VII, 153: On Midas

by Homer fl. c. 8th century BC

Translated by W. R. Paton (1916–18) · From The Greek Anthology (Loeb, vol. II, 1917)

Death & Dying

I am a maiden of brass, and rest on Midas’ tomb. As long as water flows, and tall trees put forth their leaves, abiding here upon the tearful tomb, I tell the passers-by that Midas is buried here. Here ends the collection of fictitious epitaphs on celebrities, but a few more nill be found scattered im other parts of the book.

This translation is printed as prose in its source edition.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note
