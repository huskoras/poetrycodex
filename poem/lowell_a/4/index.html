Time & Brevity

Petals

by Amy Lowell 1874–1925

From A Dome of Many-Coloured Glass (1912): Lyrical Poems

Time & Brevity

Life is a stream
On which we strew
Petal by petal the flower of our heart;
The end lost in dream,
They float past our view,
We only watch their glad, early start.

Freighted with hope,
Crimsoned with joy,
We scatter the leaves of our opening rose;
Their widening scope,
Their distant employ,
We never shall know. And the stream as it flows
Sweeps them away,
Each one is gone
Ever beyond into infinite ways.
We alone stay
While years hurry on,
The flower fared forth, though its fragrance still stays.

Source Amy Lowell, A Dome of Many-Coloured Glass (Boston: Houghton Mifflin Company, 1912). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 261. (details from catalogue records, not yet checked against the title page)
