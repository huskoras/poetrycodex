Religion

Psalms of the Sisters X: Upasamā

by The Sisters of the Therigatha traditionally 6th–5th century BCE; composition uncertain

Translated by Mrs. C. A. F. Rhys Davids (1909) · From Psalms of the Early Buddhists I: Psalms of the Sisters (1909)

Religion

Upasamā! cross thou serene and calm
The raging difficult Flood where death doth reign.
Bear to this end thy last incarnate frame,
For thou hast vanquished Māra and his host.

Source Mrs. C. A. F. Rhys Davids, trans., Psalms of the Early Buddhists, I: Psalms of the Sisters (translated from the Therigatha) (London: Published for the Pali Text Society by Henry Frowde (Oxford University Press), 1909). Digital text: sacred-texts bud/pos.
