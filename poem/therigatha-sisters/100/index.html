Therīgāthā 2.10: Sāmā (Psalms of the Sisters XXVIII: Sāmā)

by The Sisters of the Therigatha traditionally 6th–5th century BCE; composition uncertain

From Therīgāthā (Pali, Mahāsaṅgīti text)

Religion

“Catukkhattuṁ pañcakkhattuṁ,
vihārā upanikkhamiṁ;
Aladdhā cetaso santiṁ,
citte avasavattinī;
Tassā me aṭṭhamī ratti,
yato taṇhā samūhatā.

Bahūhi dukkhadhammehi,
appamādaratāya me;
Taṇhakkhayo anuppatto,
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanan”ti.
