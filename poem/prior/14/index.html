The Lady Who Offers Her Looking-Glass to Venus

by Matthew Prior 1664–1721

From Poems on Several Occasions

Time & BrevityLove

VENUS, take my votive glass ;
Since I am not what I was,
What from this day I shall be,
Venus, let me never see.

Source Matthew Prior, The Poetical Works of Matthew Prior, with Memoir by the Rev. John Mitford, Volume I, I, Aldine Edition of the British Poets (The Aldine Edition of the British Poets) (London: Bell and Daldy, 1866). Digital text: Internet Archive poeticalworks01prio. (details from catalogue records, not yet checked against the title page)

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

A four-line epigram in which a woman who is no longer beautiful offers her mirror to Venus. It is a version of a Greek epigram attributed to Plato.
