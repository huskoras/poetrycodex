Love

Lines Written in an Ovid

by Matthew Prior

From Poems on Several Occasions

LoveArts & Sciences

OVID is the surest guide,
You can name, to show the way
To any woman, maid, or bride,
Who resolves to go astray.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

A four-line epigram that calls Ovid the surest guide to any woman who means to go astray.
