To Dabbler

by Thomas Bancroft fl. 1633–1658

From Two Bookes of Epigrammes, and Epitaphs (1639), Book I

Social Commentaries

Thy Muses looser Robes with many a tricke
Are jagg'd, pink't, stucke with Flowers of Rhetorick,
That smell all Poesie; yet please they none.
How happens that? they're out of fashion.

Source Thomas Bancroft, Two Bookes of Epigrammes, and Epitaphs. Dedicated to Two Top-Branches of Gentry (London: Printed by I. Okes, for Matthew Walbancke, 1639). Digital text: EEBO-TCP A03415.
