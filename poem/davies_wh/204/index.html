The Muse

by W. H. Davies 1871–1940

From Collected Poems (1916)

Arts & Sciences

I have no ale,
No wine I want ;
No ornaments,
My meat is scant.

No maid is near,
I have no wife ;
But here's my pipe
And, on my life :

With it to smoke.
And woo the Muse,
To be a king
I would not choose.

But I crave all,
When she does fail —
Wife, ornaments.
Meat, wine and ale.
