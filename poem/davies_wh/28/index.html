Time & Brevity

My Youth

by W. H. Davies

From Foliage (1913)

Time & BrevityArts & Sciences

My youth was my old age,
Weary and long;
It had too many cares
To think of song;
My moulting days all came
When I was young.

Now, in life's prime, my soul
Comes out in flower;
Late, as with Robin, comes
My singing power;
I was not born to joy
Till this late hour.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

'My youth was my old age, weary and long.' Now, in life's prime, his singing comes late, 'as with Robin'. He was not born to joy till this late hour.
