The Life Divine

by W. H. Davies 1871–1940

From The Bird of Paradise and Other Poems (1914)

Arts & Sciences

Give me the poet's life divine,
Forever fresh and young;
The only hours that vex his soul
Are hours that give no song;
If he but can,
A homeless man,
Turn suffering into songs divine —
That poet's life is still divine,
His life is still divine.

If but the Muse will help his soul
To sing a grief that's wild,
No faithless spouse can pull him down,
Nor disobedient child;
Let her but prove
His faithful love,
To sing his cares in songs divine —
That poet's life is still divine,
His life is still divine.
