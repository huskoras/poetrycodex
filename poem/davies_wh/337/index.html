Love

Come, Let Me Close

by W. H. Davies 1871–1940

From The Bird of Paradise and Other Poems (1914)

Love

Come, let me close thine eyes with kisses —
And those two lips that day and night
Are opened to a cherry's size,
And cry for Love to kiss them tight.

Let me enjoy thy bosom now,
Sweet Lady, let my head rock there;
When it is wedged between thy breasts,
Throw over it thy silken hair.

Let me lie close before He comes.
To clasp thy bosom like a leech:
I mean that babe, who'll lie between,
Over whose body I must reach;

That tyrant babe, whom thou wilt love
Above all earthly things the best:
Though laughing he will trample, kick
And knuckle into each soft breast.
