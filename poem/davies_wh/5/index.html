Nature

A May Morning

by W. H. Davies

From Foliage (1913)

NatureLiving

The sky is clear,
The sun is bright;
The cows are red,
The sheep are white;
Trees in the meadows
Make happy shadows.

Birds in the hedge
Are perched and sing;
Swallows and larks
Are on the wing:
Two merry cuckoos
Are making echoes.

Bird and the beast
Have the dew yet;
My road shines dry,
Theirs bright and wet:
Death gives no warning,
On this May morning.

I see no Christ
Nailed on a tree,
Dying for sin;
No sin I see:
No thoughts for sadness,
All thoughts for gladness.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

A clear May morning with red cows, white sheep and two cuckoos making echoes. The last stanza sees 'no Christ nailed on a tree, dying for sin', 'No thoughts for sadness, all thoughts for gladness'.
