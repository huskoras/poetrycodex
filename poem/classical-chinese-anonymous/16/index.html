Opportunity

by Anonymous (Classical Chinese)

Translated by Herbert A. Giles (1898) · From Chinese Poetry in English Verse (1898)

Living

The cup's in the hand, seize the hour ere 'tis fled;
How seldom in life is the moon overhead!
