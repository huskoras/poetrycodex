Love

Ai Ai Thinks of the Man She Loves

by Anonymous (Classical Chinese)

Translated by Florence Ayscough and Amy Lowell (1921) · From Fir-Flower Tablets (1921)

Love

How often must I pass the moonlight nights alone?
I gaze far--far--for the Seven Scents Chariot.
My girdle drops because my waist is shrunken.
The golden hairpins of my disordered head-dress are all askew.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

From "Songs of the Courtesans", written during the Liang Dynasty (A.D. 502-557); the book gives no author beyond the singer, Ai Ai.
