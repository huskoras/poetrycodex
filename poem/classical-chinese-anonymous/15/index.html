Living

Integer Vitæ

by Anonymous (Classical Chinese) various

Translated by Herbert A. Giles (1898) · From Chinese Poetry in English Verse (1898)

Living

Riches and rank — a morning dream in spring;
Fame — but an unsubstantial cloud above;
Thy very body is not thine for ay;
Hate is the end of love.

Fix not a golden collar on thy neck;
Be not with chain of jade in service bound;
Pure heart and few desires: earth's dust shake off —
And happiness is found.

Source Herbert A. Giles, trans., Chinese Poetry in English Verse (London / Shanghai: Bernard Quaritch; Kelly & Walsh, Ltd, 1898). Digital text: Internet Archive cu31924008867222.
