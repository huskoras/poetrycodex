To Our Mocking-Bird (2)

by Sidney Lanier 1842–1881

Death & Dying

The history of this bird's life is given at length under the title of "Bob",
in `The Independent' of August 3, 1882, and will show that he deserved
to be immortal -- as we hope he is.

Source Sidney Lanier, Poems of Sidney Lanier, Edited by His Wife, with a Memorial by William Hayes Ward, Edited by Mary Day Lanier (the author's wife) (New York: Charles Scribner's Sons, 1884).
