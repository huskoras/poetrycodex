The Mind

And round the Well how oft my Soul doth grope

by Abu al-Ala al-Ma'arri 973–1057

Translated by Ameen Rihani (1918) · From The Luzumiyat of Abu'l-Ala (1918)

The Mind

And round the Well how oft my Soul doth grope
Athirst; but lo! my Bucket hath no Rope:
I cry for water, and the deep, dark Well
Echoes my wailing cry, but not my hope.

Source Abu'l-Ala al-Ma'arri, The Luzumiyat of Abu'l-Ala, selected from his Luzum ma la Yalzam and Suct uz-Zand and…, trans. Ameen Rihani, Second edition (first published 1918) (New York: James T. White & Co, 1920). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 50457.
