Living

There was a Young Lady of Norway

by Edward Lear

From A Book of Nonsense

Living

There was a Young Lady of Norway,
Who casually sat in a doorway;
When the door squeezed her flat, she exclaimed, "What of that?"
This courageous Young Lady of Norway.
