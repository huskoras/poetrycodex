Death & Dying

SKKS 00788: たまゆらの つゆも涙も (Tamayura no tsuyu mo namida mo)

by Fujiwara no Teika d. 1242

From 新古今和歌集 (Shin Kokinshū)

Death & Dying

たまゆらのつゆも涙もとゝまらすなき人こふるやとの秋風

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Composed after his mother's death, on an autumn day of wind and rain, when he visited the place where she had lived.
