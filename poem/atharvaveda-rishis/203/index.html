Atharva Veda 6.40: A prayer for peace and security

by The Rishis of the Atharva Veda

Translated by Ralph T. H. Griffith (1895–96) · From The Hymns of the Atharva-Veda (1895–96)

Religion

Here may we dwell, O Heaven and Earth, in safety. May Savitar and Soma send us safety.
Our safety be the wide air: ours be safety through the oblation of the Seven Rishis.

May the Four Quarters give this hamlet power: Savitar favour us and make us happy!
May Indra make us free from foes and danger: may wrath of Kings be turned to other places.

Make thou us free from enemies both from below and from above.
O Indra, give us perfect peace, peace from behind and from be-fore.
