Religion

Easter.

by George Herbert

Religion

RIse heart; thy Lord is risen. Sing his praise
Without delayes,
…ho takes thee by the hand, that thou likewise
With him mayst rise:
…hat, as his death calcined thee to dust,
…is life may make thee gold, and much more just.

…wake, my lute, and struggle for thy part
With all thy art.
The crosse taught all wood to resound his name,
Who bore the same.
…is streched sinews taught all strings, what key
… best to celebrate this most high day.

Consort both heart and lute, and twist a song
Pleasant and long:
Or since all musick is but three parts vied
And multiplied;
O let thy blessed Spirit bear a part,
And make up our defects with his sweet art.

… got me flowers to straw thy way;
… got me boughs off many a tree:
But thou wast up by break of day,
And brought'st thy sweets along with thee.

The Sunne arising in the East,
Though he give light, & th' East perfume;
If they should offer to contest
With thy arising, they presume.

Can there be any day but this,
Though many sunnes to shine endeavour?
We count three hundred, but we misse:
There is but one, and that one ever.
