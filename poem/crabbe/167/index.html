Time & Brevity

Lines (Aldborough, 1823)

by George Crabbe 1754–1832

From Miscellaneous Verses

Time & Brevity

Thus once again, my native place, I come
Thee to salute--my earliest, latest home.
Much are we alter'd both, but I behold
In thee a youth renew'd--whilst I am old.
The works of man from dying we may save;
But man himself moves onward to the grave.

Source George Crabbe, Poems by George Crabbe, Volume III, III (Cambridge English Classics) (Cambridge: At the University Press, 1907). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 59204.
