On Mr Fanning the Engastrometh

by Robert Heath fl. 1636–1659

From Clarastella (1650), Epigrams, Lib. 2

Social Commentaries

To speak within, and to ones selfe, and yet
Bee heard, is much, yet Fanning doth it:
So tall and stout a man, 'tis strange to see't
So like a coward should his words down eat:
The belly hath no ears they say; yet his
Hath ears to hear, and tongue to talk, I wis.

Source Robert Heath, Clarastella; together with poems occasional, elegies, epigrams, satyrs (London: Printed for Humphrey Moseley, and are to be sold at his shop at the sign of the Prince's Arms in St Paul's Churchyard, 1650). Digital text: EEBO-TCP A86166.
