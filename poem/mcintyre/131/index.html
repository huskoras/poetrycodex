Arts & Sciences

Millar

by James McIntyre

From Poems (1889)

Arts & Sciences

And Millar poet of Sierras,
For bold deeds he doth prepare us,
And now he lives by the golden gate,
Honored in California's state,
To poet 'tis position grand,
Commissioner of Forest land.
