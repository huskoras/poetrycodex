Nature

(after Richepin.)

by Andrew Lang

From Rhymes a la Mode

Nature

LIGHT has flown!
Through the grey
The wind’s way
The sea’s moan
Sound alone!
For the day
These repay
And atone!

Scarce I know,
Listening so
To the streams
Of the sea,
If old dreams
Sing to me!
