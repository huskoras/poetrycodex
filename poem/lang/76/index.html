Love

Sonnet

by Andrew Lang

From Rhymes a la Mode

Love

LOVE died here
Long ago;—
O’er his bier,
Lying low,
Poppies throw;
Shed no tear;
Year by year,
Roses blow!

Year by year,
Adon—dear
To Love’s Queen—
Does not die!
Wakes when green
May is nigh!
