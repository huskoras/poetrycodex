Living

Chorus Matronarum.

by Robert Herrick

From Hesperides (1648)

Living

SEt you to your Wheele, and wax
SRich, by the Ductile Wool and Flax.
Yarne is an Income; and the Huswives thread
The Larder fils with meat; the Bin with bread.
