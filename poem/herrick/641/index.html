Chorus Virginum.

by Robert Herrick

From Hesperides (1648)

Living

GOddesse of Pleasure, Youth and Peace,
Give them the blessing of encrease:
And thou Lucina, that do'it heare
The vowes of those, that children beare:
When as her Aprill …
drawes neare,
Be thou then propitious there.
