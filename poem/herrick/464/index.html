Living

Upon Huncks. Epig.

by Robert Herrick

From Hesperides (1648)

Living

HUncks ha's no money (he do's sweare, or say)
About him, when the Taverns shot's to pay.
If he ha's none in's pockets, trust me, Huncks
Ha's none at home, in Coffers, Desks, or Trunks.
