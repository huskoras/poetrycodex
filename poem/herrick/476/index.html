Living

Upon his departure hence.

by Robert Herrick

From Hesperides (1648)

Living

THus I
Passe by,
And die:
As One,
Unknown,
And gon:
I'm made
A shade,
And laid
I'th grave,
There have
My Cave.
Where tell
I dwell,
Farewell.
