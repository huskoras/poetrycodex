Living

Upon Chub.

by Robert Herrick

From Hesperides (1648)

Living

WHen Chub brings in his harvest, still he cries,
Aha my boyes! heres wheat for Christmas Pies!
Soone after, he for beere so scores his wheat,
That at the tide, he has not bread to eate.
