Living

On himselfe.

by Robert Herrick

Living

LOve-sick I am, and must endure
A desp'rate grief, that finds no cure.
Ah me! I try; and trying, prove,
No Herbs have power to cure Love.
Onely our Soveraign salve, I know,
And that is Death, the end of Woe.
