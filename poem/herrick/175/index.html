Living

Upon the same.

by Robert Herrick

From Hesperides (1648)

Living

I Ask't thee oft, what Poets thou hast read,
And lik'st the best? Still thou reply'st, The dead.
I shall, ere long, with green turfs cover'd be;
Then sure thou't like, or thou wilt envie me.
