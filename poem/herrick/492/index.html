Living

Fresh Cheese and Cream.

by Robert Herrick

From Hesperides (1648)

Living

WO'd yee have fresh Cheese and Cream?
Iulia's Breast can give you them:
And if more; Each Nipple cries,
To your Cream, her's Straw berries.
