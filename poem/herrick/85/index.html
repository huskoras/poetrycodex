Love

Kissing Vsurie.

by Robert Herrick

From Hesperides (1648)

Love

BIancha, Let
Me pay the debt
I owe thee for a kisse
Thou lend'st to me;
And I to thee
Will render ten for this:
If thou wilt say,
Ten will not pay
For that so rich a one;
Ile cleare the somme,
If it will come
Unto a Million.
By this I guesse,
Of happinesse
Who has a little measure:
He must of right,
To th'utmost mite,
Make payment for his pleasure.
