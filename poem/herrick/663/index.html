Living

A Bacchanalian Verse.

by Robert Herrick

From Hesperides (1648)

Living

FIll me a mighty Bowle
Up to the brim:
That I may drink
Unto my Johnsons soule.

Crowne it agen agen;
And thrice repeat
That happy heat;
To drink to Thee my Ben.

Well I can quaffe, I see,
To th'number five,
Or nine; but thrive
In frenzie ne'r like thee.
