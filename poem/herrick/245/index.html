Living

Draw Gloves.

by Robert Herrick

From Hesperides (1648)

Living

AT Draw-Gloves we'l play,
And prethee, let's lay
A wager, and let it be this;
Who first to the Summe
Of twenty shall come,
Shall have for his winning a kisse.
