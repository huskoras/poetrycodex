Nature

To Cherry-blossomes.

by Robert Herrick

From Hesperides (1648)

Nature

YE may simper, blush, and smile,
And perfume the aire a while:
But (sweet things) ye must be gone;
Fruit, ye know, is comming on:
Then, Ah! Then, where is your grace,
When as Cherries come in place?
