Living

Cheerfulnesse in Charitie: or, The sweet Sacrifice.

by Robert Herrick

From Hesperides (1648)

Living

'TIs not a thousand Bullocks thies
Can please those Heav'nly Deities,
If the Vower don't express
In his Offering, Cheerfulness.
