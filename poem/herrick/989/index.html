Living

Ceremony upon Candlemas Eve.

by Robert Herrick 1591–1674

From Hesperides (1648)

Living

DOwn with the Rosemary, and so
Down with the Baies, & misletoe:
Down with the Holly, Ivie, all,
Wherewith ye drest the Christmas Hall:
That so the superstious find
No one least Branch there left behind:
For look how many leaves there be
Neglected there (maids trust to me)
So many Goblins you shall see.

Source Robert Herrick, Hesperides: or, The Works Both Humane & Divine of Robert Herrick, Esq (London: Printed for John Williams and Francis Eglesfield, 1648). Digital text: EEBO-TCP A43441.
