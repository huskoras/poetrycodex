Love

Upon Love.

by Robert Herrick

From Hesperides (1648)

Love

LOve, I have broke
Thy yoke;
The neck is free:
But when I'm next
Love vext,
Then shackell me.

'Tis better yet
To fret
The feet or hands;
Then to enthrall,
Or gall
The neck with bands.
