Love

Love kill'd by Lack.

by Robert Herrick

From Hesperides (1648)

Love

LEt me be warme; let me be fully fed:
Luxurious Love by Wealth is nourished.
Let me be leane, and cold, and once grown poore,
I shall dislike, what once I lov'd before.
