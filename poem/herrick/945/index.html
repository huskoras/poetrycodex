Living

To Silvia.

by Robert Herrick

From Hesperides (1648)

Living

PArdon my trespasse (Silvia) I confesse,
My kisse out-went the bounds of shamfastnesse:
None is discreet at all times; no, not Jove
Himselfe, at one time, can be wise, and Love.
