Living

Cruelty.

by Robert Herrick

From Hesperides (1648)

Living

TIs but a dog-like madnesse in bad Kings,
For to delight in wounds and murderings.
As some plants prosper best by cuts and blowes;
So Kings by killing doe encrease their foes.
