Living

Upon Strut.

by Robert Herrick

From Hesperides (1648)

Living

STrut, once a Fore-man of a Shop we knew;
But turn'd a Ladies Usher now, ('tis true:)
Tell me, has Strut got ere a title more?
No; he's but Fore-man, as he was before.
