Love

The Jimmall Ring, or True-love-knot.

by Robert Herrick

From Hesperides (1648)

Love

THou sent'st to me a True-love-knot; but I
Return'd a Ring of Jimmals, to imply
Thy Love had one knot, mine a triple tye.
