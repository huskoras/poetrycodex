Love

Upon Julia's haire fill'd with Dew.

by Robert Herrick

From Hesperides (1648)

Love

DEw sate on Julia's haire,
And spangled too,
Like Leaves that laden are
With trembling Dew:
Or glitter'd to my sight,
As when the Beames
Have their reflected light,
Daunc't by the Streames.
