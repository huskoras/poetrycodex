Living

Distrust.

by Robert Herrick

From Hesperides (1648)

Living

TO safe-guard Man from wrongs, there nothing must
Be truer to him, then a wise Distrust.
And to thy selfe be best this sentence knowne,
Heare all men speak; but credit few or none.
