A Moral Alphabet: Q

by Hilaire Belloc

From A Moral Alphabet (1899)

Social Commentaries

Q for Quinine, which children take
With Jam and little bits of cake.

MORAL.
How idiotic! Can Quinine
Replace Cold Baths and Sound Hygiene?
