Religion

Luke 16. Dives asking a drop.

by Richard Crashaw

Religion

A Drop, one drop, how sweetly one faire drop
Would tremble on my pearle-tipt fingers top?
My wealth is gone, ô goe it where it will,
Spare this one Iewell; I'le be Dives still.
