Religion

But now they have seen, and hated.

by Richard Crashaw

Religion

SEene? and yet hated thee? they did not see,
They saw Thee not, that saw and hated thee:
No, no, they saw the not, ô Life, ô Love,
Who saw ought in thee, that their hate could move.
