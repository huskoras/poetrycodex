Religion

On the still surviving markes of our Saviours wounds.

by Richard Crashaw

Religion

WHat ever story of their crueltie,
Or Naile, or Thorne, or Speare have writ in Thee,
Are in another sence
Still legible;
Sweet is the difference:
Once I did spell.
Every red letter
A wound of thine,
Now, (what is better)
Balsome for mine.
