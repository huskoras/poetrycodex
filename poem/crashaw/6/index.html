Religion

Luk. 15. On the Prodigall.

by Richard Crashaw

Religion

TEll me bright Boy, tell me my golden Lad,
Whither away so frolick? why so glad?
What all thy Wealth in counsaile? all thy state?
Are Husks so deare? troth 'tis a mighty rate.
