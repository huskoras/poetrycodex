Religion

Act. 5. The sicke implore St. Peter's shadow.

by Richard Crashaw

Religion

VNder thy shadow may I lurke a while,
Death's busie search I'le easily beguile:
Thy shadow Peter, must shew me the Sun,
My light's thy shadowes shadow, or 'tis done.
