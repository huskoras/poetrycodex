Religion

Vpon the same.

by Richard Crashaw

Religion

PAllas saw Venus arm'd, and streight she cry'd,
Come if thou dar'st, thus, thus let us be try'd.
Why foole! saies Venus, thus provok'st thou mee,
That being nak't, thou know'st could conquer thee?
