The Conclusion of All

by Joseph Hall 1574–1656

From Virgidemiarum: Toothless Satires (1602), Book III: Moral

Social Commentaries

THus haue I writ in smoother Cedar tree,
So gentle Satyrs, pend so easily.
Henceforth I write in crabbed oake-tree rindes
Search they that meane the secret meaning finde.
Holde out ye guiltie, and ye galled hides,
And meete my far-fetch't stripes with waiting sides.

Source Joseph Hall, Virgidemiarum, Sixe Bookes, Books I-III (Toothless Satires); this group Book III (London: Printed by John Harison, for Robert Dexter, 1602). Digital text: EEBO-TCP A71323.
