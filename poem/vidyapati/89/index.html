Love

Her wandering hair was mingled with the circle of her face

by Vidyapati c. 1352-1448

Translated by Ananda Coomaraswamy and Arun Sen (1915) · From Bangiya Padabali (trans. 1915)

Love

Sakhī:
Her wandering hair was mingled with the circle of her face--
A wreath of clouds across the moon:
Jewelled earrings swung from her ears,
Her tilka ran with sweat.

(Beauty, of fortune-yielding face:
If you should still wage Rati's war,
How may Hari-Hara save?)

Bracelets musical, and bangles noisy,
Anklets clinking:
Drunk with the wine of love, Love yielded,--
Victory, Victory! by beat of drum!

For when from the loins arose a muffled sound,
The warrior was crushed:
Vidyāpati's Master wins such bliss,--
Yamunā and Gangā mingling.

Source Vidyapati Thakura, Vidyapati: Bangiya Padabali, Songs of the Love of Radha and Krishna, translated into…, trans. Ananda Coomaraswamy and Arun Sen (London: The Old Bourne Press, 15 Holborn, E.C, 1915). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 38174.
