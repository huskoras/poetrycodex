Love

Your high round breasts--like golden cups

by Vidyapati c. 1352-1448

Translated by Ananda Coomaraswamy and Arun Sen (1915) · From Bangiya Padabali (trans. 1915)

Love

Krishna:
Your high round breasts--like golden cups--
And curving eyes, have stolen my wits away:
O lady fair, forbear your bitter fury,
And give the frenzied bee his draught of honey!

I clasp your hands, my fair sweet girl,
Be not so cruel, have pity on my lot:
How many times must I advise you
I may no more sustain the sting of love!

Vidyāpati says: You know full well.
That hope deferred is worse than death.

Source Vidyapati Thakura, Vidyapati: Bangiya Padabali, Songs of the Love of Radha and Krishna, translated into…, trans. Ananda Coomaraswamy and Arun Sen (London: The Old Bourne Press, 15 Holborn, E.C, 1915). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 38174.
