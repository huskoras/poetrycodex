Love

Canto Tertio.

by Sir Philip Sidney

Love

MY Loue bound me with a kisse
That I should no longer staie;
VVhen I felt so sweete a blisse,
I had lesse power to passe away:
Alas that women do no not knowe
Kisses make men loath to goe.
