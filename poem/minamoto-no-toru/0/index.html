Nature

Ah! why does love distract my thoughts

by Minamoto no Tōru

Translated by William N. Porter (1909) · From A Hundred Verses from Old Japan (Hyakunin Isshu), poem 14

Nature

Ah! why does love distract my thoughts,
Disordering my will!
I’m like the pattern on the cloth
Of Michinoku hill,—
All in confusion still.
