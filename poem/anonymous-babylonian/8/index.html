Religion

Elsewhere (Assyrian Sacred Poetry)

by Anonymous Babylonian Poets c. 1800–1000 BC

Translated by H. F. Talbot (Records of the Past, 1870s; repr. 1901) · From Babylonian and Assyrian Literature (1901)

Religion

O Thou; thy words who can resist? who can rival them?
Among the gods thy brothers, thou hast no equal!
