Two at a Fireside

by Edwin Markham

From The Man with the Hoe and Other Poems (1899)

Social Commentaries

I built a chimney for a comrade old,
I did the service not for hope or hire--
And then I traveled on in winter’s cold,
Yet all the day I glowed before the fire.
