Religion

Crossing 31: Only a portion of my gift is in

by Rabindranath Tagore 1861–1941

Translated by Rabindranath Tagore (1918) · From Lover's Gift and Crossing (1918)

Religion

Only a portion of my gift is in this world, the rest of it is in my dreams.

You, who ever elude my touch, come there in secret silence, hiding your lamp.

I shall know you by the thrill in the darkness, by the whisper of the unseen worlds, by the breath of the unknown shore;—I shall know you by the sudden delight of my heart melting into sadness of tears.

This translation is printed as prose in its source edition.
