Stray Birds 200: The burning log bursts in flame and cries

by Rabindranath Tagore 1861–1941

Translated by Rabindranath Tagore (1916) · From Stray Birds (1916)

Living

The burning log bursts in flame and cries,—"This is my flower, my death."
