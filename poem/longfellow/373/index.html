Poetic Aphorisms: Sin

by Henry Wadsworth Longfellow 1807–1882

From Translations

Social Commentaries

Man-like is it to fall into sin,
Fiend-like is it to dwell therein,
Christ-like is it for sin to grieve,
God-like is it all sin to leave.

✦ ✦ ✦
