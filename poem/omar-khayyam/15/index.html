Religion

Quatrain 16

by Omar Khayyam 1048–1131

Translated by E. H. Whinfield (1883) · From The Quatrains (Whinfield)

Religion

O Thou! to please whose love and wrath as well,
Allah created heaven and likewise hell;
Thou hast thy court in heaven, and I have naught,
Why not admit me in thy courts to dwell?

Source Omar Khayyam, The Sufistic Quatrains of Omar Khayyam in Definitive Form, including the translations of…, trans. Edward Henry Whinfield (Universal Classics Library) (New York and London: M. Walter Dunne, 1903). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 38511.
