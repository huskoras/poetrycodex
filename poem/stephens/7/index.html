Nature

The Watcher

by James Stephens 1882–1950

From The Oxford Book of Victorian Verse (1912)

Nature

A rose for a young head,
A ring for a bride,
Joy for the homestead
Clean and wide —
Who 's that waiting
In the rain outside?
A heart for an old friend,
A hand for the new:
Love can to earth lend
Heaven's hue —
Who 's that standing
In the silver dew?

A smile for the parting,
A tear as they go,
God's sweethearting
Ends just so —
Who 's that watching
Where the black winds blow?
He who is waiting
In the rain outside,
He who is standing
Where the dew drops wide,
He who is watching
In the wind must ride

(Tho' the pale hands cling)
With the rose
And the ring
And the bride,
Must ride
With the red of the rose,
And the gold of the ring,
And the lips and the hair of the bride.

Source Arthur Quiller-Couch, ed., The Oxford Book of Victorian Verse (Oxford: Clarendon Press (Henry Frowde, publisher to the University of Oxford), 1912). Digital text: Internet Archive cu31924013295757.
