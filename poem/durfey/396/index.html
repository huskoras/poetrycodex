Love

Song: What sayest thou

by Thomas D'Urfey 1653–1723

From Wit and Mirth: or Pills to Purge Melancholy, vol. 6 (1719-20)

Love

What sayest thou,
If one should thrust thee thro'?
What sayest thou,
If one shou'd Plough?
I say Sir, you may do what you please,
I shall scarce stir,
Tho' you ne'er cease,
Thro', thro', you may thrust me thro'.
Such Death is a Pleasure,
When Life's a Disease.
