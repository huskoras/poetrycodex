Love

Book XII, 29

by Alcaeus of Messene

Translated by W. R. Paton (1916–18) · From The Greek Anthology (Loeb, vol. IV, 1918)

Love

Protarchus is fair and does not wish it; but later he will, and his youth races on holding a torch.
