Religion

The Child on Calvary

by John Banister Tabb 1845–1909

From Child Verse (1900)

Religion

The Cross is tall,
And I too small
To reach His hand
Or touch His feet;
But on the sand
His footprints I have found,
And it is sweet
To kiss the holy ground.
