Living

David Gam

by Owain Glyndŵr c. 1354–1416

Translated by George Borrow (Welsh Poems and Ballads, 1915) · From Welsh Poems and Ballads (1915)

Living

Shouldst thou a little red man descry
Asking about his dwelling fair,
Tell him it under the bank doth lie,
And its brow the mark of the coal doth bear.

Source George Borrow, trans., Welsh Poems and Ballads, With an introduction by Ernest Rhys (London: Jarrold & Sons, 1915). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 54851.
