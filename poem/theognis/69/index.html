Fragment LXVIII: Friend ! if your sense and judgment had been wholly

by Theognis of Megara fl. 6th century BC

Translated by John Hookham Frere (1842; Bohn 1856) · From Fragments of Theognis (Frere, 1842)

Social Commentaries

Friend ! if your sense and judgment had been wholly
Or nearly equal to your pride and folly,
You might have seen yourself approv’d and priz’d,
As much precisely as you ’re now despis’d.
