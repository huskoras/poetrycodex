Death & Dying

Epitaph of Euripides (Fragment 31)

by Timotheus of Miletus c. 446 – c. 357 BC

Translated by J. M. Edmonds (1927) · From Lyra Graeca, vol. III (Loeb, 1927): Timotheus

Death & Dying

Though his bones lie in Macedon where his life was ended, the whole of Greece is the monument of Euripides; but his birthplace was Athens, the Greece of Greece, and giving much joy by his Muses, he hath the thanks for it from many men,

This translation is printed as prose in its source edition.
