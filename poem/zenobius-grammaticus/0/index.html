Arts & Sciences

Book IX, 711

by Zenobius the Grammarian

Translated by W. R. Paton (1916–18) · From The Greek Anthology (Loeb, vol. III, 1917)

Arts & Sciences

The painter wished to depict Grammar herself, and having painted Victor, said: “I have attained my end.”
