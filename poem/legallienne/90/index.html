Living

Snatch

by Richard Le Gallienne 1866–1947

From Robert Louis Stevenson: An Elegy, and Other Poems (1895)

Living

From tavern to tavern
Youth passes along,
With an armful of girl
And a heart full of song.

From flower to flower
The butterfly sips,
O passionate limbs
And importunate lips!

From candle to candle
The moth loves to fly,
O sweet, sweet to burn!
And still sweeter to die!

Source Richard Le Gallienne, Robert Louis Stevenson: An Elegy, and Other Poems Mainly Personal (London and Boston: John Lane (London); Copeland and Day (Boston), 1895). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 10910. (details from catalogue records, not yet checked against the title page)
