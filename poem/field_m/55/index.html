Love

Say, if a gallant rose my bower doth scale

by Michael Field 1846–1914 (Bradley) and 1862–1913 (Cooper)

From Underneath the Bough (1898)

Love

Say, if a gallant rose my bower doth scale,
Higher and higher.
And, tho' she twine the other side the pale.
Toward me doth sigh her
Perfume, her damask mouth —
Roses will love the south —
Can I deny her?

I have a lady loves me in despite
Of bonds that tie her.
And bid her honest Corin's flame requite;
When I espy her,
Kisses are near their birth —
Love cannot live in dearth —
Say, shall I fly her?

Source Michael Field, Underneath the Bough: A Book of Verses (Portland, Maine: Thomas B. Mosher, 1898). Digital text: Internet Archive underneathboughb00fielrich.
