Nature

Dawn in the Island Camp

by William Wilfred Campbell

From Songs of the Great Dominion (1889)

Nature

RED in the mists of the morning, angry, coloured with fire,
Beats the great Lake in its beauty,
Rocks the wild lake in its ire.
Tossing from headland to headland, tipped with the glories of dawn,
With gleaming wide reaches of beaches
That stretch out, far, wind-swept, and wan.

Behind, the wild tangle of island swept and drenched by the gales of the night ;
In front, lone stretches of water
Flame-bathed by the incoming light ;
Dim the dark reels and dips under, night wavers and ceases to be,
As God sends the manifold mystery
Of the morning and lake round to me.
