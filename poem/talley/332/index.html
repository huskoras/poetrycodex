Speak Softly

by Thomas W. Talley (collector) 1870–1952

From Negro Folk Rhymes (1922)

Living

"Wus dat you spoke,
Or a fence rail broke?"
Br'er Rabbit say to de Jay
W'en you don't speak sof',
Yō' baits comes off;
An' de fish jes swim away.
