Love

After Schiller

by Thomas Hardy

From Poems of the Past and the Present

Love

KNIGHT, a true sister-love
This heart retains;
Ask me no other love,
That way lie pains!

Calm must I view thee come,
Calm see thee go;
Tale-telling tears of thine
I must not know!
