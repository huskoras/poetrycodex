Time & Brevity

The Echo-elf answers

by Thomas Hardy 1840–1928

From Human Shows, Far Phantasies, Songs and Trifles (1925)

Time & Brevity

How much shall I love her?
For life, or not long?
“Not long.”

Alas! When forget her?
In years, or by Tune?
“By June.”

And whom woo I after?
No one, or a throng?
“A throng.”

Of these shall I wed one
Long hence, or quite soon?
“Quite soon.”

And which will my bride be?
The right or the wrong?
“The wrong.”

And my remedy — what kind?
Wealth-wove, or earth-hewn?
“Earth-hewn.”

Source Thomas Hardy, Human Shows, Far Phantasies, Songs, and Trifles (London: Macmillan, 1925). Digital text: Internet Archive humanshowsfarpha0000hard.
