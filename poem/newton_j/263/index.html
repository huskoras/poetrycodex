Religion

Before Sermon (Book III, Hymn 90)

by John Newton 1725–1807

From Olney Hymns, Book III (1779)

Religion

Now, LORD, inspire the preacher's heart,
And teach his tongue to speak;
Food to the hungry soul impart,
And cordials to the weak.

Furnish us all with light and pow'rs
To walk in Wisdom's ways;
So shall the benefit be ours,
And thou shalt have the praise.

Source John Newton and William Cowper, Olney Hymns, in Three Books (Book III: On the Progress and Changes of the Spiritual Life), Book III (London: W. Oliver, 1779). Digital text: Christian Classics Ethereal Library newton/olneyhymns.
