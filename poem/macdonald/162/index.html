Religion

Come Down

by George MacDonald

From The poetical works of George MacDonald in two volumes — Volume 2

Religion

Still am I haunting
Thy door with my prayers;
Still they are panting
Up thy steep stairs!
Wouldst thou not rather
Come down to my heart,
And there, O my Father,
Be what thou art?
