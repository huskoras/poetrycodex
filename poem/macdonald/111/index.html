Religion

To-morrow

by George MacDonald 1824–1905

From The poetical works of George MacDonald in two volumes — Volume 1

Religion

My TO-MORROW is but a flitting
Fancy of the brain;
God's TO-MORROW an angel sitting,
Ready for joy or pain.

My TO-MORROW has no soul,
Dead as yesterdays;
God's--a brimming silver bowl
Of life that gleams and plays.

My TO-MORROW, I mock you away!
Shadowless nothing, thou!
God's TO-MORROW, come, dear day,
For God is in thee now.

Source George MacDonald, The Poetical Works of George MacDonald in Two Volumes, I (London: Chatto & Windus, 1893). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 9543.
