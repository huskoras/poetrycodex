Death & Dying

Book VII, 360

by Anonymous (Greek Anthology)

Translated by W. R. Paton (1916–18) · From The Greek Anthology (Loeb, vol. II, 1917)

Death & Dying

Having killed me with your hands you build me a tomb, not to bury me, but to hide me. May you meet with the same fate!
