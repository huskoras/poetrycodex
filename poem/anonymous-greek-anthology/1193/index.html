Hope and Experience (Mackail)

by Anonymous (Greek Anthology) various, 7th c. BC – 10th c. AD

Translated by J. W. Mackail (1890) · From Select Epigrams from the Greek Anthology (1890)

Social Commentaries

Whoso has married once and again seeks a second wedding, is a shipwrecked man who sails twice through a difficult gulf.

Source J. W. Mackail, trans., Select Epigrams from the Greek Anthology, Edited with a Revised Text, Translation, and Notes (London: Longmans, Green, and Co, 1890). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 2378.
