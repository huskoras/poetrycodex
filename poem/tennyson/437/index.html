Nature

The Oak

by Alfred, Lord Tennyson 1809–1892

From Demeter and Other Poems (1889)

Nature

LIVE thy Life,
Young and old,
Like yon oak,
Bright in spring,
Living gold;
Summer-rich
Then; and then
Autumn-changed,
Soberer-hued
Gold again.

All his leaves
Fall'n at length,
Look, he stands,
Trunk and bough,
Naked strength.
