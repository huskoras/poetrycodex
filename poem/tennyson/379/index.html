To the Princess Frederica of Hanover on Her Marriage

by Alfred, Lord Tennyson 1809–1892

From Ballads and Other Poems (1880)

Relationships

O YOU that were eyes and light to the
King till he past away
From the darkness of life—
He saw not his daughter—he blest her: the blind King sees you to-day,
He blesses the wife.
