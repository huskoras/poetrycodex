Sonnet 13

by William Drummond of Hawthornden

Love

NOr Arne, nor Mincius, nor stately Tiber,
Sebethus, nor the Flood into whose streames
He fell who burnt the world with borrowed beames,
Gold-rolling Tagus, Munda, famous Iber,
Sorgue, Rosne, Loire, Garron, nor proud-banked Sein…,
Peneus, Phasis Xanthus, humble Ladon,
Nor She whose Nymphes excell her loved Adon
Faire Tamesis, nor Ister large, nor Rheine,
Euphrates, Tigr…s, Indus, Hermus, Gange,
Pearly Hydaspes, Serpent-like Meander,
The Floud which robbed Hero of Leander,
Nile that far far his hidden Head doth range,
Have ever had so rare a cause of praise,
As Ora where this Northerne Phoenix stayes.
