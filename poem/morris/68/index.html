Nature

Silk Embroidery

by William Morris

From Poems By The Way & Love Is Enough

Nature

Lo silken my garden,
and silken my sky,
And silken my apple-boughs
hanging on high;
All wrought by the Worm
in the peasant carle's cot
On the Mulberry leafage
when summer was hot!
