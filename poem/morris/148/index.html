Relationships

In Prison

by William Morris 1834–1896

From The Defence of Guenevere and Other Poems

Relationships

Wearily, drearily,
Half the day long,
Flap the great banners
High over the stone;
Strangely and eerily
Sounds the wind's song,
Bending the banner-poles.

While, all alone,
Watching the loophole's spark,
Lie I, with life all dark,
Feet tether'd, hands fetter'd
Fast to the stone,
The grim walls, square letter'd
With prison'd men's groan.

Still strain the banner-poles
Through the wind's song,
Westward the banner rolls
Over my wrong.

Source William Morris, The Defence of Guenevere and Other Poems, Reprinted from the Kelmscott Press Edition as… (London, New York, Bombay and Calcutta: Longmans, Green, and Co, 1908). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 22650.
