Nature

Fly away, fly away over the sea

by Christina Rossetti

From Sing-Song (1872/1893)

Nature

Fly away, fly away over the sea,
Sun-loving swallow, for summer is done;
Come again, come again, come back to me,
Bringing the summer and bringing the sun.
