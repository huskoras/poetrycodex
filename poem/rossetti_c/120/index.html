Living

Margaret has a milking-pail

by Christina Rossetti

From Sing-Song (1872/1893)

Living

Margaret has a milking-pail,
And she rises early;
Thomas has a threshing-flail,
And he’s up betimes.
Sometimes crossing through the grass
Where the dew lies pearly,
They say “Good morrow” as they pass
By the leafy limes.
