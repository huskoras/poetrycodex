Kookoorookoo! kookoorookoo!”

by Christina Rossetti

From Sing-Song (1872/1893)

Living

“Kookoorookoo! kookoorookoo!”
Early birds begin their singing;
“Kikirikee! kikirikee!”
The day, the day, the day is springing.
