Living

Tell me a tale—

by Christina Rossetti

From Sing-Song (1872/1893)

Living

Tell me a tale—
What shall I tell?—
Two mournful sisters,
And a tolling knell,
Tolling ding and tolling dong,
Ding dong bell.
