Religion

Up, my drowsing eyes!

by Christina Rossetti 1830–1894

From Verses (1893)

Religion

Up, my drowsing eyes!
Up, my sinking heart!
Up to Jesus Christ arise!
Claim your part
In all raptures of the skies.

Yet a little while,
Yet a little way,
Saints shall reap and rest and smile
All the day.
Up! let’s trudge another mile.
