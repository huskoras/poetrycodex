Living

Rushes in a watery place

by Christina Rossetti

From Sing-Song (1872/1893)

Living

Rushes in a watery place,
And reeds in a hollow;
A soaring skylark in the sky,
A darting swallow;
And where pale blossom used to hang
Ripe fruit to follow.
