A New Baby

by Anonymous Chinese nursery rhymes traditional (collected in Peking, 1890s)

Translated by Isaac Taylor Headland (1900) · From Chinese Mother Goose Rhymes (1900)

Relationships

A gilt-wood mace,
And silvered things,
My grandfather plays,
And grandmother sings;
My grandmother sings till broad daylight,
And a baby comes to our home at night;
They place the child by the pot on the ground,
And it eats rice soup with a sucking sound.

Source Isaac Taylor Headland, trans., Chinese Mother Goose Rhymes, Translated and Illustrated by Isaac Taylor Headland (New York; Chicago; Toronto: Fleming H. Revell Company, 1900). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 40425.
