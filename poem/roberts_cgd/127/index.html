Love

In the Crowd

by Charles G. D. Roberts 1860–1943

From New York Nocturnes and Other Poems (1898)

Love

I walk the city square with thee.
The night is loud; the pavements roar.
Their eddying mirth and misery
Encircle thee and me.

The street is full of lights and cries.
The crowd but brings thee close to me.
I only hear thy low replies;
I only see thine eyes.

Source Charles G. D. Roberts, New York Nocturnes and Other Poems (Boston, New York and London: Lamson, Wolffe and Company, 1898). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 56418.
