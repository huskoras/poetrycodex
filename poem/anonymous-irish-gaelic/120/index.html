Love

My Sorrow on the Sea

by Anonymous (Irish Gaelic, later) c. 13th–18th century

Translated by George Sigerson (1907) · From Bards of the Gael and Gall (1907)

Love

My grief and my sorrow —
The great wide sea!
It flows night and morrow
'Tween my Love and me.

I'm left here despairing,
Pining away,
No hope p'er it faring
For ever and aye.

O woe! to be with him
My Vourneen fair,
In the lowlands of Leinster
Or highlands of Clare.

Last night I was sleeping
On rush-bed gray,
This morn, I was weeping,
And cast it away.

My Love had come to me
In swiftest of ships,
To comfort, to woo me
His lips on my lips.
