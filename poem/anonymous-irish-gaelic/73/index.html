Love

I Follow a Star

by Anonymous (Irish Gaelic, later)

Translated by Seosamh Mac Cathmhaoil (in Hull, The Poem-Book of the Gael, 1913) · From The Poem-Book of the Gael (1913)

Love

I follow a star
Burning deep in the blue,
A sign on the hills
Lit for me and for you!

Moon-red is the star,
Halo-winged like a rood,
Christ's heart in its heart set,
Streaming with blood.

Follow the gilly
Beyond to the west;
He leads where the Christ lies
On Mary's white breast.

King, priest, and prophet--
A child, and no more--
Adonai the Maker!
Come, let us adore.

Translation by the author.
