Relationships

An Appeal

by Baha al-Din Zuhayr 1186–1258

Translated by Edward Henry Palmer (1877) · From The Poetical Works of Beha-ed-Din Zoheir of Egypt (1877)

Relationships

Yes! the tale is as you say,
I'll tell it though our mentors flout us;
Yes! and what care I, I pray?
Let folks say what they like about us!

Let other lovers fear disgrace,
Let others in their love despair;
In my heart Some-one holds her place.
There is no change of feeling there!

The blamer scolds, he does not know
How long my tale of love may be.
Ah! dear, my heart's a heart, I trow,
That ne'er will tire or turn from thee.

When will fate make thee kind, I pray,
And stop all words us twain between? —
Ah me! reproaches every day;
My envoy is tired out, I ween.
