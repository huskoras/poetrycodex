Religion

Book VI, 199

by Antiphilus of Byzantium 1st century AD

Translated by W. R. Paton (1916–18) · From The Greek Anthology (Loeb, vol. I, 1916)

Religion

Artemis, goddess of the road, Antiphilus dedicates to thee this hat from his head, a token of his wayfaring; for thou hast hearkened to his vows, thou hast blessed his paths. The gift is not great, but given in piety, and let no covetous traveller lay his hand on my offering; it is not safe to despoil a shrine of even little gifts.

This translation is printed as prose in its source edition.

Source Various ancient epigrammatists, The Greek Anthology, with an English Translation by W. R. Paton, Vol. I: Books I-VI, I (Books I-VI), trans. W. R. Paton, First printing (1916) (Loeb Classical Library) (London; New York: William Heinemann; G. P. Putnam's Sons, 1916). Digital text: Internet Archive greekanthology01pato.
