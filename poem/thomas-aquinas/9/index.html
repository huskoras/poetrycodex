Religion

Lauda Sion Salvatorem (Part II)

by St. Thomas Aquinas dates not given in the source

Translated by Edward Caswall (1849) · From Lyra Catholica (1849)

Religion

Lo! upon the Altar lies,
Hidden deep from human eyes,
Bread of Angels from the skies,

Made the food of mortal man:
Children’s meat to dogs denied;
In old types foresignified;

In the manna Heav’n-supplied,
Isaac, and the Paschal Lamb.
Jesu! Shepherd of the sheep!

Thou thy flock in safety keep,
Living Bread! thy life supply;
Strengthen us, or else we die;

Fill us with celestial grace:
Thou, who feedest us below!
Source of all we have or know!

Grant that with thy Saints above,
Sitting at the feast of love,
We may see Thee face to face.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Latin: Lauda Sion Salvatorem. Continuation of the hymn above, as printed in Lyra Catholica (1849) among the Hymns and Sequences of the Roman Missal.
