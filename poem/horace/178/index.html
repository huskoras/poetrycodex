Odes 1.30 (Creech): O Venus

by Horace (Quintus Horatius Flaccus) 65–8 BC

Translated by Thomas Creech (1684) · From Odes, Book I (Creech, 1684)

Living

KInd Venus leave the Paphian Isle,
And live with Glycera a while;
A noble Temple she prepares,
With Incense sweet thine Altars smoak,
Thy presence numerous Vows invoak;
She calls Thee with a thousand Prayers.

The Graces with their Zones unloos'd,
The Nymphs their beauties all expos'd
From every Spring, and every Plain;
Thy powerful, hot, and winged Boy,
And Youth that's dull without thy joy,
And Mercury compose thy Train.

Source Horace, The Odes, Satyrs, and Epistles of Horace Done into English, trans. Thomas Creech (London: Printed for Jacob Tonson, and sold by Tim. Goodwin at the Maiden-head against St. Dunstan's Church in Fleet Street, 1684). Digital text: EEBO-TCP A44471.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

He begs Venus to come to the Temple which his Glycera had prepar'd.
