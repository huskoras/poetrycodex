Social Commentaries

Book XI, 104

by Lucillius 1st century AD

Translated by W. R. Paton (1916–18) · From The Greek Anthology (Loeb, vol. IV, 1918)

Social Commentaries

Poor Menestratus once, riding on an ant as if it were ‘an elephant, was suddenly stretched on his back. When it trod on him and he was breathing his last, “O Envy!” he exclaimed, “thus riding perished Phaethon too.”

This translation is printed as prose in its source edition.
