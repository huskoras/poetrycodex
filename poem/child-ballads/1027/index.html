Death & Dying

Lamkin (Child 93S)

by Traditional Ballads (Child collection) traditional; texts recorded c. 13th–19th century

From The English and Scottish Popular Ballads, ed. F. J. Child (1882–98)

Death & Dying

Lambkin was as brave a builder
as eer built a stane,
And he built Lord Cassillis house,
an for payment he gat nane.

My lord said to my lady,
when he went abroad,
Tak care o fause Lamkin,
for he sleeps in the wood.
