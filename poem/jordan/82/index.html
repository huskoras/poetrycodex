On Ribaldry.

by Thomas Jordan

From Divinity and Morality in Robes of Poetry ([1660?])

Social Commentaries

OF all detested vices, none (to me)
Appears more vain then verbal Rihaldry:
For he who to such talk his mind doth bend,
Is like that dog, whose tail is at's tongues end.
