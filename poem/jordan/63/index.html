Perjurie.

by Thomas Jordan

From Divinity and Morality in Robes of Poetry ([1660?])

Social Commentaries

THou shalt not make thy Makers name to be
A Covenant to cover falsitie.

When thou wouldst vow a vow to God, first weigh it,
Then (though it be unto thy loss) obey it.

Good Zechariah (in the flying Roll)
Saw that the perjur'd Man would lose his soule.
