An Epigram on one, who said, He liv'd by his wits.

by Thomas Jordan

From Divinity and Morality in Robes of Poetry ([1660?])

Social Commentaries

PAmphlet last week in his fantastique fits,
Was ask'd how he did live, He said, By's wits:
Pamphlet I see will tell lyes by the Clock,
How can he live upon so poor a stock?
