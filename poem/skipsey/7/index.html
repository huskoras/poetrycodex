The Golden Lot

by Joseph Skipsey 1832–1903

From The Poets and the Poetry of the Century, ed. Alfred H. Miles

Social Commentaries

In the coal-pit, or the factory,
I toil by night and day.
And still to the music of labour
I lilt my heart-felt lay;
I lilt my heart-felt lay —
And the gloom of the deep, deep mine.
Or the din of the factory dieth away.
And a Golden Lot ii mine.

Source Alfred H. Miles, ed., The Poets and the Poetry of the Century, Vols. I, II, IV, V, VI, VIII and X (the 222 poems are spread over these volumes), First edition, in ten volumes (1891-97); not the twelve-volume reissue of 1905-07 'The Poets and the Poetry of the Nineteenth Century' (London: Hutchinson & Co, 1891-1897).
