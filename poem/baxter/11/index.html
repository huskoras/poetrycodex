Religion

Psalm XII

by Richard Baxter 1615–1691

From Paraphrase on the Psalms of David in Metre (1692)

Religion

Help, Lord, for upright godly men
[from us] are taken hence away;
And from among the sons of men
The faithful [daily] do decay.

Unto his neighbour every one
Dont speak with [lies and] vanity;
With a false double heart they talk,
And lips of [fraud and] flattery.

God will cut off all flattering lips,
[And] the [proud] tongue that speaketh thus,

Our word shall stand; our tongue is ours,
What Lord is [Ruler] over us?

For the oppression of the poor,
And needy's sighs, I'le [now a-] rise,
And them in safety set, saith God,
From those that them [with scorn] despise.

The words of God are words most pure;
Like [purest] silver fully try'd
In earthen furnace, many times
Refin'd and [throughly] purify'd.

Lord, thou wilt thine save and preserve
For ever from this [wicked] race.

The wicked then are bold and brisk,
When vile men get in highest place.

Source Richard Baxter, Mr. Richard Baxter's Paraphrase on the Psalms of David in Metre, with Other Hymns, Left… (London: Printed for Thomas Parkhurst and Jonathan Robinson, 1692). Digital text: EEBO-TCP A27939.
