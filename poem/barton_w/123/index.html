Religion

Psalm 124

by William Barton 1598?–1678

From The Book of Psalms in Metre (1644)

Religion

BUt that the LORD (may Israel say)
But that the LORD to us did stick:
When en'mies rose to hunt their prey,
They had devoured Israel quick:
Whose kindling fury nothing stopt.
The flouds did rise, the waves did roll,
Our soul had then been over-topt,
The waters proud had drown'd our soul.
Blessed be God that gave us not
Into their cruell teeth a prey:
Like bird from snare of fowler got,
So is our soul escap't away.
The snare is broke that held the game,
By that good means do we evade:
Our help is in Jehovahs Name,
Who hath both earth and heaven made.
