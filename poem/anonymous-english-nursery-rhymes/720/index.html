The Tarts

by Anonymous (English nursery rhymes) traditional; recorded 17th–19th century

From The Real Mother Goose, illus. Blanche Fisher Wright (1916)

Mythology & Folklore

The Queen of Hearts,
She made some tarts,
All on a summer's day;
The Knave of Hearts,
He stole the tarts,
And took them clean away.

The King of Hearts
Called for the tarts,
And beat the Knave full sore;
The Knave of Hearts
Brought back the tarts,
And vowed he'd steal no more.
