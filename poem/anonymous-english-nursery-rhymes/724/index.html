The Old Woman of Harrow

by Anonymous (English nursery rhymes) traditional; recorded 17th–19th century

From The Real Mother Goose, illus. Blanche Fisher Wright (1916)

Mythology & Folklore

There was an old woman of Harrow,
Who visited in a wheelbarrow;
And her servant before,
Knocked loud at each door,
To announce the old woman of Harrow.
