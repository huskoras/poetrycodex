‘joy is Fleet’

by George Meredith

From Poems — Volume 3

Love

JOY is fleet,
Sorrow slow.
Love, so sweet,
Sorrow will sow.
Love, that has flown
Ere day’s decline,
Love to have known,
Sorrow, be mine!
