Epigram XXXV. — On a Hare caught by a Sea-Dog

by Decimus Magnus Ausonius

Translated by Hugh G. Evelyn-White (1919–21) · From Book XIX. — Epigrams of Ausonius on Various Matters

Living

Once on the strand of Sicily a sea-dog snapped up a hare speeding before the hounds. Then said the hare: "Against me both sea and land direct their ravages, perchance heaven also; since there is a Dog among the stars."

This translation is printed as prose in its source edition.
