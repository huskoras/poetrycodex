Epigram 6: Faire Betrice tucks her coates vp somewhat

by John Taylor 1580–1653

From The Sculler (1612)

Social Commentaries

Faire Betrice tucks her coates vp somewhat hie,
Her pretty Leg and Foot cause men should spie:
Saies one you haue a handsome Leg sweet Ducke,
I haue two (quoth she) or els I had hard lucke:
There's two indeed, I thinke th'are twinnes (quoth he)
They are, and are not, honest friend (quoth she)
Their birth was both at once I dare be sworne,
But yet betweene them both a man was borne.

Source John Taylor, The Sculler rowing from Tiber to Thames with his boate laden with a hotch-potch, or… (London: Printed by E[dward] A[llde]; to be sold [by Nathaniel Butter] at the Pied Bull near St Austin's Gate, 1612). Digital text: EEBO-TCP A13493.
