Epigram 7: The way to make a Welch-man thirst

by John Taylor 1580–1653

From The Sculler (1612)

Social Commentaries

The way to make a Welch-man thirst for blisse,
And say his prayers daily on his knees:
Is to perswade him that most certaine tis,
The Moone is made of nothing but greene Cheese.
And heele desire of God no greater boone.
But place in heauen to feede vpon the Moone.
