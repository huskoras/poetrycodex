Religion

Epigram 9: It is an Art beyond the worke

by John Taylor

From The Sculler (1612)

Religion

It is an Art beyond the worke of Nature,
The Pope should be Creator, and a creature:
Betwixt the Pope and God there's one thing od,
For though God all things made, the Pope makes God.
