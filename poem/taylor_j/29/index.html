Epigram 24: Who dares for Gluttony the Pope accuse

by John Taylor

From The Sculler (1612)

Religion

Who dares for Gluttony the Pope accuse,
Or gainst voluptuous dyet make's complaints?
His Holynes so many Fasts doth vse,
As , and fasting dayes, and Eeues of Saints.
Yet where Pride, , and Auarice are found,
Heart gnawing Enuy, and fell murdering Wrath,
There rauenous Gluttony must needs abound,
Else other vices will be out of breath.
For Papists fasts are generally more deare,
Then feasts of with all their cheare.
