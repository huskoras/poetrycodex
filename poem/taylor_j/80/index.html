Epigram 43: A French and English man at dinner

by John Taylor

From The Sculler (1612)

Social Commentaries

A French and English man at dinner sate,
And neither vnderstanding others prate,
The Frenchman sayes, mange proface Mounsieure,
The Englishman begins to storme and sweare:
By all the Deuills, and the Deuills dams,
