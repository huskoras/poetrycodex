Enough of mournful melodies, my lute

by William Watson

From The Poems of William Watson (1893)

Arts & Sciences

Enough of mournful melodies, my lute!
Be henceforth joyous, or be henceforth mute.
Song's breath is wasted when it does but fan
The smouldering infelicity of man.
