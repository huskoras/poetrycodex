MYS 4262: 唐国に行き足らはして (Karakuni ni yuki tarawashite)

by Classical Japanese Poets 7th–10th centuries

From 万葉集 (Man'yōshū), after Saitō Mokichi, 万葉秀歌

Relationships

唐国に行き足らはして還り来むますら健男に御酒奉る

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Poet: Tajihi no Takanushi (多治比鷹主). Book 19. Composed at a farewell banquet for the deputy envoy to Tang, Ōtomo no Komaro.
