The Mind

XI. In a lonely place

by Stephen Crane 1871–1900

From The Black Riders and Other Lines (1895)

The Mind

In a lonely place,
I encountered a sage
Who sat, all still,
Regarding a newspaper.
He accosted me:
"Sir, what is this?"
Then I saw that I was greater,
Aye, greater than this sage.
I answered him at once,
"Old, old man, it is the wisdom of the age."
The sage looked upon me with admiration.

Source Stephen Crane, The Black Riders and Other Lines (Boston: Copeland & Day (reprinted by courtesy of Small, Maynard & Company), 1895). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 40786.
