The Mind

LXV. Once, I knew a fine song

by Stephen Crane 1871–1900

From The Black Riders and Other Lines (1895)

The Mind

Once, I knew a fine song,
--It is true, believe me,--
It was all of birds,
And I held them in a basket;
When I opened the wicket,
Heavens! They all flew away.
I cried, "Come back, little thoughts!"
But they only laughed.
They flew on
Until they were as sand
Thrown between me and the sky.

Source Stephen Crane, The Black Riders and Other Lines (Boston: Copeland & Day (reprinted by courtesy of Small, Maynard & Company), 1895). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 40786.
