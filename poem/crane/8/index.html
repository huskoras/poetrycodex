The Mind

IX. I stood upon a high place

by Stephen Crane

From The Black Riders and Other Lines (1895)

The Mind

I stood upon a high place,
And saw, below, many devils
Running, leaping,
And carousing in sin.
One looked up, grinning,
And said, "Comrade! Brother!"
