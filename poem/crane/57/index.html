The Mind

LVIII. The sage lectured brilliantly

by Stephen Crane 1871–1900

From The Black Riders and Other Lines (1895)

The Mind

The sage lectured brilliantly.
Before him, two images:
"Now this one is a devil,
"And this one is me."
He turned away.
Then a cunning pupil
Changed the positions.
Turned the sage again:
"Now this one is a devil,
"And this one is me."
The pupils sat, all grinning,
And rejoiced in the game.
But the sage was a sage.

Source Stephen Crane, The Black Riders and Other Lines (Boston: Copeland & Day (reprinted by courtesy of Small, Maynard & Company), 1895). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 40786.
