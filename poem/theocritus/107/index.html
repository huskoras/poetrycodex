Nature

Epigram 1 (Edmonds)

by Theocritus fl. c. 270 BC

Translated by J. M. Edmonds (1912) · From The Greek Bucolic Poets (Loeb, 1912)

Nature

Those dewy roses and that thick bushy thyme are an offering to the Ladies of Helicon, and since ’tis the Delphian Rock hath made it honoured, the dark-leaved bay, Pythian Healer, is for thee; and yon horny white he-goat that crops the outmost sprays of the terebinth-tree is to be the blood-offering upon the altar.

This translation is printed as prose in its source edition.

Source Theocritus; Bion; Moschus, The Greek Bucolic Poets, trans. J. M. Edmonds (Loeb Classical Library) (London; New York: William Heinemann; G. P. Putnam's Sons, 1912). Digital text: Internet Archive greekbucolicpo00theo.
