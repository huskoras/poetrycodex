Nature

Epigram 24 (Edmonds)

by Theocritus

Translated by J. M. Edmonds (1912) · From The Greek Bucolic Poets (Loeb, 1912)

Nature

These offerings Apollo had possessed before; but the base you see below them is younger, than this by twenty years and that by seven, this by five and that by twelve, and this again by two hundred. For when you reckon them that is what it comes to.

This translation is printed as prose in its source edition.
