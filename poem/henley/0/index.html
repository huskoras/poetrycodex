Religion

To My Wife

by William Ernest Henley

Religion

_Take_, _dear_, _my little sheaf of songs_,
_For_, _old or new_,
_All that is good in them belongs_
_Only to you_;

_And_, _singing as when all was young_,
_They will recall_
_Those others_, _lived but left unsung_—
_The bent of all_.

W. E. H

APRIL 1888
SEPTEMBER 1897.
