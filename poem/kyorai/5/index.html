Nature

Kokoro naki (English)

by Mukai Kyorai 1651–1704

Translated by Basil Hall Chamberlain (1902) · From Basho and the Japanese Poetical Epigram (1902)

Nature

The heartless Government Office,—ay! and the cuckoo.
