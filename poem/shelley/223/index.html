Love

Kissing Helena

by Percy Bysshe Shelley 1792–1822

Translated by Percy Bysshe Shelley · From Complete Poetical Works, Oxford ed. Hutchinson (1914)

Love

Kissing Helena, together
With my kiss, my soul beside it
Came to my lips, and there I kept it,—
For the poor thing had wandered thither,
To follow where the kiss should guide it,
Oh, cruel I, to intercept it!

Source Percy Bysshe Shelley, trans., The Complete Poetical Works of Percy Bysshe Shelley, including materials never before…, Single-volume edition (Gutenberg file labelled Volume 1), Oxford Edition (London: Humphrey Milford, Oxford University Press, 1914).
