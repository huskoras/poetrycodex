Riddle 37: Bellows

by Anonymous (Old English) c. 7th–11th century

Translated by R. K. Gordon, Anglo-Saxon Poetry (Everyman’s Library, 1926) · From Exeter Book Riddles (selection)

Arts & Sciences

I saw the creature; its belly was at the back, mightily swollen; a servant was behind, a strong man; and he had endured much when its fullness flew out through its eye. It does not always die when it must yield its store to the other, but revival comes again to its bosom, its breath is exalted; it creates a son, it is its own father.

This translation is printed as prose in its source edition.
