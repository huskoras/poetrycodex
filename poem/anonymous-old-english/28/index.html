Riddle XLVII. A Bookworm

by Anonymous (Old English)

Translated by Cosette Faust Newton & Stith Thompson (1918) · From Exeter Book Riddles (selection)

Arts & Sciences

A moth ate a word. To me that seemed
A curious happening when I heard of that wonder,
That a worm should swallow the word of a man,
A thief in the dark eat a thoughtful discourse
And the strong base it stood on. He stole, but he was not
A whit the wiser when the word had been swallowed.

Codex Note

One of roughly ninety riddles preserved in the Exeter Book; this is one of a small selection translated by Faust & Thompson. The manuscript gives no solutions — titles here follow the translators' own guesses.
