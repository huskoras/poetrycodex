Genesis 15:6: Father, in Abraham's steps I tread

by Charles Wesley 1707–1788

From Short Hymns on Select Passages of the Holy Scriptures (1762) · Osborn ed., vol. 9 (1870)

Religion

Father, in Abraham's steps I tread,
Receive Thine evangelic word,
Who gav'st to suffer in my stead
Thine only Son, my God and Lord:
The faith, which now I act on Thee,
Who didst again my Surety raise,
Is counted righteousness to me,
And I am saved through Jesu's grace.

Source Charles Wesley, The Poetical Works of John and Charles Wesley, Vol. IX (London: Wesleyan-Methodist Conference Office, 1870). Digital text: Internet Archive poeticalworksofj09wesl.
