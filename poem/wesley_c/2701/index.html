Deuteronomy 32:10: He found him in a desert land, &c

by Charles Wesley 1707–1788

From Short Hymns on Select Passages of the Holy Scriptures (1762) · Osborn ed., vol. 9 (1870)

Religion

He found me in a desert place,
Where far from God I roved.
And led mc through the legal maze.
And chasten'd whom He loved;
Kept as the apple of His eye,
With tenderest care caress'd,
And saved my soul, while sin was nigh.
And hid me in His breast.
