Religion

Hymns for Children, 85

by Charles Wesley 1707–1788

From Hymns for Children (1763) · Osborn ed., vol. 6 (1870)

Religion

Jesus His own disciples chid,
Who, out of false esteem,
The parents foolishly forbid
That brought their babes to Him.
Methinks even now I hear Him say,
In fervent charity,
"I will not have them kept away,
Bring all your babes to Me."
Though men our simpleness despise,
Our Saviour doth maintain
They must be small in their own eyes,
If they with us would reign.
Ta little ones, and not to men,
Is grace and glory given;
Children they must become again.
Or never enter heaven.

Source Charles Wesley, The Poetical Works of John and Charles Wesley, Vol. VI (of 13), ed. G. Osborn, D.D (London: Wesleyan-Methodist Conference Office, 1870). Digital text: Internet Archive poeticalworksofj0006wesl.
