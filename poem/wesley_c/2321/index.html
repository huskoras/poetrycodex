Prayer for the Rev. Mr. John Fletcher, June 30Th, 1776

by Charles Wesley 1707–1788

From Hymns and Poems, Chiefly Relating to Events in the Personal History of the Rev. Charles Wesley · Osborn ed., vol. 8 (1870)

Religion

I Jesus, Thy feeble servant see,
Sick is the man beloved by Thee:

Thy name to magnify.
To spread Thy gospel truths again.
His precious soul in life detain,
Nor suffer him to die.
Hymns and Poems. 41o

Source Charles Wesley, The Poetical Works of John and Charles Wesley, Vol. VIII (London: Wesleyan-Methodist Conference Office, 1870). Digital text: Internet Archive poeticalworksofj08wesl.
