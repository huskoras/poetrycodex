1 Samuel 30:5: Let earth and hell their powers engage

by Charles Wesley 1707–1788

From Short Hymns on Select Passages of the Holy Scriptures (1762) · Osborn ed., vol. 9 (1870)

Religion

Let earth and hell their powers engage.
And fierce temptations rise,
Above their impotence of rage
My soul to Jesus flies:
Louder than all their threats I hear
The voice of Love Divine,
Nor can I faint nor can I fear.
Who know that God is mine.

Source Charles Wesley, The Poetical Works of John and Charles Wesley, Vol. IX (London: Wesleyan-Methodist Conference Office, 1870). Digital text: Internet Archive poeticalworksofj09wesl.
