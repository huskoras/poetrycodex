Religion

John 5:28, 29: Marvel not at this: for the hour is coming, &c

by Charles Wesley 1707–1788

From Short Hymns on Select Passages of the Holy Scriptures (1762) · Osborn ed., vol. 11 (1871)

Religion

Cannot doubt the power Divine
Dead souls, or bodies dead to save;
Death oft hath heard that voice of Thine,
Heard from the bed, the bier, the grave:
That voice our mouldering dust again
Shall from earth's lowest centre hear,
And ocean pay its debt of man,
And all before Thy throne appear.

Source Charles Wesley, The Poetical Works of John and Charles Wesley, Vol. XI (of 13), ed. G. Osborn, D.D (London: Wesleyan-Methodist Conference Office, 1871). Digital text: Internet Archive poeticalworksofj0011wesl.
