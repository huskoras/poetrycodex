Acts 5:18: And laid... hands on the apostles, and put them, &c

by Charles Wesley 1707–1788

From Short Hymns on Select Passages of the Holy Scriptures (1762) · Osborn ed., vol. 12 (1872)

Religion

Bonds for ornaments we take
When our faith by bonds is tried:
Jesus bore them for our sake.

Bore, ennobled, sanctified:
Suffering for His cause alone
Confessors of Jesus' name
Sing we into dangers thrown,
(dory in our Master's shame.

Men should patiently endure,
Leave their Cod to act for them:

Bars and chains cannot secure
Whom He willeth to redeem:
Christ, whose might invincible
Burst the prison of the grave,
Christ who shuts and opens hell
Can He not from dungeons save?

Source Charles Wesley, ed. George Osborn, The Poetical Works of John and Charles Wesley, Reprinted from the Originals, with the…, Vol. XII, Collected edition in 13 vols., 1868-72 (Osborn) (London: Wesleyan-Methodist Conference Office, 1871). Digital text: Internet Archive poeticalworksofj12wesl.
