Religion

Matthew 1:1: Through earth the blessing spread

by Charles Wesley 1707–1788

From Short Hymns on Select Passages of the Holy Scriptures (1762) · Osborn ed., vol. 10 (1871)

Religion

Through earth the blessing spread:
Derived from AbraJiam s seed,
Abraham s promised Son and God,
God in us Thyself reveal,
Jesus, come, on all bestow'd,
All with grace and glory fill!
