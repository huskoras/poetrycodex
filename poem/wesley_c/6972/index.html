2 Timothy 4:7: Tuts blessed word be mine

by Charles Wesley 1707–1788

From Short Hymns on Select Passages of the Holy Scriptures (1762) · Osborn ed., vol. 13 (1872)

Religion

Tuts blessed word be mine,
Just as the port is gain'd,
"Kept by the power of grace Divine
I have the faith maintain'd!"
The' apostles of my Lord,
To whom it first was given,
They could not speak a greater word,
Nor all the saints in heaven.
