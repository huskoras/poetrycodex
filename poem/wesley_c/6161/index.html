Religion

Acts 1:26: When reason can no further go

by Charles Wesley 1707–1788

From Short Hymns on Select Passages of the Holy Scriptures (1762) · Osborn ed., vol. 12 (1872)

Religion

When reason can no further go,
And providential openings fail,
Dost Thou not, Lord, Thy counsel show,
Thy secret mind infallible,
To souls who in Thy ways stand still,
And dread to miss Thy perfect will?

In solemn doubts of import great,
We know, Thou wilt for us decide.
Thy people waiting at Thy feet
By sure unerring wisdom guide.
Dispose the lot, Thine own decree
To' explain, and clearly speak for Thee.

Source Charles Wesley, ed. George Osborn, The Poetical Works of John and Charles Wesley, Reprinted from the Originals, with the…, Vol. XII, Collected edition in 13 vols., 1868-72 (Osborn) (London: Wesleyan-Methodist Conference Office, 1871). Digital text: Internet Archive poeticalworksofj12wesl.
