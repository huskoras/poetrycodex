Song of Solomon 3:4: Hold Him with a trembling hand

by Charles Wesley 1707–1788

From Short Hymns on Select Passages of the Holy Scriptures (1762) · Osborn ed., vol. 9 (1870)

Religion

Hold Him with a trembling hand,
But till His face He show.
And bring me to the heavenly land,
I will not let Him go.
