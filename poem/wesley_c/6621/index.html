Religion

Acts 20: Nor need we mournfully complain

by Charles Wesley 1707–1788

From Short Hymns on Select Passages of the Holy Scriptures (1762) · Osborn ed., vol. 12 (1872)

Religion

Nor need we mournfully complain
O'erwhelm'd with grief so quickly o'er;

So soon we all shall meet again
Triumphant on the' eternal shore.
Bishop supreme, Thy people bless
With bishops after Thy own heart,
Who may, as vessels of Thy grace,
Blessings to all our souls impart,
Greatest, as ministers of all
Their apostolic function prove,
As genuine successors of Paul
In zeal, fidelity, and love.

Source Charles Wesley, ed. George Osborn, The Poetical Works of John and Charles Wesley, Reprinted from the Originals, with the…, Vol. XII, Collected edition in 13 vols., 1868-72 (Osborn) (London: Wesleyan-Methodist Conference Office, 1871). Digital text: Internet Archive poeticalworksofj12wesl.
