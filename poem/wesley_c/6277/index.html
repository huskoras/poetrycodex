Acts 7: Another king arose, which knew not Joseph

by Charles Wesley 1707–1788

From Short Hymns on Select Passages of the Holy Scriptures (1762) · Osborn ed., vol. 12 (1872)

Religion

Who is so great a God as ours!
He can His church sustain,
Without the help of earthly powers
The countenance of man:
Let worldly potentates disown,
We rest beneath His wings,
And know we are to Jesus known.
The sovereign King of kings.

Source Charles Wesley, ed. George Osborn, The Poetical Works of John and Charles Wesley, Reprinted from the Originals, with the…, Vol. XII, Collected edition in 13 vols., 1868-72 (Osborn) (London: Wesleyan-Methodist Conference Office, 1871). Digital text: Internet Archive poeticalworksofj12wesl.
