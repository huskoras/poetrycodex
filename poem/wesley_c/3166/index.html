Psalm 14:7: O That all the mournful nation

by Charles Wesley 1707–1788

From Short Hymns on Select Passages of the Holy Scriptures (1762) · Osborn ed., vol. 9 (1870)

Religion

O That all the mournful nation
Might with me. Taste and see
Jesus's salvation!

O that all who 7vould rely on
Jesu's love. Now might prove
Safety is in Sion /

Jesus from our sins shall save us,

When He frees our souls from prison,

All the gospel-season.

O THAT I could, in every place,
By faith behold Jehovah's face,
My strict Observer see;
Present my heart and reins to try,
And feel the influence of His eye
For ever fix'd on me!

Discerning Thee, my Saviour, stand
My Advocate at God's right hand,
I never shall remove;
I cannot fall, upheld by Thee,
Or sin against the majesty
Of omnipresent Love.

Now, Saviour, now appear, appear,
And let me always see Thee near.
And know as I am known:
My spirit to Thyself unite.
And bear me through a sea of light
To that eternal throne.

Source Charles Wesley, The Poetical Works of John and Charles Wesley, Vol. IX (London: Wesleyan-Methodist Conference Office, 1870). Digital text: Internet Archive poeticalworksofj09wesl.
