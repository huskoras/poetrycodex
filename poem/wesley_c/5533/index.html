Religion

John 4:31: His disciples prayed Him, saying. Master, eat

by Charles Wesley 1707–1788

From Short Hymns on Select Passages of the Holy Scriptures (1762) · Osborn ed., vol. 11 (1871)

Religion

Jksus keeps the soul in view
His converted messenger,
Doth in mind and heart pursue,
Fills with zeal and acts in her,
Acts on those to whom He sends,
Those He for His Father seeks;
Still upon her tongue attends,
Blesses every word she speaks.

Souls to Avin is Jesu's meat;
Jesus thus instructs His own
Nature's cravings to forget,
Living not by bread alone:
Preachers by the gospel live,
Feed on that themselves dispense,
Strength and nourishment receive,
More than life derive from thence.

Source Charles Wesley, The Poetical Works of John and Charles Wesley, Vol. XI (of 13), ed. G. Osborn, D.D (London: Wesleyan-Methodist Conference Office, 1871). Digital text: Internet Archive poeticalworksofj0011wesl.
