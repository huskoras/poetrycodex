Jeremiah 3:14, 15: The promise we for Israel plead

by Charles Wesley 1707–1788

From Short Hymns on Select Passages of the Holy Scriptures (1762) · Osborn ed., vol. 10 (1871)

Religion

The promise we for Israel plead:
O that the once-beloved seed
Back to their Lord might come!
Now bid them look on Thee, and mourn;
Where'er dispersed, collect, and turn,
And bring Thy wanderers home.

Yo Jews the gospel-faith impart,
And pastors after Thine own heart,
Thine ancient flock to feed
With knowledge of the Crucified,
The God who by their malice died,
And suffer'd in their stead.

Source Charles Wesley, collected and arranged by G. Osborn, The Poetical Works of John and Charles Wesley, Reprinted from the Originals, with the…, Vol. X (of 13) (London: Wesleyan-Methodist Conference Office (2 Castle Street, City Road; sold at 66 Paternoster Row), 1871). Digital text: Internet Archive poeticalworksofj10wesl.
