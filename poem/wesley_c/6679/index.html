Religion

Acts: A judge so late, he quits his place

by Charles Wesley 1707–1788

From Short Hymns on Select Passages of the Holy Scriptures (1762) · Osborn ed., vol. 12 (1872)

Religion

A judge so late, he quits his place,
(Oh how contemptible and base,
When malice stoops so low!)
See the high priest informer turns,
So fierce his righteous fury burns
inst die church's too.

In ours, in every age are seen
Elders and venerable men
Who sacred things abuse,
Men with infernal malice fraught,
Men in the school of Satan taught
The brethren to accuse.

Swift are the steps of angry zeal,
When bigots set on fire of hell
Their utmost powers employ,
To' excite the world against the just,
Hunt clown the men who Jesus trust,
And innocents destroy.

Source Charles Wesley, ed. George Osborn, The Poetical Works of John and Charles Wesley, Reprinted from the Originals, with the…, Vol. XII, Collected edition in 13 vols., 1868-72 (Osborn) (London: Wesleyan-Methodist Conference Office, 1871). Digital text: Internet Archive poeticalworksofj12wesl.
