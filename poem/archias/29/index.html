Apollo and Marsyas (2) (Mackail)

by Archias

Translated by J. W. Mackail (1890) · From Select Epigrams from the Greek Anthology (1890)

Arts & Sciences

Thou hangest high where the winds lash thy wild body, O wretched one, swinging from a shaggy pine; thou hangest high, for thou didst stand up to strife against Phoebus, O Satyr, dweller on the cliff of Celaenae; and we nymphs shall no longer as before hear the honey-sounding cry of thy flute on the Phrygian hills.

This translation is printed as prose in its source edition.
