Time & Brevity

LXXXVI

by Edward FitzGerald

From The Rubaiyat of Omar Khayyam

Time & Brevity

After a momentary silence spake
Some Vessel of a more ungainly Make;
"They sneer at me for leaning all awry:
What! did the Hand then of the Potter shake?"
