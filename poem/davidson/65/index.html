Death & Dying

The Unresigned Mourner

by John Davidson 1857–1909

From The Last Ballad and Other Poems (1899)

Death & Dying

Unwilling tears on silken lashes,
Sighs and lamentations deep!
Why do you sit in dust and ashes,
Lady, lady, why do you weep?

"Because, although my soul that hastened
To welcome love is now bereft
Of happiness, I live unchastened,
And curse the bitter anguish left."
