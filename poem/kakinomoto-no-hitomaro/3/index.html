Love

On Parting from His Wife

by Kakinomoto no Hitomaro fl. c. 680–700

Translated by Clara A. Walsh (1910) · From The Master-Singers of Japan

Love

Not yet, O Hill! high hill of Autumn scatter
Red leaves and gold athwart the distant view.
Let me gaze on, a little instant longer,
Where she I love leans toward me through the blue!

Source Clara A. Walsh, trans., The Master-Singers of Japan, Being Verse Translations from the Japanese Poets (The Wisdom of the East Series) (London: John Murray, 1910). Digital text: sacred-texts msj/index.htm.
