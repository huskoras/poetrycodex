MYS 2862: 山川の水隠に生ふる (Yamagawa no migomori ni ouru)

by Kakinomoto no Hitomaro fl. c. 680–700

From 万葉集 (Man'yōshū), after Saitō Mokichi, 万葉秀歌

Love

山川の水隠に生ふる山菅の止まずも妹が思ほゆるかも

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

From the Hitomaro Collection (人麻呂歌集); attribution uncertain.
