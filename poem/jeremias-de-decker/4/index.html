Death & Dying

Learning and Wisdom

by Jeremias de Decker 1609–1666

Translated by John Bowring and Harry S. van Dyk (1824) · From Batavian Anthology (1824)

Death & Dying

To know is good, I own—to do is better still:
Him who knows much and well—call learned if you will;
But there are seasons oft when he shall win the prize,
And wear it proudly too—whose deeds, not words, are wise.
