Religion

Advent shadows gather deep

by Frances Ridley Havergal 1836–1879

From Loyal Responses

Religion

Advent shadows gather deep,
Wars and desolations,
Troubled wakings, troubled sleep,
Rushing of the nations.
Advent glory, grand and clear,
Herald flashes flingeth ;
And the Judge who draweth near,
Full salvation bringeth.

Source Frances Ridley Havergal, with a prefatory note by Maria V. G. Havergal, The Poetical Works of Frances Ridley Havergal. Author's Authorized Edition, Author's Authorized Edition (library edition in one volume) (Toronto: Toronto Willard Tract Depôt (cor. Yonge and Temperance Streets), n.d).
