As the sounding shell conveys

by Frances Ridley Havergal 1836–1879

From Birthday Poems

Relationships

As the sounding shell conveys
The murmur of the sea,
So let this tiny token raise
Some memory of me ;
For loving thought of prayer and praise
Fail not to rise for thee.

Source Frances Ridley Havergal; prefatory note by Maria V. G. Havergal, The Poetical Works of Frances Ridley Havergal, Author's Authorized Edition (Toronto: Toronto Willard Tract Depot).
