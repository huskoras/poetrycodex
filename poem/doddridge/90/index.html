Religion

XCI. Laying hold on GOD'S Strength, that we may be at Peace with him.

by Philip Doddridge

From Hymns founded on various Texts in the Holy Scriptures (1755)

Religion

THUS saith Jehovah from his Seat,
"Who shall presume my Wrath to meet?
"What Rebel-Men or Angels dare
"To wage with me unequal War?

"Close let the Thorns and Briars stand
"In thick Array on either Hand;
"Forth shall my flaming Terrors fly;
"At once they kindle, blaze and dye.

"Presumptuous Sinners, yet be wise
"E'er this o'erwhelming Ruin rise;
"Your vain tumultuous Efforts cease,
"And seek in suppliant Crouds for Peace".

Great GOD, we bless the gentle Sound,
And bow submissive to the Ground;
Thy prostrate Foes let Pity raise,
And form a People to thy Praise.

His thund'ring Storms are silent now;
Calm are the Terrors of his Brow,
Since Jesus makes the Father known,
Our guardian Shield, our chearing Sun.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Text: Isaiah xxvii. 5.
