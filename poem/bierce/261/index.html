An Enemy to Law and Order

by Ambrose Bierce

From Shapes of Clay (1903)

History & Politics

A is defrauded of his land by B,
Who's driven from the premises by C.
D buys the place with coin of plundered E.
"That A's an Anarchist!" says F to G.
