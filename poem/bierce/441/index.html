Death & Dying

Like a cold fish escaping from its tank

by Ambrose Bierce

From Black Beetles in Amber (1892)

Death & Dying

Like a cold fish escaping from its tank,
Hence fled the soul of Joe Russel, crank.
He cried: "Cold water!" roaring like a beast.
'Twas thrown upon him and the music ceased.
