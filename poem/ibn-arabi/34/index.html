Love

Three full moons

by Ibn al-Arabi 1165–1240

Translated by Reynold A. Nicholson (1911) · From The Tarjuman al-Ashwaq (Nicholson, 1911)

Love

Three full moons, unadorned by any ornament, went forth to at-Tan‘ím with veiled faces.
They unveiled shining faces like suns and cried with a loud voice 'Labbayka', visiting the holy shrines.
And they approached, walking slowly as the qaṭá birds walk, in gowns of striped Yemen cloth.
