Love

A Song, "Miranda hides her"

by Anne Finch, Countess of Winchilsea 1661–1720

From The Poems of Anne Countess of Winchilsea, ed. Myra Reynolds (1903)

Love

Miranda, hides her from the Sun,
Beneath those shady beaches nigh,
Whilst I, by her bright rayes undone,
Can no where for refreshment fly.
In that fair grove, att height of noone,
His fiercest glorys, she defies ;
I have alas! such shelter, none,
No safe umbrella, 'gainst her eyes.
Thus, does th' unequal hand of fate
Refuse itts' favours to devide,
Giving to her, a safe retreat,
And all ofensive arms beside.
