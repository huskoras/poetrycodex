Sama Veda II.6.3.8: We call upon Sarasvan as unmarried men who

by The Rishis of the Sama Veda c. 1200–1000 BCE (traditional; uncertain)

Translated by Ralph T. H. Griffith (1895) · From Hymns of the Samaveda (1895)

Religion

1. We call upon Sarasvan as unmarried men who long for wives,
As bounteous men who yearn for sons.
