Sama Veda II.4.1.11: For Indra girt by Maruts, flow, thou Indu

by The Rishis of the Sama Veda c. 1200–1000 BCE (traditional; uncertain)

Translated by Ralph T. H. Griffith (1895) · From Hymns of the Samaveda (1895)

Religion

1. For Indra girt by Maruts, flow, thou Indu, very rich in meath,
To seat thee in the place of song!

2. Sage: who know the lore of speech deck thee, the strong sustainer, well:
Men make thee bright and beautiful.

3. Let Mitra, Varuna, Aryaman drink Pavamana's juice, yea, thine.
Sage! let the Maruts drink thereof.

Source Ralph T. H. Griffith, trans., The Hymns of the Samaveda, Translated with a Popular Commentary (Benares: E. J. Lazarus and Co, 1893). Digital text: sacred-texts hin/sv.
