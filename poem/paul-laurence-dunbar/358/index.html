Love

Love's Draft

by Paul Laurence Dunbar 1872–1906

Love

The draft of love was cool and sweet
You gave me in the cup,
But, ah, love's fire is keen and fleet,
And I am burning up.

Unless the tears I shed for you
Shall quench this burning flame,
It will consume me through and through,
And leave but ash--a name.

Source Paul Laurence Dunbar, The Complete Poems of Paul Laurence Dunbar, with the Introduction to "Lyrics of Lowly Life" by W (New York: Dodd, Mead and Company, 1922). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 18338.
