Nature

Song

by Bliss Carman 1861–1929

From Lyra Celtica (1924)

Nature

Love, by that loosened hair
Well now I know
Where the lost Lilith went
So long ago.

Love, by those starry eyes
I understand
How the sea-maidens lure
Mortals from land.

Love, by that welling laugh
Joy claims his own
Sea-born and wind-wayward
Child of the sun.

Source E. A. Sharp and J. Matthay, with introduction and notes by William Sharp, ed., Lyra Celtica: An Anthology of Representative Celtic Poetry (Ancient Irish, Alban, Gaelic…, Second edition (revised and enlarged); first edition 1896 (The Celtic Library) (Edinburgh: John Grant, 1924). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 64260.
