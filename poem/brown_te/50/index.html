Dante and Ariosto

by Thomas Edward Brown

From Aspects and Characters

Arts & Sciences

If Dante breathes on me his awful breath,
I rise and go; but I am sad as death —
I go; but, turning, who is that I see?
I whisper: — " Ariosto, wait for me! "
