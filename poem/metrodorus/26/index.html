Arts & Sciences

Book XIV, 144

by Metrodorus

Translated by W. R. Paton (1916–18) · From The Greek Anthology (Loeb, vol. V, 1918)

Arts & Sciences

A. How heavy is the base I stand on together with myself! B. And my base together with myself weighs the same number of talents. A. But I alone weigh twice as much as your base. B. And I alone weigh three times the weight of yours.

This translation is printed as prose in its source edition.
