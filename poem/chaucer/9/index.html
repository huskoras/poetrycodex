Living

What shul thise clothes many-fold,
Lo! this hote somers day?--
After greet heet cometh cold;
No man caste his pilche away.

Of al this world the wyde compas
Hit wol not in myn armes tweyne.--
Who-so mochel wol embrace
Litel therof he shal distreyne.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Two four-line proverbs ascribed to Chaucer in the manuscripts, one on summer heat and the cold that follows, one on over-reaching: ‘Who-so mochel wol embrace / Litel therof he shal distreyne’. Text from W. W. Skeat’s Oxford edition (1899).
