The Misanthrope

by Johann Wolfgang von Goethe 1749–1832

Translated by Edgar Alfred Bowring (1853) · From Songs

Living

AT first awhile sits he,
With calm, unruffled brow;
His features then I see,
Distorted hideously,--
An owl's they might be now.

What is it, askest thou?
Is't love, or is't ennui?
'Tis both at once, I vow.

Source Johann Wolfgang von Goethe, The Poems of Goethe, Translated in the Original Metres, trans. Edgar Alfred Bowring, Second edition (London: George Bell and Sons, 1874). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 1287.
