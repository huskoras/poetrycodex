Roman Elegy 3: Alexander, and Caesar, and Henry, and Fred'rick, the mighty

by Johann Wolfgang von Goethe 1749–1832

Translated by Edgar Alfred Bowring (1853) · From Elegies

Love

ALEXANDER, and Caesar, and Henry, and Fred'rick, the mighty,
On me would gladly bestow half of the glory they earn'd,
Could I but grant unto each one night on the couch where I'm lying;
But they, by Orcus's night, sternly, alas! are held down.
Therefore rejoice, oh thou living one, blest in thy love-lighted homestead,
Ere the dark Lethe's sad wave wetteth thy fugitive foot.

Source Johann Wolfgang von Goethe, The Poems of Goethe, Translated in the Original Metres, trans. Edgar Alfred Bowring, Second edition (London: George Bell and Sons, 1874). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 1287. (details from catalogue records, not yet checked against the title page)
