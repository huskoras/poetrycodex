Death & Dying

Epitaph.

by Sir John Suckling

Death & Dying

Here lies Don Alonzo,
Slain by a wound received under
His left Pappe,
The Orifice of which was so
Small, no Chirurgion could
Discover it.
Reader,
If thou wouldst avoid so strange
A Death,
Look not upon Lucinda'es eyes.
