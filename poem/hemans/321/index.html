Nature

The Bell at Sea

by Felicia Hemans 1793–1835

From Miscellaneous Poems

Nature

When the tide’s billowy swell
Had reach’d its height,
Then toll’d the rock’s lone bell
Sternly by night.

Far over cliff and surge
Swept the deep sound,
Making each wild wind’s dirge
Still more profound.

Yet that funereal tone
The sailor bless’d,
Steering through darkness on
With fearless breast.

E’en so may we, that float
On life’s wide sea,
Welcome each warning note,
Stern though it be!

Source Felicia Hemans, The Poems of Felicia Hemans: Complete Copyright Edition (London and Edinburgh: William P. Nimmo, 1875). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 66785.
