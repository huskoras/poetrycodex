Love

On one whose days were labour and sorrow (Fragment 120) (Way)

by Sappho

Translated by Arthur S. Way (1920) · From Sappho and the Vigil of Venus (1920)

Love

O'er the fisherman Pelagon here by Meniskus his father are planted
His oar and his weel, for memorials of days ever travail-haunted.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Way weaves several fragments in the same metre into one connected poem (his preface); the joins are his.
