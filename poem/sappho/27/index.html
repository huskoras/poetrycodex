Fragment 31

by Sappho c. 630 – c. 570 BC

Translated by Henry Thornton Wharton (1885) · From Fragments

Relationships

Leto and Niobe were friends full dear.

Source Sappho, Sappho: Memoir, Text, Selected Renderings, and a Literal Translation, trans. Henry Thornton Wharton, Fifth edition (London (and New York): John Lane, The Bodley Head, 1908). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 57390.
