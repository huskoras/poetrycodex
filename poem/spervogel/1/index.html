The Hedgehog

by Spervogel fl. c. 1170–1190

Translated by Frank C. Nicholson (1907) · From Old German Love Songs (1907)

Living

What the hedgehog said is known:
"There's nought like quarters of one's own."
Kerling, build a house and store
All thy gear within the door!
The lords are all grown greedy.
Whoever has not hearth and home,
How often is he hard bestead and needy!
