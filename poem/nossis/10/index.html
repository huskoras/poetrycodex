Arts & Sciences

Book IX, 604

by Nossis fl. c. 300 BC

Translated by W. R. Paton (1916–18) · From The Greek Anthology (Loeb, vol. III, 1917)

Arts & Sciences

This is the picture of Thaumareta. Well did the painter render the bearing and the beauty of the gentle-eyed lady! Thy little house-dog would fawn upon thee if it saw thee here, thinking that it looked on the mistress of its home.

This translation is printed as prose in its source edition.

Source Various epigrammatists, The Greek Anthology, with an English Translation by W. R. Paton, III (Book IX: The Declamatory Epigrams), trans. W. R. Paton (The Loeb Classical Library) (London; New York: William Heinemann; G. P. Putnam's Sons, 1917). Digital text: Internet Archive greekanthology0003wrpa.
