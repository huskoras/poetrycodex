Sorrento

by Frederick Locker-Lampson 1821–1895

From A Selection from the Works of Frederick Locker (1865)

Social Commentaries

Sorrento! Love's Star! Land
Of myrtle and vine,
I come from a far land
To kneel at thy shrine;
Thy brows wear a garland,
Oh, weave one for mine!

Thine image, fair city,
Smiles fair in the sea,--
A youth sings a pretty
Song, tempered with glee,--
The mirth and the ditty
Are mournful to me.

Ah, sea boy, how strange is
The carol you sing!
Let Psyche, who ranges
The gardens of Spring,
Remember the changes
December will bring.

Source Frederick Locker, A Selection from the Works of Frederick Locker, with Illustrations by Richard Doyle (Moxon's Miniature Poets) (London: Edward Moxon & Co, 1865). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 38463.
