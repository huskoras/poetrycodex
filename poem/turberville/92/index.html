Of the fond discord of the two Theban brothers, Octocles and Polynices

by George Turberville c. 1540–c. 1610

From Epitaphes, Epigrams, Songs and Sonets (1567)

Mythology & Folklore

IN death you part the fire,
you cut the cruell flame:
If so you had deuided Thebes
you might enioyde the same.
