Living

Approves my brother Laias this intent?

by Matthew Arnold

Living

_AEpytus_

Yes, and alone is with me here to share.

_Merope_

And what of thine Arcadian mate, who bears
Suspicion from thy grandsire of thy death,
For whom, as I suppose, thou passest here?

_AEpytus_
