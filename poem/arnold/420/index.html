Living

And patiently exact

by Matthew Arnold

Living

This universal God
Alike to any act
Proceeds at any nod,
And quietly declaims the cursings of himself.

This is not what man hates,
Yet he can curse but this.
Harsh Gods and hostile Fates
Are dreams! this only _is_--
Is everywhere; sustains the wise, the foolish elf.
