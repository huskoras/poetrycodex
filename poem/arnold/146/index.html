Living

Thanks, gracious one!

by Matthew Arnold

Living

Ah, the sweet fumes again!
More soft, ah me,
More subtle-winding
That Pan's flute-music!
Faint--faint! Ah me,
Again the sweet sleep!

_Circe_

Hist! Thou--within there!
Come forth, Ulysses!
Art tired with hunting?
While we range the woodland,
See what the day brings.

_Ulysses_
