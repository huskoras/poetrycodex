Death & Dying

Remember, to revenge his death I come!

by Matthew Arnold

Death & Dying

_Merope_

Ah ... revenge! _ant._
That word! it kills me! I see
Once more roll back on my house,
Never to ebb, the accurst
All-flooding ocean of blood.

_AEpytus_
