Uncle Joe

by Harry Graham 1874–1936

From Ruthless Rhymes for Heartless Homes (1902)

Social Commentaries

An Angel bore dear Uncle Joe
To rest beyond the stars.
I miss him, oh! I miss him so,--
He had such good cigars.
