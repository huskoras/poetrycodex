Nature

Mannahatta

by Walt Whitman

From Leaves of Grass

Nature

My city’s fit and noble name resumed,
Choice aboriginal name, with marvellous beauty, meaning,
A rocky founded island--shores where ever gayly dash the coming,
going, hurrying sea waves.
