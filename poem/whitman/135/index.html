Religion

Hast Never Come to Thee an Hour

by Walt Whitman

Religion

Hast never come to thee an hour,
A sudden gleam divine, precipitating, bursting all these bubbles,
fashions, wealth?
These eager business aims--books, politics, art, amours,
To utter nothingness?
