On the Bust of Helen by Canova

by Lord Byron 1788–1824

From Poems 1816–1823

Arts & Sciences

In this belovéd marble view
Above the works and thoughts of Man,
What Nature could but would not do,
And Beauty and Canova can!
Beyond Imagination's power,
Beyond the Bard's defeated art,
With Immortality her dower,
Behold the Helen of the heart.

Source Lord Byron, The Works of Lord Byron: A New, Revised and Enlarged Edition, with Illustrations. Poetry, IV, ed. Ernest Hartley Coleridge (London / New York: John Murray; Charles Scribner's Sons, 1901). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 20158.
