Death & Dying

The Quatrain that Villon Made when He Was Doomed to Die

by François Villon 1431–after 1463

Translated by John Payne (1878; Mosher edition, 1909) · From Divers Poems

Death & Dying

FRANÇOIS am I, — woe worth it me!
At Paris born, near Pontoise citie,
Whose neck, in the bight of a rope of three,
Must prove how heavy my buttocks be.
