Nature

An Inquiry

by Oliver Herford 1863–1935

From The Kitten's Garden of Verses (1911)

Nature

A Birdie cocked his little head,
Winked his eye at me and said,
"Say, are you a Pussy Willer,
Or just a Kitty-Catty pillar?"

Source Oliver Herford, The Kitten's Garden of Verses (New York: Charles Scribner's Sons, 1911). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 23433.
