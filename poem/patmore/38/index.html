Religion

Magna Est Veritas

by Coventry Patmore 1823–1896

From The Unknown Eros (1890), Book I

Religion

Here, in this little Bay,
Full of tumultuous life and great repose,
Where, twice a day,
The purposeless, glad ocean comes and goes,
Under high cliffs, and far from the huge town,
I sit me down.
For want of me the world's course will not fail:
When all its work is done, the lie shall rot;
The truth is great, and shall prevail,
When none cares whether it prevail or not.

Source Coventry Patmore, The Unknown Eros, Third edition (London: George Bell and Sons, 1890). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 13672. (details from catalogue records, not yet checked against the title page)
