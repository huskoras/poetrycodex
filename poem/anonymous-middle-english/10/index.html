Death & Dying

Erthe toc of erthe

by Anonymous (Middle English)

From Harley Lyrics (British Library, MS Harley 2253)

Death & DyingTime & Brevity

Erþe toc of erþe erþe wyþ woh,
Erþe oþer erþe to þe erþe droh,
Erþe leyde erþe in erþene þroh,
Þo heuede erþe of erþe erþe ynoh.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

The earliest English version of the ‘Erthe upon erthe’ memento mori, four lines copied about 1307: earth took earth with woe, earth laid earth in an earthen trough, and then had earth enough. Later versions grow into long moral poems. Text from Hilda M. R. Murray, Erthe upon Erthe (Early English Text Society, 1911).
