Mirie It Is While Sumer Ilast

by Anonymous (Middle English)

NatureTime & Brevity

Mirie it is while sumer ilast
With fugheles song;
Oc nu necheth windes blast
And weder strong.
Ei, ei, what this nicht is long!
And ich with wel michel wrong
Soregh and murne and fast.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Seven lines on a summer ended and a winter night that is too long, dated about 1225: among the earliest secular songs in English. Text from E. K. Chambers and F. Sidgwick, Early English Lyrics (1907).
