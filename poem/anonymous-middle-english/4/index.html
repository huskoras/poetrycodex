Love

Now Springs the Spray

by Anonymous (Middle English)

From Lincoln’s Inn MS Hale 135

LoveNature

Nou sprinkes þe sprai,
Al for loue icche am so seek
Þat slepen I ne mai.

Als I me rode þis endre dai
O mi playinge,
Seih I hwar a litel mai
Bigan to singge:
'Þe clot him clingge!
Wai es him i louue-longinge
Sal libben ai!'
Nou sprinkes, &c.

Son icche herde þat mirie note,
Þider I drogh;
I fonde hire in an herber swot
Vnder a bogh,
With ioie inogh.
Son I asked: 'Þou mirie mai,
Hwi sinkestou ai?'
Nou sprinkes, &c.

Þan answerde þat maiden swote
Midde wordes fewe:
'Mi lemman me haues bihot
Of louue trewe:
He chaunges anewe.
Yiif I mai, it shal him rewe
Bi þis dai.'
Nou sprinkes, &c.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

A spring song with a refrain, written about 1300: riding out one day, the poet overhears a jilted girl vowing that her false lover will regret it. Text from Kenneth Sisam, Fourteenth Century Verse & Prose (Oxford, 1921).
