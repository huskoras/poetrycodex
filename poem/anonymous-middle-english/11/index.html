Icham of Irlaunde

by Anonymous (Middle English)

Arts & Sciences

Icham of Irlaunde,
Ant of the holy londe
Of Irlande.
Gode sire, pray ich þe,
For of saynte charité,
Come ant daunce wyt me
In Irlaunde.

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Seven lines in which a dancer from Ireland begs a gentleman, ‘for seynte charité’, to dance with her — a minstrel’s song written on a flyleaf of a Bodleian manuscript. Text from Kenneth Sisam, Fourteenth Century Verse & Prose (Oxford, 1921).
