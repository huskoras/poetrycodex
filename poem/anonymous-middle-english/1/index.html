Nature

Sumer Is Icumen In

by Anonymous (Middle English)

From Reading Abbey manuscript (British Library, Harley 978)

Nature

Sumer is icumen in,
Lhude sing cuccu!
Groweth sed, and bloweth med,
And springth the wude nu--
Sing cuccu!

Awe bleteth after lomb,
Lhouth after calve cu;
Bulluc sterteth, bucke verteth,
Murie sing cuccu!

Cuccu, cuccu, well singes thu, cuccu:
Ne swike thu naver nu;
Sing cuccu, nu, sing cuccu,
Sing cuccu, sing cuccu, nu!

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

The ‘Cuckoo Song’, copied at Reading Abbey in the thirteenth century with music for a round in four voices over a two-voice ground. Only the English words are given here. Text from Quiller-Couch’s Oxford Book of English Verse (1900).
