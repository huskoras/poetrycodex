MYS 3570: 葦の葉に夕霧立ちて (Ashi no ha ni yūgiri tachite)

by Anonymous (Man'yōshū) 7th–8th century

From 万葉集 (Man'yōshū), after Saitō Mokichi, 万葉秀歌

Relationships

葦の葉に夕霧立ちて鴨が音の寒き夕へし汝をば偲はむ

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Sakimori-uta (frontier guard's song), Book 14. 題詞: 防人の歌
