MYS 2517: たらちねの母に障らば (Tarachine no haha ni sawaraba)

by Anonymous (Man'yōshū) 7th–8th century

From 万葉集 (Man'yōshū), after Saitō Mokichi, 万葉秀歌

Love

たらちねの母に障らばいたづらに汝も吾も事成るべしや

✦ ✦ ✦

Codex Note

Anonymous love poem (相聞), Book 11.
