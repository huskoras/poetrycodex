Doomed

by Gerald Massey 1828–1907

From The Poetical Works (1861)

Social Commentaries

As the White Snow crowns the Hills, and the arms
of Ether fills,
With the lustre of its loveliness — a presence as of
light,
And it looks up in Heaven's face with all a Virgin's
trusting grace:

So the Maiden walkt on Purity's white height.
But the Snow will blush for bliss, at the red Dawn's
fervent kiss;

And fall from its high throne, and lose the bright-
ness from its brow;
And be trodden on the. high ways, and be trampled in
the bye ways:
So the Maiden's life is stain'd and trampled now.
