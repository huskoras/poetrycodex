Relationships

To the Rev. Mr. Newton, on His Return from Ramsgate

by William Cowper 1731–1800

From Poems

Relationships

That ocean you have late survey'd,
Those rocks I too have seen;
But I, afflicted and dismay'd,
You, tranquil and serene.

You from the flood-controlling steep
Saw stretch'd before your view,
With conscious joy, the threatening deep,
No longer such to you.

To me the waves, that ceaseless broke
Upon the dangerous coast,
Hoarsely and ominously spoke
Of all my treasure lost.

Your sea of troubles you have past,
And found the peaceful shore;
I, tempest-toss'd, and wreck'd at last,
Come home to port no more.

Source William Cowper, The Works of William Cowper, ed. T. S. Grimshawe, Complete in one volume (London: William Tegg and Co, 1849). Digital text: Project Gutenberg 47790.
