To a Babe

by Alexander Pushkin

Translated by Ivan Panin (1888) · From Poems (Panin, 1888)

Relationships

Child, I dare not over thee
Pronounce a blessing;
Thou art of consolation a quiet angel
May then happy be thy lot....
