Religion

Evening Hymn (Twilight is stealing)

by Cecil Frances Alexander 1818–1895

From Poems (1896)

Religion

Twilight is stealing
O'er hill and lea,
Vesper bells pealing
Call us to Thee.

Low at Thy mercy seat,
Father, Thy children meet;
Craving Thy guidance sweet,
Bend we the knee.

Lowly confessing
Trespass and shame,
Loudly expressing
Faith in Thy name.

Praising with one accord,
Hearing Thy Holy Word,
Here, let our service, Lord,
Be without blame.

Source Cecil Frances Alexander, Poems, ed. William Alexander (London; New York: Macmillan and Co., Ltd.; Macmillan & Co, 1896). Digital text: Internet Archive poemsfra0alex.
