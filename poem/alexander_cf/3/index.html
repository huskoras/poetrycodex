Religion

There is a Green Hill far Away

by Cecil Frances Alexander 1818–1895

From Poems (1896)

Religion

There is a green hill far away,
Without a city wall,
Where the dear Lord was crucified
Who died to save us all.

We may not know, we cannot tell
What pains He had to bear,
But we believe it was for us
He hung and suffer'd there.

He died that we might be forgiven,
He died to make as good;
That we might go at last to Heav'n,
Saved by His precious Blood.

There was no other good enough
To pay the price of sin;
He only could unlock the gate
Of Heav'n, and let us in.

Oh, dearly, dearly has He loved,
And we must love Him too,
And trust in His redeeming Blood,
And try His works to do. Amen.

Source Cecil Frances Alexander, Poems, ed. William Alexander (London; New York: Macmillan and Co., Ltd.; Macmillan & Co, 1896). Digital text: Internet Archive poemsfra0alex.
