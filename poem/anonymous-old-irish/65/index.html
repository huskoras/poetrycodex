Nature

First Winter-Song

by Anonymous (Old Irish)

Translated by Alfred Perceval Graves (in Hull, The Poem-Book of the Gael, 1913) · From The Poem-Book of the Gael (1913)

Nature

Take my tidings!
Stags contend;
Snows descend--
Summer's end!

A chill wind raging;
The sun low keeping,
Swift to set
O'er seas high sweeping.

Dull red the fern;
Shapes are shadows:
Wild geese mourn
O'er misty meadows.

Keen cold limes
Each weaker wing.
Icy times--
Such I sing!
Take my tidings!
