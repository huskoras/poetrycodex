Religion

The Rune of St Patrick

by Anonymous (Old Irish) c. 8th–12th century

Translated by Charles Mangan (in Lyra Celtica, 1896) · From Lyra Celtica (1924)

Religion

“The Faedh Fiada”; or, “The Cry of the Deer.”

At Tara to-day in this fateful hour
I place all Heaven with its power,
And the sun with its brightness,
And the snow with its whiteness,
And fire with all the strength it hath,
And lightning with its rapid wrath,
And the winds with their swiftness along their path,
And the sea with its deepness,
And the rocks with their steepness,
And the earth with its starkness:
All these I place,
By God’s almighty help and grace,
Between myself and the powers of darkness.
