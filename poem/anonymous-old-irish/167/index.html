Death & Dying

The First Elegy

by Anonymous (Old Irish) c. 8th–12th century

Translated by George Sigerson (1907) · From Bards of the Gael and Gall (1907)

Death & Dying

Sate we sole, in cliff-bower —
Chill winds shower —
I tremble yet — shock of dread
Sped death's power.

The tale I tell: fate has felled
Fail most fine.
She a man, bare, beheld,
In sun shine,

Shock of death, death's dread power,
Lowered fell fete,
Bare I came, hence her shame,
Stilled she sate.
