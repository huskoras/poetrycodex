Eduardo Pondal

1835–1917

Eduardo Pondal Abente (Ponteceso, A Coruña, 1835 – A Coruña, 1917) was a Galician poet of the Rexurdimento, the nineteenth-century revival of literature in the Galician language. He trained as a physician in Santiago de Compostela, where he moved in the circle of the Galician revival.

His book Queixumes dos pinos (“Complaints of the Pines”), printed at A Coruña in 1886, gathers his Galician poems of the Bergantiños country, the Celtic past and the bard’s lonely wandering. His later Os Eoas and the hymn “Os pinos” (set to music by Pascual Veiga) made him the author of the text of the Galician anthem.
