Nicarchus

1st century AD (Paton’s date)

Nicarchus is one of the epigrammatists whose short poems were gathered in the Greek Anthology. Paton's index of authors dates the poet to 1st century AD.

This archive carries the 5 epigrams ascribed to the poet in the first volume of W. R. Paton's prose translation (Books I–VI) (Book V: 38, 39, 40; Book VI: 31, 285).
