Romantic

Reginald Heber

1783–1826

Reginald Heber (1783–1826) was an English Anglican bishop of Calcutta, a man of letters and a hymn-writer.

The poems here are taken from Alfred H. Miles (ed.), The Poets and the Poetry of the Century (London: Hutchinson, 1891–1897), vol. 10, from the Internet Archive scans of that edition. Spelling is that of the anthology; Miles's biographical essay and extracts from longer poems are omitted, and only poems that could be read cleanly from the scan are included. Further poems come from The Poetical Works of Reginald Heber, D.D. (London and New York: Frederick Warne, The Lansdowne Poets).
