Subramania Bharati

1882–1921

Subramania Bharati (Chinnaswami Subramania Bharati, 11 December 1882 – 11 September 1921) was a Tamil poet, journalist and nationalist, born at Ettayapuram in the south of Tamil Nadu. He worked on the Tamil daily Swadesamitran, and in 1908, to escape arrest for his part in the nationalist agitation, he moved to Pondicherry, then under French rule, where he lived in exile for about a decade and edited the weekly India.

His songs of freedom, his hymns to Kannan (Krishna) and the Mother, his long narrative Panchali Sapatham and the dream-allegory Kuyil Pattu brought Tamil poetry out of its older courtly manner and into the speech of the street, the school and the meeting. He returned to British India in 1918, was briefly imprisoned, and died in Madras in 1921, aged thirty-eight. Tamil readers know him as Mahakavi, the great poet.
