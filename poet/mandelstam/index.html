Russian & Slavic

Osip Mandelstam

1891–1938

Осип Эмильевич Мандельштам (1891–1938), born in Warsaw and raised in Saint Petersburg, was one of the central poets of Russian Acmeism, a circle around the journal Гиперборей and the “Guild of Poets” that included Nikolay Gumilyov and Anna Akhmatova. His first book, Камень (“Stone”), appeared in 1913 with 23 poems, and a second, enlarged edition of 67 poems was published at the end of 1915 (dated 1916).

The later books Tristia (1922) and the Воронежские тетради belong to the years after the revolution. Arrested in 1934 for an epigram on Stalin and exiled to Voronezh, he was arrested again in May 1938 and sentenced to five years in a labour camp; he died in a transit camp near Vladivostok in December 1938. The poems here are those printed before November 1917, given in the pre-reform spelling of their early printings.
