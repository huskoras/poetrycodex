Bliss Carman

1861–1929

Bliss Carman (1861–1929) was a Canadian-born poet, born in Fredericton, New Brunswick, who spent much of his career in the United States.

With Richard Hovey he wrote the vagabond lyrics of Songs from Vagabondia (1894).
