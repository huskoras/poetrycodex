Victorian

Edward FitzGerald

1809–1883

Edward FitzGerald (1809–1883) was an English writer whose free translation and adaptation of the medieval Persian quatrains of Omar Khayyam, The Rubaiyat of Omar Khayyam, became one of the most quoted poems in the English language.

Reworked by FitzGerald through several editions, its meditations on wine, transience, and fate gave English verse a strikingly fatalistic, epicurean voice unlike anything else in the period.
