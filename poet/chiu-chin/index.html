Ch'iu Chin

1875–1907

Ch'iu Chin (Qiu Jin, 秋瑾, 1875–1907) was a Chinese poet, feminist and revolutionary, born in Fujian and raised in a Zhejiang family. After an unhappy arranged marriage she left for Japan in 1904 to study, joined revolutionary societies, and took the style name Jingxiong, 'rival of men'.

Back in China she edited a women's journal and headed the Datong school at Shaoxing, a training ground for revolutionaries. After the failure of a planned uprising in 1907 she was arrested and beheaded at Shaoxing. Her shi and ci, written in classical forms, speak of swords, exile and the nation's fate in a voice of open defiance.
