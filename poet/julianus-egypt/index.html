Julian, Prefect of Egypt

6th century AD

Julian, Prefect of Egypt, was a Byzantine official and epigrammatist of the sixth century, included in the Cycle of Agathias.

The epigrams here are those that Paton's first volume ascribes to him, including some in Book V and the dedications in Book VI.
