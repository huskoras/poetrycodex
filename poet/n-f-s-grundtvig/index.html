N. F. S. Grundtvig

1783–1872

Nikolai Frederik Severin Grundtvig (1783–1872) was a Danish pastor, poet, historian and educator, born at Udby in Zealand. He is regarded as the greatest Danish hymn-writer, and his work shaped Danish church life, folk-high-school education and national identity. He also translated Beowulf and Saxo Grammaticus and wrote widely on Norse mythology.

The poems here are hymns and spiritual songs from the first volume of Psalmer og aandelige Sange (1868), the collected edition of his hymns, which appeared partly after his death in 1872. The texts are taken from Kalliope, with the spelling and punctuation of the original edition.
