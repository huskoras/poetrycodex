Hungarian

Mihály Babits

1883–1941

Mihály Babits (1883–1941) was a Hungarian poet, novelist, essayist and translator, a leading member of the first generation of the review Nyugat ("West"). Born in Szekszárd, he studied in Budapest, taught as a schoolmaster in provincial towns, and from 1908 belonged to the Nyugat circle, later becoming editor of the review.

His books of verse include Levelek Irisz koszorújából (1909), Herceg, hátha megjön a tél is! (1911), Recitatív (1916), Nyugtalanság völgye (1920), Sziget és tenger (1925), Az istenek halnak, az ember él (1929) and Versenyt az esztendőkkel (1933); his long poem Jónás könyve appeared in 1938. He also translated Dante's Divine Comedy into Hungarian. He died in Budapest in 1941. This selection takes poems whose Hungarian Wikisource transcriptions are rated 75% for text quality, arranged by book.
