American

John H. Bryant

1807–1902

John Howard Bryant (1807–1902) was a poet and farmer, born in Cummington, Massachusetts, and a younger brother of William Cullen Bryant, who settled in Illinois.

Griswold included 10 of his poems in The Poets and Poetry of America (16th edition, Philadelphia: Parry & McMillan, 1855), the anthology from which the poems here are taken.
