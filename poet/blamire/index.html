Susanna Blamire

1747–1794

Susanna Blamire, called the Muse of Cumberland, was born at Cardew Hall near Carlisle. She wrote in standard English and in Scots, and her poems were collected and published after her death.

Gilfillan prints two of her songs, The Nabob and What Ails this Heart o' Mine?
