Metaphysical & Cavalier

Alexander Brome

1620–1666

Alexander Brome (1620 – 30 June 1666) was an English poet and attorney, a staunch Royalist who made his name with satirical ballads, drinking songs and love lyrics written during the Civil War and the Interregnum, many of which circulated in manuscript and print against the Parliamentary cause.

Songs and Other Poems appeared in 1661 and in an enlarged second edition in 1664, the text used here. It gathers his songs, ballads, verse epistles to friends, and a section of epigrams translated from the classical tradition, together with pieces on the Restoration of Charles II in 1660.
