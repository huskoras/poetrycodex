American

Guy Wetmore Carryl

1873–1904

Guy Wetmore Carryl (1873–1904) was an American humorist and poet, born in New York City, the son of Charles Edward Carryl, who died young.

He is remembered for Fables for the Frivolous (1898), which retold Aesop and the fairy tales in comic verse.
