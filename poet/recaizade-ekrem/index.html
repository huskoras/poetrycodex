Turkish & Ottoman

Recaizade Mahmut Ekrem

1847–1914

Recaizade Mahmut Ekrem (1847–1914) was a writer of the Tanzimat period, the Ottoman reform era, and one of the figures who helped turn Turkish literature toward new subjects and a plainer prose. He was born in Istanbul. He is remembered above all for his novel Araba Sevdası (1896) and for his critical writing, and he also wrote plays and a good deal of verse.

Nağme-i Seher ('Song of the Dawn') was a collection of his poems, dated 1288 AH (about 1871). It gathers classical forms (tevhîd and na't, praise poems, chronogram verses, gazels) alongside lighter songs (şarkı). The poems below are given in the Vikikaynak (tr.wikisource.org) Latin-alphabet transcription of the 1871 book, which keeps the book's circumflexed Ottoman spelling.
