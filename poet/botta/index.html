American

Anne Lynch Botta

1815–1891

Anne Charlotte Lynch Botta (1815–1891) was an American poet and teacher, born in Bennington, Vermont, and raised in Providence, Rhode Island.

From the 1840s she hosted a celebrated literary salon at her New York home, which drew the city’s writers and artists, and she published her Poems in 1848 and again in an enlarged edition in 1853.
