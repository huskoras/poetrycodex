Afanasy Fet

1820–1892

Афанасий Афанасьевич Фет (Шеншин), 1820–1892, was a Russian lyric poet, born in the Oryol province. He was brought up as the son of the landowner Afanasy Shenshin, but while he was a boy the legitimacy of his birth was questioned and he lost the Shenshin name and the rights of a nobleman; he published as Fet, and the name Shenshin was formally restored to him only in 1873.

Fet wrote short lyrics of mood and moment: moonlit gardens, snow, evening, nightingales, the half-spoken feeling, often built on sound and music more than on argument. His collections of 1850 and 1856 made his name; after years of farming his own estate he returned to print in the 1880s with Вечерние огни (Evening Lights), whose four issues of 1883 to 1891 hold much of his finest work. He died in Moscow in 1892.

This selection takes the poems of the four issues of Вечерние огни, then Шёпот, робкое дыханье and Я пришёл к тебе с приветом, then the earliest lyrics of the 1850 collection in its own order, from the texts held on Russian Wikisource. His translations are left out. Several of those texts are in modern spelling, without ё, as printed in later Soviet-era editions.
