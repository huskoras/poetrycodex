Victorian

Arthur Hugh Clough

1819–1861

Arthur Hugh Clough (1819–1861) was an English poet and close friend of Matthew Arnold, whose verse candidly registers the Victorian crisis of religious faith and doubt.

His long poems Amours de Voyage and The Bothie of Tober-na-Vuolich experiment with conversational, unglamorous realism unusual for their time.
