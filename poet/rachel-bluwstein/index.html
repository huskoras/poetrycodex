Rachel (Rachel Bluwstein)

1890–1931

רחל בלובשטיין (Rachel Bluwstein, 1890–1931), who signed her poems simply “Rachel”, was a Hebrew poet born in Saratov, Russia. She settled in Palestine in 1909, worked on the land at the Kinneret farm and later studied agronomy in France; illness brought her to Tel Aviv, where she died in 1931.

Her poems, short, plain-spoken lyrics of love, loneliness, the Galilee landscape and illness, appeared in two books in her lifetime, Safiah (1927) and Mineged (1930); a third, Nevo, followed in 1932. The selection here takes poems from those three books only.
