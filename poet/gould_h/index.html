American

Hannah Flagg Gould

1789–1865

Hannah Flagg Gould (1789–1865) was born in Lancaster, Vermont, and lived most of her life in Newburyport, Massachusetts. She was a popular American writer of light and children's verse, as well as of occasional, sacred and memorial poems, and her work appeared in periodicals and in several volumes of poetry for children and adults.

The poems here come from two of her books: The Mother's Dream, and Other Poems (Boston: Crosby, Nichols, 1853) and The Youth's Coronal (1851), a gift book of verse for young readers. Poems that appear in both books are given once, in their 1853 text.
