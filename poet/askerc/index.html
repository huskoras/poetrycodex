Anton Aškerc

1856–1912

Anton Aškerc (1856–1912) was a Slovene poet, born in Lower Styria, then part of the Austrian Empire. He was ordained a Catholic priest and served in parishes before leaving the priesthood and settling in Ljubljana, where he worked as the city archivist until his death.

His Balade in romance (1890) made his name: narrative poems drawn from Slovene and Slavic history, legend and folk tradition, often turning on the wrongs done to peasants and the powerless, and written in a plain, forceful language. Lirske in epske poezije (1896) and Nove poezije (1900) followed, with travel poems, religious and social verse, and further ballads. He was among the most widely read Slovene poets of his generation.
