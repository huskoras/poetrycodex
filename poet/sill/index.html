American

Edward Rowland Sill

1841–1887

Edward Rowland Sill (1841–1887) was an American poet and teacher, born in Windsor, Connecticut, who taught English at the University of California.

His thoughtful short poems, among them “The Fool’s Prayer”, were widely admired and several were collected after his death.
