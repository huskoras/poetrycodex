Phaedrus

c. 15 BC - c. AD 50

Phaedrus was a Roman fabulist of the first century AD. By his own account he was born in Macedonia or Thrace and became a freedman of the emperor Augustus. He is the first author known to have turned the Greek fables attributed to Aesop into Latin verse, writing them in iambic senarii.

His five books of fables, composed in the reigns of Tiberius and after, mingle animal tales with anecdotes and with pointed remarks on his own critics and on life under the emperors. These English verse renderings are by Christopher Smart, as printed in Bohn's Classical Library (1887).
