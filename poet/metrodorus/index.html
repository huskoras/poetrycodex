Metrodorus

date uncertain (Paton)

Metrodorus is one of the epigrammatists whose short poems were gathered in the Greek Anthology. Paton's index of authors records the poet's date as uncertain.

This archive carries the 2 epigrams ascribed to the poet in the third volume of W. R. Paton's prose translation (Book IX: 360, 712).
