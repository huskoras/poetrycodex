Theodoretus Grammaticus

Theodoretus Grammaticus is one of the named epigrammatists of the Greek Anthology. Paton’s Index of Authors gives no date for him.

This archive carries the 1 epigram ascribed to the poet in the fifth volume of W. R. Paton's prose translation (Loeb, 1918): Book XVI: 34.
