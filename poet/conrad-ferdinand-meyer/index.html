Conrad Ferdinand Meyer

1825–1898

Conrad Ferdinand Meyer (1825–1898) was a Swiss poet and prose writer who wrote in German. Born in Zurich into a patrician family, he came late to literature after years of uncertainty and illness, and found his voice in the 1860s and 1870s with the verse epic Huttens letzte Tage (1871) and the historical novel Jürg Jenatsch (1876).

His Gedichte of 1882 gathered and enlarged the ballads and lyrics he had been publishing since the 1860s, arranging them in sections such as Vorsaal, Stunde, In den Bergen and Reise. Their polished, sculptural style and historical and Mediterranean subjects made him a leading German-language poet of the later nineteenth century; he died at Kilchberg near Zurich. This selection follows the first edition of 1882 (Leipzig: Haessel), in the order of its contents.
