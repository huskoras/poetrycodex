John Marriott

1780–1825

The Reverend John Marriott (1780–1825) was an English clergyman and poet, tutor to the family of the Duke of Buccleuch, the chief of Walter Scott’s clan, and later a curate and rector in Devon.

He wrote verses on Border subjects for the later editions of Scott’s Minstrelsy of the Scottish Border, including The Feast of Spurs and Archie Armstrong’s Aith.

Poems (3)

  2. On a Visit Paid to the Ruins of Melrose Abbey by the Countess of Dalkeith and her Son Lord Scott
  3. Archie Armstrong’s Aith
