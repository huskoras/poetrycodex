Metaphysical & Cavalier

Robert Heath

fl. 1636–1659

Robert Heath, styled Esquire on his title page, was an English poet of the Caroline and Interregnum years. Very little is securely known of his life, and he is remembered almost entirely for a single printed volume, which gives its name to a wider circle of friends and patrons named only by their initials in his dedications and elegies.

Clarastella, together with Poems Occasional, Elegies, Epigrams, Satyrs was printed in London in 1650 for Humphrey Moseley. It opens with a sequence of love poems addressed to the fictional Clarastella, then turns to occasional verse, elegies for friends and for Royalist casualties of the Civil War, two books of short satirical epigrams, and four longer verse satires on the follies of the age.
