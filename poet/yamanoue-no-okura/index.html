Yamanoue no Okura

c. 660 – c. 733

Yamanoue no Okura (山上憶良, c. 660 – c. 733) was a Nara-period court official and poet. He travelled to Tang China in 702 as a member of the Japanese embassy, later served as governor of Chikuzen in Kyushu, and is remembered as the Man'yōshū's poet of poverty, old age, sickness and love for his children.

The originals of the poems chosen by Saitō Mokichi in his 万葉秀歌 (1938) are given here from the 1891 Kogi reading text of the Man'yōshū (鹿持雅澄訓訂) on Wikisource, each titled by its Kokka taikan number.
