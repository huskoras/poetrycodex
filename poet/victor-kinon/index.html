French

Victor Kinon

1873–1953

Victor Kinon (born Tirlemont, 13 March 1873; died La Panne, 23 October 1953) was a Belgian poet writing in French who studied law at Louvain and made his career in the Ministry of Justice.

Jethro Bithell's 1911 anthology classes him as a Fleming with Walloon admixture and prints five of his poems.
