Anna Hempstead Branch

1875–1937

Anna Hempstead Branch (1875–1937) was an American poet, born in New London, Connecticut, and a graduate of Smith College.

Her volumes include The Heart of the Road (1901) and Rose of the Wind (1910), and her long poem “The Monk in the Kitchen” is among her best known.
