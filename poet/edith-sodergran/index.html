Edith Södergran

1892–1923

Edith Södergran (1892–1923) was a Finland-Swedish poet, born in St Petersburg and brought up there and in the Karelian Isthmus village of Raivola (now Roshchino), where she died of tuberculosis in 1923. She wrote in Swedish, the language of her schooling, and made herself one of the first modernist voices in the Nordic countries.

She published four books in her lifetime: Dikter (1916), Septemberlyran (1918), Rosenaltaret (1919) and Framtidens skugga (1920). This selection takes the poems of Dikter and Septemberlyran now transcribed on Swedish Wikisource, in the order of the printed books.
