Matthías Jochumsson

1835–1920

Matthías Jochumsson was born on 11 November 1835 at Skógar in Þorskafjörður, in the north-west of Iceland, and died at Akureyri on 18 November 1920. He became a pastor, and from 1887 served the parish of Akureyri. He was at once a clergyman, a poet, a playwright and one of the great translators of his country, rendering Shakespeare, Byron, Tegnér and many other poets into Icelandic.

He wrote Lofsöngur ("Ó, Guð vors lands") in 1874 for the celebration of a thousand years of settlement in Iceland, and it became the national anthem. The texts here are taken from volumes I, III and V of his collected Ljóðmæli (Seyðisfjörður, 1903–1906), in the spelling of that edition (eg, bygð, z); the plays, the prose and his translations of other poets' work are not included.
