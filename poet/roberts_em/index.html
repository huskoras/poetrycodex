American

Elizabeth Madox Roberts

1881–1941

Elizabeth Madox Roberts (1881-1941) was born in Perryville, Kentucky, and spent most of her life in the state. She attended the University of Chicago as an adult student, graduating in 1921, and her first book, a collection of poems, appeared in 1915.

Under the Tree (New York: B. W. Huebsch, 1922) is a book of poems about childhood in the Kentucky countryside, seen through a child's eyes. She is best known for her novels, including The Time of Man (1926), and she died in 1941.
