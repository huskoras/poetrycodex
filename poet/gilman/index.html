American

Charlotte Perkins Gilman

1860–1935

Charlotte Perkins Gilman (1860–1935) was an American writer and lecturer on women’s rights and social reform, born in Hartford, Connecticut.

She is best known for Women and Economics (1898) and the story “The Yellow Wall-Paper”, and she also published satirical verse in In This Our World (1893).
