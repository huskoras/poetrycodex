Metaphysical & Cavalier

Sir William Davenant

1606–1668

Sir William Davenant (1606–1668) was an English poet and playwright who served as Poet Laureate from 1638. A bridge between the ages of Shakespeare and Dryden, he helped reopen and reshape the English theatre after the Restoration.

His most ambitious work is the unfinished heroic poem Gondibert (1651); the collection Madagascar; with Other Poems (1638) gathers his courtly and occasional verse.
