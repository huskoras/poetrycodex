William Cowper

1731–1800

William Cowper (1731–1800) wrote from a life shadowed by recurring breakdowns and religious despair, retired to the Buckinghamshire village of Olney under the care of friends.

His comic ballad John Gilpin made him briefly famous, but his lasting achievement is The Task, a long meditative poem on rural retirement, and the Olney Hymns he wrote with John Newton, including “God Moves in a Mysterious Way.”
