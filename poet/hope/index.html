Victorian

Laurence Hope

1865–1904

Laurence Hope was the pen name of Adela Florence Nicolson (née Cory, 1865–1904), who was raised partly in India and married Colonel Malcolm Nicolson. She took her own life in 1904, two months after his death.

Her passionate verse was presented as translated from Indian poets, but the poems are her own, and she is best known for ‘Kashmiri Song’ (‘Pale hands I love beside the Shalimar’), which was set to music and made famous. The poems here come from India’s Love Lyrics (the American title of The Garden of Kama, 1901) and the posthumous Last Poems (1905).
