Rupert Brooke

1887–1915

Rupert Brooke (1887–1915) was an English poet, born at Rugby and educated at King’s College, Cambridge, where he became a fellow. He was a leading figure among the Georgian poets, and travelled in America and the South Seas in 1913–1914.

He joined the Royal Naval Division in 1914 and wrote the sonnet sequence 1914, which includes “The Soldier.” He died of blood poisoning aboard a French hospital ship off the Greek island of Skyros on 23 April 1915, on his way to Gallipoli, and was buried there.
