Abd-ul-Kadir Khan Khattak

b. 1652 (Raverty)

Abd-ul-Kadir Khan, a son of Khushhal Khan and brother of Ashraf, was born in 1652. Raverty calls him the most eloquent writer and poet among Khushhal's sons, and says he was as good with the sword as with the pen, fighting the Mughals with the confederate Afghans.

He disputed the chieftainship with his nephew Afzal, and was put to death with ten of his brothers and a number of their sons at Zaman Garrai. His odes are deeply coloured by Sufi mysticism and are highly valued by Afghans.
