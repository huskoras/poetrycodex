Helen Gray Cone

1859–1934

Helen Gray Cone (1859–1934) was an American poet and teacher, born in New York City. She graduated from the Normal College in 1876 and from 1899 was professor of English literature at Hunter College.

Her volumes of verse include Oberon and Puck (1885), The Ride to the Lady (1893) and Soldiers of the Light (1911).
