Kanteletar (Finnish folk songs, ed. Lönnrot)

1840

The Kanteletar is a collection of anonymous Finnish folk songs gathered and edited by Elias Lönnrot (1802–1884), first printed in 1840. Its full title is Kanteletar taikka Suomen kansan wanhoja lauluja ja wirsiä, 'Kanteletar, or old songs and hymns of the Finnish people'.

Lönnrot drew the songs from singers whom he met on his journeys through Finland and Karelia, as he had done for the Kalevala. The first book holds songs common to all singers, with wedding songs, shepherds' songs and children's songs; the second holds songs of girls, women, boys and men. This archive gives the songs of the first book in the 1840 spelling, titled as printed.
