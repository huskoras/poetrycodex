Antiphilus of Byzantium

1st century AD

Antiphilus of Byzantium wrote in the first century AD and is one of the poets of the Garland of Philippus. His epigrams include love poems, dedications and little pictures of daily life.

The epigrams here are those that Paton's first volume ascribes to him.
