Gabriello Chiabrera

1552–1638

Gabriello Chiabrera (born in Savona in 1552, died there in 1638) was an Italian poet of the late Renaissance and early Baroque, often called the Italian Pindar. Educated in Rome, he spent much of his life in his native Liguria and wrote for the courts of Genoa, Florence, Mantua and Savoy. He is remembered for the new metres and Greek-inspired manner with which he enlarged the range of Italian lyric verse.

This selection follows the Milan 1834 edition of his works (Bettoni) as transcribed on Italian Wikisource, and takes three of its sections: the light, song-like Canzonette (about love, beauty and the pleasures of the moment), the Canzonette morali (devotional and moral reflections) and the Epitaffj, short funerary inscriptions for friends and notable figures. William Wordsworth translated several of the Epitaffi into English; those versions are kept under Wordsworth in this archive.
