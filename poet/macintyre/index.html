Duncan Macintyre

1724–1812

Duncan Macintyre (1724–1812) wrote in Gaelic; Charles Rogers printed English verse renderings of the bard's songs in The Modern Scottish Minstrel (six volumes, Edinburgh: Adam & Charles Black, 1855–57).

This archive holds 1 English rendering from the Gaelic in volume 1 of the anthology; each is credited to the translator whom Rogers names. Dates are those given in Rogers's memoir.

In 1912 George Calder published The Gaelic Songs of Duncan MacIntyre (Edinburgh: John Grant), the bard's Gaelic text with his own English verse translation, made line by line, on the facing page. This archive gives 63 of those songs, each twice, as the Gaelic original and as Calder's English version (126 poems, in the book's order, Moladh Beinn-dobhrain among them), besides the 4 English renderings by other translators already held here, so that 130 poems now stand under his name. Calder's introduction, notes, vocabulary and the numbers printed beside the lines are left out; the songs of his Appendix I are included.
