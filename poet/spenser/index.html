Tudor & Elizabethan

Edmund Spenser

1552–1599

Edmund Spenser was an English poet best known for his epic ‘The Faerie Queene,’ but he also wrote a substantial body of shorter verse, including the pastoral ‘Shepheardes Calender,’ the sonnet sequence ‘Amoretti,’ and the wedding hymns ‘Epithalamion’ and ‘Prothalamion.’

He spent much of his career in Ireland as a colonial administrator, and his poetry stands at the centre of English Renaissance literature.

Major Works (2)

  1. The Faerie Queene — Epic poem, 1590–1596
  2. The Shepheard’s Calender — Pastoral poem, 1579
