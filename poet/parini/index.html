Italian

Giuseppe Parini

1729–1799

Giuseppe Parini (1729–1799) was born at Bosisio in Lombardy and took holy orders in 1754. He worked as a tutor in noble Milanese households and later held a chair of rhetoric at the Brera in Milan, and he served the Austrian administration of Lombardy in cultural and civic posts. He is the chief Italian poet of the Enlightenment.

His long satirical poem Il Giorno, begun in the early 1760s, follows a young nobleman through his day with mock-serious instruction, and is written in blank hendecasyllables. The Odi, composed between about 1757 and the 1790s, are civic and moral lyrics on health, education, music, work and the duties of the powerful. Parini died in Milan in 1799.
