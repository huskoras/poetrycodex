Nordic

Johan Herman Wessel

1742–1785

Johan Herman Wessel was born in 1742 at Jonsrud near Vestby in Norway and settled in Copenhagen as a young man, where he became the liveliest member of Det Norske Selskab, the circle of Norwegians in the Danish capital. He lived by his wits and his pen, and is remembered as the comic poet of the Dano-Norwegian Enlightenment.

His verse tales and epigrams, written in a brisk, mock-solemn manner with a moral tagged on at the end, were gathered after his death; Smeden og Bageren is the best known. His parody of French tragedy, the play Kierlighed uden Strømper (1772), made his name. He died in Copenhagen in 1785.
