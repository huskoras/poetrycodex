Louise Labé

c. 1524–1566

Louise Labé (c. 1524–1566) was a poet of Lyon, the daughter and wife of rope-makers, and known in her own day as la Belle Cordière. She moved among the humanists and printers of the city, and the Lyon school of poets gathered around her.

Her one book, the Euvres of 1555, joins a prose Débat de Folie et d'Amour to three elegies and twenty-four sonnets of love, the first of them written in Italian. This edition follows the printed text of 1555 as transcribed by Charles Boy (1887), in its original spelling.
