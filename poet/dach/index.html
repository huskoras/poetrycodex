German

Simon Dach

1605–1659

Simon Dach was born at Memel in 1605 and died at Königsberg in 1659. He became professor of poetry at the University of Königsberg in 1639 and was the centre of a circle of poets in that city, writing a large body of occasional verse and some hymns.

The English versions here are Catherine Winkworth's in Lyra Germanica (1855).
