Khushhal Khan Khattak

1613–1689

Khushhal Khan, chief of the Khattak tribe, was born in 1613 and succeeded his father in 1640 after the latter was wounded fighting the Yusufzis. He served the Mughal emperors Shah Jahan and Aurangzeb, then fell under Aurangzeb's suspicion, was sent away as a prisoner, and afterwards led Afghan resistance to the Mughals, dying free in the mountains.

Raverty calls him alike a warrior and a poet. Unlike the Sufi poets in this volume he writes on whatever was on his mind: spring and love, wine, the faults of tribes and rulers, and the call to Afghan unity against the Mughals.
