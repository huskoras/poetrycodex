Krzysztof Kamil Baczyński

1921-1944

Krzysztof Kamil Baczyński (1921-1944) was a Polish poet, born in Warsaw, and one of the best-known voices of the wartime generation sometimes called the Columbuses. A schoolboy when the German invasion came in 1939, he wrote through the occupation, publishing in the underground press under pseudonyms such as Jan Bugaj, and joined the Home Army.

He fell on 4 August 1944, in the first days of the Warsaw Uprising, aged twenty-two. This selection is taken from the manuscript book he copied out in 1942-1944, known as Kodeks 42-44, together with a few poems from the 1944 manuscript Krzyż człowieczy; the texts follow the Polish Wikisource transcription of his autograph, with the pre-war spelling kept.
