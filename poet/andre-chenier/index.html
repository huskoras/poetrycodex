French

André Chénier

1762–1794

André Chénier (1762–1794) was born in Constantinople to a French father and a Greek mother, grew up in France and in the 1780s served as secretary to the French embassy in London. Back in Paris from 1790 he wrote verse in the manner of the ancients, Greek idylls and elegies of love above all, and also political pamphlets and satirical iambs against the excesses of the Revolution.

Arrested in March 1794 and held in the prison of Saint-Lazare, he was guillotined on 25 July 1794, two days before the fall of Robespierre. Almost none of his poems had appeared in his lifetime; they were first collected in 1819 by Henri de Latouche, and that edition made him a hero of the young Romantic poets. The texts here are from French Wikisource’s transcription of Latouche’s Œuvres complètes (1819): the Idylles, Élégies, Odes and the four Iambes, with a selection of the fragments.
