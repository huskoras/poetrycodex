Silvije Strahimir Kranjčević

1865–1908

Silvije Strahimir Kranjčević (1865–1908) was a Croatian poet, born at Senj on the Adriatic coast. He trained as a teacher and priest in Senj and Zagreb, worked as a schoolmaster and journalist, and from 1893 lived in Sarajevo, where he edited the literary review Nada until his death.

Regarded as one of the central poets of Croatian realism, and a forerunner of modernism, he joined patriotic feeling to social protest and a questioning, often tragic, philosophical and religious vision. His books include Bugarkinje (1885), Izabrane pjesme (1898) and Trzaji (1902); poems such as Mojsije, Moj dom and Eli! Eli! lamâ azâvtani?! remain staples of the Croatian school canon.
