Victorian

Adam Lindsay Gordon

1833–1870

Adam Lindsay Gordon (1833–1870) was an Anglo-Australian poet, born in the Azores and educated in England, who emigrated to South Australia in 1853 and worked as a mounted trooper, horse-breaker and steeplechase rider.

He published Sea Spray and Smoke Drift (1867) and Bush Ballads and Galloping Rhymes (1870), and died by his own hand at Brighton, Victoria.
