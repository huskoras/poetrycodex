Tudor & Elizabethan

Sir John Harington

1560–1612

Sir John Harington (1560–1612) was an English courtier, translator and epigrammatist, born at Kelston in Somerset. A godson of Queen Elizabeth I, he made his name at court with wit that sometimes cost him her favour; he translated Ariosto's Orlando Furioso into English verse (1591) and wrote a satirical pamphlet on a water closet, The Metamorphosis of Ajax.

His Latin-flavoured English epigrams, pointed and often bawdy or political, circulated in manuscript during his lifetime and were collected after his death. This selection follows the 1618 printing, The Most Elegant and Witty Epigrams, in four books, taken from the Text Creation Partnership transcription. Epigrams whose transcription is too badly damaged to be read with their point intact have been left out.
