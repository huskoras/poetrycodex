Park Benjamin

1809–1864

Park Benjamin (1809–1864) was a poet and editor born in British Guiana, active in the New York and Boston magazines of the 1830s and 1840s.

Griswold included 17 of his poems in The Poets and Poetry of America (16th edition, Philadelphia: Parry & McMillan, 1855), the anthology from which the poems here are taken.
