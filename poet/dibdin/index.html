Restoration & Augustan

Charles Dibdin

1745–1814

Charles Dibdin (1745–1814), born at Southampton, was a singer, composer, actor and dramatist who wrote the words and the music of his own songs. He first won notice at Drury Lane in the late 1760s, and went on to write a long run of comic operas and other pieces for the London stage.

From the late 1780s he gave one-man “table entertainments”, in which he sang his own songs at the keyboard, and the ballads of the sea that he wrote for them made his name. The songs here come from his own 1790 selection, taken from his table entertainments and from songs printed without any stage piece; songs from his operas and other stage pieces are left out.
