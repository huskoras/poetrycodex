Indian

Tiruvalluvar

traditionally c. 1st century BC – 5th century AD

Tiruvalluvar is the traditional name of the Tamil poet-sage who composed the Tirukkural (the ‘Sacred Kural’), and almost nothing is securely known of his life; scholars have placed him anywhere between the first century BC and about the fifth century AD.

The Tirukkural consists of 1,330 couplets in 133 chapters of ten, arranged in three books on Virtue, Wealth and Love. It is given here, chapter by chapter, in G. U. Pope’s English verse translation of 1886.
