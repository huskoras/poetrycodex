Emma Lazarus

1849–1887

Emma Lazarus (1849–1887) was an American poet and essayist, born in New York City to a prosperous Sephardic Jewish family. She published her first book of verse at seventeen, won the admiration and friendship of Ralph Waldo Emerson, and wrote narrative poems such as Admetus and Tannhauser before turning to lyric and sonnet forms.

From 1881, after the pogroms in Russia, she took up Jewish themes in poems such as The Banner of the Jew and The Crowing of the Red Cock, and worked on behalf of refugees. Her sonnet The New Colossus (1883), written for a fund-raising auction for the Statue of Liberty pedestal, is now inscribed there. She died in New York at the age of 38.
