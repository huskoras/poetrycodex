Alice Milligan

1865–1953

Alice Milligan was born in Omagh, County Tyrone, in 1865 and died there in 1953. A Protestant by upbringing, she became a leading figure of the Irish cultural revival: she learned Irish, worked for the Gaelic League, and edited the Belfast journal The Northern Patriot and later the Shan Van Vocht with Anna Johnston (Ethna Carbery).

Hero Lays (1908), published in Dublin by Maunsel and printed here in full, gathers her ballads and lyrics on the heroes of Irish legend and the patriots of the nineteenth century, from Lugh and Cuchulain to the Fenians of 1867.
