Victorian

Timothy Daniel Sullivan

1827–1914

Timothy Daniel Sullivan (1827–1914), born at Bantry, County Cork, began contributing to The Nation as a young man and later became its proprietor and editor. His song "God Save Ireland" became one of the best known of the Irish nationalist songs.

The poems here are taken from H. Halliday Sparling's anthology Irish Minstrelsy (London: Walter Scott, 1888).
