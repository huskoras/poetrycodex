Romanian

Ion Pillat

1891–1945

Ion Pillat (1891–1945) was a Romanian poet, essayist, anthologist and editor, born in Miorcani in the county of Botoșani and raised largely on his family's estate at Florica, on the river Argeș. Through his mother he was related to the Brătianu family, and the politician Ion C. Brătianu, his grandfather, is often recalled in his verse. He studied in Paris, began publishing in the first decade of the century, and was later elected a corresponding member of the Romanian Academy.

Pillat is counted among the leading traditionalist poets of the interwar years. His best-known volume, Pe Argeș în sus (1923), turns to the house, the orchards and the family dead of Florica, and to the slow passing of the past into the present, in measured rhymed stanzas. The poems here are taken from that book.
