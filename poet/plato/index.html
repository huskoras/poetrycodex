Ancient Greek & Roman

Plato

c. 428 – 348 BC

Plato (c. 428 – 348 BC) is credited in the Anthology with a number of short epigrams, love poems and epitaphs among them. Their authenticity is doubtful, and Paton's index gives them to Plato without comment.

The epigrams here are those that Paton's first volume ascribes to Plato.
