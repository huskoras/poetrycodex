Victorian

Adelaide Anne Procter

1825–1864

Adelaide Anne Procter (1825–1864) was an English poet and social activist, a devout Catholic and one of the best-selling poets of the Victorian era after Tennyson himself.

Her collection <em>Legends and Lyrics</em> gathers narrative ballads and devotional verse, often concerned with charity, suffering, and quiet endurance.
