Metaphysical & Cavalier

Samuel Daniel

1562–1619

Samuel Daniel (1562–1619) was an English poet and historian, admired for the plain, measured elegance of his style.

His sonnet sequence Delia and the narrative poem The Complaint of Rosamond made his early reputation.
