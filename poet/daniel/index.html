Metaphysical & Cavalier

Samuel Daniel

1562–1619

Samuel Daniel (1562–1619) was an English poet and historian, admired for the plain, measured elegance of his style.

His sonnet sequence <em>Delia</em> and the narrative poem <em>The Complaint of Rosamond</em> made his early reputation.
