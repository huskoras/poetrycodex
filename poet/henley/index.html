Victorian

William Ernest Henley

1849–1903

William Ernest Henley (1849–1903) was an English poet and editor whose leg was amputated in youth after tubercular illness, an experience recorded directly in his sequence <em>In Hospital</em>.

He is best remembered for <em>Invictus</em>, and his robust, muscular verse and criticism made him an influential figure among younger writers of the 1890s.
