Metaphysical & Cavalier

William Hammond

b. 1614

William Hammond is a Royalist-period lyric poet known from a single book, Poems, published in London by Thomas Dring in 1655. Its title page gives only the initials "W. H."; the attribution to William Hammond, born in 1614, comes from the library catalogue record of the volume.

The book gathers love lyrics, pastoral dialogues, verse letters, elegies and English versions of Horace. Many of the poems are addressed to a close family circle: his kinsman T. S., a brother, H. S., mourned as drowned, a sister, Mrs. S., and an uncle, Mr. G. Sandys. Little else about his life can be stated from the book itself.
