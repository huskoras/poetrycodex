Oleksandr Oles

1878-1944

Oleksandr Oles (born Oleksandr Kandyba, 1878-1944) was a Ukrainian poet and playwright, born in Bilopillia in the Kharkiv region. His first collection, Z zhurboiu radist obnialas ("Joy Embraced Sorrow", 1907), made him one of the best-loved Ukrainian lyric poets of the early twentieth century, and his short songs of love, nature and national hope were set to music and learned by heart. He also wrote the dramatic poem Po dorozi v kazku (1911).

After the revolutionary years he left Ukraine and lived in exile in Vienna and then Prague, where he died in 1944. This selection draws on the 1919 Kyiv-Vienna edition of Z zhurboiu radist obnialas, Book I, in its old orthography as printed, with the later-famous 'O slovo ridne! Orle skutyi!' (1907) from Ukrainian Wikisource in modern spelling. The dramatic sketches are left out.
