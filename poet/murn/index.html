Josip Murn

1879-1901

Josip Murn, who wrote under the pen name Aleksandrov (Ljubljana, 1879 – Ljubljana, 1901), was a Slovene poet of the Moderna, the modernist generation around Ivan Cankar, Oton Župančič and Dragotin Kette. The son of a poor family, he studied at the Ljubljana gymnasium and briefly at the seminary, worked as a clerk, and died of tuberculosis at twenty-two.

He published little in his lifetime. His collected verse appeared posthumously in 1903 as Pesmi in romance, edited by Cankar and Župančič and later known as Topol samujoč (The Lonely Poplar). Combining the manner of folk song and the village ballad with a symbolist sense of loneliness, autumn and snow, it is counted among the most influential bodies of Slovene poetry after Prešeren.
