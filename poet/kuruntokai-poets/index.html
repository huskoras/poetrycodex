Kuruntokai Poets

c. 1st–3rd century CE

The Kuruntokai (குறுந்தொகை, “Collection of Short Poems”) is one of the eight anthologies of Sangam literature, the earliest surviving poetry in Tamil. It gathers 401 short akam poems of four to eight lines each, love poems in which a lover, a beloved, her confidante and other speakers voice longing, meeting, separation and return against the landscapes of the Tamil country.

The poems are the work of some two hundred named and unnamed poets, among them Kapilar, Auvaiyār, Paraṇar and Ammūvaṉār, and were composed over roughly the first to third centuries CE. Each poem is traditionally assigned to a landscape and a speaker, which this edition records in its notes. The Tamil text here is the classical text as transcribed on Tamil Wikisource, and begins with the invocation.
