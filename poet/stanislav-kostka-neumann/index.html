Stanislav Kostka Neumann

1875–1947

Stanislav Kostka Neumann (1875–1947) was a Czech poet, journalist, critic and translator from French and Russian. Born in Prague, he began as an anarchist and a leading voice of the Czech decadent and symbolist generation, edited the review Nový kult, and later turned to a vitalist celebration of nature and then to communism. He was named a National Artist in 1945.

Kniha lesů, vod a strání (The Book of Woods, Waters and Slopes, 1914) is his best-known collection: a cycle of nature poems following the year from autumn to the following autumn, set in the wooded valley of the river Svitava. This text follows the Wikisource transcription of the 1955 Prague edition, with its illustrations by Josef Čapek and any editorial matter left out.
