Romantic

Samuel Rogers

1763–1855

Samuel Rogers was an English banker and poet whose The Pleasures of Memory (1792) made his name. He was a well-known host and patron in London literary society.

Palgrave includes two of his short poems.

Major Works (3)

  1. Italy — Descriptive poem, 1822–28; illustrated edition 1830
  2. The Pleasures of Memory — Didactic poem, 1792
  3. The Voyage of Columbus — Narrative poem (fragments), 1810–12
