Victorian

Sabine Baring-Gould

1834–1924

Sabine Baring-Gould (1834–1924) was an English clergyman, antiquary and novelist, and rector of Lew Trenchard in Devon from 1881. He is best remembered for the hymns “Onward, Christian Soldiers” and “Now the day is over”, and for collecting the folk songs of the West Country.
