Archibald Lampman

1861–1899

Archibald Lampman (1861–1899), born at Morpeth, Ontario, was a Post Office clerk in Ottawa and the leading poet of the Confederation generation.

His first book, Among the Millet and Other Poems, appeared in 1888. One of the poets chosen by W. D. Lighthall for Songs of the Great Dominion (London, 1889), an anthology of Canadian verse.
