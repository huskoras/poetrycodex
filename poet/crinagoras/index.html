Crinagoras of Mytilene

c. 70 BC – after AD 11

Crinagoras of Mytilene (c. 70 BC – after AD 11) belonged to the circle of Augustus and Octavia and served as an envoy of his city to Rome. He is among the main poets of the Garland of Philippus.

The epigrams here are those that Paton's first volume ascribes to him.
