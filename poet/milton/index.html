Metaphysical & Cavalier

John Milton

1608–1674

John Milton (1608–1674) was an English poet and polemicist, author of the epic Paradise Lost. He went blind in mid-life and composed his greatest work by dictation.

Though he predates the Romantic movement, his blank verse and his figure of Satan shaped nearly every English poet who followed.
