W. J. Turner

1889–1946

Walter James Turner (1889–1946) was a poet and critic, born in Melbourne, Australia, who settled in England and became known as a music and drama critic as well as a Georgian poet.

His poem Romance, here taken from Untermeyer's Modern British Poetry (1920), is his most famous.
