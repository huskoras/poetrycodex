Victorian

Andrew Barton 'Banjo' Paterson

1864–1941

Andrew Barton 'Banjo' Paterson (1864–1941) was an Australian bush poet, solicitor and journalist, born near Orange, New South Wales, who reported the Boer War as a correspondent.

He published The Man from Snowy River and Other Verses (1895) and Rio Grande's Last Race (1902), and wrote the words of 'Waltzing Matilda'.
