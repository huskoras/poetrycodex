Aeschines the Orator

4th century BC (Paton’s date)

Aeschines the Orator is one of the epigrammatists whose short poems were gathered in the Greek Anthology. Paton's index of authors dates the poet to 4th century BC.

This archive carries the 1 epigram ascribed to the poet in the first volume of W. R. Paton's prose translation (Books I–VI) (Book VI: 330).
