Victorian

Richard Le Gallienne

1866–1947

Richard Le Gallienne (1866–1947) was an English poet, critic and novelist, born at Liverpool. A member of the 1890s circle of the Rhymers’ Club and the Yellow Book, he moved to the United States in 1903 and spent much of his later life there.

His books of verse include English Poems (1892), New Poems (1909) and The Lonely Dancer (1913), and a free paraphrase of the Rubaiyat of Omar Khayyam.
