Aubrey de Vere

1814–1902

Aubrey de Vere was born at Curragh Chase, County Limerick, and published poetry, criticism and travel writing. He became a Roman Catholic in 1851.

His Irish poems include The Wedding of the Clans and The Bard Ethell. He is not to be confused with Edward de Vere, Earl of Oxford.
