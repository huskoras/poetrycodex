Celtic

Anonymous (Cornish)

late 14th century

The Ordinalia is a cycle of three Middle Cornish mystery plays, Origo Mundi (the beginning of the world), Passio Domini Nostri Jhesu Christi (the Passion) and Resurrexio Domini Nostri Jhesu Christi (the Resurrection), which together run to some 8,700 lines of rhymed stanzas. They are generally thought to have been written in the later fourteenth century, probably in the collegiate church of Glasney near Penryn, and nothing is known of the author or authors.

The cycle survives in a single manuscript of about 1400, now in the Bodleian Library, Oxford (MS Bodley 791), and was first printed in Edwin Norris's edition with a facing English version, The Ancient Cornish Drama (Oxford, 1859). It is the largest surviving text in medieval Cornish, and the plays were acted in the round, with the stages of heaven and hell set around the playing place.
