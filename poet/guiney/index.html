Louise Imogen Guiney

1861–1920

Louise Imogen Guiney (1861–1920) was an American poet and essayist, born in Boston, who spent her later years in England.

Her poems appeared in volumes such as Songs at the Start (1884) and A Roadside Harp (1893).
