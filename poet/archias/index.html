Archias

1st century BC

Archias is the name of several epigrammatists in the Anthology, and Paton's index lists the author of these as probably the second of that name; the editor places him in the first century BC. His epigrams are mainly dedications and love poems.

The epigrams here are those that Paton's first volume ascribes to Archias without qualification.
