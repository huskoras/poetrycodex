Romantic

Percy Bysshe Shelley

1792–1822

Percy Bysshe Shelley (1792–1822) was an English Romantic poet, radical in politics and idealist in temper.

Author of ‘Ozymandias’ and ‘Ode to the West Wind,’ he drowned off the Italian coast before his thirtieth birthday.
