José Asunción Silva

1865–1896

José Asunción Silva (1865–1896) was a Colombian poet, born in Bogotá, and one of the founding voices of Latin American Modernismo. He travelled in Europe as a young man, absorbing Baudelaire, Verlaine and the French and English Symbolists, and returned to Bogotá, where he worked in his family's business.

His short, restless life was marked by family losses and financial ruin, and he died in Bogotá in 1896. His best-known poem, the "Nocturno" beginning "Una noche", is celebrated for its long, tolling lines. His verse was gathered after his death, and he also wrote the novel De sobremesa, which is not included here. The texts are transcribed on Spanish Wikisource.
