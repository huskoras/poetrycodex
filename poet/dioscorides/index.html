Ancient Greek & Roman

Dioscorides

fl. late 3rd century BC

Dioscorides was a Hellenistic epigrammatist whose several dozen poems in the Anthology include love poems, epitaphs and dedications. The editor places him in the late third century BC; Paton's index dates him to the second.

The epigrams here are the ones that Paton's first volume ascribes to him.
