Taras Shevchenko

1814–1861

Taras Shevchenko (1814–1861) is the national poet of Ukraine. Born a serf in the village of Moryntsi, he was freed in 1838 with the help of the painter Karl Briullov and the poet Vasily Zhukovsky, studied at the Imperial Academy of Arts in St Petersburg, and published his first book of verse, the Kobzar, in 1840.

In 1847 he was arrested for his membership in the Cyril and Methodius Brotherhood and conscripted as a private soldier in the Orenburg garrison, with a ban on writing and painting; he returned to St Petersburg only in 1858 and died there three years later. The texts here follow the posthumous Prague edition of the Kobzar (1876), in its original orthography, as transcribed on Ukrainian Wikisource.
