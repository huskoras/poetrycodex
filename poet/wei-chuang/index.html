Wei Chuang

graduated AD 902

Wei Chuang was a late T’ang poet who took his degree in AD 902, according to the chronology of poets in The Jade Mountain.

The poems here are Witter Bynner and Kiang Kang-hu’s versions in The Jade Mountain (1929), given with the poet’s name as spelled here.
