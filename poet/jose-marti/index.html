José Martí

1853–1895

José Martí (1853–1895) was a Cuban poet, essayist and revolutionary, born in Havana. Arrested as a schoolboy for opposing Spanish rule, he was sentenced to forced labour and exiled to Spain; he later lived in Mexico, Guatemala, Venezuela and, for most of his last fifteen years, New York, writing for the press and organising the Cuban independence movement.

His poetry helped to prepare the way for Spanish-American modernismo. Ismaelillo (1882), a small book of poems to his infant son, is often counted among its first works, and Versos sencillos (1891) is a sequence of plain, song-like verses on sincerity, friendship, love and the struggle for freedom. He was killed in action at Dos Ríos, Cuba, in May 1895. Texts: Spanish Wikisource transcription (CC BY-SA); the poems themselves are in the public domain.
