Romantic

Samuel Taylor Coleridge

1772–1834

Samuel Taylor Coleridge (1772–1834) was an English poet, critic, and philosopher, co-founder of the Romantic movement.

His visionary poems ‘The Rime of the Ancient Mariner’ and ‘Kubla Khan’ remain touchstones of the imagination he spent his life theorising.
