Lu Yu

1125–1209

Lu Yu (1125–1209) was a poet of the Sung dynasty. The four short poems here are Waley’s versions of village scenes and a day on the water.

The poems here are Arthur Waley’s versions in A Hundred and Seventy Chinese Poems (1918), given with the poet’s name as spelled in those books.
