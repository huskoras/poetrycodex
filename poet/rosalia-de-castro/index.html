Rosalía de Castro

1837–1885

Rosalía de Castro (Santiago de Compostela, 1837 – Padrón, 1885) is the founding voice of modern Galician poetry and one of the major lyric poets of nineteenth-century Spain. Married to the historian Manuel Murguía, she wrote novels and poems in both Spanish and Galician, a language that had hardly been used for literature for centuries.

Her Cantares gallegos (1863), the first book of poems of the Galician Rexurdimento, drew on the songs and customs of the Galician countryside; Follas novas (1880) turned to grief, exile and emigration in the same language. En las orillas del Sar (1884), her last book of verse, is in Spanish and is often counted among the finest Spanish poetry of its century.
