Romanian

Ion Minulescu

1881–1944

Ion Minulescu (1881–1944) was born in Bucharest and became the leading Romanian Symbolist poet of the early twentieth century. He worked as a journalist, editor and literary organiser, and his Bucharest circle drew young writers to the new school.

His first book, Romanțe pentru mai târziu (1908), brought the mannered, musical and exotic idiom of French Symbolism into Romanian verse; it was followed by De vorbă cu mine însumi (1913), Strofe pentru toată lumea (1930) and other collections. He died in Bucharest in 1944.
