Anvari

1126–1189

Anvari (انوری, Awhad al-Din Muhammad of Abivard, died c. 1189) was a Persian court poet of the Seljuk sultan Sanjar at Merv, born in the Khavaran district of Khorasan. Learned in the sciences of his day, above all astronomy, he made his name chiefly as a master of the qasida, and Persian tradition ranks him among the three "prophets" of poetry beside Ferdowsi and Saadi.

This selection follows the poems of his Divan that E. G. Browne quotes or translates in A Literary History of Persia, vol. II (1906), among them the elegy known in English as the "Tears of Khorasan", on the ravages of the Ghuzz Turks; each is given whole from Ganjoor, with the page of Browne in its note. Each bayt stands on one line, the two hemistichs separated by a wide space. The Persian text is public domain.
