Ancient Greek & Roman

Martial

c. 40–c. 104

Martial (Marcus Valerius Martialis) was born at Bilbilis in Spain and made his career as an epigrammatist at Rome under the Flavian emperors. His twelve books of epigrams, brief and often barbed poems on the manners, vanities and vices of the city, made the epigram a lasting literary form.

The English verse here is the 1656 version by R. Fletcher, published as Ex otio negotium, or Martiall his epigrams translated. It renders the epigrams in rhymed couplets and stanzas, keeps the Latin addressees as headings, and preserves the spelling of the printed book.
