Victorian

Alice Meynell

1847–1922

Alice Meynell (1847–1922) was an English poet, essayist, and suffragist, admired in her lifetime for the precision and restraint of her devout, closely observed lyrics.

A convert to Catholicism, she wrote often of motherhood, nature, and quiet spiritual attention.
