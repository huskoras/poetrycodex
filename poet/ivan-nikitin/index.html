Ivan Nikitin (Иван Никитин)

1824–1861

Ivan Savvich Nikitin (1824–1861) was a Russian poet, born in Voronezh, the son of a candle-maker. He was educated at the Voronezh seminary, left it, and later ran a small inn and a bookshop in his native town. Self-taught and close to the life of tradesmen, carters and peasants, he began publishing in the early 1850s.

His verse, much of it in the manner of folk song, joined landscape and pity for the poor, and his long poem Kulak (1858) became his best-known work. Poor health and hard circumstances marked his short career, and he died in Voronezh of consumption in October 1861.
