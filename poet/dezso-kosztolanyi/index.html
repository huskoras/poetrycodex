Hungarian

Dezső Kosztolányi

1885–1936

Dezső Kosztolányi was born in Szabadka (now Subotica, Serbia) in 1885 and died in Budapest in 1936. He became one of the central figures of the first Nyugat generation of Hungarian writers, working as a poet, journalist, translator, essayist, short-story writer and novelist.

His verse books of the 1910s, among them A szegény kisgyermek panaszai (The Complaints of the Poor Little Child), Mágia, Mák and Kenyér és bor, are given here as printed in his lifetime by Tevan of Békéscsaba and Athenaeum of Budapest. They move between the child's-eye cycle that made his name, urban melancholy, and the sober domestic and wartime poems of his middle years.
