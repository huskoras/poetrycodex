Shen Yue

441–513

Shen Yue (441–513), courtesy name Xiuwen, of Wukang in Wuxing (modern Zhejiang), served three dynasties, Song, Qi and Liang, and rose under the Liang to high office. He was a leading historian of the age and the compiler of the Song shu, the official history of the Liu Song dynasty.

With Xie Tiao and Wang Rong he led the Yongming style of the 480s, and he is remembered for setting out the theory of the four tones of Chinese and for his rules against tonal faults in verse. His own poems range from landscape and recluse pieces to palace-style songs and the temple hymns of the Liang court.
