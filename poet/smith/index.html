Romantic

Charlotte Smith

1749–1806

Charlotte Smith (1749–1806) was an English poet and novelist, born Charlotte Turner and raised in Sussex and Surrey. Married young to a spendthrift husband who was imprisoned for debt in 1783, she turned to writing to support her many children.

Her Elegiac Sonnets (1784), expanded through many editions, is often credited with reviving the English sonnet as a form for personal feeling, and its melancholy landscapes of the Sussex downs and coast lie behind much Romantic poetry. She was also a successful novelist, and her long blank-verse poem Beachy Head appeared after her death in 1807.
