The Hebrew Bible

c. 1000–300 BC

The Hebrew Bible (תנ״ך, Tanakh) is the scripture of Judaism, and its poetic books, here the Psalms, the Song of Songs and Lamentations, were composed in Biblical Hebrew between roughly 1000 and 300 BC.

The text is that of the Leningrad Codex (1008) in the Westminster Leningrad Codex edition, the Masoretic text with its vowel points, as digitised by the Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible project.
