al-Sharif al-Radi

970–1015

الشريف الرضي (al-Sharif al-Radi, 970–1015) was a Baghdad poet of the Abbasid age, a Hashimite of the line of the Prophet, remembered chiefly for his elegies and for his love poems of the Hijaz.
