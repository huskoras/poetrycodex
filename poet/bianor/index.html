Bianor of Bithynia

1st century AD (Paton’s date)

Bianor of Bithynia is one of the epigrammatists whose short poems were gathered in the Greek Anthology. Paton's index of authors dates the poet to 1st century AD. Paton's index lists the poet among the writers of the Wreath of Philippus, the Garland compiled in the first century AD.

This archive carries the 5 epigrams ascribed to the poet in the second volume of W. R. Paton's prose translation (Book VII: 49, 387, 388, 396, 644).
