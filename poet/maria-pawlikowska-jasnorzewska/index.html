Maria Pawlikowska-Jasnorzewska

1891–1945

Maria Pawlikowska-Jasnorzewska (1891–1945) was born in Kraków, the daughter of the painter Wojciech Kossak and sister of the writer Magdalena Samozwaniec. She became one of the best-known Polish women poets of the interwar years, a writer close to the Skamander circle, famous for short, ironic and sensuous lyrics, often only a few lines long, about love, the body, gardens and the passing of time.

Her first book, Niebieskie migdały, appeared in 1922, and Różowa magja (1924), Pocałunki (1926), Surowy jedwab (1932) and Krystalizacje (1937) followed; she also wrote plays. She left Poland after the outbreak of the Second World War and died in Manchester in 1945. This selection gives the whole of Różowa magja (1924) and the whole of Surowy jedwab (1932), in the books' original spelling.
