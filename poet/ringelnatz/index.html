German

Joachim Ringelnatz

1883–1934

Joachim Ringelnatz (born Hans Bötticher in Wurzen, Saxony, in 1883) was a German poet, cabaret performer and painter. He went to sea as a young man, worked at many trades, and served in the navy during the First World War. From 1920 he performed his verse in Munich and Berlin cabarets, and his sailor figure Kuttel Daddeldu became his best-known creation.

His poems combine nonsense, parody and plain-spoken melancholy, often in very short, rhymed forms about everyday objects, animals and small disasters. The Nazis banned his work, and he died of tuberculosis in Berlin in 1934. This selection draws on the TextGrid Digitale Bibliothek edition of his collected poems.
