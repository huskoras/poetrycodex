Tomás Antônio Gonzaga

1744–1810

Tomás Antônio Gonzaga (1744-1810) was a Portuguese-born magistrate and poet of the Brazilian Arcadian school, who studied law at Coimbra and served as a judge in Minas Gerais, where he took the pastoral name Dirceu.

Accused of taking part in the Inconfidência Mineira conspiracy of 1789, he was imprisoned in Rio de Janeiro and in 1792 sentenced to exile in Mozambique, where he lived until his death. His lyrics to Marília, Marília de Dirceu, were published in Lisbon in parts from 1792 to 1799 and remained among the most widely read poems in the Portuguese language.
