Romantic

Leigh Hunt

1784–1859

Leigh Hunt (1784–1859) was an English poet, essayist and editor, best remembered today as the friend and champion of Keats and Shelley. As editor of The Examiner he was imprisoned from 1813 to 1815 for libelling the Prince Regent, and he received visitors in his cell.

His own poems range from the narrative Story of Rimini (1816) to light verse, sonnets and short lyrics. “Abou Ben Adhem” and “Jenny Kiss’d Me” are the two that most readers know.
