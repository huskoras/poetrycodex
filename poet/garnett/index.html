Richard Garnett

1835–1906

Richard Garnett (1835–1906) was an English librarian, scholar, biographer and poet. He worked in the British Museum Library from 1851 and became Keeper of Printed Books in 1890; he also wrote many biographies and contributed widely to the Dictionary of National Biography.

His own verse includes Io in Egypt and other poems (1859), the sonnets and lyrics of Poems (1893) besides the tales of The Twilight of the Gods (1888) and several ballads such as the two printed here.
