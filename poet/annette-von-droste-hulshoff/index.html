Annette von Droste-Hülshoff

1797–1848

Annette von Droste-Hülshoff (1797–1848) was a Westphalian noblewoman who became one of the major German poets of the nineteenth century. Born at Burg Hülshoff near Münster, she wrote ballads, nature lyrics and religious verse, and the novella Die Judenbuche.

Her Gedichte appeared in 1844, and Levin Schücking edited her Gesammelte Schriften in 1878–1879. The poems here are German originals taken from the German Wikisource transcriptions of those two editions.
