American

Arthur Upson

1877–1908

Arthur Upson (1877–1908) was an American poet, born at Camden, New York, and educated at Camden Academy and the University of Minnesota. He died in August 1908, at the age of about thirty-one.

His books include Westwind Songs (1902), Octaves in an Oxford Garden (1902), The City: A Poem Drama (1905) and The Tides of Spring (1907).
