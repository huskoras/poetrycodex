Modern Greek

Kostas Karyotakis

1896–1928

Kostas Karyotakis (born in Tripoli, Arcadia, in 1896) was a Greek poet who worked as a civil servant after studying law in Athens. His early books, Ο πόνος του ανθρώπου και των πραγμάτων (1919) and Νηπενθή (1921), were followed in 1927 by Ελεγεία και Σάτιρες (Elegies and Satires), his last collection, in which tender, death-haunted lyrics sit beside bitter mockery of public life, journalism, officialdom and literary vanity.

His official posts took him far from Athens, and in 1928 he was serving in Preveza, in north-western Greece, where he died on 21 July 1928, at the age of 31. The late poem Πρέβεζα, set in that provincial town, is among the last he wrote. Later Greek poets of the twentieth century often returned to his short, rhymed, disillusioned verse, and the text here follows the 1927 edition in the polytonic spelling of the book, as transcribed on Greek Wikisource.
