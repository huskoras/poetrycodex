Meng Hao-jan

689–740

Meng Hao-jan (689–740) was the inseparable friend of the poet Wang Wei. He spent the first forty years of his life in study, but having failed to obtain his doctor’s degree he returned to the hills of his native province and gave his remaining years to composition.

The poems here are L. Cranmer-Byng’s versions in A Lute of Jade (1909), given with the poet’s name as spelled in those books.
