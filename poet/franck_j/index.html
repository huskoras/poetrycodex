German

Johann Franck

1618–1677

Johann Franck was born at Guben in Lusatia in 1618 and died there in 1677. A lawyer, he served as burgomaster of Guben and as its representative in the Lusatian diet, and wrote hymns and occasional verse alongside his public duties.

The English versions here are Catherine Winkworth's in Lyra Germanica (1855).

Poems (3)

  1. Let who will in thee rejoice
  2. Redeemer of the nations, come
  3. Light of the Gentile world
