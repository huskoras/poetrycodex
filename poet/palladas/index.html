Ancient Greek & Roman

Palladas of Alexandria

4th century AD

Palladas of Alexandria was a grammarian and the last great epigrammatist of pagan Alexandria, known for bitter, satirical verse. The editor dates him to the fourth century AD; Paton's index says the fifth.

The epigrams here are those that Paton's first volume ascribes to him.
