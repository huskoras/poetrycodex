Adam Asnyk

1838–1897

Adam Asnyk (1838–1897) was a Polish poet and dramatist of the Positivist era, born in Kalisz and long resident in Kraków. He also published under the pseudonym El...y. His lyric verse, with its clear, song-like diction, made him among the most widely read Polish poets of his generation; the poem “Do młodych” (To the Young) became a much-quoted statement of the Positivist outlook.

The poems here are Polish originals from Wolne Lektury, set from Pisma, vol. III (ed. Ferdynand Hoesick, Warszawa 1924), together with four short lyrics taken from the 1898 Gebethner i Wolff edition of the Poezye. They include the cycle of sonnets Nad głębiami (Over the Depths) and the sonnet sequence Ze sceny świata (From the Stage of the World), each sonnet kept as a separate poem.
