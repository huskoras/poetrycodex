Victorian

William Larminie

1849–1900

William Larminie (1849–1900) was an Irish poet and folklorist, born in Castlebar, County Mayo, who died at Bray, County Wicklow. He drew his subjects from Irish legend and from the landscape of the west, and later collected and published the folk-tales of the western counties.

His books were Glanlua and Other Poems (1889) and Fand and Other Poems (1892), the latter with the long dramatic poem Moytura. In a note to the second he explains that he often uses assonance instead of rhyme, in the manner of the Irish Gaelic bards, and the lineation of these poems keeps that experiment.
