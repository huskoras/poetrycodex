Ernest Rhys

1859–1946

Ernest Rhys (1859–1946) was a Welsh-descended poet, editor and critic, born in London and brought up partly in Carmarthen. He founded and edited Everyman's Library for J. M. Dent, and his own poems appeared in the Rhymers' Club books and in A London Rose and Other Rhymes (1894).

Lyra Celtica calls him the leader of the neo-Celtic movement in Wales and prints 'The Night Ride' and part of 'The House of Hendra'.
