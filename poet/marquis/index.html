American

Don Marquis

1878–1937

Don Marquis (1878–1937) was an American humorist, born in Walnut, Illinois, who wrote newspaper columns in New York.

He is best remembered for archy and mehitabel, comic verses supposedly typed by a cockroach, but he also wrote serious poems.
