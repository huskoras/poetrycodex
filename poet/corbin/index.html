Alice Corbin (Henderson)

1881–1949

Alice Corbin Henderson (1881–1949) was an American poet and editor, born in St. Louis, who helped edit Poetry magazine in Chicago before moving to New Mexico.

She published as Alice Corbin; her books include The Spinning Woman of the Sky (1912) and Red Earth (1920).
