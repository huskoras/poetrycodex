Anna Maria Lenngren

1754–1817

Anna Maria Lenngren (born Malmstedt, 1754–1817) was a Swedish poet of the Gustavian age, the daughter of a professor at Uppsala. In 1780 she married Carl Peter Lenngren, who edited the Stockholm newspaper Stockholms Posten, where much of her verse first appeared without her name.

Her poems are light, exact and often satirical: portraits of vain countesses, idle gentlemen and anxious parents, fables and imitations of La Fontaine and Voltaire, drinking songs, and a few quiet elegies. They were gathered after her death as Skaldeförsök (1819) and reprinted many times as Samlade skaldeförsök. The Swedish texts here follow the 12th edition of 1890, with notes by Karl Warburg, and the 1912 volume Gustavianernas poesi in Sveriges national-litteratur; the English versions are by Charles Wharton Stork (1917).
