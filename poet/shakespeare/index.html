William Shakespeare

1564-1616

William Shakespeare (1564-1616) was an English poet and playwright from Stratford-upon-Avon, widely regarded as the greatest writer in the English language.

Beyond his plays, his non-dramatic verse includes the 154 Sonnets, the narrative poems Venus and Adonis and The Rape of Lucrece, and the shorter pieces collected as The Passionate Pilgrim.
