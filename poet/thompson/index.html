Victorian

Francis Thompson

1859–1907

Francis Thompson (1859–1907) was an English poet who spent years destitute and addicted to opium on the streets of London before his talent was discovered and he found refuge among Catholic friends.

His best-known poem, The Hound of Heaven, describes a soul fleeing and finally yielding to divine pursuit.
