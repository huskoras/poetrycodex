John Davies of Hereford

c. 1565–1618

John Davies of Hereford (c. 1565–1618) was an Anglo-Welsh poet and the writing-master of Hereford, later teaching penmanship in Oxford and London. He added "of Hereford" to his name to distinguish himself from his contemporary Sir John Davies, the author of Nosce Teipsum, who has a separate entry in this archive.

The Muses Sacrifice, or Divine Meditations (1612) gathers his devotional verse: meditations and penitential poems, versions of the seven penitential psalms in The Dolefull Dove, funeral elegies in Obit-Rights, and a long run of fourteen-line Essaies. It is dedicated to the Countesses of Bedford and Pembroke and to Lady Cary.
