Henry Howard, Earl of Surrey

1517–1547

Henry Howard, Earl of Surrey, was an English nobleman and poet who, alongside his friend Sir Thomas Wyatt, helped pioneer the English sonnet and was the first to write English verse in blank verse.

He was executed for treason in the last weeks of Henry VIII’s reign, at the age of twenty-nine.
