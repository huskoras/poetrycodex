Augusto dos Anjos

1884–1914

Augusto de Carvalho Rodrigues dos Anjos (1884–1914) was a Brazilian poet born in Paraíba, in the north-east of the country. He studied law at Recife and worked as a teacher in Paraíba before moving to Rio de Janeiro, where he continued to teach.

His only book in his lifetime, Eu (1912), brought together the poems on which his reputation rests: sonnets and longer poems that set the vocabulary of nineteenth-century science (evolution, monism, physiology) against themes of decay, death and pity. He died of pneumonia at Leopoldina, Minas Gerais, in 1914; a larger posthumous edition followed in 1920. He is usually placed with Brazilian pre-Modernism.
