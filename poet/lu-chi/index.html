Lu Chi

dates not given

Lu Chi is a Chinese poet named in Charles Budd’s Chinese Poems (1912). Budd, who spells the name Luh-Ki, gives no dates or note for him.

The poems here are Charles Budd’s versions in Chinese Poems (1912), given under the poet’s name as Budd romanises it.
