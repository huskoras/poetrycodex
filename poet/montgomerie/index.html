Tudor & Elizabethan

Alexander Montgomerie

c. 1545–1598

Alexander Montgomerie was a Scottish Jacobean courtier and makar, master poet at the court of James VI, best known for the allegorical dream-poem ‘The Cherrie and the Slae.’

A Catholic at a Protestant court, he later fell from royal favour over his religion and political dealings.
