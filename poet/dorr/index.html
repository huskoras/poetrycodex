American

Julia C. R. Dorr

1825–1913

Julia Caroline Ripley Dorr (1825–1913) was an American poet, novelist and travel writer, born in Charleston, South Carolina, and long associated with Vermont, whose centennial and landscape she celebrated in verse. She was best known for her poetry, which is gentle, devotional and carefully shaped, and which was widely printed in the American magazines of her day.

This selection follows her Complete Edition of Poems (Scribner, 1892), which gathers her earlier books in their own sections, and her later volume Afterglow (Scribner, 1900). Each book opens with a verse dedication to “S. M. D.”, her husband, and both dedications are kept here as poems.
