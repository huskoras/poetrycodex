Tu Fu

712–770

Tu Fu (712–770), whom his countrymen called the God of Verse, was the friend of Li Po and a master of craftsmanship. Cranmer-Byng describes his work as underlain by a continual sadness, and notes his poems of rural life and of the horrors of civil war.

The poems here are L. Cranmer-Byng’s versions in A Lute of Jade (1909), given with the poet’s name as spelled in those books.
