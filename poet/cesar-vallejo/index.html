César Vallejo

1892–1938

César Vallejo (1892–1938), born in Santiago de Chuco in the Peruvian Andes, is one of the great poets of the Spanish language. He studied in Trujillo and moved to Lima, where he published his first book, Los heraldos negros (1918), a work that joins late modernista manner to a personal vein of grief and doubt.

Trilce (1922), written in part during a spell in prison in Trujillo in 1920–21, broke radically with convention in its syntax, vocabulary and typography and is a landmark of the Latin American avant-garde. He settled in Paris in 1923, never returned to Peru, and died there in 1938. His posthumous collections, Poemas humanos and España, aparta de mí este cáliz, are not included here.
