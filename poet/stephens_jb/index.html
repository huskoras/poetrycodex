Victorian

James Brunton Stephens

1835–1902

James Brunton Stephens (1835–1902) was a Scottish-born poet who emigrated to Queensland in 1866 and worked as a teacher and civil servant.

His books include Convict Once (1871) and Miscellaneous Poems; his poem The Dominion of Australia (1877) looked forward to federation.
