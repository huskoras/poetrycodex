Mukai Kyorai

1651–1704

向井去来 (Mukai Kyorai, 1651–1704) was a disciple of Matsuo Bashō and one of the leading poets of his school. He kept a hermitage, the Rakushisha, at Saga near Kyoto, and with Nozawa Bonchō edited the school's anthology Sarumino (1691).

These are the hokku attributed to Kyorai in Basil Hall Chamberlain's study of Bashō and the Japanese epigram (1902), each given in romanised Japanese with Chamberlain's English rendering.
