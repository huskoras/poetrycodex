Restoration & Augustan

John Dryden

1631–1700

John Dryden (1631–1700) was England’s dominant literary figure of the later 17th century — poet, satirist, playwright, and critic — and was made the first official Poet Laureate in 1668.

His satires — Absalom and Achitophel, Mac Flecknoe, The Medal — and his public odes set the model for Augustan verse that Pope and Swift would inherit.
