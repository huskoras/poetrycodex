Romantic

James Clarence Mangan

1803–1849

James Clarence Mangan was born in Dublin and worked as a scrivener's clerk and library assistant. He contributed poems and translations, many of them English versions from the Irish, to The Nation and other periodicals.

He lived in poverty and ill health and died of cholera in 1849. His "Dark Rosaleen" is the most widely known of his poems.
