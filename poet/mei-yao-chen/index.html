Mei Yao-ch’en

1002–1060

Mei Yao-ch’en (梅堯臣; Mei Yaochen, 1002–1060), styled Sheng-yü and known as Master Wan-ling, was a poet of the Northern Sung dynasty. A long-serving minor official, he was a close friend of Ou-yang Hsiu, who praised his verse, and is remembered as a master of the plain, unadorned style that turned Sung poetry away from the ornate manner of the preceding age.

The 40 poems here are taken from the selection of his work in Wu Chih-chen’s Sung shih ch’ao (1671), in the traditional characters of the chinese-poetry dataset, with a pinyin romanisation added to each title.
