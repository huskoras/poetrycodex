Sophronius the Patriarch

date not given by Paton

Sophronius the Patriarch is one of the epigrammatists whose short poems were gathered in the Greek Anthology. Paton's index of authors gives no date for the poet.

This archive carries the 2 epigrams ascribed to the poet in the second volume of W. R. Paton's prose translation (Book VII: 679, 680).
