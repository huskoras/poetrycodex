António Ferreira

1528–1569

António Ferreira was born in Lisbon in 1528 and studied at Coimbra, where he came under the influence of Sá de Miranda. He was a judge in Lisbon and is counted the chief poet of the Portuguese classical school, which took its forms and its measured, Horatian manner from Italy and from antiquity.

He wrote sonnets, odes, elegies and epistles, and the tragedy Castro, on the murdered Inês de Castro, one of the first regular tragedies of the Peninsula. He died of the plague at Lisbon in 1569. This selection gives a sonnet on the death of his wife, an ode to Sá de Meneses and a chorus from Castro.

Poems (3)

  2. Ode a António de Sá de Meneses
  3. Coro (from Castro)
