Korean

Yun Dongju

1917–1945

Yun Dongju (윤동주, 尹東柱, 1917–1945) was born in Myeongdong village near Longjing in the Korean community of northern Jiandao (Manchuria), into a Christian family. He studied at Yonhi College in Seoul, graduating in 1941, and then went to Japan, where he studied at Rikkyo University in Tokyo and afterwards at Doshisha University in Kyoto.

In July 1943 he was arrested by the Japanese police on a charge of anti-Japanese activity and was later sentenced to two years in prison. He died in Fukuoka prison in February 1945, aged twenty-seven. His poems, written in Korean under colonial rule, were gathered after his death: 하늘과 바람과 별과 시 (Heaven, Wind, Star and Poem), the first edition published by Jeongeumsa in 1948, holds thirty-one of them.
