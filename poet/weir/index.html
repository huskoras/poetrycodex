Arthur Weir

1864–1902

Arthur Weir (1864–1902) was a Montreal poet and journalist.

His volume Fleurs-de-Lys and Other Poems appeared in 1889. One of the poets chosen by W. D. Lighthall for Songs of the Great Dominion (London, 1889), an anthology of Canadian verse.
