Hsieh T’iao

5th century AD

Hsieh T’iao lived in the fifth century AD. His “Song of the Men of Chin-ling” was written for troops marching back into the capital.

The poems here are Arthur Waley’s version in A Hundred and Seventy Chinese Poems (1918), given with the poet’s name as spelled in those books.
