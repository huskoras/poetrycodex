Callicter

date unknown (Paton)

Callicter is one of the named epigrammatists whose short poems are preserved in the Greek Anthology. Paton's Index of Authors says the poet's date is unknown.

This archive carries the 9 epigrams ascribed to the poet in the fourth volume of W. R. Paton's prose translation (Loeb, 1918): Book XI: 2, 5, 6, 118, 119, 120, 121, 122, 333.
