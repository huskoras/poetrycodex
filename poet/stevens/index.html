American

Wallace Stevens

1879–1955

Wallace Stevens (1879–1955) was an American poet, born in Reading, Pennsylvania, who spent his working life as an insurance executive in Hartford, Connecticut.

“Peter Quince at the Clavier”, which this archive takes from Untermeyer’s 1921 anthology, first appeared in 1915 and was later collected in Harmonium (1923).
