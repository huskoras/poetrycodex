Russian & Slavic

Jan Kollár

1793–1852

Jan Kollár (Ján Kollár, 1793–1852) was a Slovak poet who wrote in Czech, a Protestant minister and a leading voice of Slavic literary solidarity. Bowring calls him John Kollár and describes him as a Bohemian minister at Pesth, in Hungary.

His sonnet cycle Slávy dcera (The Daughter of Sláva) joins love for his Mína to grief and hope for the Slavic peoples. Bowring translated a large selection, keeping the sonnet form; the numbered sonnets here keep Kollár's numbering, and seven further sonnets Bowring received in manuscript are unnumbered.
