American

John Shaw

1778–1809

John Shaw (1778–1809) was an American physician and poet, born in Annapolis, Maryland, and a graduate of St. John's College, who served as a ship's surgeon and died abroad.

Griswold included 3 of his poems in The Poets and Poetry of America (16th edition, Philadelphia: Parry & McMillan, 1855), the anthology from which the poems here are taken.
