Victorian

Richard Chenevix Trench

1807–1886

Richard Chenevix Trench (1807–1886) was an Anglican churchman who became Archbishop of Dublin, and also a poet and philologist.

The poems here are taken from Alfred H. Miles (ed.), The Poets and the Poetry of the Century (London: Hutchinson, 1891–1897), vols. 4, 10, from the Internet Archive scans of that edition. Spelling is that of the anthology; Miles's biographical essay and extracts from longer poems are omitted, and only poems that could be read cleanly from the scan are included.

Poems (13)

  2. The Kingdom of God
  3. A wretched thing it were, to have our heart
  4. As when unto a mother, having chid
  5. Returning Home
  6. What, many times I musing asked, is man.
  7. This Did Not Once So Trouble Me
  8. Gibraltar
  9. If there had anywhere appeared in space
  10. O life, O death, O world, O time
  11. After the Battle
  12. All beautiful things bring sadness, nor alone
  13. Harmosan
