Innokenty Annensky

1855–1909

Innokenty Fyodorovich Annensky (1855–1909) was a Russian poet, critic, classical scholar and schoolmaster. He taught Greek and Latin, directed the gymnasium at Tsarskoye Selo and translated the whole of Euripides into Russian, and he published his first book of poems, Quiet Songs (Тихие песни, 1904), under the pseudonym 'Nik. T-o' (Nobody).

He is counted among the forerunners of Russian Symbolism and Acmeism: his compressed, anguished lyrics of fear, boredom, music and the inner life were little noticed in his lifetime. He died suddenly of a heart attack at the Tsarskoye Selo railway station in 1909, and his second book, The Cypress Chest (Кипарисовый ларец), appeared posthumously in 1910, arranged by his son. This selection takes poems from both books, in book order, preceded by five of his best-known lyrics.
