Martin Codax

fl. c. 1250

Martin Codax was a Galician jogral, or minstrel, who seems to have come from Vigo, on the Atlantic coast of Galicia, and who was active around the middle of the thirteenth century. Nothing is known of his life beyond his name and the songs that bear it.

His seven cantigas de amigo, in which a girl speaks of her absent lover, survive in a single parchment sheet, the Vindel parchment, and six of them carry their melodies, which makes them rare witnesses to the music of the Galician-Portuguese lyric. The poems form a cycle around the sea and the shrine at Vigo, and the best known, 'Ondas do mar de Vigo', is among the most loved songs of the medieval Iberian tradition.

Poems (7)

  1. Ondas do mar de Vigo
  2. Mandad’ ei comigo
  3. Mia irmana fremosa
  4. Ai Deus, sab’ ora meu amigo
  5. Quantas sabedes amar amigo
  6. En o sagrad’ en Vigo
  7. Ai ondas que eu vin veer
