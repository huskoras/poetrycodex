Theodor Storm

1817–1888

Theodor Storm (1817–1888), born in Husum on the North Sea coast of Schleswig, trained as a lawyer and worked as an advocate and judge in Husum, Potsdam and Heiligenstadt. He is best remembered for the novellas Immensee and Der Schimmelreiter, but he began and remained a lyric poet, and the short, songlike poems of his Sommergeschichten und Lieder (1851) made his name.

Storm's verse is brief, musical and close to folk song, turning on love, loss, the passing of time, the heath and the sea. He also wrote a few poems in his native Low German, and after Denmark's rule over Schleswig-Holstein he left Husum for Prussian service in 1853. The texts here follow the poems as printed in his lifetime, as transcribed on the German Wikisource.
