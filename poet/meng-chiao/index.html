Meng Chiao

c. AD 790

Meng Chiao (Meng Jiao) was a poet of the T'ang dynasty. Ayscough and Lowell's Contents dates him to circa A.D. 790; the editor's reference dates for him are about 751-814.

The archive holds one short poem from Fir-Flower Tablets (1921), a mother's thread sewn into a travelling son's clothes, which the book prints under the air "The Wanderer".
