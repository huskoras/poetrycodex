Albert Samain

1858–1900

Albert Samain was born in Lille in 1858 and died at Magny-les-Hameaux in 1900. He was largely self-taught and made his living as a clerk.

A Symbolist poet, he was one of the founders of the review Mercure de France. His first collection, Au jardin de l’infante (1893), won him wide recognition for its soft, melancholy music and its refined, twilit imagery.
