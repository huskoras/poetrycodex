Victorian

Dante Gabriel Rossetti

1828–1882

Dante Gabriel Rossetti (1828–1882) was an English poet and painter, a founder of the Pre-Raphaelite Brotherhood, who worked in sister arts of image and verse throughout his career.

His sonnet sequence The House of Life traces the arc of love and loss with dense, jewelled imagery, while The Blessed Damozel imagines a dead woman watching for her lover from heaven.
