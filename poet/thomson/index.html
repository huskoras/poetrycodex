Restoration & Augustan

James Thomson

1700–1748

James Thomson was a Scottish poet who made his name with The Seasons (1726-30), a landmark of eighteenth-century nature poetry.

Palgrave includes "Rule Britannia", written for the masque Alfred, and a short love song.

Major Works (3)

  1. The Castle of Indolence: An Allegorical Poem — Allegorical poem, 1748
  2. Liberty — Philosophical poem, 1735-36
  3. The Seasons — Poem sequence, 1730
