Stella Benson

1892–1933

Stella Benson (1892–1933) was an English novelist, poet and travel writer. Born in Shropshire, she was a supporter of women's suffrage, spent time in California in the 1910s, and published her early verse in the volumes I Pose (1915) and This Is the End (1917); Twenty (1918) gathered most of it together.

From 1920 she lived mainly in China, where her husband worked in the Chinese Maritime Customs Service, and her later novels and travel writing drew on those years. She also campaigned against the licensed brothels of Hong Kong. She won the Femina Vie Heureuse prize for The Far-Away Bride (published in America as Tobit Transplanted) and died of pneumonia in Tonkin in 1933.
