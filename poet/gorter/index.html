Dutch & Flemish

Herman Gorter

1864–1927

Herman Gorter (1864–1927) was a Dutch poet and, later, a political writer. Trained as a classical scholar at the University of Amsterdam, he joined the circle of the Tachtigers, the "Eighties" generation of Dutch writers who rejected the moralising verse of earlier decades in favour of intense personal sensation and musical language.

His long poem Mei (1889), a lyrical narrative of a girl's spring and death, became the founding work of the movement. He went on to write the vast Pan (1912) and, after turning to socialism and Marxism in the 1890s, became a figure of the Dutch and international left, writing political theory and propaganda alongside his poetry.
