Metaphysical & Cavalier

Richard Baxter

1615–1691

Richard Baxter (1615–1691) was an English Puritan minister, theologian and controversialist, long associated with Kidderminster in Worcestershire. A prolific author of devotional prose, he was a leading Nonconformist voice after the Restoration and suffered fines and imprisonment for his beliefs.

His metrical Paraphrase on the Psalms of David was published in 1692, the year after his death, 'left fitted for the press by his own hand', together with a handful of hymns. In it he marks his own additions to the biblical text in square brackets. Several psalms are followed by alternative versions, including one by George Herbert.
