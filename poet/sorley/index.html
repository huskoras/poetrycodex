Charles Hamilton Sorley

1895–1915

Charles Hamilton Sorley (1895–1915) was a Scottish-born English poet, educated at Marlborough College. He was commissioned in the Suffolk Regiment in 1914 and was killed near Hulluch, in France, on 13 October 1915, aged twenty.

His only book, Marlborough and Other Poems, appeared posthumously in 1916. The poems here come from Untermeyer's Modern British Poetry (1920) and from the 1916 book itself (Cambridge, third edition).
