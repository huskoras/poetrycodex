Ma'ruf al-Rusafi

1875–1945

Ma'ruf al-Rusafi (معروف الرصافي, 1875–1945) was an Iraqi poet born in Baghdad, one of the leading voices of the Arabic poetic revival of the early twentieth century. He taught and wrote in Baghdad and Istanbul and served as a deputy in the Ottoman parliament.

His verse keeps the classical qasida form but turns it to the questions of his day: political freedom, social reform, education, the condition of women and the stiffness of inherited custom. His Diwan was first printed in 1910 and reprinted in his lifetime. This selection gathers the longest poems transcribed on his Arabic Wikisource category page.
