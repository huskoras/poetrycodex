Nordic

Carl Michael Bellman

1740–1795

Carl Michael Bellman (1740–1795) was a Swedish poet, songwriter and performer, born in Stockholm. He worked in the royal court circle of Gustav III and became the best-loved voice of Stockholm's taverns, parks and archipelago in the later eighteenth century, setting his own words to tunes he borrowed or adapted and singing them to his own accompaniment.

His two great collections are Fredmans epistlar (1790), eighty-two verse epistles, and Fredmans sånger (1791). Their imagined speaker is the Stockholm watchmaker Jean Fredman and his circle, among them the fiddler Movitz and the nymph Ulla Winblad, whose comic, tender and elegiac scenes of drinking, love and death have made Bellman a national poet in Sweden. The texts here are those of the Swedish Wikisource transcriptions.
