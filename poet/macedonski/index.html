Romanian

Alexandru Macedonski

1854–1920

Alexandru Macedonski (1854–1920) was a Romanian poet, born in Bucharest, and the chief figure of Romanian Symbolism. He founded the review Literatorul in 1880 and for four decades defended a poetry of music, colour and artifice against the more traditional currents of his day, often in open quarrel with the Junimea critics.

His principal collections include Excelsior (1895) and Flori sacre (1912); his cycle of the Nopți, long meditative poems each named for a month, is his best-known work. In his last years he turned to the rondel, a fixed form with refrains, gathering them in Poema rondelurilor (1927).
