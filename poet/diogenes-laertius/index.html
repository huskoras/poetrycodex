Diogenes Laertius

3rd century AD (Paton’s date)

Diogenes Laertius, author of the Lives of Eminent Philosophers (third century AD), is credited in Paton's index with the verse epitaphs on the philosophers that fill the middle of Book VII. Paton's Index of Authors includes the poet among those of Book VII. Paton's index of authors dates the poet to 3rd century AD.

This archive carries the 38 epigrams ascribed to the poet in the second volume of W. R. Paton's prose translation (Book VII: 57, 85, 87, 88, 91, 92, 95, 96, 97, 98, 101, 102, 104, 105, 106, 107, 108, 109, 110, 111, 112, 113, 114, 115, 116, 118, 121, 122, 123, 124, 126, 127, 129, 130, 133, 620, 706, 744).
