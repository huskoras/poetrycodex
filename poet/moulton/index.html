American

Louise Chandler Moulton

1835–1908

Louise Chandler Moulton (1835–1908) was an American poet and journalist, born at Pomfret, Connecticut, and long resident in Boston. She contributed to the Boston Herald, and spent many seasons in London, where she knew leading writers of the day.

Her verse, collected in several volumes, is lyrical and often melancholy.
