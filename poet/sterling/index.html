American

George Sterling

1869–1926

George Sterling (1869–1926) was an American poet, born in Sag Harbor, New York, and a central figure of the bohemian literary circle of San Francisco and Carmel.

His books include The Testimony of the Suns (1903) and A Wine of Wizardry (1909).
