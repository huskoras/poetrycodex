American

Duncan Campbell Scott

1862–1947

Duncan Campbell Scott (1862–1947), born in Ottawa, was a civil servant in the Department of Indian Affairs and one of the Confederation poets.

His first collection, The Magic House and Other Poems, appeared in 1893; the poems here predate it. One of the poets chosen by W. D. Lighthall for Songs of the Great Dominion (London, 1889), an anthology of Canadian verse.
