Indian

Zahir Dehlvi

1835–1911

Zahir is described in the book as a well-known modern poet who lived at Rampur at the court of Nawab Kalbe Ali Khan, afterwards at the court of the Nawab of Tonk, and finally at Hyderabad, in the literary circle of the Nizam, by whom he was much appreciated and rewarded.

The 2 pieces given here are drawn from Hindustani Lyrics (London: The Sufi Publishing Society, 1919), in the English verse of Inayat Khan and Jessie Duncan Westbrook. They are free renderings, not literal translations, and the original Urdu poems are untitled; each is named here by its first line.

Poems (2)

  1. Whence did the yearning of the soul arise
  2. Thy beauty flashes like a sword
