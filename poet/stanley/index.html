Metaphysical & Cavalier

Thomas Stanley

1625–1678

Thomas Stanley (1625–1678) was an English poet, translator and scholar, remembered for his History of Philosophy (1655–62) and for his edition of Aeschylus. Educated at Cambridge, he lived in London and moved among Royalist writers such as Shirley and Fanshawe.

His own poems, first printed in 1647 and reprinted in 1651 and 1656, are polished, graceful love lyrics addressed mostly to a lady called Chariessa or Celia. This selection takes his original poems from George Saintsbury's Minor Poets of the Caroline Period, vol. III (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1921) and leaves out the pieces that Stanley translated from other poets.
