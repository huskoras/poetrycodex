Victorian

Gerald Massey

1828–1907

Gerald Massey (1828–1907) was an English poet of working-class origins, born at Tring in Hertfordshire. His early collections, such as Poems and Chansons (1846) and The Ballad of Babe Christabel (1854), won him a wide readership.

The poems here come from The Poetical Works of Gerald Massey (1861) and My Lyrical Life (1889).
