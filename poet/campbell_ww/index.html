William Wilfred Campbell

1860–1918

William Wilfred Campbell (1860–1918), born at Newmarket, Ontario, was an Anglican clergyman who later became a civil servant and one of the Confederation poets.

His Lake Lyrics and Other Poems appeared in 1889. One of the poets chosen by W. D. Lighthall for Songs of the Great Dominion (London, 1889), an anthology of Canadian verse.
