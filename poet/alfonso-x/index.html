Alfonso X of Castile

1221–1284

Alfonso X (1221–1284), called El Sabio, the Learned, was king of Castile and León from 1252. He gathered scholars, translators and jurists at his court, and under his patronage appeared works of law, history, astronomy and games that helped to make Castilian a language of learning.

For his poetry he used Galician-Portuguese, then the language of lyric in the Peninsula. He is the author or inspirer of the Cantigas de Santa Maria, over four hundred songs in praise of the Virgin and in narration of her miracles, preserved in richly illuminated manuscripts, and he also left a few secular cantigas, including satirical ones.
