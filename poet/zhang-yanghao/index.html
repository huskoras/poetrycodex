Zhang Yanghao

1270–1329

Zhang Yanghao (1270–1329), courtesy name Xiyun, was a Yuan official from Jinan who rose to high office as a censor and Hanlin academician, and is counted among the leading writers of sanqu, the Yuan dynasty's tune-based song verse.

In 1329 he was called out of retirement to organise famine relief in Shaanxi, where he died within months. The sanqu on Tongguan, written on that journey, closes on the line that people suffer whether a dynasty rises or falls.
