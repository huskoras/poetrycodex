Victorian

Jean Ingelow

1820–1897

Jean Ingelow (1820–1897) was an English poet and novelist, immensely popular in her lifetime, admired for narrative ballads and devotional lyrics of home, sea, and childhood.

Her poem The High Tide on the Coast of Lincolnshire (1571), a ballad of a great flood, remained a schoolroom favourite for generations.
