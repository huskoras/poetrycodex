Clemens Brentano

1778–1842

Clemens Brentano was born in 1778 at Ehrenbreitstein, near Koblenz, into a Frankfurt merchant family of Italian descent, and died in 1842 at Aschaffenburg. His sister Bettina married Achim von Arnim.

He belonged to the Heidelberg circle of German Romantics, and with Achim von Arnim he collected and reworked folk songs in the three-volume Des Knaben Wunderhorn (1805–08). His own poems, among them the ballad of the Lore Lay from his novel Godwi (1801), shaped the legend of the Rhine maiden and later song settings.
