German

Andreas Gryphius

1616–1664

Andreas Gryphius (Andreas Greif) was born at Glogau in Silesia in 1616 and died there in 1664. Orphaned young and driven from place to place by the Thirty Years' War, he studied at Danzig and Leiden, travelled in France and Italy, and in 1650 settled in Glogau as syndic of the Silesian estates.

He is the great poet and tragedian of the German Baroque. His sonnets, first collected in 1637 and revised for the editions of 1643, 1650, 1658 and 1663, turn on the vanity of earthly things, the horrors of war and the hope of salvation; 'Es ist alles eitel' and 'Thränen des Vaterlandes' are among the best-known German poems of the seventeenth century. The texts here follow the Wikisource transcriptions of the 1658 and 1663 printings, with the original spelling.
