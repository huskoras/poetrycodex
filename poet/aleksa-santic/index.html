Aleksa Šantić

1868–1924

Aleksa Šantić (Алекса Шантић, 1868–1924) was a Herzegovinian Serb poet born and long resident in Mostar, where he worked in the family's trading business and edited the literary magazine Zora (1896–1901). His lyrics combine the urban, partly Oriental culture of Mostar with love poetry, nostalgia, social protest and the national aspirations of the Serbs under Austro-Hungarian rule.

His best-known poems include the sevdah-style love song "Емина", the patriotic "Остајте овдје" and "Моја отаџбина", and the evocative "Вече на шкољу". His collected Пјесме appeared with the Serbian Literary Cooperative in 1911; he became a member of the Serbian Royal Academy in 1914. The selection here is from his lifetime collections, in the Cyrillic and ijekavian spelling of the Serbian Wikisource transcriptions.
