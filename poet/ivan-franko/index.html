Russian & Slavic

Ivan Franko

1856–1916

Іван Якович Франко (1856–1916) was a Ukrainian poet, prose writer, scholar, translator and public figure of Austrian Galicia, born in the village of Nahuievychi near Drohobych and active chiefly in Lviv. He studied at Lviv University, was imprisoned for his political and socialist activity in the late 1870s and again in 1889, and took a doctorate at Vienna in 1893.

З вершин і низин ("From Peaks and Lowlands") was his first collection of verse, first printed in 1887; the enlarged edition of 1893, published in Lviv by his wife Olha Franko, is the text given here. It includes the Prison Sonnets written in 1889, the hymn that opens the book ("Вічний революціонер") and "Каменярі" ("The Stonebreakers"), a poem of 1878 that became a byword for the labour and sacrifice of a national cause.
