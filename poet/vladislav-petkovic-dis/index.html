Vladislav Petković Dis

1880-1917

Vladislav Petković Dis (Владислав Петковић Дис, 1880–1917) was a Serbian poet, a leading figure of Serbian Symbolism and Impressionism at the turn of the twentieth century. He worked as a teacher and as a customs official, and wrote under the pen-name Dis. His poetry is dark and musical, preoccupied with death, solitude, the dissolving of the self and the memory of a lost love.

His first book, Утопљене душе (Drowned Souls, 1911), made his reputation, and a second collection, Ми чекамо цара (We Await the Emperor, 1913), answered the Balkan Wars in patriotic verse. He died in 1917, drowned when the ship on which he was travelling was torpedoed in the Ionian Sea. The poems here are his Serbian originals, in Cyrillic.
