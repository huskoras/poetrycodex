Hartley Coleridge

1796–1849

Hartley Coleridge was an English poet and essayist, the eldest son of Samuel Taylor Coleridge. He spent much of his life in the Lake District, writing sonnets and short lyrics.

Palgrave includes one of his lyrics, "She is not fair to outward view".
