Anonymous (Breton songs of the Barzaz Breiz, as edited by La Villemarqué)

songs collected and edited 1839–1846

Barzaz Breiz, “Bards of Brittany”, is the best-known collection of Breton popular songs. It was gathered, edited and published by Théodore Hersart de La Villemarqué (1815–1895), first in 1839 and then in enlarged editions up to the fourth of 1846, from which these Breton texts are taken. Each song is printed there in Breton on the left-hand page, with La Villemarqué’s French translation facing it.

La Villemarqué did not simply write down what peasants sang to him. He collected, reworked and in part composed these songs, smoothing verses, joining versions and adding lines of his own, and he later withheld his manuscript notebooks. This dispute, the “Barzaz Breiz affair”, ran through the nineteenth century and was largely settled against him in the 1960s and 1970s, when his notebooks were found to differ from his printed texts. The songs are therefore filed here as “Breton songs as edited by La Villemarqué”, not as untouched folk tradition, and not under his name alone. Only the Breton text is given; his French translations, arguments and notes are left out.

The selection takes the first fifty songs, in the order of the 1846 edition, that Wikisource presents in a bilingual Breton and French layout. Each title is the Breton title as printed, followed by La Villemarqué’s French title in parentheses. The dialect each song was collected in (Cornouaille, Léon, Tréguier, Vannes) is named in the edition and is not repeated here.
