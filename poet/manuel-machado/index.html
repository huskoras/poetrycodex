Manuel Machado

1874–1947

Manuel Machado Ruiz (Seville, 1874 – Madrid, 1947) was a Spanish poet, the elder brother of Antonio Machado. He grew up in Seville and Madrid, studied at the Institución Libre de Enseñanza, and spent time in Paris, where he knew the French Symbolists and Parnassians and translated Verlaine.

His books Alma (1902), Caprichos (1905) and Cante hondo (1912) made him one of the leading voices of Spanish Modernismo, joining elegant, melancholy cosmopolitanism with the Andalusian popular song. He also wrote plays with his brother, worked as a librarian and archivist, and was elected to the Real Academia Española in 1938.
