Nordic

Viktor Rydberg

1828–1895

Viktor Rydberg (1828–1895) was a Swedish writer, journalist and scholar, born in Jönköping and educated at Lund. Best known in his lifetime for historical novels such as Singoalla and Den siste atenaren, and for studies of religion and mythology, he became a professor at Stockholm and a member of the Swedish Academy.

His poems, gathered in Dikter (1882) and a second collection (1891), join an idealist philosophy to a classical and Nordic imagination. "Tomten" (1881), the quiet midwinter portrait of the farm's guardian goblin, became one of Sweden's best-loved poems, while the cantata for Uppsala's jubilee of 1877 and the satire "Den nya Grottesången" show his public and critical voice.
