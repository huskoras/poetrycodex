American

Henry van Dyke

1852–1933

Henry van Dyke (1852–1933) was an American clergyman, professor and poet, born at Germantown, Pennsylvania. He was a Presbyterian minister in New York, a professor of English literature at Princeton, and United States minister to the Netherlands and Luxembourg from 1913 to 1916.

He wrote many volumes of verse and essays, and the story The Other Wise Man (1895). His nature lyrics on American birds were among his most anthologised poems.
