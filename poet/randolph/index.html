Metaphysical & Cavalier

Thomas Randolph

1605–1635

Thomas Randolph (1605–1635) was an English poet and dramatist, a celebrated wit at Cambridge and an adopted ‘son’ of Ben Jonson.

He died at twenty-nine; his collected Poems appeared three years later.
