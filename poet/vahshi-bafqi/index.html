Vahshi Bafqi

1532–1583

وحشی بافقی (Vahshi Bafqi), whose given name was Kamal al-Din, took the pen-name Vahshi, 'the wild one'. He was born in Bafq, in the province of Yazd, in the first half of the tenth century of the Hijra (the sixteenth century CE), and he is counted among the finest Persian poets of the Safavid age. He travelled little beyond Kashan and Iraq, and he is buried in Yazd.

His surviving work is a divan of ghazals, qasidas and other forms, and the masnavis Khuld-e Barin, Nazir o Manzur and Farhad o Shirin, the last left unfinished at his death and completed long afterwards by Vesal of Shiraz. The poems here are sixty of the divan's best-known pieces, in Persian, chosen by the number of recitations Ganjoor records for them; the text comes from Ganjoor (ganjoor.net).
