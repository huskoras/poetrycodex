Alfred de Musset

1810–1857

Alfred de Musset (1810–1857) was a French poet, playwright and prose writer, born in Paris and among the youngest of the Romantic generation. His first book, Contes d’Espagne et d’Italie (1830), made his name when he was nineteen.

His best-known poems, among them the four Nuits (1835–37), Rolla (1833) and the Lettre à M. de Lamartine (1836), turn on love, loss and the poet’s calling; among his plays is Lorenzaccio (1834). He entered the Académie française in 1852. This selection is drawn from his Premières poésies and Poésies nouvelles.
