al-Hutay'a

d. c. 665

الحطيئة, known as al-Hutay'a, was an Arab poet who lived through the transition from pre-Islamic to early Islamic times; he died c. 665.

He is remembered for his praise poems and for satire, including verses against his own family. The poems here are the Arabic originals, transcribed on Arabic Wikisource.

  57. ألم تر أن ذبيانا وعبسا (Alam tara anna Dhubyānan wa-ʿAbsan)
