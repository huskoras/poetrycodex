Ancient Greek & Roman

Leonidas of Tarentum

fl. early 3rd century BC

Leonidas of Tarentum, a Hellenistic epigrammatist of the early third century BC, is known for epitaphs and for dedications by poor country folk and craftsmen, often with a touch of humour about poverty.

In this archive every epigram that Paton heads simply “Leonidas” is filed here; the isopsepha of Leonidas of Alexandria (Book VI, 321–329) are not included.
