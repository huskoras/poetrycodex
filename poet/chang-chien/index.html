Ch’ang Ch’ien

c. AD 720

Ch’ang Ch’ien (c. AD 720) was, according to Cranmer-Byng, a philosopher-poet of the Taoist school who spent his life far from the court, trying to set himself in harmony with the universe.

The poems here are L. Cranmer-Byng’s version in A Lute of Jade (1909), given with the poet’s name as spelled in those books.

Poems (4)

  1. A Night on the Mountain
  2. Dhyana
  3. The Tomb of Chao-Chün
  4. On the Lake near the Western Mountains
