Metaphysical & Cavalier

Ben Jonson

1572-1637

Ben Jonson (1572-1637) was an English poet and playwright, the dominant literary figure of the Jacobean stage and a rival and friend of Shakespeare.

Beyond his comedies and tragedies, he wrote a substantial body of lyric and occasional verse, including epigrams and elegies collected here from a posthumous volume of his poems and prose "Discoveries."
