German

Martin Luther

1483–1546

Martin Luther was born at Eisleben in 1483 and died there in 1546. An Augustinian friar and professor at the University of Wittenberg, he was the leading figure of the German Reformation, and he wrote some three dozen hymns, among them Ein feste Burg and Christ lag in Todesbanden, which helped establish congregational singing in German.

The English versions here are Catherine Winkworth's in Lyra Germanica (1855).
