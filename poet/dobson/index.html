Victorian

Austin Dobson

1840–1921

Austin Dobson (1840–1921) was an English poet and man of letters who spent his working life as a clerk in the Board of Trade. In his spare hours he became the chief English master of light verse and of the French fixed forms — the rondeau, ballade, triolet and villanelle — which he handled with polish and wit.

He was also a respected biographer and essayist of the eighteenth century, writing lives of Hogarth, Fielding and Goldsmith, among others. His Collected Poems appeared in 1895.
