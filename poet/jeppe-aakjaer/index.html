Nordic

Jeppe Aakjær

1866–1930

Jeppe Aakjær (1866–1930) was a Danish poet and novelist, born on a farm at Aakjær in Fly parish near Skive, in Salling, in the heart of Jutland. The son of a peasant farmer, he trained as a teacher and student before turning to writing, and became the best-known voice of the Jutland regionalist movement in Danish literature.

His poems draw on the heath, the fjord, the farmer's year and the Jutlandic dialect, and often speak for the poor, tenant farmers and farm labourers, with a socialist and humanitarian edge. The collection Rugens Sange (1906) made him widely loved, and many of its songs, among them "Jens Vejmand", entered the Danish song tradition; "Jeg er Havren" and other later lyrics did the same.

He lived for many years at Jenle near Grinderslev, and died there in 1930. This selection is taken from the Kalliope edition of his collections from Derude fra Kjærene (1899) to Vejr og Vind og Folkesind (1916), with his own spelling, including the Jutlandic dialect poems, kept as printed.
