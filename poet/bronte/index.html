Victorian

Emily Brontë

1818–1848

Emily Brontë (1818–1848) is best known as the author of Wuthering Heights, but she also wrote intense, visionary lyric poetry, much of it set in the imagined world of Gondal she created with her sister Anne.

Published pseudonymously as "Ellis Bell" alongside her sisters' verse in 1846, her poems of longing, imprisonment, and the moors were far better received by contemporary critics than the sisters' novels first were.
