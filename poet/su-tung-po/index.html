Su Tung-p’o

1036–1101

Su Tung-p’o (Su Shi, 1036–1101) was one of the foremost poets of the Sung dynasty. Waley includes his well-known humorous quatrain written on the birth of a son.

The poems here are Arthur Waley’s version in A Hundred and Seventy Chinese Poems (1918), given with the poet’s name as spelled in those books.
