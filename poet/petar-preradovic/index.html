Petar Preradović

1818–1872

Petar Preradović (1818–1872) was a Croatian soldier and poet, the leading Romantic voice of the Illyrian movement. Born at Grabrovnica in Slavonia, he was educated for a military career in Wiener Neustadt and served as an officer in the Austrian army, rising in time to the rank of general. He wrote his early verse in German, and turned to Croatian under the influence of the Illyrian revival.

His first book, Pervenci (1846), collected love lyrics, patriotic songs and ballads; its poems on the mother tongue, the homeland and exile (among them Rodu o jeziku, Zora puca, bit će dana and Putnik) became canonical in Croatian literature. He died at Fahrafeld near Vienna in 1872, and his remains were later brought to Zagreb.
