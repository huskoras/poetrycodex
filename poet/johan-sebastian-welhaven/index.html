Johan Sebastian Welhaven

1807–1873

Johan Sebastian Cammermeyer Welhaven was born in Bergen on 22 December 1807 and died in Christiania (Oslo) on 21 October 1873. A poet, critic and later professor of philosophy at the university in Christiania, he was a leading voice of Norwegian national Romanticism.

In the 1830s he opposed Henrik Wergeland's exuberant manner with a more restrained, classical and reflective ideal, a quarrel that shaped Norwegian literary life. His collection Digte appeared in 1838, followed by Nyere Digte in 1844; the texts here keep the Dano-Norwegian spelling of the books.
