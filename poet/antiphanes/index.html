Antiphanes of Macedonia

Augustan age (Paton’s date)

Antiphanes of Macedonia is one of the epigrammatists whose short poems were gathered in the Greek Anthology. Paton's index of authors dates the poet to Augustan age. Paton's index lists the poet among the writers of the Wreath of Philippus, the Garland compiled in the first century AD.

This archive carries the 5 epigrams ascribed to the poet in the third volume of W. R. Paton's prose translation (Book IX: 84, 245, 256, 258, 409).
