František Ladislav Čelakovský

1799–1852

František Ladislav Čelakovský (1799–1852) was a Czech poet, translator and philologist, born in Strakonice, Bohemia. A central figure of the Czech National Revival, he studied in Linz and Prague and edited the Prague newspapers Pražské noviny and its literary supplement Česká včela.

His best-known works are the folk-inspired collections Ohlas písní ruských (Echo of Russian Songs, 1829) and Ohlas písní českých (1839), which imitate the style of Slavic folk song. He also translated widely from Slavic literatures and, after a period in Breslau, became professor of Slavic philology at the University of Prague in 1849.
