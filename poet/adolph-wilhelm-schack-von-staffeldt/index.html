Adolph Wilhelm Schack von Staffeldt

1769–1826

Adolph Wilhelm Schack von Staffeldt was born in 1769 at Gartz in Pomerania, of a German noble family, and made his career in Danish service as a civil servant and court official. Though German by birth, he wrote in Danish, and he is counted the first Danish Romantic lyricist, a poet of visionary odes, ballads and nature hymns who prized imagination over the Enlightenment manner of his predecessors.

His Digte appeared in Copenhagen in 1804 and his Nye Digte, with a large sequence of sonnets, in Kiel in 1808; the opening poem of the first book, Indvielsen, is his best-known piece. Often overshadowed by his younger rival Oehlenschläger, he was rediscovered by later generations as a master of the Danish Romantic lyric. He died at Schleswig in 1826.
