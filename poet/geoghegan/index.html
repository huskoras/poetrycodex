Arthur Gerald Geoghegan

1810–1889

Arthur Gerald Geoghegan (1810–1889), born in Dublin, wrote The Monks of Kilcrea, a series of verse stories with songs and interludes, which had reached a third edition by 1861.

The poems here are taken from H. Halliday Sparling's anthology Irish Minstrelsy (London: Walter Scott, 1888).
