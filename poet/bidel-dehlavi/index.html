Bidel Dehlavi

1642–1720

میرزا عبدالقادر بیدل دهلوی (Mirza Abd al-Qadir Bidel Dehlavi, 1642–1720) was born in Azimabad (Patna) in Bihar and died in Delhi. He wrote in Persian and is counted among the foremost poets of the Indian style (sabk-e hindi), known for dense imagery and abstract, philosophical conceits.

His works include ghazals, qasidas, ruba'is and several masnavis, together with prose writings such as the autobiographical Chahar Unsur. The poems here are ghazals from his Divan, chosen by the number of recitations and songs Ganjoor records for each.
