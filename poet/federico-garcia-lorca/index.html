Federico García Lorca

1898–1936

Federico García Lorca was born on 5 June 1898 in Fuente Vaqueros, near Granada. He studied in Granada and from 1919 lived at the Residencia de Estudiantes in Madrid, where he became friends with Salvador Dalí and Luis Buñuel. His first book of verse, Libro de poemas, appeared in 1921, and Romancero gitano (1928) made him famous across the Spanish-speaking world.

A central figure of the Generation of '27, Lorca also wrote and staged plays, among them Bodas de sangre, Yerma and La casa de Bernarda Alba, and directed the touring student theatre La Barraca. He travelled to New York and Cuba in 1929–30. He was killed in August 1936, near Granada, in the first weeks of the Spanish Civil War. The poems here are from the two books named, in their editions of 1921 and 1928.
