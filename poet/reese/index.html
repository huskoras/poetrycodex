American

Lizette Woodworth Reese

1856–1935

Lizette Woodworth Reese (1856–1935) was an American poet, born near Baltimore, Maryland, who taught English in Baltimore schools for many years.

Her first book, A Branch of May (1887), began a long career of spare, finely made lyrics, among them the sonnet “Tears”.
