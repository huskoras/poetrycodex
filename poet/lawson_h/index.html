Victorian

Henry Lawson

1867–1922

Henry Lawson (1867–1922) was an Australian poet and short-story writer, born near Grenfell, New South Wales.

His books include In the Days When the World Was Wide, and Other Verses (1896) and While the Billy Boils (1896); he is regarded as the voice of the bush and the working man.
