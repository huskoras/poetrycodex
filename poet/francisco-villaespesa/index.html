Francisco Villaespesa

1877–1936

Francisco Villaespesa Martín (Laujar de Andarax, Almería, 1877 – Madrid, 1936) was a Spanish modernista poet, dramatist and novelist. He began as a follower of Rubén Darío and made his name with books of verse such as Tristitiae rerum (1906) and Los remansos del crepúsculo (1911), together with Andalusian and Alhambra-themed collections.

Besides lyric poetry he wrote many verse plays, among them El alcázar de las perlas (1911) and Doña María de Padilla (1913). The Oxford Book of Spanish Verse (1913) prints three of his poems, and an early poem, "¡Lucha!", appeared in the review Germinal in 1897.
