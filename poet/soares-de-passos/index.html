António Augusto Soares de Passos

1826–1860

António Augusto Soares de Passos was born in Porto in 1826 and died there in 1860, at the age of thirty-three. (The Oxford Book of Portuguese Verse misprints his death year as 1870.) He was the leading poet of the Coimbra ultra-Romantic school, a generation of young writers whose verse is steeped in melancholy, longing and the shadow of death.

His ballad 'O Noivado do Sepulchro', in which a dead man rises from his grave to claim his dead beloved, is his most famous poem, and 'O Firmamento', a hymn to God and the stars, was admired enough to be chosen for the Oxford Book of Portuguese Verse (1925). The poems here are taken from his Poesias (Porto, 1856), the one collection he published, in the spelling of the original edition.
