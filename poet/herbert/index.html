Metaphysical & Cavalier

George Herbert

1593–1633

George Herbert (1593–1633) was a Welsh-born English poet and priest, a central figure of the metaphysical school.

His single collection, <em>The Temple</em> (1633), published just after his death, gathers the devotional lyrics for which he is loved.
