Yang Wan-li

1127–1206

Yang Wan-li (楊萬里, 1127–1206) was a Sung poet.

The poems here are from the Qianjiashi (千家詩, Poems of a Thousand Poets), the Song-Ming schoolroom anthology of Tang and Song verse attributed to Xie Fangde and Wang Xiang, taken from the chinese-poetry dataset (MIT); the original characters are given as printed there.
