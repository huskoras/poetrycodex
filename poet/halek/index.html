Vítězslav Hálek

1835–1874

Vítězslav Hálek (1835–1874) was a Czech poet, prose writer and journalist, a leading figure of the Czech literary generation of the 1850s and 1860s. His lyric collection Večerní písně (Evening Songs, 1859) made his name with its tender, song-like poems of love and nature.

The poems here are drawn from Joseph Štýbr's English translation of Evening Songs, published in Boston in 1920.
