Metaphysical & Cavalier

Sir John Suckling

1609–1641

Sir John Suckling (1609–1641) was an English Cavalier poet and courtier, famed for his wit and his invention of the card game cribbage.

His collected verse, Fragmenta Aurea (1646), mixes light, conversational love lyrics with plays and prose.
