Lorentzos Mavilis (Λορέντζος Μαβίλης)

1860–1912

Lorentzos Mavilis (Λορέντζος Μαβίλης, 1860–1912) was a Greek poet of the Ionian Islands, born on Ithaca on 6 September 1860 into a family of Corfiot standing and Spanish descent. He grew up in Corfu, studied in Germany, and belonged to the Heptanesian circle around Iakovos Polylas, to whom one of his sonnets is addressed. He was also known as a composer of chess problems.

He is regarded as the leading Greek sonneteer. His sonnets, written in the demotic language and dated by month and year at their foot (many in the mid-1890s), were not gathered in a book in his lifetime; they were collected after his death in the volume Τα έργα του Λορέντσου Μαβίλη (1922). He fought as a volunteer in the Garibaldian legion in the Balkan War and was killed at Driskos in Epirus on 28 November 1912.

The fifty sonnets here are taken from Greek Wikisource, which prints them from the 1922 edition and from periodical printings, in polytonic spelling as printed. The date lines at the foot of each poem are left out.
