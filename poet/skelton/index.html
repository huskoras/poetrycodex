Tudor & Elizabethan

John Skelton

c. 1463–1529

John Skelton was an English poet and tutor to the young Prince Henry, later Henry VIII, remembered for the idiosyncratic short-lined verse form later called ‘Skeltonics.’

He wrote both courtly lyrics of praise and sharp satire of clergy and court, and was crowned poet laureate by three universities.
