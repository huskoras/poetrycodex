Sir Philip Sidney

1554–1586

Sir Philip Sidney (1554–1586) was an English poet, courtier, and soldier, killed at the Battle of Zutphen at thirty-one.

His sonnet sequence Astrophel and Stella (1591) is the first great sequence in English, chronicling an unrequited, thinly-veiled autobiographical love.
