Isaac Hawkins Browne

1705–1760

Isaac Hawkins Browne was an English poet and member of parliament, born at Burton-on-Trent in 1705. He is best remembered for the Pipe of Tobacco (1736), a set of imitations of Thomson, Pope, Swift and other poets, in which he praised tobacco in their manner.

Gilfillan prints three of the imitations, of Thomson, Pope and Swift.
