Đura Jakšić

1832–1878

Đura Jakšić (1832–1878) was a Serbian poet, storyteller, dramatist and painter, born in the Banat village of Srpska Crnja and active chiefly in Belgrade, where he died young. He worked as a schoolteacher and a painter, and lived the uncertain life of a bohemian. He left more than a hundred poems, some forty tales, three verse dramas and an unfinished historical novel.

He is regarded as the greatest lyric poet of Serbian Romanticism. His verse joins patriotic anger, grief over his people's subjection and a restless private melancholy, in songs such as 'Otadžbina', 'Na Liparu' and 'Padajte, braćo!'. The texts here follow Jeremija Živanović's edition of his collected works (1931), as transcribed on Serbian Wikisource.
