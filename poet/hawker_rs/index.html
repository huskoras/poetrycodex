Robert Stephen Hawker

1803–1875

Robert Stephen Hawker (1803–1875) was an English clergyman, poet and antiquary of an old Cornish family, born at Plymouth, who was vicar of the remote Cornish parish of Morwenstow from 1834 until his death. His best-known book is Cornish Ballads (1869).

Lyra Celtica prints two of his poems, 'Featherstone's Doom' and 'Trebarrow'; this file also holds 'The Doom-Well of St Madron' from Popular British Ballads (1894).
