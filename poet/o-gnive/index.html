Celtic

O'Gnive

c. 1560

O'Gnive was the bard of Shane O'Neill, and is dated in Eleanor Hull's The Poem-Book of the Gael to about 1560.

His poem on the downfall of the Gael is given here in the English version by Sir Samuel Ferguson, as printed in Hull's anthology (London, 1913).
