Korean

Hwang Jini

c.1506-c.1560

Hwang Jini (黃眞伊, c. 1506 - c. 1560) was a kisaeng, a professional entertainer and poet, of Gaeseong in the Joseon kingdom of Korea. She was famed in her own time for her beauty, her learning and her skill in song and verse, and she became a figure of many later tales.

Only a handful of her sijo, the three-line songs of Joseon Korea, survive, preserved in the eighteenth-century anthologies. This selection takes those printed under her name in Kim Cheontaek's 청구영언 (Cheonggu yeongeon, 1728); they speak of love, longing and parting.

Poems (4)

  1. 청산리 벽계수야 (Cheongsalli byeokgyesuya)
  2. 동지ㅅ달 기나긴 밤을 (Dongjitdal ginagin bameul)
  3. 내 언제 무신하여 (Nae eonje musinhayeo)
  4. 어져 내 일이야 (Eojyeo nae iriya)
