American

Phoebe Cary

1824–1871

Phoebe Cary (1824–1871), sister of the poet Alice Cary, was an American poet and parodist.

The poems here are taken from American War Ballads and Lyrics, edited by George Cary Eggleston (New York and London: G. P. Putnam’s Sons, 1889), where it is printed under his or her name: Ready.

Many more of her poems are taken from The Poetical Works of Alice and Phoebe Cary (Household Edition, Boston and New York: Houghton, Mifflin and Company, The Riverside Press, 1888), where they stand in the second part, “Phoebe Cary’s Poems.”
