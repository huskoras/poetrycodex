Jiří Wolker

1900–1924

Jiří Wolker (1900–1924) was a Czech poet, born in Prostějov. He studied law in Prague, was a leading figure of the Devětsil avant-garde circle and the Czech proletarian poetry movement, and also wrote ballads and plays.

He suffered from tuberculosis from his youth and died at twenty-three. His first collection, Host do domu (1921), was followed by Těžká hodina (1922), which gathers his best-known ballads and lyrics of social compassion and love; Tuberkulosa and other late poems appeared after his death.
