American

Joaquin Miller

1841–1913

Joaquin Miller (the pen name of Cincinnatus Heine Miller, 1841–1913) was an American poet of the Pacific coast who styled himself the “Poet of the Sierras”.

His Songs of the Sierras (1871) won him a reputation in England as well as in the United States, and he later lived in the hills above Oakland, California.
