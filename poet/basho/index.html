Matsuo Bashō

1644–1694

松尾芭蕉 (Matsuo Bashō, 1644–1694) was born in Iga Province and died in Osaka. He raised the opening verse of the linked-verse tradition, the hokku, to a serious art, and is counted the greatest master of the form later called haiku. These are hokku from a 1903 collection arranged by season word, with its season headings kept as the collection names.
