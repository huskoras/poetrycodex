Vitalis (Erik Sjöberg)

1794–1828

Vitalis was the pen name of Erik Sjöberg (1794–1828), a Swedish poet born at Ludgo in Södermanland. He wrote under the name Vitalis, and his verse of religious feeling, nature and love made him a well-known voice of Swedish Romantic poetry before an early death from tuberculosis in Stockholm.

His poems were gathered after his death as Samlade dikter (Stockholm: Johan Hörberg, 1828), whose first part, Alfvarsamma Dikter, holds the serious poems; a second part gathers his humorous verse. Gosse and Craigie's Oxford Book of Scandinavian Verse (1925) prints two of them, 'Enslingens sång i den stora öknen' and 'Lifvet och döden'.
