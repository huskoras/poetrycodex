Korean

Kim Sowol

1902–1934

Kim Sowol (김소월 金素月, real name Kim Jeong-sik 김정식, 1902–1934) was a Korean poet born in North Pyongan Province. He is remembered as one of the first great lyric poets of modern Korean, writing in a plain, song-like idiom drawn from folk rhythms.

Azaleas (진달래꽃), published by Maemunsa in December 1925, was the only collection he issued in his lifetime. It carries his best-known poems of parting, longing and homeland, among them the title poem. He died in 1934, aged thirty-two. The texts here follow the 1925 orthography, with the old Hangul letter clusters kept as printed.
