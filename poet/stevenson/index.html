Victorian

Robert Louis Stevenson

1850–1894

Robert Louis Stevenson (1850–1894) was a Scottish novelist and poet, best known for <em>Treasure Island</em> and <em>Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde</em>, who also wrote widely loved verse.

<em>A Child's Garden of Verses</em> captures the imaginative world of childhood, while <em>Songs of Travel</em>, mostly written in his final years in Samoa, turns to restlessness, distance, and mortality.
