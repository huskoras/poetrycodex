Victorian

Robert Louis Stevenson

1850–1894

Robert Louis Stevenson (1850–1894) was a Scottish novelist and poet, best known for Treasure Island and Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde, who also wrote widely loved verse.

A Child's Garden of Verses captures the imaginative world of childhood, while Songs of Travel, mostly written in his final years in Samoa, turns to restlessness, distance, and mortality.
