Johann Peter Hebel

1760–1826

Johann Peter Hebel (1760–1826) was an Alemannic poet, prelate and schoolman of Karlsruhe. Born in Basel and raised in the village of Hausen in the Wiesental, he trained as a theologian, taught at the Lörrach gymnasium and from 1791 served at the grammar school in Karlsruhe, rising to Prälat of the Baden Protestant church in 1819.

His Allemannische Gedichte (1803) are written in the dialect of the Wiesental, the Upper Rhine country between Basel and the Black Forest, and treat village life, nature and the supernatural in a plain and musical speech. Goethe praised them in a review of 1805, and they stand beside his prose calendar stories, the Schatzkästlein des rheinischen Hausfreundes (1811), as his lasting work. This selection gives the 1803 book in its order, with two poems from the later collected edition of 1834.
