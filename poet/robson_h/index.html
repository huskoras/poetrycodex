Henry Robson

1775–1850

Henry Robson is represented here by verse printed in John Bell's Rhymes of Northern Bards (Newcastle, 1812), 4 pieces and in The Newcastle Song Book; or, Tyne-side Songster (Newcastle: W. & T. Fordyce, 1842), 10 pieces.

Bell notes that Henry Robson was born at Benwell, near Newcastle.
