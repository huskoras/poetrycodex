Sir Edwin Arnold

1832–1904

Edwin Arnold (1832–1904) was an English poet, journalist and orientalist. Educated at King's College School, London, and University College, Oxford, he became principal of the Government Sanskrit College at Poona, India, in 1856, and returned to England in 1861.

He then worked for many years on the staff of the Daily Telegraph, serving as its editor-in-chief, and was made a Knight Commander of the Star of India. His verse epic The Light of Asia (1879), a life of the Buddha, was immensely popular in Britain and America and did much to introduce Buddhism to Western readers. He also translated Indian and Persian works and wrote further poems on Eastern themes.
