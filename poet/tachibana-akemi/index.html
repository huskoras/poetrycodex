Tachibana Akemi 橘曙覧

1812–1868

Tachibana Akemi (1812–1868) was a Japanese poet of the late Edo period, born in Fukui in Echizen province. He was a scholar of national learning (kokugaku) in the tradition of Motoori Norinaga and wrote waka in plain, direct language.

He lived most of his life in poverty, declining service to the local lord. He is best remembered for the Dokurakugin, a sequence of 52 tanka each beginning with the words "tanoshimi wa" ("the pleasure is"), which celebrate small daily joys of family, reading, food and friendship.
