Hungarian

Árpád Tóth

1886–1928

Hungarian poet and translator (1886–1928), a member of the second generation of the journal Nyugat. His delicate, melancholy verse of city cafés, autumn evenings and fading love made him one of the distinctive voices of the period.

His first collection, Hajnali szerenád (Dawn Serenade), appeared in 1913 under the imprint of Nyugat; it is read here in full, in the order of the printed book.
