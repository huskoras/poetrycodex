Thomas Buchanan Read

1822–1872

Thomas Buchanan Read (1822–1872) was a Pennsylvania-born poet and portrait painter, later the author of Sheridan's Ride.

Griswold included 9 of his poems in The Poets and Poetry of America (16th edition, Philadelphia: Parry & McMillan, 1855), the anthology from which the poems here are taken.
