William Alexander, Earl of Stirling

1580–1640

William Alexander, later Earl of Stirling, was a Scottish courtier and poet who served James VI and I. He published the sonnet sequence Aurora in 1604 and a set of verse tragedies, the Monarchicke Tragedies.

Palgrave includes one poem by him, the song "To Aurora".
