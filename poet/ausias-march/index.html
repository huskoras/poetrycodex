Ausiàs March

c. 1400–1459

Ausiàs March (born c. 1400; died in Valencia, 1459) was a Valencian knight and lord of Beniarjó, who spent much of his life on his estates near Gandia and in the city of Valencia. He took part in Aragonese military expeditions in his youth.

He is the leading Catalan-language poet of the fifteenth century. Writing in his own Valencian speech rather than the Occitan of the troubadours, he turned the courtly love lyric into a probing poetry of desire, conscience and death. His cants d'amor, cants de mort, cants morals and a single cant espiritual, some 128 poems in all, were first printed in Valencia in 1539 and were read and imitated by Spanish poets of the sixteenth century.
