Na-lan Hsing-te

1655–1685

Na-lan Hsing-te (納蘭性德, Nalan Xingde, 1655–1685) was a Manchu courtier-poet of the early Qing dynasty and a master of the ci, the lyric form written to set tunes. He served at the court of the Kangxi emperor and died at thirty.

The poems here are the ci collected as the Yinshui ci (飲水詞, Nalan ci), taken from the chinese-poetry dataset (MIT); the characters are given in the simplified form of that file.
