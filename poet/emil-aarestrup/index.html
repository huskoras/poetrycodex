Emil Aarestrup

1800–1856

Emil Aarestrup (1800–1856) was a Danish physician and poet, born in Copenhagen and later settled in Odense, where he died. He trained as a doctor and wrote verse alongside his medical work.

His only collection published in his lifetime, Digte (1838), was coolly received, but its sensuous, ironic and finely turned lyrics came to be counted among the best of Danish poetry of the period.
