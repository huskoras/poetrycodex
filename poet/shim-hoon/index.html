Korean

Shim Hoon

1901–1936

Shim Hoon (심훈, 沈熏, 1901–1936) was a Korean poet, novelist and film-maker, born near Seoul. He was imprisoned in 1919 for taking part in the March First Movement, spent some time in China in the early 1920s, and afterwards worked as a journalist and in the film world. In his last years he lived at Dangjin in South Chungcheong, where he wrote the novel Sangnoksu.

He prepared a book of his verse and prose, 그날이 오면 (The Day That Comes), for the press in 1932, but the colonial censorship stopped its publication. He died in 1936, aged thirty-five, and his family printed the book as he had left it in 1949, through Hanseong Doseo.
