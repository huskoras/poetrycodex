Gonçalves Dias

1823–1864

Antônio Gonçalves Dias (Caxias, Maranhão, 1823 – 1864) was a Brazilian Romantic poet, often called the national poet of Brazil. He studied law at Coimbra, where in 1843 he wrote the "Canção do exílio", and returned to Brazil to work as a teacher, journalist and civil servant.

His Primeiros Cantos (1846), Segundos Cantos and Últimos Cantos (1851) brought Indianist themes, the landscape of Brazil and a free handling of Portuguese metres into the language of poetry; "I-Juca-Pirama" is the best known of his epic-lyric poems on the indigenous peoples. He also compiled a Tupi dictionary, and died in 1864 in a shipwreck off the coast of Maranhão on his way home from Europe.
