Antun Branko Šimić

1898–1925

Antun Branko Šimić was born in Drinovci, in Herzegovina, in 1898, and died in Zagreb in 1925, at the age of twenty-six. He is the leading poet of Croatian Expressionism and a founder of modern Croatian verse; he also edited the avant-garde review Vijavica and the journal Juriš.

Preobraženja (Zagreb, 1920), "Transfigurations", is the only book he published in his lifetime. Its short free-verse poems of night, stars, the body, prayer and the city broke with the metrical habits of earlier Croatian poetry. The poems here are the forty-two of that book.
