American

Richard Hovey

1864–1900

Richard Hovey (1864–1900) was an American poet, born in Normal, Illinois, who lectured at Columbia University.

With Bliss Carman he wrote Songs from Vagabondia (1894), and he left unfinished a cycle of dramas on the Arthurian legend.
