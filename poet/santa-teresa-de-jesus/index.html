Santa Teresa de Jesús

1515–1582

Teresa de Cepeda y Ahumada, born in Ávila in 1515, entered the Carmelite convent of the Encarnación at about twenty. After long years of illness and prayer she began, in 1562, to reform the order, founding the first house of the Discalced Carmelites at San José in Ávila and many more across Spain.

She is best known for her prose, above all the Libro de la vida and Las moradas, but she also wrote short devotional poems, often glosses and refrains meant to be sung in her convents. She died at Alba de Tormes in 1582, was canonised in 1622 and was named a Doctor of the Church in 1970.

Poems (5)

  1. Nada te turbe
  2. Si el amor que me tenéis
  3. Vivo sin vivir en mí
  4. Vuestra soy, para vos nací
  5. Yo toda me entregué y dí
