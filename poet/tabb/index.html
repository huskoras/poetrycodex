John Banister Tabb

1845–1909

John Banister Tabb (1845–1909) was an American poet and Roman Catholic priest, born in Amelia County, Virginia. He served in the Confederate army, was captured and imprisoned at Point Lookout, where he met Sidney Lanier, and later taught at St Charles College in Maryland.

He wrote very short, finished lyrics, often of four lines, on nature, childhood, and faith.
