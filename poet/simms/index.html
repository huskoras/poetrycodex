William Gilmore Simms

1806–1870

William Gilmore Simms (1806–1870) was a Charleston, South Carolina, novelist, historian and poet, author of Guy Rivers and The Yemassee.

Griswold included 7 of his poems in The Poets and Poetry of America (16th edition, Philadelphia: Parry & McMillan, 1855), the anthology from which the poems here are taken.

  2. The Brooklet
  3. The Shaded Water

  5. The Lost Pleiad
  6. The Edge of the Swamp
  7. Changes of Home
