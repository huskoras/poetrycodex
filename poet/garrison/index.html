Theodosia Garrison

1874–1944

Theodosia Garrison (1874–1944), later Mrs Frederick J. Faulks, was an American poet, born at Newark, New Jersey. She was educated at private schools in New York and contributed regularly to magazines.

Her two collections of verse are Joy o’ Life (1908) and The Earth Cry (1910).
