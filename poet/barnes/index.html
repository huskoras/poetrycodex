Victorian

William Barnes

1801–1886

William Barnes (1801–1886) was an English poet, philologist and clergyman, born at Rushay in the Vale of Blackmore, Dorset. A largely self-taught scholar, he worked as a schoolmaster in Dorchester and later became rector of Winterborne Came, near Dorchester.

He is best known for his poems in the Dorset dialect, collected in three series of Poems of Rural Life in the Dorset Dialect (1844, 1859, 1862) and gathered in one volume in 1879. Thomas Hardy, who knew him in Dorchester, admired him and wrote of his death in a poem.
