Labid

6th–7th century

Labid ibn Rabi'a (لبيد بن ربيعة) of the 'Amir ibn Sa'sa'a tribe was one of the great poets of the Jahiliyya, and his ode is counted among the Mu'allaqat. Tradition makes him the poet of the camp-ruin and the elegy, a knight and a generous host of his people, and says that he lived to a great age. The dates of his birth and death are uncertain.

He is said to have met the Prophet and to have embraced Islam, after which, according to the reports, he wrote little verse. Here his Mu'allaqa comes first, followed by the shorter pieces of his diwan that run to three or more verses. The texts are from the Arabic Wikisource transcriptions and keep the vowel marks of that source.
