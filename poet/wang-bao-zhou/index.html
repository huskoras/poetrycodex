Wang Bao 王褒 (Northern Zhou)

c. 513–576

Wang Bao (c. 513-576), courtesy name Ziyuan, was a courtier and poet of the Liang dynasty. When Liang Jiangling fell to the Western Wei in 554 he was taken north to Chang'an, where he served the Western Wei and then the Northern Zhou, and he is remembered for frontier poems written in exile.

He is not the Western Han writer Wang Bao, author of the Chuci piece Jiu huai; the two share a name and nothing else. The texts here are from zh.wikisource, drawn from the Wang sikong ji and the Gushi yuan.
