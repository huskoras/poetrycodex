Wu Wen-ying

c. 1200–c. 1260

Wu Wen-ying (吳文英; Wu Wenying; c. 1200–c. 1260) was a writer of ci, the Chinese song lyrics composed to set tunes, in the Song dynasty. His dense, allusive style made him the great model of the later ci; his pen name was Meng-ch'uang.

The 25 ci here are from the chinese-poetry dataset of Zhu Zumou's 1924 anthology Three Hundred Song Lyrics (宋词三百首), in simplified characters, with the tune name as title.
