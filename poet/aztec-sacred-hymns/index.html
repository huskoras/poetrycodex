Aztec Sacred Hymns (Anonymous)

These twenty hymns to the gods of the Aztec pantheon were taken down in Nahuatl in the sixteenth century from the informants of the Franciscan friar Bernardino de Sahagún, and survive in manuscript copies. Their language is archaic and heavily laced with refrain syllables, and the Sahagún informants' own Nahuatl commentary on them is not given here.

The Nahuatl texts are given as printed by Daniel G. Brinton in Rig Veda Americanus (1890), the eighth volume of his Library of Aboriginal American Literature, together with his English versions of each hymn.
