Lysistratus the Seer

date not given by Paton

Lysistratus the Seer is one of the epigrammatists whose short poems were gathered in the Greek Anthology. Paton's index of authors gives no firm date for the poet. Paton's index lists him as a seer.

This archive carries the 1 epigram ascribed to the poet in the third volume of W. R. Paton's prose translation (Book IX: 509).
