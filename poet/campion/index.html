Tudor & Elizabethan

Thomas Campion

1567–1620

Thomas Campion was an English composer, physician and poet who wrote and set to music his own delicate, classically influenced lyrics.

He also published a treatise arguing for quantitative, unrhymed verse in English, though his own most enduring songs are rhymed airs.
