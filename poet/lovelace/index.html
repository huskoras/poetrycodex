Metaphysical & Cavalier

Richard Lovelace

1617–1657

Richard Lovelace (1617–1657) was an English Cavalier poet who fought and was imprisoned for the Royalist cause.

Best remembered for ‘To Althea, from Prison’ and ‘To Lucasta, Going to the Wars,’ his posthumous Lucasta volumes appeared in 1659.
