American

Isabel Ecclestone Mackay

1875–1928

Isabel Ecclestone Mackay (1875–1928) was a Canadian poet and novelist who wrote under her married name; her verse volume Fires of Driftwood appeared in 1912.

The poems given here are taken from The Oxford Book of Canadian Verse, chosen by Wilfred Campbell (Toronto: Oxford University Press, 1913).
