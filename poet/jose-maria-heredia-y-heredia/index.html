José María Heredia

1803–1839

José María Heredia y Heredia (Santiago de Cuba, 1803 – Toluca, Mexico, 1839) was a Cuban poet, often regarded as the first great poet of Cuba and a forerunner of Romanticism in the Spanish-speaking Americas.

Implicated in a conspiracy against Spanish colonial rule, he was condemned to exile in 1823 and lived in the United States and Mexico. His ode 'Niágara', written in 1824 before the falls, is his best-known work and a landmark of Spanish-American Romantic verse.
