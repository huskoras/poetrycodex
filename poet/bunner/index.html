American

H. C. Bunner

1855–1896

H. C. Bunner (Henry Cuyler Bunner, 1855–1896) was an American writer, born in Oswego, New York, who edited the humorous weekly Puck from 1877 until his death.

He was a master of light and occasional verse, collected in volumes such as Airs from Arcady and Elsewhere (1884).
