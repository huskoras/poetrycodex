Victorian

Robert Browning

1812–1889

Robert Browning (1812–1889) was an English poet and playwright, master of the dramatic monologue — a poem spoken entirely in an invented character's voice.

This selection gathers his early collections <em>Dramatic Lyrics</em> (1842) and <em>Dramatic Romances</em> (1845); his stage plays and longer closet-dramas (Pauline, Paracelsus, Sordello, Pippa Passes) are not included here.
