Lady Mary Wortley Montagu

1689–1762

Lady Mary Wortley Montagu (1689–1762) was an English aristocrat, letter-writer and poet, famous for her Turkish Embassy Letters and for introducing smallpox inoculation to England. Her town eclogues and verse epistles circulated in manuscript and print in her lifetime.

The two poems 'The Lover' and 'On Seeing a Portrait of Sir Robert Walpole' follow Frederick Locker's Lyra Elegantiarum (London: E. Moxon, 1867); the other verse here follows The Works of the Right Honourable Lady Mary Wortley Montagu, vol. V: Poems (London, 1817, ed. Dallaway), with the letters, essays and notes left out and the Italian and French pieces omitted. Of the six Town Eclogues, five are given here; Roxana and Thursday ('The Bassette Table') are held under Pope.
