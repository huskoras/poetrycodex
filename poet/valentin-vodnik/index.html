Valentin Vodnik

1758–1819

Valentin Vodnik (1758–1819) was a Franciscan priest, schoolteacher and writer, born near Ljubljana in Carniola, then a Habsburg crown land. He edited the Lublanske novice (1797–1800), the first newspaper in Slovene, and wrote almanac pieces, a cookery book, school texts and a grammar. Under the French Illyrian Provinces (1809–1813) he directed schools in Ljubljana, and his poem on Illyria's awakening greets Napoleon's rule.

Vodnik is regarded as the first Slovene poet of note. His collection Pesme za pokušino (Ljubljana, 1806), printed in the old Bohorič alphabet, is the first independent volume of Slovene verse; it holds light songs, fables, calendar epigrams and the mountain poem on Mount Vršac. His 'Zadovoljni Kranjec' and 'Dramilo' became emblems of Slovene national awakening, and later poems appeared posthumously in the Krajnska čbelica almanac (1830s). Texts here keep the spelling of the printed sources.
