Ahmad Shah Abdali

c. 1723–1773

Ahmad Shah, of the Saddozi family of the Abdali tribe, was freed from captivity in Kandahar by Nadir Shah and crowned king of Afghanistan in 1747, when Raverty says he was about twenty-three. He founded the Durrani monarchy, conquered Kashmir and the Panjab, and entered Delhi, and died in June 1773.

Raverty's notice speaks of the Shah as a Molawi who held regular assemblies of learned men and had time for literature. The poems chosen here are mainly poems of love and longing, with two on divine themes.
