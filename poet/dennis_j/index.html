John Dennis

1657–1734

John Dennis (1657–1734) was an English critic and dramatist, educated at Harrow and Cambridge. He wrote tragedies and comedies and a body of critical prose, and became notorious as the longtime antagonist of Alexander Pope, who satirised him in print.

His Miscellanies in Verse and Prose (1693) is an early volume of his verse: a Pindaric ode on William III, occasional and amatory pieces, burlesque fables with their morals, and versions of Horace, Boileau and Juvenal.
