Poul Martin Møller

1794–1838

Poul Martin Møller (1794-1838) was a Danish poet and philosopher, born in Uldum in Jutland and educated at the University of Copenhagen. He sailed to China as a ship's chaplain in 1819-21, and later taught philosophy at the University of Copenhagen and, briefly, in Christiania (Oslo), where he held a chair from 1826 to 1828 before returning to Copenhagen as professor.

He is remembered above all for the unfinished novella En dansk Students Eventyr (A Danish Student's Tale), whose portrait of a student divided against himself anticipated the concerns of existential philosophy; Søren Kierkegaard, who had been his pupil, admired it and dedicated The Concept of Anxiety to his memory. His light, ironic and often sensuous verse, together with his student songs, circulated widely, and his collected writings appeared posthumously in 1839-43; the poems here are from the third edition of Efterladte Skrifter (1855-56).
