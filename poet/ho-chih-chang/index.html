Chinese & Japanese

Ho Chih-chang

b. AD 659

Ho Chih-chang, born in AD 659, was a T’ang official and poet, and an early admirer and friend of Li Po. His poem here is a homecoming after long absence.

The poems here are Herbert A. Giles’s versions in Chinese Poetry in English Verse (1898), given with the poet’s name as spelled in that book.
