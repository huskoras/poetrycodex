Victorian

John Savage

1828–1888

John Savage (1828–1888), born in Dublin, was a Fenian, journalist and poet who was concerned in an attempted insurrection in Munster in 1849 and later lived in America, where he wrote on Irish revolutionary history and Celtic archaeology.

The poems here are taken from H. Halliday Sparling's anthology Irish Minstrelsy (London: Walter Scott, 1888).
