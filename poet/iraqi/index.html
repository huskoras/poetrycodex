Fakhr al-Din Iraqi

1213–1289

Fakhr al-Din Iraqi (فخرالدین عراقی, Ibrahim of Hamadan, 1213–1289) was a Persian Sufi poet. Born near Hamadan, he left for India as a young man and studied at Multan under the Suhrawardi shaykh Baha al-Din Zakariyya, whose daughter he is said to have married. After the shaykh's death he travelled to Anatolia, where he came under the influence of Sadr al-Din Qunawi and the circle of Rumi, and later lived in Damascus, where he died.

This selection comes from the ghazals of his Divan as given on Ganjoor, chosen by the number of recitations and linked songs recorded there and kept in Ganjoor's numbering. Each bayt stands on one line, the two hemistichs separated by a wide space. The Persian text is public domain.
