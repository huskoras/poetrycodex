Svatopluk Čech

1846–1908

Svatopluk Čech (born 21 February 1846 in Ostředek, died 23 February 1908 in Prague) was a Czech poet, prose writer, journalist and traveller. One of the leading Czech poets of the later nineteenth century, he is remembered above all for his epic and lyrical-epic poems on Czech history and rural life, among them Adamité, Václav z Michalovic, Ve stínu lípy and the satirical Hanuman.

His cycle Písně otroka (Songs of the Slave, 1895), twenty-three poems in which the voice of a slave stands for a humiliated nation, became one of the most widely read books of Czech social lyric poetry. This selection gives the whole cycle, the idyllic epic Ve stínu lípy (In the Shade of the Linden, 1886) and a number of his shorter lyrics.
