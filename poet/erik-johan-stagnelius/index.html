Erik Johan Stagnelius

1793–1823

Erik Johan Stagnelius (1793–1823) was a Swedish poet and dramatist, born on the island of Öland, the son of a clergyman who later became a bishop. He studied at Lund and Uppsala, worked for a time as a clerk in Stockholm, and lived there in poverty and poor health until his early death at the age of thirty.

His reputation rests on lyrics and plays shaped by Platonic and Gnostic ideas, in which the soul longs to be freed from matter and returned to the divine. Little of his work was widely read in his lifetime: most of his verse first appeared in the collected edition of 1824–26, edited by Hammarsköld, and he is now counted among the major voices of Swedish Romantic poetry. The poems here are taken from Swedish Wikisource and keep the printed spelling; his plays are not included.
