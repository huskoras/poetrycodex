Zheng Guangzu

fl. c. 1290–1330

Zheng Guangzu (鄭光祖, courtesy name Dehui) was a playwright of the Yuan dynasty, born in Xiangling in Pingyang (modern Shanxi) and later living in Hangzhou, where he held a minor post. He is counted, with Guan Hanqing, Ma Zhiyuan and Bai Pu, among the four great masters of Yuan drama. The Register of Ghosts (錄鬼簿, c. 1330) lists him among the dramatists and records that he was widely admired.

Of the eighteen or so plays credited to him, about eight survive. The best known is The Soul of Qiannü Leaves Her Body (迷青瑣倩女離魂), a play of love in which a young woman's soul leaves her sick body to follow her betrothed to the capital. He also wrote songs in a polished, lyrical style that set him apart from his more colloquial contemporaries.
