Sana'i

c. 1080–c. 1131

Sana'i of Ghazna (سنایی غزنوی, Abu'l-Majd Majdud ibn Adam) was a Persian poet of the late 11th and early 12th century, born in Ghazna in present-day Afghanistan. Ganjoor gives his birth as 463 or 473 AH and his death as 525 or 535 AH, that is, c. 1080 and c. 1131; the dates are approximate.

He is counted among the first great poets of Persian mystical verse. His works include a divan of qasidas, ghazals and quatrains, and the long mystical poem Hadiqat al-haqiqa, which is not part of this selection. The poems here are 150 ghazals from the Divan, taken from Ganjoor's text and chosen by how often Ganjoor lists a recitation or a song for them, in the order of that count and then of Ganjoor's numbering.
