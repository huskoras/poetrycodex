Korean

Park In-ro

1561–1642

Park In-ro (박인로, 朴仁老, 1561–1642), pen name Nogye (蘆溪), was a Joseon soldier and poet. When the Japanese invaded in 1592 he joined the volunteer forces of Jeong Se-a, and he later served in the navy under the commander Seong Yun-mun; he passed the military examination in 1599 and held posts as a gate guard and royal escort before retiring to his home village, Nogye, to read and write.

He is ranked with Jeong Cheol and Yun Seondo among the three great poets of Joseon. His gasa, long verse narratives, include Taepyeongsa (1598), Seonsangtan (1605), written aboard ship while he was stationed at Busan, and Nuhangsa (1611), on poverty and contentment in a back alley. He also left sijo series such as Johongsiga. His works were gathered in the Nogyejip (蘆溪集).
