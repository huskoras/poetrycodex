Victorian

Eugene Lee-Hamilton

1845–1907

Eugene Lee-Hamilton (1845–1907) was an English poet, born in London and brought up largely on the Continent; he was the half-brother of the writer Vernon Lee. A long illness left him an invalid for many years, a circumstance that colours much of his work, and he is remembered chiefly as a sonneteer, the author of Sonnets of the Wingless Hours (1894).

The poems here are taken from Apollo and Marsyas, and Other Poems (London: Elliot Stock, 1884): dramatic dialogues, narrative poems and odes, followed by a closing group of sonnets. The prose Introduction to the volume is not included.
