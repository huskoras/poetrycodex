Georges Rodenbach

1855–1898

Georges Rodenbach (1855–1898) was a Belgian Symbolist poet and novelist, born in Tournai, who spent his last years in Paris, where he died in 1898. He is remembered above all as the poet of Bruges, the grey, canal-ridden Flemish city whose stillness shaped his poetry and his novel Bruges-la-Morte (1892).

Le Règne du silence (1891) is a long poem in six parts, one of which, Du silence, had appeared in 1888 as a small book; Les Vies encloses (1896) gathers sequences of numbered pieces on mirrors, aquariums, sick-room windows and clouds. Both books keep to the Symbolist themes of silence, reflection and the soul closed in on itself.
