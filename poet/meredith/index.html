Victorian

George Meredith

1828–1909

George Meredith (1828–1909) was an English novelist and poet whose sonnet sequence <em>Modern Love</em> (1862) gave unusually candid, ironic voice to a marriage's collapse.

His nature poetry, especially <em>The Lark Ascending</em>, prizes close observation and intellectual vigour over conventional sentiment.
