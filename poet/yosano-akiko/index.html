Yosano Akiko

1878–1942

与謝野晶子 (Yosano Akiko, 1878–1942) was a Japanese poet, born Hō Shō in Sakai, near Osaka. She came to prominence with the tanka collection Midaregami (Tangled Hair, 1901), a book of passionate, sensuous love poetry that stirred controversy in Meiji Japan.

She published in the journal Myōjō and married its editor, the poet Yosano Tekkan. Besides poetry she wrote essays and a modern-Japanese version of The Tale of Genji. This file holds all 399 tanka of the 1901 first edition of Midaregami, in six sections, from the Aozora Bunko text.
