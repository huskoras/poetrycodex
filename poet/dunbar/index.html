Tudor & Elizabethan

William Dunbar

c. 1460–c. 1520

William Dunbar was a Scottish makar (court poet) attached to the household of King James IV, celebrated for the range and metrical virtuosity of his verse.

His poems move between satire, comic invective, and devotional lyric, and include the famous elegy ‘Lament for the Makers,’ with its refrain ‘Timor Mortis conturbat me.’
