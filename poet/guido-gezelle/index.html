Guido Gezelle

1830–1899

Guido Gezelle (1830–1899) was born in Bruges, the son of a gardener, and became a Catholic priest. A teacher at the minor seminary in Roeselare and later a curate in Bruges and Kortrijk, he wrote in his native West Flemish rather than in standard Dutch, and for much of his life was a controversial and neglected figure.

His verse is rooted in the Flemish countryside, in birds, trees and the turning seasons, and in a devout, sometimes anguished religious feeling, written with a startling musical and verbal inventiveness. Kerkhofblommen (1858) was an early prose elegy with verse; Tijdkrans (1893), Rijmsnoer om en om het jaar (1897) and the posthumous Laatste verzen (1901) belong to the later work that made him the great poet of Flanders.
