Tudor & Elizabethan

Sir Walter Raleigh

1552–1618

Sir Walter Raleigh was an English courtier, explorer and poet, a favourite of Elizabeth I who later fell from favour and was ultimately executed under James I.

His surviving poems, several written while imprisoned in the Tower of London, meditate with stoic clarity on time, fortune and mortality.
