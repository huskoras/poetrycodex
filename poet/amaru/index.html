Amaru

c. 7th–8th century CE

Amaru (अमरु) is the name attached to a Sanskrit poet of about the seventh or eighth century CE, of whom almost nothing is securely known.

The Amarushataka, the “hundred verses of Amaru”, is a celebrated collection of single-stanza love poems, each a small scene of lovers, quarrels, longing and reconciliation; the number and order of its verses differ from one manuscript tradition to another.
