Ancient Greek & Roman

Nossis

fl. c. 300 BC

Nossis of Locri, in southern Italy, wrote epigrams around 300 BC. Her short poems often record dedications by women and portraits of women, and she is often linked with Sappho.

The epigrams here are the ones that Paton's first volume ascribes to her.
