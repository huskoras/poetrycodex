Phillis Wheatley

c. 1753–1784

Phillis Wheatley (c. 1753–1784) was born in West Africa, kidnapped and brought to Boston as a child in 1761, and purchased by the merchant John Wheatley. Taught to read and write in his household, she began publishing verse as a teenager.

Poems on Various Subjects, Religious and Moral (1773) was the first book of poems published by an African American. A group of Boston notables attested in its front matter that she had written the poems; she was freed shortly after the book appeared.
