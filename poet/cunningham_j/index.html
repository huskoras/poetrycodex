Restoration & Augustan

John Cunningham

1729–1773

John Cunningham was an actor and pastoral poet, born in Dublin, who spent much of his life in the north of England and died at Newcastle in 1773.

Gilfillan prints his ballad May-Eve, or Kate of Aberdeen.
