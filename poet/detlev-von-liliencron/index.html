German

Detlev von Liliencron

1844–1909

Detlev von Liliencron (1844–1909) was born in Kiel and served as an officer in the Prussian army, fighting in the wars of 1866 and 1870–71 and being wounded several times. After leaving the service he spent a short time in America, then held posts as a district official on the North Sea island of Pellworm and later lived near Hamburg.

He is remembered as a leading voice of German impressionist and realist verse, with poems of soldiering, the North German coast and fleeting love. His books include Adjutantenritte, Der Haidegänger, Poggfred and Bunte Beute. The poems here are those printed under his name in Hans Bethge's anthology Deutsche Lyrik seit Liliencron.
