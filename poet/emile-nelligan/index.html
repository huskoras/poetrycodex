Émile Nelligan

1879–1941

Émile Nelligan was born in Montreal, Quebec, in 1879, the son of an Irish-born father and a French-Canadian mother. He left school early to write, and between 1896 and 1899 he published poems in Montreal reviews and read them before the École littéraire de Montréal.

In 1899 he was interned in a psychiatric hospital, where he remained until his death in 1941. His only book, Émile Nelligan et son œuvre (1903), was gathered by his friend and editor Louis Dantin. It has long been counted among the foundations of modern Quebec poetry.
