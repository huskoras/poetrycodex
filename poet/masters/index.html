American

Edgar Lee Masters

1868–1950

Edgar Lee Masters (1868–1950) was an American lawyer and writer, born in Garnett, Kansas, and raised in Petersburg and Lewistown, Illinois. He practised law in Chicago for three decades, partly as law partner of Clarence Darrow, and wrote poetry, plays, novels and biography alongside his legal career.

Spoon River Anthology, first published in 1915 and expanded in 1916 with The Spooniad and the Epilogue, is a sequence of free-verse epitaphs spoken by the dead of an imagined Midwestern town, drawing on the Illinois towns of his youth. It was an immediate sensation and remains his most famous work; the later volumes never matched its success.
