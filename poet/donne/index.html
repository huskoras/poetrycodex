Metaphysical & Cavalier

John Donne

1572–1631

John Donne (1572–1631) was an English poet and cleric, the foremost of the metaphysical poets.

His verse — by turns erotic, witty, and devout — moved from the love poems of his youth to the religious sonnets of his years as Dean of St Paul's.
