Victorian

Henry Kendall

1839–1882

Henry Kendall (1839–1882) was an Australian poet, born at Ulladulla, New South Wales, who worked as a clerk, a journalist and finally an inspector of forests.

His books include Poems and Songs (1862), Leaves from Australian Forests (1869) and Songs from the Mountains (1880).
