Dutch & Flemish

Antoni Christiaan Winand Staring

1767–1840

A. C. W. Staring (1767–1840) was a Gelderland landowner who lived at De Wildenborch, near Vorden, and spent his life managing his estates rather than pursuing a literary career. He wrote verse throughout his life, in a classicising manner formed in the last decades of the eighteenth century and carried through the Napoleonic years into the early nineteenth century.

His Gedichten, first collected in 1820 and revised by him in later editions, contain verse tales on Gelderland and Dutch themes, songs, occasional pieces and many puntdichten, short epigrams. He is valued above all for wit and compression: plain Dutch, a dry humour and an unwillingness to waste a word. This selection is taken from the 1900 edition of the Gedichten with Nicolaas Beets's introduction.
