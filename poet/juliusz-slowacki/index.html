Russian & Slavic

Juliusz Słowacki

1809–1849

Juliusz Słowacki (1809–1849) was born in Krzemieniec, in the Volhynia region of the Russian partition of Poland, and grew up in Wilno, where his stepfather taught at the university. He worked as a clerk in Warsaw until the November Uprising of 1830–31, which sent him abroad; he never returned to Poland.

He spent the rest of his life in emigration, in Geneva, Italy, the Near East and above all Paris, where he died in 1849. With Adam Mickiewicz and Zygmunt Krasiński he is counted among the three great Romantic bards of Polish literature, and is best known for his dramas, the poem Beniowski and the visionary prose-poem Anhelli. The poems here are lyrics, hymns and occasional verses in Polish, from the texts of Wolne Lektury.
