Folgore da San Gimignano

fl. c. 1300

Folgore da San Gimignano is the name under which a Tuscan poet of the late thirteenth and early fourteenth centuries is known; Folgore appears to be a nickname, and his given name is commonly reported as Giacomo or Jacopo di Michele. Little is documented of his life beyond what the poems and a few records suggest: he belonged to the Guelf commune of San Gimignano and is thought to have been alive around 1300.

His reputation rests on the Sonetti dei mesi, a cycle of sonnets that gives each month of the year its pleasures (hawking and the hunt, jousting and garlands, fishing and banquets, games by the winter fire) as a gift to a company of young Sienese gentlemen. Written in the comic-realistic manner of the age but with a courtly gaiety, the cycle was translated into English by Dante Gabriel Rossetti in his Early Italian Poets (1861).

  8. Di luglio in Siena, su la saliciata
  9. D’agosto sí vi do trenta castella
  10. Di settembre vi do diletti tanti
  11. Di ottobre nel contá, c’ha buono stallo
  12. E di novembre Petriuolo, il bagno
  13. E di dicembre una cittá in piano
  14. Sonetto mio, a Niccolò di Nisi
