Hedvig Charlotta Nordenflycht

1718–1763

Hedvig Charlotta Nordenflycht (1718-1763) was a Swedish poet, born and active in Stockholm, and one of the leading voices of Swedish poetry in the middle of the eighteenth century. Widowed young, she made grief and faithful love the matter of her early verse, and she later became the centre of a literary circle in the capital, which brought younger poets such as Gyllenborg and Creutz into her company.

Her poetry joins the sentiment of the age to a sustained, often passionate personal voice, and she also wrote in defence of women's capacity for learning and authorship. The two poems here, 'Hos min Vän' and 'Lefnadsbeslut', are taken from the Oxford Book of Scandinavian Verse (1925), in the original Swedish with the spelling of her day.
