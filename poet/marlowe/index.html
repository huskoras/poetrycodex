Tudor & Elizabethan

Christopher Marlowe

1564-1593

Christopher Marlowe (1564-1593) was an English poet and dramatist, the most celebrated of the University Wits and the leading figure of Elizabethan tragedy before Shakespeare.

Alongside his plays he left a body of non-dramatic verse, including the unfinished mythological poem Hero and Leander and a translation of Ovid's Amores, and was killed in a Deptford tavern brawl at twenty-nine.
