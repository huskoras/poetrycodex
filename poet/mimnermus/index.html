Mimnermus

7th century BC (Paton’s date)

Mimnermus is one of the epigrammatists whose short poems were gathered in the Greek Anthology. Paton's index of authors dates the poet to 7th century BC.

This archive carries the 1 epigram ascribed to the poet in the third volume of W. R. Paton's prose translation (Book IX: 50).
