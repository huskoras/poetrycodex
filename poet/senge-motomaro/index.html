Senge Motomaro

1888–1948

千家元麿 (Senge Motomaro, 1888–1948) was born in Tokyo, the son of a family of hereditary priests of the Izumo Shrine. He came to poetry in the circle of the Shirakaba (White Birch) group around Mushanokōji Saneatsu, whose humanist ideals of sincerity and affirmation of ordinary life shaped his work.

His first book, 自分は見た (Jibun wa mita, "I Saw"), appeared in 1918 with a preface by Mushanokōji and a binding by Kishida Ryūsei, and made his name. Written in plain colloquial free verse, its poems look closely at children, a wife, birds, weather and the poor of the Sugamo district of Tokyo, and treat them with unforced tenderness. This selection takes 50 poems from that 1918 collection (about 98 in all); the text is that of Aozora Bunko, with reading aids and editorial marks removed and the old kanji and kana kept.
