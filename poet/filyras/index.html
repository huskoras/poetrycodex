Modern Greek

Romos Filyras

1888–1942

Romos Filyras was the pen name of Ioannis (Giagkos) Oikonomopoulos, a Greek poet born in Kiato, Corinthia. Greek Wikipedia gives his birth year as 1888, following the civil register; Greek Wikisource gives 1898. He published his first poems as a boy, studied law in Athens, fought in the Balkan Wars, and issued two collections, Rhoda ston afro (1911) and Gyrismoi (1918), together with a prose work, O theatrinos tis zois (1916).

His mental health failed from about 1920, and from 1927 he lived in the Dromokaiteio psychiatric hospital in Athens, where he went on writing and gave poems away to visitors. He died there on 9 September 1942. His verse mixes symbolist and post-Palamas lyricism with a strain of bitterness over frustrated hopes; here it is gathered from the transcriptions on Greek Wikisource.
