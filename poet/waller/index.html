Metaphysical & Cavalier

Edmund Waller

1606–1687

Edmund Waller (1606–1687) was an English poet and politician whose smooth, regular couplets influenced the coming Augustan style.

He wrote equally fluent verse for both sides of the Civil War, a flexibility that shadowed his reputation.
