George Monck Berkeley

1763–1793

George Monck Berkeley was the grandson of Bishop George Berkeley, the philosopher, and a member of the Inner Temple. He died in 1793 at the age of twenty-nine, and his mother, Eliza Berkeley, published his Poems in 1797.
