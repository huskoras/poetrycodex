'Unsuri of Balkh

d. 1039/40

'Unsuri (Abu'l-Qasim Hasan ibn Ahmad 'Unsuri, of Balkh, in what is now northern Afghanistan) was the poet laureate of Sultan Mahmud of Ghazna, the title of malik al-shu'ara, "king of poets," being given to him at the Ghaznavid court. Before joining the sultan he is said to have served Mahmud's brother, the amir Nasr ibn Sebuktigin, who had called him to Ghazna. He died in 1039/40.

He is remembered chiefly for his panegyric qasidas in praise of Mahmud and his campaigns, written in a splendid, courtly style that later critics took as a model. This selection gives the first fifty qasidas of his Divan as Ganjoor prints it, opening with the praise of Sultan Mahmud, together with Reynold A. Nicholson's English version (1922) of the qasida describing the sultan's campaign, which is the original of the thirteenth qasida here.
