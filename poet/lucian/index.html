Ancient Greek & Roman

Lucian

2nd century AD (Paton’s date)

Lucian is one of the epigrammatists whose short poems were gathered in the Greek Anthology. Paton's index of authors dates the poet to 2nd century AD.

This archive carries the 1 epigram ascribed to the poet in the first volume of W. R. Paton's prose translation (Books I–VI) (Book VI: 164).
