Jarir

c. 653–c. 728

Jarir ibn ‘Atiyya al-Khatafi (جرير بن عطية الخطفي, c. 653–c. 728) was born into the Banu Kulayb of the Tamim tribe in the Yamama region of central Arabia and made his career in Basra and at the Umayyad court. With al-Farazdaq and al-Akhtal he formed the great triad of Umayyad poets, and he is remembered above all for the naqa’id, the verse flytings in which he and al-Farazdaq answered one another's boasts and insults, line for line and rhyme for rhyme, for some forty years.

His diwan runs from tender nasib, the lament at the abandoned campsite and the beloved's departure, to lampoons and to panegyric of al-Hajjaj and the caliphs of the house of Marwan. He is counted a master of the clear, melodious qasida. The texts here are taken from his diwan as transcribed on Arabic Wikisource, with the vocalisation of the source; the pieces of three or more lines are given in long form, naqa’id first, then the longest poems.

  131. عَجِبتُ لرَحلٍ من عَدِيّ مُشَمَّسٍ (‘Ajibtu li-raḥlin min ‘Adiyyin mushammasin)
  132. لا تحسبي سبسبَ العراقِ (Lā taḥsabī sabsaba al-‘Irāqi)
  133. لَوْلا ابنُ حَكّامِ وَأشْرَافَ قَوْمِهِ (Lawlā ibnu Ḥakkāmin wa-ashrāfu qawmihi)
  134. إني لوصالٌ بغيرِ شناءةٍ (Innī lawaṣṣālun bi-ghayri shanā’atin)
  135. سيخزى إذا ضنتْ حلائبُ مالكٍ (Sayukhzā idhā ḍannat ḥalā’ibu Mālikin)
  136. تقولُ ذاتُ المطرفِ الهفهافِ (Taqūlu dhātu al-miṭrafi al-hafhāfi)
  137. عَرِينٌ مِنْ عُرَيْنَةَ لَيْسَ مِنّا (‘Arīnun min ‘Urayna laysa minnā)
  138. ما بالٌ شِرْب بَني الدَّلَنْطَى ثابِتاً (Mā bālu shirbi Banī al-Dalanṭā thābitan)
  139. مَتى ما التَوَى بالظّاعنينَ نَزيعُ (Matā mā iltawā bi-al-ẓā‘ināna nazī‘u)
  140. أسَرَى الخالِدَةَ الخَيالُ، وَلا أرَى (Asarā al-Khālidata al-khayālu wa-lā arā)
  141. ألمْ يكُنْ في وُسُومٍ قدْ وَسَمتُ بِها (Alam yakun fī wusūmin qad wasamtu bihā)
  142. زَارَ القُبُورَ أبُو مالِكٍ (Zāra al-qubūra Abū Mālikin)
  143. قد أرقَصَتْ أُمُّ البعَيثِ حِجَجَا (Qad arqaṣat Ummu al-Ba‘īthi ḥijajā)
  144. لَقَدْ كانَ ظَنّي يا ابنَ سَعدٍ سَعادةً (Laqad kāna ẓannī yā ibna Sa‘din sa‘ādatan)
  145. لعمري لئنْ خلىَّ جبيرٌ مكانهُ (La‘amrī la-in khallā Jubayrun makānahu)
  146. يا عقبَ لا عقبَ لي في البيتِ أسمعهُ (Yā ‘aqba lā ‘aqba lī fī al-bayti asma‘uhu)
  147. يا عَينُ جُودي بدَمْعٍ هاجَهُ الذِّكَرُ (Yā ‘aynu jūdī bi-dam‘in hājahu al-dhikaru)
  148. يا تَيْمُ! ما القارُونَ في شِدّةِ القِرَى (Yā Taymu mā al-Qārūna fī shiddati al-qirā)
  149. أتَنْفي قُرُوماً مِنْ مَعَدٍّ لغَيْرِهِمْ (Atanfī qurūman min Ma‘addin li-ghayrihim)
  150. أتجعلُ يا بنَ القينِ أولادَ دارمٍ (Ataj‘alu yā ibna al-qayni awlāda Dārimin)
