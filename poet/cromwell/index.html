American

Gladys Cromwell

1885–1919

Gladys Cromwell (1885–1919) was an American poet, born in New York City, who with her twin sister served with the Red Cross in France during the First World War.

Her poems were collected in The Gates of Utterance (1915) and in a volume published after her death in 1919.
