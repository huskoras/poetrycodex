William Douw Lighthall

1857–1954

William Douw Lighthall (1857–1954) was a Montreal lawyer, author and civic reformer, later mayor of Westmount, who edited a series of anthologies of Canadian poetry.

His own verse appeared in Thoughts, Moods and Ideals (1887); the poems here are those he included in his own anthology, Songs of the Great Dominion (1889).
