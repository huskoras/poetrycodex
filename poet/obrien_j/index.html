John O'Brien

1878–1952

John O'Brien was the pen name of Patrick Joseph Hartigan (1878–1952), an Australian Catholic priest and poet. Born in New South Wales to Irish-Australian parents, he was ordained in 1903 and spent his working life as a priest in country parishes, where the speech and stories of Irish-Australian farming families came to fill his verse.

He wrote under a pseudonym because clerical authors were expected to keep out of print. Around the Boree Log and Other Verses (Angus & Robertson, 1921) sold very widely in Australia and made him a popular bush poet. Its humorous and sentimental ballads of selectors' children, priests and country churches, including 'Said Hanrahan', are written largely in Irish-Australian dialect. He was later made a monsignor.
