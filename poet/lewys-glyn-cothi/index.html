Celtic

Lewys Glyn Cothi

c. 1420–1490

Lewys Glyn Cothi was a fifteenth-century Welsh poet from the Cothi valley in Carmarthenshire, a wandering bard who sang for patrons across Wales and the borders.

His elegy for his young son Siôn is given in English verse by Alfred Perceval Graves in A Celtic Psaltery (1917).
