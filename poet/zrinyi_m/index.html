Hungarian

Miklós Zrínyi

1620–1664

Hungarian-Croatian soldier, statesman and poet, author of the epic Zrínyiad, who died in 1664 (Bowring gives 1616–1665).

His poems here are read in John Bowring's English versions from Poetry of the Magyars (1830), an early English anthology of Hungarian verse.
