Yaakov Steinberg

1887–1947

Yaakov Steinberg (יעקב שטיינברג, 1887–1947) was a Hebrew poet, short-story writer and critic of the generation after Bialik. His verse, much of it in rhymed, vocalised stanzas, turns on solitude, evening, the city, love and ageing, in a quiet, introspective voice.

His collected writings were published by Dvir, and the poems here are taken from that edition as digitised by Project Ben-Yehuda.
