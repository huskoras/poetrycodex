American

Nathaniel Parker Willis

1806–1867

Nathaniel Parker Willis (1806–1867) was an American poet, editor and journalist, born at Portland, Maine, and educated at Yale. He edited the New-York Mirror and later the Home Journal, and was among the best-paid magazine writers of his time.

His early scriptural and sentimental poems made him widely popular, and he was a prominent figure of the New York literary scene.
