Erik Gustaf Geijer

1783-1847

Erik Gustaf Geijer (1783-1847) was a Swedish poet, historian and composer, and a leading figure of the Gothic League, the Romantic circle that turned Swedish letters towards the Viking and peasant past. A professor of history at Uppsala, he also wrote the standard Romantic-era history of Sweden and set many of his own songs to music.

The poems here are in the English translation of Charles Wharton Stork, from his Anthology of Swedish Lyrics (1917).
