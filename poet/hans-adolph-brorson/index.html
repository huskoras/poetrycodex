Nordic

Hans Adolph Brorson

1694–1764

Hans Adolph Brorson (1694-1764) was a Danish pastor and bishop, and with Thomas Kingo the foremost hymn writer in the Danish language. Born in Randerup in southern Jutland, he studied theology in Copenhagen under the influence of Pietism, served as a pastor in Tønder, and in 1741 became bishop of Ribe, where he died.

His hymns, warm, personal and full of the imagery of the Song of Songs, appeared in Nogle Jule-Psalmer (1732), Troens rare Klenodie (1739) and, after his death, Svane-Sang (1765). Several of them, among them 'Den yndigste Rose er funden', 'I denne søde Juletid' and 'Den store hvide Flok vi see', remain in the Danish hymnal today. The texts are given here in the spelling of the early printings as edited on Kalliope.
