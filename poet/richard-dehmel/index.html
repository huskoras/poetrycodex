Richard Dehmel

1863–1920

Richard Dehmel (1863–1920) was born at Wendisch-Hermsdorf in Brandenburg and studied in Berlin and Leipzig. He worked for some years for an insurance company before turning wholly to writing, and later lived in Berlin and, from 1901, in Blankenese near Hamburg.

His first book of poems, Erlösungen, appeared in 1891, and Aber die Liebe (1893) was followed by Weib und Welt (1896) and Schöne wilde Welt (1913). His poems of love, desire and conscience stirred controversy in his day, and one of them, Verklärte Nacht, gave Arnold Schoenberg the subject of his string sextet of 1899. The poems here are taken from the German Wikisource transcriptions of his first printings.
