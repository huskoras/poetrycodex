Angelo Poliziano

1454–1494

Angelo Poliziano (Angelo Ambrogini, 1454–1494), called Poliziano or Politian after his birthplace Montepulciano, was a scholar and poet of Renaissance Florence. Brought up in the household of Lorenzo de' Medici, he became tutor to Lorenzo's children and held the chair of Greek and Latin eloquence at the Florentine studio.

He wrote in Latin, Greek and Italian. His Italian masterpiece, the Stanze per la giostra, is an unfinished poem in ottava rima begun about 1475 to celebrate the tournament won by Lorenzo's brother Giuliano, and it was widely admired for its classical imagery and polished style. He also wrote the pastoral play Orfeo, which is not included here.

Major Works (2)

  1. Fabula di Orfeo — Verse drama, 1480
  2. Stanze per la giostra — Poem in ottava rima, 1475–1478
