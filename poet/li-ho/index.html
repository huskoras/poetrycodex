Li Ho

9th century AD

Li Ho is dated by Giles to the 9th century AD. His poem here is a young man’s memory of wine and horses.

The poems here are Herbert A. Giles’s versions in Chinese Poetry in English Verse (1898), given with the poet’s name as spelled in that book.
