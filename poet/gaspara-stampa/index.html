Gaspara Stampa

c. 1523–1554

Gaspara Stampa (c. 1523–1554) was born in Padua and, after her father’s death, moved with her mother, sister and brother to Venice, where she became known as a singer and lutenist and took part in the learned circles of the city. Her Rime were printed after her death, in 1554, in an edition prepared by her sister Cassandra.

Her poems form a sustained lyric record of her love for Count Collaltino di Collalto, from the first joy of the attachment through his absence and neglect to her disillusion and her turn towards religious feeling. She is regarded as one of the most original women poets of the Italian Renaissance. The poems here are taken from the Rime d’amore as printed in Abdelkader Salza’s edition of 1913 (Laterza, Bari), in its order and spelling.
