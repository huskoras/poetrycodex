Jessie Mackay

1864–1938

Jessie Mackay (1864–1938) was a New Zealand poet and journalist, born in Canterbury.

Her first volume was The Spirit of the Rangatira and Other Ballads (1889); she was a leading New Zealand verse writer of her time.
