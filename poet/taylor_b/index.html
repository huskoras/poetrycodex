American

Bayard Taylor

1825–1878

Bayard Taylor (1825–1878) was an American poet, travel writer and translator, born at Kennett Square, Pennsylvania. His accounts of journeys through Europe, Africa, and Asia made him a celebrated figure, and his verse translation of Goethe’s Faust (1870–71) was long standard.

He served as United States minister to Germany and died at Berlin in 1878. His lyrics, such as the “Bedouin Song”, were popular in his lifetime.
