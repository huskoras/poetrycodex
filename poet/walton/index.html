Metaphysical & Cavalier

Izaak Walton

1593–1683

Izaak Walton was an English biographer and angler, the author of The Compleat Angler (1653) and of the Lives of Donne, Wotton, Hooker, Herbert and Sanderson.

Gilfillan prints one poem under his name, The Angler's Wish, a short pastoral lyric of the fisherman's contented life.
