Ancient Greek & Roman

Callimachus

c. 310 – c. 240 BC

Callimachus of Cyrene was a Greek poet and scholar who worked at the Library of Alexandria under the early Ptolemies. He wrote on a vast range of subjects, and his catalogue of the library’s holdings, the Pinakes, was a founding work of bibliography.

Of his poetry six hymns and a body of epigrams survive complete. This selection gives the six Hymns, to Zeus, Apollo, Artemis, Delos, Pallas and Demeter, in A. W. Mair’s prose translation for the Loeb Classical Library (1921).
