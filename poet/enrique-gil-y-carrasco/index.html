Enrique Gil y Carrasco

1815–1846

Enrique Gil y Carrasco (Villafranca del Bierzo, 1815 – Berlin, 1846) was a Spanish Romantic poet, critic and novelist. He studied in Madrid, wrote for the literary journals of the 1830s and 1840s, and is best remembered for the historical novel El señor de Bembibre (1844).

His few poems, among them the elegiac 'La violeta', are marked by melancholy and a feeling for landscape. He entered the diplomatic service and died in Berlin at thirty-one, as secretary of the Spanish legation.
