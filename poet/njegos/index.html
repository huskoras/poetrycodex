Petar II Petrović-Njegoš

1813–1851

Petar II Petrović-Njegoš (1813–1851) was Prince-Bishop (Vladika) of Montenegro and the greatest poet of the South Slavs. Born at Njeguši, he succeeded his uncle Petar I as ruler of the mountain principality in 1830, at the age of seventeen, and governed it as both bishop and prince until his death at Cetinje. He wrote in the language of the Serbian and Montenegrin oral epic, and is buried in the chapel on Mount Lovćen.

His major works are Luča mikrokozma (1845), Gorski vijenac (The Mountain Wreath, Vienna, 1847) and Lažni car Šćepan Mali (1851). The Mountain Wreath, a verse drama of the Orthodox Montenegrins and their Muslim neighbours in the time of Vladika Danilo, has been read ever since as the national epic of Montenegro and Serbia. It is given here in the original Cyrillic text and in the English verse translation by James W. Wiles (1930).
