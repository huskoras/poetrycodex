Metaphysical & Cavalier

Michael Drayton

1563–1631

Michael Drayton (1563–1631) was an English poet whose vast body of work spans the sonnet sequence Idea, historical verse, and the topographical epic Poly-Olbion.

His Barons' Wars retells the civil strife of Edward II's reign in stately ottava rima.

Major Works (2)

  2. Poly-Olbion — Topographical epic, 1612–1622
