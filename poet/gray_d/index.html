Victorian

David Gray

1838–1861

David Gray (1838–1861) was a Scottish poet from Merkland, Kirkintilloch, who died in his home town aged twenty-three. His friend Robert Buchanan wrote his biography.

The poems here are taken from Arthur Quiller-Couch's anthology The Oxford Book of Victorian Verse (Clarendon Press, 1912).
