Almeida Garrett

1799–1854

João Baptista da Silva Leitão de Almeida Garrett (1799–1854) was a Portuguese poet, dramatist and prose writer, born in Porto, a leading figure of Portuguese Romanticism. He died in Lisbon in 1854.

Folhas Caídas (Fallen Leaves) was published in 1853. Its short lyrics, in two books, are the poems of love for which he is chiefly remembered; the text here follows the Portuguese Wikisource transcription.
