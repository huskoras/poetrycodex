Victorian

Rev. H. H. Dugmore

1810–1896

Rev. H. H. Dugmore (Henry Hare Dugmore, 1810–1896, Wesleyan missionary in the Eastern Cape) is one of the named contributors to The Poetry of South Africa, collected and arranged by A. Wilmot (London: Sampson Low, Marston, Searle & Rivington; Cape Town: J. C. Juta & Co., 1887), an anthology of verse from the Cape Colony, Natal and the Transvaal.

This archive holds 18 poems printed under this name in that volume, in Wilmot’s spelling. His pieces carry datelines such as Stormberg (1874) and the English Channel (1859), and several are set at Cove Rock and Bathurst in the Eastern Cape. Dates are those of a standard reference.
