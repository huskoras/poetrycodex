Victorian

Robert Dwyer Joyce

1830–1883

Robert Dwyer Joyce (1830–1883), born in County Limerick, was a physician, journalist and poet. He emigrated to Boston in 1866, achieved distinction there, and returned to Dublin shortly before his death.

The poems here are taken from H. Halliday Sparling's anthology Irish Minstrelsy (London: Walter Scott, 1888).
