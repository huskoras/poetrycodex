Theodore Harding Rand

1835–1900

Theodore Harding Rand (1835–1900) was a Canadian educator and poet, a Doctor of Civil Law who served as Superintendent of Education in Nova Scotia and in New Brunswick and later taught at McMaster University, Toronto. He is also named on the title page of Song-Waves as the author of A Treasury of Canadian Verse.

His verse appeared in At Minas Basin, and Other Poems (Toronto: William Briggs, 1897), mostly sonnets and lyrics of the Bay of Fundy and the Annapolis Valley, and in Song-Waves (Toronto: William Briggs, 1900), a sequence of short untitled lyrics followed by a handful of titled poems.
