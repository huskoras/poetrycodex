Metaphysical & Cavalier

Thomas Bancroft

fl. 1633–1658

Thomas Bancroft (fl. 1633–1658) was an English epigrammatist and verse satirist. Little is securely recorded of his life; his poems are addressed to gentry and neighbours of the English Midlands, and he published two books of epigrams and epitaphs in London in 1639.

Two Bookes of Epigrammes, and Epitaphs was dedicated to Sir Charles Shirley, Baronet, and William Davenport, Esquire, and printed by I. Okes for Matthew Walbancke. The first book is largely satirical and occasional, mixing portraits of types and named acquaintances with epitaphs; the second turns often to scriptural and devotional themes. A later volume, Time's out of Tune, appeared in 1658.
