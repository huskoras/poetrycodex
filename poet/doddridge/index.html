Restoration & Augustan

Philip Doddridge

1702–1751

Philip Doddridge (1702-1751) was an English Nonconformist minister, teacher and writer. Born in London, he became pastor of the Independent congregation at Northampton in 1729 and ran an academy there for training ministers. His best-known prose works include The Family Expositor and The Rise and Progress of Religion in the Soul.

He wrote many hymns, usually composed to accompany the conclusion of his Sunday sermons, and they circulated in manuscript and in print among Dissenting congregations. The present collection of 370 hymns was published after his death in 1755 from his manuscript by his friend Job Orton. Several of its hymns, such as O God of Bethel, by whose hand, remained in common use.
