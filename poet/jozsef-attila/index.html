Hungarian

Attila József

1905–1937

József Attila (1905–1937) was a Hungarian poet, among the most important of the twentieth century. Born in Budapest to a poor family and orphaned young, he was raised in foster care and by relatives, studied at Szeged, Vienna and Paris, and lived by poems, journalism and odd jobs. His first book, Szépség koldusa ("The Beggar of Beauty"), appeared in 1922, when he was seventeen.

He wrote of poverty, work, love and the city with a Marxist conviction and a rare lyric precision, and his later poems ("Külvárosi éj", "Eszmélet", "A Dunánál", "Karóval jöttél") are among the finest in the language. Nagyon fáj (1936) was his last book in his lifetime. He died under a train at Balatonszárszó on 3 December 1937. This selection takes poems from the Hungarian Wikisource author page, with the date of writing in each note.
