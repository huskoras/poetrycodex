Russian & Slavic

Fyodor Tyutchev

1803–1873

Фёдор Иванович Тютчев (1803–1873), known in English as Fyodor Tyutchev, was a Russian poet and diplomat. Born at Ovstug, he spent some twenty years in the Russian diplomatic service in Munich and Turin, and later served as a censor in St Petersburg.

A selection of his short lyrics appeared in Nekrasov’s journal Sovremennik in 1854, and collections of his poems followed in 1854 and 1868. The poems here are taken from the Complete Works of 1913 (Полное собрание сочинений), printed in the pre-reform Russian spelling, and are limited to those published in his lifetime.
