Leontius Scholasticus

6th century AD (Paton’s date)

Leontius Scholasticus is one of the epigrammatists whose short poems were gathered in the Greek Anthology. Paton's index of authors dates the poet to 6th century AD. Paton's index lists the poet among the writers of the Cycle of Agathias, compiled in the sixth century AD.

This archive carries the 6 epigrams ascribed to the poet in the second volume of W. R. Paton's prose translation (Book VII: 149, 150, 571, 573, 575, 579).
