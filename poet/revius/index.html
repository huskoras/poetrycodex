Jacobus Revius

1586–1658

Jacobus Revius (Jacob Revius) was born in Deventer in 1586 and died in Leiden in 1658. A Calvinist minister and theologian, he served the Reformed church in Deventer and took part in the work on the States Bible translation of 1637, and in his later years he was a regent of the States College at Leiden.

His poems appeared in 1630 as Over-Ysselsche sangen en dichten, a large collection in two books: a long cycle of short poems that follows the course of sacred history from creation to the last judgement, and a second book of the life and passion of Christ, doctrine, prayer and the last things.

He is remembered above all as the great religious sonneteer of the Dutch Golden Age. The passion sonnets, 'Hy droech onse smerten' in particular, have been reprinted in Dutch anthologies for generations. The texts here keep the spelling of the 1630 printing.
