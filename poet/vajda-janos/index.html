Hungarian

János Vajda

1827–1897

János Vajda (Pest, 1827 – Budapest, 1897) was a Hungarian poet and journalist. He took part in the 1848–49 revolution and its press, lived a poor and unsettled life afterwards, and came to be regarded in his later decades as one of the most original Hungarian lyric voices of the generation after Petőfi.

His best-known poems are the love lyrics addressed to Gina, the unfulfilled passion of his life, together with late philosophical and elegiac verse on death, infinity and the transience of human life, such as Húsz év múlva, A vaáli erdőben and Nádas tavon. His work was largely neglected in his lifetime and recognised only after his death.
