Joan Salvat-Papasseit

1894–1924

Joan Salvat-Papasseit was born in Barcelona in 1894 into a poor family and left school early to work, first as a messenger and then in the port and as a clerk and bookseller. Largely self-taught, he became a socialist and anarchist-leaning journalist and edited small reviews such as Un Enemic del Poble and Arc Voltaic.

He was the leading Catalan avant-garde poet of his generation. His Poemes en ondes hertzianes (1919) took Futurist and Cubist typography into Catalan, and his later books, La gesta dels estels (1922), L'irradiador del port i les gavines (1921) and the posthumous Óssa Menor (1925), turn to a plainer, sensuous lyric of the sea and of love. He died of tuberculosis in Barcelona in 1924, aged thirty.
