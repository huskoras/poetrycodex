William Collins

1721–1759

William Collins (1721–1759) was born in Chichester and educated at Winchester and Oxford. He went to London to live by his pen, and his small output of odes, published in 1746, made little stir at first but is now ranked among the finest lyric poetry of the century.

His odes to Evening, Fear and Simplicity, and ‘How sleep the brave,’ are often seen as turning eighteenth-century verse toward feeling and imagination. His mind failed in his later years and he died in Chichester at thirty-seven.
