American

Edwin Markham

1852–1940

Edwin Markham (1852–1940) was an American poet, born in Oregon City, Oregon, and raised in California, who later taught and lived in New York.

“The Man with the Hoe” (1899), written after seeing Millet’s painting, made him famous as a poet of social protest.
