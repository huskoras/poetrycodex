Alonso de Ercilla

1533–1594

Alonso de Ercilla y Zúñiga (1533–1594) was a Spanish soldier, courtier and poet, born in Madrid. As a young page he travelled in the household of Prince Philip (later Philip II) and in 1555 sailed to Peru, then to Chile, where he took part in the campaigns against the Mapuche (Araucanians) in 1557–59.

He wrote the first part of his epic La Araucana, much of it in the field, and published it in 1569; the second part followed in 1578 and the complete poem of three parts and thirty-seven cantos in 1589. Written in ottava rima, it is the foundational epic of Chile and is admired for its sympathetic portrait of the Mapuche leaders Lautaro, Caupolicán and Colocolo.

Major Works (1)

  1. La Araucana — Epic poem, 1569, 1578, 1589
