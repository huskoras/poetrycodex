Albert Mockel

1866–1945

Albert Mockel (born Ougrée, 27 December 1866; died Ixelles, 30 January 1945) was a Belgian Symbolist poet and writer, a Walloon militant, and a member of the Académie royale de langue et de littérature françaises.

Jethro Bithell's 1911 anthology classes him as a Walloon of distant German extraction and notes that he wrote a study of the sculptor Victor Rousseau.
