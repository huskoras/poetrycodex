Gottfried Keller

1819–1890

Gottfried Keller (1819–1890) was a Swiss poet and novelist, born in Zürich, and one of the foremost writers of German-language Poetic Realism. He trained as a landscape painter before turning to literature, and his lyric poetry first appeared in Gedichte (1846), followed by Neuere Gedichte (1851).

From 1861 to 1876 he served as Staatsschreiber (state secretary) of the canton of Zürich, and wrote much of his best-known prose, including Der grüne Heinrich and Die Leute von Seldwyla, alongside his office. His poems were gathered and revised in Gesammelte Gedichte (1883); the nature poems and evening songs selected here are drawn from that collection.
