Vittorio Alfieri

1749–1803

Vittorio Alfieri (1749-1803) was born in Asti, in Piedmont, and was educated at the Turin Academy. After years of travel across Europe he settled to writing in his late twenties, and produced the series of tragedies on which his fame rests, as well as satires, an autobiography and a large body of lyric verse.

His Rime, written between 1776 and the 1790s, are chiefly sonnets, many of them addressed to the Countess of Albany, with whom he lived from the 1780s until his death in Florence in 1803. The sonnets here follow the order of the 1903 Paravia printing of the Rime varie.
