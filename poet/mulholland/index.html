Rosa Mulholland, Lady Gilbert

1841–1921

Rosa Mulholland was born in Belfast and became a prolific novelist and poet. In 1891 she married the historian Sir John Gilbert and was afterwards known as Lady Gilbert.

Yeats's anthology prints her poem under the name Rosa Gilbert.
