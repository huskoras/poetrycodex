Paul the Silentiary

d. c. 575

Paul the Silentiary, a court official under Justinian (d. c. 575), wrote the great verse description of Hagia Sophia as well as many polished epigrams, most of them erotic.

He is one of the best represented poets of the Cycle of Agathias in Book V, and the epigrams here are those that Paton ascribes to him.
