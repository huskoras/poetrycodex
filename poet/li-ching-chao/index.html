Li Ch’ing-chao

1084–c. 1155

Li Ch’ing-chao (李清照; Li Qingzhao; 1084–c. 1155) was a writer of ci, the Chinese song lyrics composed to set tunes, in the Song dynasty. She is widely regarded as the greatest woman poet of China.

The 5 ci here are from the chinese-poetry dataset of Zhu Zumou's 1924 anthology Three Hundred Song Lyrics (宋词三百首), in simplified characters, with the tune name as title.
