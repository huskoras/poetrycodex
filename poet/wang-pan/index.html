Wang Pan

c. 1470–1530

Wang Pan (王磐, courtesy name Hongze) was a Ming dynasty writer and painter from Gaoyou in Jiangsu. He declined an official career and lived as a private scholar, and he is remembered chiefly for his sanqu, songs written to set tunes in the vernacular-flavoured style of the Yuan lyric.

His best-known songs are satirical. In two of them he mocks the eunuch-officials who travelled the Grand Canal in grand state boats, the trumpet and the sound of its blowing standing for the havoc they brought to the people along the water.
