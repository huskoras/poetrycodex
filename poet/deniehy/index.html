Victorian

Daniel Henry Deniehy

1828–1865

Daniel Henry Deniehy (1828–1865) was an Australian-born lawyer, journalist, orator and poet, remembered for his 1853 speech mocking a colonial 'bunyip aristocracy'.

He wrote verse for Sydney newspapers and edited the weekly The Southern Cross (1859–60).
