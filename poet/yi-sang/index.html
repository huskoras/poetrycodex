Yi Sang 이상

1910–1937

Yi Sang (이상 李箱, 1910–1937) was the pen name of Kim Hae-gyeong (김해경 金海卿), a Korean poet and novelist born in Seoul. Trained as an architect, he worked for the Government-General's building office from 1929 to 1933 before turning to writing; he is counted among the pioneers of Korean modernism.

His verse appeared in periodicals such as 가톨릭청년 (Catholic Youth) and 조선중앙일보 (the Joseon Jungang Ilbo), where the serial 오감도 (Ogamdo) ran in July and August 1934. He travelled to Tokyo in 1936 and died there in April 1937. The texts here are the poems printed in his lifetime, in the original orthography as transcribed on Korean Wikisource.
