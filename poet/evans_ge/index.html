Victorian

George Essex Evans

1863–1909

George Essex Evans (1863–1909) was an English-born poet who emigrated to Queensland as a young man and farmed on the Darling Downs.

His books include Loraine, and Other Verses (1898), and he wrote the Federation 'Ode for Commonwealth Day'.
