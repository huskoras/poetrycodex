German

Friedrich Hölderlin

1770–1843

Friedrich Hölderlin was born in 1770 at Lauffen am Neckar in Württemberg and studied theology at the Tübingen seminary, where Hegel and Schelling were among his friends. He chose not to enter the ministry and worked instead as a private tutor; at Frankfurt, in the household of the banker Jakob Gontard, he fell in love with Susette Gontard, the "Diotima" of his novel Hyperion (1797–99) and of many poems.

His odes, elegies and hymns revive Greek forms and themes in German. After a breakdown in 1802 he lived in Tübingen from 1807, in the care of the carpenter Ernst Zimmer, until his death in 1843. This selection gives the poems of Gedichte (1826), edited by Ludwig Uhland and Gustav Schwab (Deutsches Textarchiv), together with poems from their first printings in the almanacs and pocketbooks of the 1790s and 1800s (de.wikisource), in the spelling of those editions.
