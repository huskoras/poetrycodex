Antun Gustav Matoš

1873–1914

Antun Gustav Matoš (1873–1914) was a Croatian poet, short-story writer, critic and essayist, born in Tovarnik in Syrmia. He spent much of his adult life in Belgrade, Paris and Zagreb, and was long associated with the Croatian Modernist movement, to which he gave its most refined and cosmopolitan voice.

His poems, mostly written between about 1906 and 1914, are known for their musical, finely wrought sonnets and for a mingling of homesickness, love and melancholy. Several, such as Notturno, Utjeha kose and Jesenje veče, are among the best known in Croatian literature. The texts here are taken from the Croatian Wikisource edition of his collected poems.
