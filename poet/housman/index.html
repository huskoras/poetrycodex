Victorian

A. E. Housman

1859–1936

A. E. Housman (1859–1936) was an English classical scholar and poet whose reputation rests on two slim volumes, A Shropshire Lad (1896) and Last Poems (1922).

His terse, ballad-like lyrics of doomed youth, soldiering, and rural England were composed in a private grief masked by classical restraint.
