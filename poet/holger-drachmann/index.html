Nordic

Holger Drachmann

1846–1908

Holger Drachmann (1846–1908) was a Danish poet, dramatist and painter, born in Copenhagen. He trained as a marine painter before turning to writing, and became a figure of the Scandinavian Modern Breakthrough and a member of the artists' colony at Skagen, where he is buried.

The poems here are taken from his early collections Digte (1872), Dæmpede Melodier (1875) and the opening poems of Sange ved Havet (1877), in the spelling of the first editions as kept by Kalliope. The sea, the coast and the life of sailors and fishermen run through them, beside political satire, ballads of Danish and Norwegian history, and love lyrics.
