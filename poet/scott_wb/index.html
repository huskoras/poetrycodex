Victorian

William Bell Scott

1812–1890

William Bell Scott (1812–1890; dates as printed in the anthology) was a Scottish painter and poet, a friend of Dante Gabriel Rossetti and master of the government school of design at Newcastle. His verse was gathered in Poems (1875).

The poems given here are taken from Edmund H. Garrett's anthology Victorian Songs (Boston: Little, Brown, 1895) and R. Brimley Johnson's Popular British Ballads (1894).
