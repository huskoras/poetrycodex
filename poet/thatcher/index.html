B. B. Thatcher

1809–1840

Benjamin B. Thatcher (1809–1840) was a lawyer and writer, born in Warren, Maine, and a graduate of Bowdoin College (1826).

Griswold included 1 of his poems in The Poets and Poetry of America (16th edition, Philadelphia: Parry & McMillan, 1855), the anthology from which the poems here are taken.
