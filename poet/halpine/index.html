Victorian

Charles Graham Halpine

1829–1868

Charles Graham Halpine (1829–1868), born at Oldcastle, County Meath, was a journalist and poet who wrote anti-secession verse as "Miles O'Reilly" and rose to brigadier-general in the American Civil War. He died in New York.

The poems here are taken from H. Halliday Sparling's anthology Irish Minstrelsy (London: Walter Scott, 1888).

Poems (6)

  1. O Lady of Kinsa
  2. The Hill of Killenarden
  3. Baron Renfrew's Ball
  4. Lecompton's Black Brigade
  5. The Song of Sherman's Army
  6. "Mr. Johnson's Policy of Reconstruction"
