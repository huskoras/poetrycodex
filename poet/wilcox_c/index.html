American

Carlos Wilcox

1794–1827

Carlos Wilcox (1794–1827) was a Congregational minister and poet, born in Newport, New Hampshire. His poems were collected posthumously in 1828.

Griswold included 10 of his poems in The Poets and Poetry of America (16th edition, Philadelphia: Parry & McMillan, 1855), the anthology from which the poems here are taken.
