American

George Santayana

1863–1952

George Santayana (1863–1952) was a philosopher, essayist and poet, born in Madrid and raised in Boston. He taught philosophy at Harvard and wrote The Sense of Beauty (1896) and Interpretations of Poetry and Religion (1900).

His poetry, mostly in sonnet form, includes Sonnets and Other Verses (1894) and Lucifer (1899). Rittenhouse printed three of his sonnets in 1913.

  2. We needs must be divided in the Tomb
