Tudor & Elizabethan

Sir John Davies

1569–1626

Sir John Davies was an English poet, lawyer and politician who later served as Attorney General for Ireland.

He is best remembered for ‘Nosce Teipsum,’ a long philosophical poem on the nature of the soul, and for witty, compact epigrams on the paradox of human self-knowledge.
