Zygmunt Krasiński

1812–1859

Zygmunt Krasiński (1812–1859) was a Polish Romantic poet and dramatist, born in Paris into an aristocratic Polish family and educated in Warsaw and Geneva. He spent most of his life abroad and wrote in Polish, though he published anonymously during his lifetime, and he is counted with Mickiewicz and Słowacki as one of the three national bards of Polish Romanticism.

He is best known for the dramas Nie-Boska komedia (1835) and Irydion (1836), which are not included here. This selection gives his religious and visionary verse, above all the Psalmy przyszłości (Psalms of the Future, 1845–48), prophetic poems that answer one another and set out his messianic view of Poland's destiny. Texts are in the original Polish, from public-domain editions digitised by Wolne Lektury.
