American

John Hay

1838–1905

John Hay (1838–1905) was an American diplomat and writer, born in Salem, Indiana, who served as private secretary to Abraham Lincoln and later as Secretary of State.

His dialect ballads, gathered in Pike County Ballads (1871) and including “Jim Bludso”, made him widely known as a poet.
