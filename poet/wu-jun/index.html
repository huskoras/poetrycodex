Wu Jun 吳均

469–520

Wu Jun (469–520) was a poet and historian of the Liang dynasty, a native of Wuxing (modern Zhejiang). Of humble origin, he won the notice of the Liang court through his verse and served as a minor official; he is remembered both for his poems and for his short prose letters on mountain and river scenery. His clear, brisk manner in landscape and frontier verse was admired and imitated, and later critics spoke of a 'Wu Jun style' (Wu Jun ti).

The poems here are drawn from the Wu Chaoqing ji, his collected works as gathered by Zhang Pu in the Han Wei Liu chao bai san jia ji (Ming) and transcribed on Chinese Wikisource, with two poems from Shen Deqian's Gushi yuan (1719), juan 13. Fifty poems are given, in the order of the collection; his prose (rhapsodies, memorials, letters and other pieces, including the letter to Zhu Yuansi) is not included.
