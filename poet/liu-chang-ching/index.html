Liu Ch’ang-ch’ing

8th century AD

Liu Ch’ang-ch’ing is dated by Giles to the 8th century AD, in the T’ang dynasty. His poem here is set at a washerwoman’s grave.

The poems here are Herbert A. Giles’s versions in Chinese Poetry in English Verse (1898), given with the poet’s name as spelled in that book.
