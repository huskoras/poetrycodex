Oliver Wendell Holmes

1809–1894

Oliver Wendell Holmes Sr. (1809–1894) was a Boston physician, Harvard professor of anatomy and physiology, essayist and poet, one of the Fireside Poets who made New England verse a household possession. His 1830 poem “Old Ironsides” is credited with helping to save the frigate Constitution from being broken up.

His genial comic and occasional verse — “The Deacon’s Masterpiece,” “The Last Leaf,” the dinner poems read for his Harvard classmates — sits beside the more serious “The Chambered Nautilus,” first printed in his Atlantic Monthly series “The Autocrat of the Breakfast-Table” (1857–58). The poems here follow his collected Poetical Works as issued in 1893.
