Nikolay Gumilev (Николай Гумилёв)

1886–1921

Nikolay Stepanovich Gumilev (1886–1921), Russian poet, critic and traveller, was born in Kronstadt and grew up at Tsarskoye Selo, where he was a pupil of the poet Innokenty Annensky. He travelled in Africa and fought as a cavalry officer in the First World War.

With Sergey Gorodetsky he founded the Poets' Guild in 1911, and with it the Acmeist movement, which prized clear images and exact craft against Symbolist vagueness. He was married for a time to Anna Akhmatova. This selection gives the poems of Kolchan (1916), Kostyor and Ognennyi stolp, the last of them his most visionary book.

He was arrested in August 1921 in Petrograd on a charge of conspiracy and shot by the Soviet authorities that month.
