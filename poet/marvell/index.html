Metaphysical & Cavalier

Andrew Marvell

1621–1678

Andrew Marvell (1621–1678) was an English metaphysical poet and politician.

Celebrated for lyrics such as ‘To His Coy Mistress’ and ‘The Garden,’ most of his poems appeared posthumously in the Miscellaneous Poems of 1681.
