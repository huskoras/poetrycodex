Victorian

Mary Howitt

1799–1888

Mary Howitt (née Botham, 1799–1888) was an English poet and writer, born at Coleford in Gloucestershire and brought up in a Quaker family in Staffordshire. Married to the writer William Howitt, she was widely read for her ballads and for verse written for children, above all “The Spider and the Fly”, and she later translated Hans Christian Andersen and Fredrika Bremer into English.

The poems here are taken from the Philadelphia edition of 1844, which gathers her hymns and fireside verses, poems of birds, flowers and country things, sketches of natural history, tales in verse and miscellaneous pieces. The memoir, the dramatic pieces The Poor Scholar and The Sorrow of Teresa, and a dramatic sketch for a parrot are not included.
