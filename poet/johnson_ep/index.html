E. Pauline Johnson

1861–1913

E. Pauline Johnson (Tekahionwake, 1861–1913) was a Mohawk-English Canadian poet and performer, born on the Six Nations reserve near Brantford, Ontario.

Her best-known collection is The White Wampum (1895); the poems here predate it. One of the poets chosen by W. D. Lighthall for Songs of the Great Dominion (London, 1889), an anthology of Canadian verse.
