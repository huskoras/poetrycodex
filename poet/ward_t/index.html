American

Thomas Ward

1807–1873

Thomas Ward (1807–1873) was a physician and poet of Newark, New Jersey, who wrote under the name Flaccus.

Griswold included 3 of his poems in The Poets and Poetry of America (16th edition, Philadelphia: Parry & McMillan, 1855), the anthology from which the poems here are taken.

Poems (3)

  1. Musings on Rivers
  2. To the Magnolia
  3. To an Infant in Heaven
