Italian

Sergio Corazzini

1886–1907

Sergio Corazzini (Rome, 6 February 1886 – Rome, 17 June 1907) was an Italian poet, the youngest and most youthful voice of the Crepuscolari (the "twilight poets"), who answered the grand rhetoric of D'Annunzio with a small, confessional, deliberately humble poetry of sickrooms, convents, gardens and Sunday evenings. He published his first book, Dolcezze, in 1904, at the age of eighteen, and followed it in quick succession with L'amaro calice and Le aureole (1905), and with Piccolo libro inutile, Elegia and Libro per la sera della domenica (1906).

He died of tuberculosis at twenty-one. His best-known poem, "Desolazione del povero poeta sentimentale" ("Desolation of the Poor Sentimental Poet"), whose refrain declares "Io non sono un poeta", became a manifesto of the Crepuscolari. The text here is taken from Liriche (Naples, Ricciardi, 1935), a scan-backed edition transcribed on Italian Wikisource (CC BY-SA), and gives the whole of the Piccolo libro inutile selection together with the verse of the other books.
