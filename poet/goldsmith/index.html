Restoration & Augustan

Oliver Goldsmith

1728–1774

Oliver Goldsmith (1728–1774) was an Irish-born writer of extraordinary range — poet, playwright, and novelist — whose warmth and clarity made him one of the best-loved authors of his age.

The Deserted Village, his elegy for a vanishing rural England, and Retaliation, his affectionate mock-epitaphs on his own dinner-table friends, remain his most-read poems.
