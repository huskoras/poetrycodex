Hungarian

Gyula Juhász

1883–1937

Gyula Juhász (1883-1937) was a Hungarian poet, born in Szeged, where he spent most of his life. He studied in Budapest, taught as a schoolmaster in provincial towns, and from 1909 his poems appeared in the review Nyugat ("West"). Melancholy, restrained and classical in form, his verse is much concerned with lost love, the Tisza landscape, antiquity and the loneliness of the provincial poet.

Among his best-known poems are those of the "Anna" cycle, including "Milyen volt...". He died in Szeged in 1937. This selection takes poems from Hungarian Wikisource, beginning with the Anna poems and then following the author page's order of composition, 1903 onward; only transcriptions without a low text-quality rating are included.
