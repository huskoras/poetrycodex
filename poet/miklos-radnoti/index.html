Hungarian

Miklós Radnóti

1909-1944

Miklós Radnóti (1909-1944) was a Hungarian poet and translator, born in Budapest. He studied at Szeged and published his first book, Pogány köszöntő, in 1930; the next years brought Ujmódi pásztorok éneke (1931), Lábadozó szél (1933), Újhold (1935), Járkálj csak, halálraítélt! (1936) and Meredek út (1938). He also translated widely from Latin, French and German.

Of Jewish descent, he was repeatedly called up for forced labour from 1940 and was shot on a forced march in November 1944. His last poems, written in a notebook found on his body, were published after the war. This selection gives the poems of his six lifetime books in the order of the Hungarian Wikisource author page, as far as the first sixty.
