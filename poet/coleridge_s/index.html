Victorian

Sara Coleridge

1802–1852

Sara Coleridge (1802–1852) was the daughter of Samuel Taylor Coleridge and Sara Fricker, and the wife of her cousin Henry Nelson Coleridge. She was a translator and editor of her father's work, and published Pretty Lessons in Verse for Good Children (1834).

Her fairy romance Phantasmion (1837), a prose tale threaded with songs, is the source of several of the lyrics Elizabeth A. Sharp chose for Women Poets of the Victorian Era (1890).
