Hieronymus van Alphen

1746-1803

Hieronymus van Alphen (1746-1803) was a Dutch jurist and poet, born at Gouda. He trained in law at Leiden, served as a pensionary of The Hague and later as a member of the Raad van State, and was a leading figure of the Dutch Enlightenment.

He is remembered above all for his children's verse, the Proeve van kleine gedigten voor kinderen (Utrecht, 1778-1782), issued in three small instalments. Their plain, moral and affectionate rhymes, such as 'De pruimeboom' and 'Het vrolijk leeren', were taught to Dutch schoolchildren for generations, and the collection is a landmark of Dutch children's literature.
