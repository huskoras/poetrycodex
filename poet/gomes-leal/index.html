Gomes Leal

1848-1921

António Duarte Gomes Leal (Lisbon, 1848 – Lisbon, 1921) was a Portuguese poet, a leading figure of the anti-clerical and satirical current of the Portuguese 1870s generation, which also produced Antero de Quental. Fiercely republican and often at odds with the Church and the monarchy, he lived largely in poverty and was known as a poète maudit of Lisbon.

Claridades do Sul (1875) gathers sonnets and longer poems in six parts, from the solar hymns of its opening to its satires of city life. The texts here follow the printed 1875 spelling.
