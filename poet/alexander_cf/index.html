Victorian

Cecil Frances Alexander

1818–1895

Cecil Frances Alexander (1818–1895) was an Anglo-Irish hymn-writer and poet, the author of “All Things Bright and Beautiful”; the anthology prints her birth year as 1823.

The poems here are taken from Alfred H. Miles (ed.), The Poets and the Poetry of the Century (London: Hutchinson, 1891–1897), vol. 10, from the Internet Archive scans of that edition. Spelling is that of the anthology; Miles's biographical essay and extracts from longer poems are omitted, and only poems that could be read cleanly from the scan are included.
