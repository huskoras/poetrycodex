American

John Pierpont

1785–1866

John Pierpont (1785–1866) was a Unitarian minister, poet and reformer, born in Litchfield, Connecticut, and author of Airs of Palestine (1816). He was long minister of the Hollis Street Church in Boston.

Griswold included 14 of his poems in The Poets and Poetry of America (16th edition, Philadelphia: Parry & McMillan, 1855), the anthology from which the poems here are taken.
