Amir Khusrau

1253–1325

Amir Khusrau Dihlavi (امیرخسرو دهلوی, 1253–1325) was born in Delhi and served several sultans of the Delhi court. He wrote in Persian and was also a musician, and he is remembered as one of the great Persian poets of India.

He is best known for his ghazals, short rhymed lyrics of love and longing. The ghazals here are from his Divan, in the Persian text published on Ganjoor, chosen by how often they have been recited and set to music.
