Jean de Sponde

1557–1595

Jean de Sponde (1557–1595) was a Basque-born humanist and poet from Mauléon in Navarre, raised a Huguenot at the court of Jeanne d'Albret. He edited and translated Homer, wrote on Aristotle and on law, and served Henri de Navarre; in 1593, shortly before his death, he converted to Catholicism.

His verse was printed only after his death, in Raphaël Du Petit Val's Premier recueil of 1604. The love sonnets (Les Amours), the Stances de la mort and above all the twelve Sonnets sur la mort were largely forgotten until the twentieth century, when critics recognised them as among the finest poems of the French Baroque.
