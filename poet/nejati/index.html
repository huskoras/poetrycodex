Turkish & Ottoman

Nejâtî

d. 1508

Nejati, whose real name was 'Isa (Jesus), was born, according to Qinali-Zada, at Adrianople but brought up at Kastamonu, and won his first laurels at Bursa.

The poems here are taken from E. J. W. Gibb's Ottoman Poems (1882), the first English verse translations of Ottoman poetry; dates and biographical details follow Gibb's Biographical Notices.

Poems (28)

  5. O Handkerchief! I Send Thee Off

  7. Câm-ı hecrün nûş ider mestâneler gördin mi hiç

  11. Lâle-hadler yine gülşende neler etmediler

  18. Şevk-i ruhsârunla dil zülf-i dü-tâdan korkmaz
  19. Through This Wrong, That Cup Should Buss the Lip of Thee a-Turn a-Turn
  20. O Heart, Strike Off Thy Name from Fellowship's Gay Muster-Roll

  26. As the Bow Is Stretched the Arrow Forward Flies
  27. O Radiant Pursuivant, O Morning Breeze
  28. Nejati, Though Thou'st Blackened the Leaves of Thy Diwan
