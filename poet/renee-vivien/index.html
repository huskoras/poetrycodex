Renée Vivien

1877–1909

Renée Vivien was the pen name of Pauline Mary Tarn (1877–1909), an English-born poet who lived in Paris and wrote in French. She published under the name Renée Vivien from 1901 and died in Paris at the age of thirty-two.

Her first books, Études et Préludes (1901) and Cendres et Poussières (1902), were issued by Alphonse Lemerre. This selection takes the poems of those two books from the Wikisource transcription of the printed editions, in the order of the volumes. Her Sapho (1903), a book of versions of Sappho, and her prose poems are not included.
