Fazio degli Uberti

c. 1305–c. 1367

Fazio degli Uberti (c. 1305–c. 1367) was a Florentine poet of the Trecento, a member of the Ghibelline family of the Uberti, who spent most of his life in exile from Florence and lived at various north-Italian courts, notably those of the Della Scala at Verona and the Visconti at Milan.

He is chiefly remembered for the Dittamondo, an unfinished allegorical and geographical poem in terza rima, modelled on Dante's Commedia, in which the poet travels the known world guided by the Roman geographer Solinus. He also wrote lyrics, among them canzoni and sonnets of love and politics; D. G. Rossetti translated his portrait of a lady's beauty in Early Italian Poets (1861).
