American

Jones Very

1813–1880

Jones Very (1813–1880) was a Salem, Massachusetts, poet, tutor and preacher, a friend of the Transcendentalists, best known for his devotional sonnets.

Griswold included 18 of his poems in The Poets and Poetry of America (16th edition, Philadelphia: Parry & McMillan, 1855), the anthology from which the poems here are taken.
