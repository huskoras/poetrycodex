American

Cale Young Rice

1872–1943

Cale Young Rice (1872–1943) was an American poet and dramatist, born at Dixon, Kentucky. He studied at Cumberland University and Harvard, and published many volumes of lyrics as well as poetic dramas such as Yolanda of Cyprus (1906).

He was married to the novelist Alice Hegan Rice. His lyric collections include From Dusk to Dusk (1898), Many Gods (1910) and Far Quests (1912).
