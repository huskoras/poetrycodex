Tobias Smollett

1721–1771

Tobias Smollett (1721–1771) was a Scottish surgeon and writer, born at Dalquhurn in Dunbartonshire and trained in Glasgow. He served as a naval surgeon's mate, settled in London, and made his name with the picaresque novels Roderick Random (1748), Peregrine Pickle (1751) and Humphry Clinker (1771).

His verse is the smaller part of his work: the satires Advice and Reproof, The Tears of Scotland, written after Culloden in 1746, and the Ode to Independence, published after his death. He died near Livorno in Italy in 1771.
