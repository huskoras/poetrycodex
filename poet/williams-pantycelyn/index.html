Celtic

William Williams Pantycelyn

1717–1791

William Williams, called Pantycelyn after his home, was a leading Welsh Methodist preacher and the greatest of Welsh hymn-writers, often called the national poet of Wales.

His hymn "Marchog Jesu!" is given in English verse by Alfred Perceval Graves in A Celtic Psaltery (1917).
