Metaphysical & Cavalier

Thomas Carew

1595–1640

Thomas Carew (1595–1640) was an English Cavalier poet at the court of Charles I, admired for the polish of his amorous lyrics.

His Poems (1640) circulated widely and shaped the courtly style later followed by Lovelace and Suckling.
