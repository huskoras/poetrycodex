William Vaughn Moody

1869–1910

William Vaughn Moody (1869–1910) was an American poet and dramatist, born in Spencer, Indiana, who taught at the University of Chicago.

His work includes the “Ode in Time of Hesitation”, the verse drama The Fire-Bringer (1904) and the play The Great Divide (1906).
