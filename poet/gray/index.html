Restoration & Augustan

Thomas Gray

1716–1771

Thomas Gray (1716–1771) was a reclusive Cambridge scholar who published only a handful of poems in his lifetime, yet his Elegy Written in a Country Churchyard became one of the most quoted poems in the English language.

His Pindaric odes The Progress of Poesy and The Bard helped carry English verse from Augustan restraint toward the Romantic sublime.
