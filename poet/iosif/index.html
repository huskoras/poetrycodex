Ștefan Octavian Iosif

1875–1913

Ștefan Octavian Iosif (1875–1913) was a Romanian poet and translator, born in Brașov in Transylvania. He settled in Bucharest, where he worked as a clerk and writer and became a leading voice of the Sămănătorul circle, whose poetry looked to village life and national history.

His first volume, Versuri, appeared in 1897 and was followed by Patriarhale (1901), a book of gentle pastoral and domestic pictures, and by later collections. He also translated widely, among others from Heine, Petőfi and Lenau, and collaborated with Dimitrie Anghel. He died in Bucharest in 1913.
