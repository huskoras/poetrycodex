Victorian

Sir Samuel Ferguson

1810–1886

Sir Samuel Ferguson was born in Belfast and trained as a barrister. He was Deputy Keeper of the Public Records of Ireland and was knighted in 1878.

His verse drew on Irish legend and history, notably in Lays of the Western Gael (1865) and the long poem Congal (1872).
