Metaphysical & Cavalier

Thomas Flatman

1635–1688

Thomas Flatman (1635–1688) was an English poet and miniature painter, born in London, educated at Winchester and New College, Oxford, and called to the bar at the Inner Temple. He became a Fellow of the Royal Society in 1668 and was admired for his portrait miniatures as much as for his verse.

His Poems and Songs went through four editions between 1674 and 1686. They range from Pindaric odes on the deaths of public figures, through devotional pieces such as the Hymn for the Morning, to witty songs and satires. The text here follows George Saintsbury's Minor Poets of the Caroline Period, vol. III (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1921), leaving out his Latin pieces and his translations and paraphrases of Horace, Ovid and others.
