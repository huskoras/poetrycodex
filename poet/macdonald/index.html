Victorian

George MacDonald

1824–1905

George MacDonald (1824–1905) was a Scottish minister turned author, celebrated for fantasy novels such as <em>Phantastes</em> and <em>The Princess and the Goblin</em>, and a lifelong writer of devotional and reflective verse.

His poems return often to faith, fatherhood, nature, and the soul's struggle toward trust in a loving God.
