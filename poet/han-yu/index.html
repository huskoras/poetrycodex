Han Yü

AD 768–824

Han Yü (AD 768–824) was one of the great prose writers and poets of the T’ang dynasty, a champion of Confucian teaching who was banished from court for protesting against the veneration of a Buddhist relic.

The poems here are Herbert A. Giles’s versions in Chinese Poetry in English Verse (1898), given with the poet’s name as spelled in that book.
