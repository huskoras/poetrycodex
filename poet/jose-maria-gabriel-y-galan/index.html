José María Gabriel y Galán

1870–1905

José María Gabriel y Galán (1870–1905) was a Spanish poet of Castile and Extremadura. Born in Frades de la Sierra, near Salamanca, he trained as a schoolteacher and took up farming in Guijo de Granadilla, in the province of Cáceres, where he lived with his wife and children until his early death in January 1905.

His verse, collected in Castellanas, Extremeñas and Campesinas and in the Obras completas that began to appear in 1909, celebrates the labour, the landscape and the faith of the peasants among whom he lived. Many poems are written in the Extremaduran country speech, whose spelling is kept here as printed. "Los pastores de mi abuelo" was chosen by James Fitzmaurice-Kelly for The Oxford Book of Spanish Verse (1913).
