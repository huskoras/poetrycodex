Georges Marlow

1872–1947

Georges Marlow (born Mechelen, 1 April 1872; died Uccle, 31 March 1947) was a Belgian poet writing in French.

Jethro Bithell's 1911 anthology classes him as a Walloon with an English grandfather and prints two of his poems.
