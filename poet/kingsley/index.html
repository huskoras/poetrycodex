Victorian

Charles Kingsley

1819–1875

Charles Kingsley (1819–1875) was an English clergyman, novelist, and poet, remembered as a children's author for <em>The Water-Babies</em> and as a muscular-Christian social reformer.

His poems range from the classical narrative <em>Andromeda</em>, written in unrhymed hexameters, to plain-spoken ballads and songs of the sea and English rural life.
