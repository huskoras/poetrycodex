Julio Herrera y Reissig

1875–1910

Julio Herrera y Reissig (1875–1910) was a Uruguayan poet, born in Montevideo, and the most original voice of Uruguayan modernismo. Bedridden by heart disease for much of his short life, he gathered friends and writers in the attic of his family house, the "Torre de los Panoramas", and died in Montevideo at thirty-five.

His verse pushes the modernista manner of Rubén Darío towards a dense, ironic and startlingly metaphoric style, whose countryside idylls and sonnet sequences look ahead to the avant-garde. Most of his books appeared in print only in part during his lifetime and were gathered after his death in the Obras completas of 1913.

The poems here come from the Spanish Wikisource transcriptions of three works printed in his lifetime: Las pascuas del tiempo (1902), the sonnet sequence Los éxtasis de la montaña (1904–1907) and La torre de las esfinges (1909), each poem under the title it bears in Spanish.
