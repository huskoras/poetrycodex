American

James T. Fields

1817–1881

James T. Fields (1817–1881) was a Portsmouth, New Hampshire-born Boston publisher, a partner in Ticknor and Fields, editor of the Atlantic Monthly and a poet.

Griswold included 13 of his poems in The Poets and Poetry of America (16th edition, Philadelphia: Parry & McMillan, 1855), the anthology from which the poems here are taken.
