Ancient Greek & Roman

Marcus Argentarius

1st century BC/AD

Marcus Argentarius was an epigrammatist of the Garland of Philippus, writing around the turn of the era. He is best known for sharply observed love and banquet epigrams.

The epigrams here are those that Paton's first volume ascribes to him by name.
