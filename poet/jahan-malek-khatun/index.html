Jahan Malek Khatun

c. 1324–after 1382

Jahan Malek Khatun (born c. 1324) was a princess of the Injuid dynasty that ruled Shiraz in the fourteenth century, and a contemporary of the poet Hafiz, who lived in the same city and court world. She wrote in Persian, chiefly ghazals, the short rhymed love lyric of the classical tradition, and her work was gathered in a divan.

Her divan, of well over a thousand ghazals together with qasidas, qit'as and ruba'is, is the largest surviving collection by any woman poet of pre-modern Persian literature. The ghazals here follow the order of the Ganjoor edition; they speak in the conventional voice of longing for an absent beloved, with a directness that has drawn growing attention to her.
