Dan Andersson

1888–1920

Dan Andersson (1888–1920) was a Swedish poet, storyteller and composer, born in Skattlösberg in the Dalarna region. He worked as a charcoal burner, farmhand and labourer in the forest and mining districts of central Sweden and drew on that life in his verse, which is counted among the finest of the Swedish working-class writers though it is not confined to that label. Several of his poems he set to music himself.

His books of verse were Kolvaktarens visor (1915) and Svarta ballader (1917), with the collection Chi-mo-ka-ma following in 1920; his prose includes the stories of Kolarhistorier (1914). He died in Stockholm in 1920. This selection follows Swedish Wikisource, which prints the poems of his lifetime books and some of his uncollected and posthumous verse, taking the two main books first in their order and keeping the printed spelling.
