American

George P. Morris

1802–1864

George P. Morris (1802–1864) was a Philadelphia-born New York editor and songwriter, co-editor of the New-York Mirror and author of Woodman, Spare That Tree.

Griswold included 11 of his poems in The Poets and Poetry of America (16th edition, Philadelphia: Parry & McMillan, 1855), the anthology from which the poems here are taken.
