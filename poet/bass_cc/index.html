American

Cora C. Bass

1868–1928

Cora C. Bass (1868–1928) was an American poet of Lowell, Massachusetts. Her verse appeared in periodicals such as The New York Observer, Zion's Herald, the Boston Transcript and the Portland Transcript before it was gathered into books.

She published Poems (Lowell: Lawler & Co., 1899), also issued under the name 'Harley Vane', and followed it with Songs for All Seasons, and Other Poems (Lowell: Lawler Printing Co., 1901). Her work is devotional, seasonal and patriotic, and includes pieces written for Memorial Day services at Huntington Hall.
