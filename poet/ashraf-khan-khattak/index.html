Ashraf Khan Khattak

1634–1693

Ashraf Khan, eldest son of Khushhal Khan, was born in 1634 and became chief of the Khattaks in 1681 when his father stepped down. His brother Bahram intrigued against him, and he was betrayed to the Mughals and sent to the fortress of Bijapur in the Deccan.

He died in captivity in 1693, aged about sixty, and Raverty says his poems were written in his leisure hours. They are chiefly poems of exile and longing, signed with a pen-name that the translator renders as 'the Separated'.
