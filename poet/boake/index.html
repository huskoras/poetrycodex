Barcroft Boake

1866–1892

Barcroft Boake (1866–1892) was an Australian bush poet and surveyor, born in Sydney. He worked as a surveyor in New South Wales and Queensland and wrote ballads of the outback, its droughts, drovers and lonely deaths.

His title poem, "Where the Dead Men Lie", appeared in the Bulletin in 1891. He died in 1892, aged twenty-six, and his poems were collected after his death in Where the Dead Men Lie, and Other Poems (1897), with a memoir by A. G. Stephens; an enlarged edition followed in 1913.
