Victorian

Oscar Wilde

1854–1900

Oscar Wilde (1854–1900) was an Irish poet, playwright, and wit, one of the most celebrated figures of Victorian literary London before his 1895 trial and imprisonment.

His early verse is lush and Romantic in the Keatsian mode, while <em>The Ballad of Reading Gaol</em>, written after his release from prison, is a stark, plainspoken account of suffering and remorse.
