Arabic & Persian

Kalim Kashani

c.1581-1651

Kalim Kashani (c. 1581-1651), born in Hamadan and raised in Kashan, was one of the foremost Persian poets of the seventeenth century. Like many Iranian poets of his age he left for Mughal India, where he found patronage at the courts of Jahangir and then Shah Jahan.

He became poet laureate (malik al-shu'ara) to Shah Jahan and is counted among the great masters of the Indian style (sabk-e hendi), prized for fresh imagery and subtle conceits. His divan contains several hundred ghazals, besides masnavis, qasidas, qit'as and ruba'is; this selection draws on his ghazals only.
