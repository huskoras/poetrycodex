Branko Radičević

1824–1853

Branko Radičević (1824–1853) was a Serbian poet of the Romantic period, born in Slavonski Brod in the Habsburg Monarchy. He attended the gymnasium at Sremski Karlovci and then studied in Vienna, where he fell in with the circle of Vuk Karadžić and the movement to write Serbian in the language of the people.

His first book, Pesme (Vienna, 1847), was printed in Karadžić's reformed orthography and brought a new, light and musical lyric voice to Serbian verse; its best-known piece is the long farewell to his school years, Đački rastanak. He died of tuberculosis in Vienna at the age of twenty-eight, and is buried at Stražilovo on Fruška Gora.
