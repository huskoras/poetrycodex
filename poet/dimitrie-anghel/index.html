Romanian

Dimitrie Anghel

1872–1914

Dimitrie Anghel (1872–1914) was a Romanian poet and prose writer, born in Cornu de Sus (Prahova County) and educated in Bucharest. He was associated with the Symbolist circles and with the review Sămănătorul, and he worked closely with his friend Ștefan Octavian Iosif, with whom he wrote verse and plays under shared and pen names.

His best-known volumes of verse are În grădină (1905), a cycle of garden and flower poems, and Fantazii (1909); his prose appeared in Fantome (1911) and later volumes. He died in Bucharest in 1914, aged forty-two.
