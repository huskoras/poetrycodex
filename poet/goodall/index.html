Charles Goodall

1671–1689

Charles Goodall (1671–1689) is known only through one posthumous-looking book, Poems and Translations, Written upon Several Occasions, and to Several Persons (1689), whose title page says merely that it is "by a late Scholar of Eaton". The attribution to Goodall, and his dates of 1671 to 1689, come from the catalogue record of the EEBO-TCP transcription, not from the printed book; on those dates he died at about eighteen.

The volume carries a prose dedication to the Countess of Clarendon and a verse dedication to Mr. Roderick, Upper-Master of Eaton-School. Its contents are schoolboy and undergraduate-age verse: love poems and epigrams to a Hemiera and an Idera, verse letters to friends and to his brother, an elegy on Oldham, and versions of Anacreon, Bion, Theocritus, Catullus, Ovid, Seneca and Juvenal, with Greek epigrams in English. The spelling of the 1689 print is kept here.
