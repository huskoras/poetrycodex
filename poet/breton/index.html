Tudor & Elizabethan

Nicholas Breton

c. 1545–1626

Nicholas Breton was a prolific English poet and prose writer of the Elizabethan and Jacobean periods, admired for pastoral lyrics such as ‘Phillida and Coridon.’

He wrote across a remarkable range of genres, from devotional verse to satire, over a career of some fifty years.
