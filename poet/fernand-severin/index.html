Fernand Séverin

1867–1931

Fernand Séverin (born Grand-Manil, 4 February 1867; died Ghent, 4 September 1931) was a Belgian poet writing in French and a member of the Académie royale de langue et de littérature françaises.

Jethro Bithell's 1911 anthology classes him as a Walloon and names him among the chief Parnassians.
