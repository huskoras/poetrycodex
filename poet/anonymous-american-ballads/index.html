Anonymous (American Ballads and Songs)

traditional and anonymous; 17th–19th century

These ballads, songs, epigrams and satires are printed without an author's name in Burton E. Stevenson's Poems of American History (1908; revised 1922), where they illustrate events from the colonial period to the Civil War.

They are given here in the order of the book, which arranges its poems by historical chapter, from early voyages and settlements through the Revolution, the War of 1812 and the war between the States. Stevenson's own headnotes are not reproduced.
