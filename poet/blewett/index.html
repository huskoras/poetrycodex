American

Jean Blewett

1862–1934

Jean Blewett (born Jean McKishnie, 1862–1934) was a Canadian poet and journalist who wrote for the Toronto press and was widely read in Canada in her day.

Her verse, much of it in Scots and rural dialect, was gathered in Heart Songs (Toronto, 1897) and The Cornflower, and Other Poems (Toronto, 1906), both given here.
