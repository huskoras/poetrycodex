American

George Henry Boker

1823–1890

George Henry Boker (1823–1890) was an American poet and dramatist, born in Philadelphia. His blank-verse tragedy Francesca da Rimini (1855) is the best known of his plays, and he later served as United States minister to Turkey and to Russia.

During the Civil War he wrote patriotic Union verse, collected in Poems of the War (1864), including “The Black Regiment” and “Dirge for a Soldier”.
