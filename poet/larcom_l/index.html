American

Lucy Larcom

1824–1893

Lucy Larcom (1824–1893) was an American journalist.

Poems of American History gathers verse on events from the Norse voyages to the Spanish-American War, arranged by historical chapter. The poems here are “Mistress Hale of Beverly”, “The Nineteenth of April” and “The Sinking of the Merrimack”; Stevenson's editorial headnotes are not reproduced.
