Korean

Kim Yeong-rang

1903–1950

Kim Yeong-rang (김영랑 金永郞, real name Kim Yun-sik 김윤식, 1903–1950) was a Korean lyric poet from Gangjin in South Jeolla Province. In 1930 he joined Jeong Ji-yong, Park Yong-cheol and others in the circle around the magazine Si Munhak (詩文學, Poetry and Literature), where his first poems appeared.

His only collection of the colonial period, Yeongnang sijip (永郞詩集), was published by Si Munhaksa in 1935. It gathers fifty-three short lyrics, none of which has a title, and they are numbered here with their first lines. He died in Seoul in 1950, during the Korean War. The texts follow the 1935 orthography as printed, not the modernised spelling.
