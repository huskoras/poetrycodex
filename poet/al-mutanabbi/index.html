al-Mutanabbi

915–965

أبو الطيب المتنبي (Abu al-Tayyib Ahmad ibn al-Husayn al-Mutanabbi, 915–965) was born in Kufa and is generally counted the greatest poet of the Arabic language. He made his name as a praise-poet at the courts of the age, above all that of Sayf al-Dawla at Aleppo, and later at the court of Kafur in Egypt; he was killed on a journey home to Iraq in 965.

The poems given here are drawn from his Diwan, in the vocalised Arabic transcription of Arabic Wikisource, which is credited as the source of the text. They include panegyrics, elegies, satires and occasional verse; each bayt (verse line of two hemistichs) stands on one line.
