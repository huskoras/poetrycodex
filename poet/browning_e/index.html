Victorian

Elizabeth Barrett Browning

1806–1861

Elizabeth Barrett Browning (1806–1861) was one of the most celebrated English poets of her day, an invalid for much of her youth who eloped to Italy with fellow poet Robert Browning in 1846.

Her Sonnets from the Portuguese, written to Robert during their courtship, remain her best-loved work, alongside socially engaged narrative poems on slavery, child labour, and women's lives.
