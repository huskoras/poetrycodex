Gonçalves de Magalhães

1811–1882

Domingos José Gonçalves de Magalhães, Visconde de Araguaia (Rio de Janeiro, 1811 – Rome, 1882), is regarded as the founder of Romanticism in Brazilian literature. His Suspiros poéticos e saudades, published in Paris in 1836, is the work that traditionally marks the movement's beginning in Brazil.

He trained as a physician but made his career as a diplomat, serving the Brazilian empire in Europe and the Americas, and was created Visconde de Araguaia. He died in Rome in 1882. His epic A Confederação dos Tamoyos (1856), on the Tamoyo Indians' league against the Portuguese, drew a famous polemic with José de Alencar.
