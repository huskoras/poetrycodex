Lesya Ukrainka

1871–1913

Lesya Ukrainka (Леся Українка) was the pen name of Larysa Kosach-Kvitka, born in 1871 at Novohrad-Volynskyi in Volhynia, the daughter of the writer Olena Pchilka. She was ill with tuberculosis from childhood and died in 1913 at Surami in Georgia. She is counted among the major Ukrainian poets and dramatists.

This selection follows her two lifetime books of verse, both printed in Lviv: На крилах пісень (On the Wings of Songs), issued by the Shevchenko Society in 1893, and Думи і мрії (Thoughts and Dreams), issued in 1899. The texts follow Wikisource's transcriptions of the printed editions and keep each book's own spelling, which differs between the two (the first uses the Kulish orthography, the second the Zhelekhivka); the stress marks of the printed text have been omitted.
