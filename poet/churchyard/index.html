Tudor & Elizabethan

Thomas Churchyard

c. 1520–1604

Thomas Churchyard was an English soldier, courtier and prolific verse-writer who served in the wars in Scotland, France and the Low Countries, and in Ireland. He was in the household of the Earl of Surrey as a young man, and he published verse and prose for more than half a century, down to the reign of James I.

This selection is drawn from A Pleasaunte Laborinth called Churchyardes Chance (London: Ihon Kyngston, 1580): epitaphs for noble and gentle friends, verses presented to Queen Elizabeth, and miscellaneous poems on court, friendship, love and the world. Spelling is that of the printed book.
