Otokar Březina

1868–1929

Otokar Březina (1868–1929) was a Czech Symbolist poet, born Václav Jebavý at Počátky in Bohemia. He worked as a village schoolteacher at Jaroměřice nad Rokytnou and lived there in seclusion until his death. His mystical, visionary verse made him the leading figure of Czech Symbolism.

He published five books of poems: Tajemné dálky (1895), Svítání na západě (1896), Větry od pólů (1897), Stavitelé chrámu (1899) and Ruce (1901), followed by a book of essays, Hudba pramenů (1903). This selection takes poems from each of the five books, in their order, from Wikisource's transcriptions of the first editions, and keeps the printed spelling.
