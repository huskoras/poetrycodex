Ramón López Velarde

1888–1921

Ramón López Velarde (Jerez, Zacatecas, 1888 – Mexico City, 1921) was a Mexican poet, one of the most original voices of Spanish-American poetry in the generation after Modernismo. He grew up in the provincial towns of Zacatecas and Aguascalientes, studied law in San Luis Potosí, and moved to Mexico City in 1912, where he worked as a lawyer, teacher and journalist.

His first mature book, La sangre devota (1916), and Zozobra (1919) set provincial Catholic piety against erotic longing in a new, startling imagery. His best-known poem, La suave Patria (1921), a celebration of everyday Mexico in two "acts" with a Proemio and an Intermedio, appeared shortly before his early death from pneumonia at the age of thirty-three.
