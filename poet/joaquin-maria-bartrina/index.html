Joaquín María Bartrina

1850–1880

Joaquín María Bartrina i d'Aixemús (Reus, 26 April 1850 – Barcelona, 4 August 1880) was a Catalan poet who wrote chiefly in Spanish. Consumption cut his life short at thirty, and his friends gathered what he had left into the posthumous Obras en prosa y verso (Barcelona, 1881), which also holds a smaller group of poems in Catalan.

He is remembered above all for Algo (1876), a small volume of short, ironic pieces — epigrams, fables and the brief 'arabescos' — marked by pessimism and bitter wit. The Oxford Book of Spanish Verse (1913) prints two of them, 'Arabesco' and 'Fabulita'; this selection adds the Spanish-language poems of the 1881 collection.
