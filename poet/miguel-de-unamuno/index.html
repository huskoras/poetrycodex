Miguel de Unamuno

1864–1936

Miguel de Unamuno y Jugo (1864–1936) was a Basque-born Spanish writer and philosopher, born in Bilbao on 29 September 1864. He taught Greek and later served as rector of the University of Salamanca, the city whose Castilian landscape fills much of his verse, and he died there on 31 December 1936.

Best known for his novels and essays, Unamuno wrote a large body of poetry in which religious doubt, the hunger for immortality and the Spanish land are constant themes. The poems here are the sonnets of his Rosario de sonetos líricos (1911), transcribed on Spanish Wikisource from the Madrid edition printed by the Imprenta Española.
