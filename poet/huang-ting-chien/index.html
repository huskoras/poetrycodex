Huang T’ing-chien

11th–12th century AD

Huang T’ing-chien was a poet and calligrapher of the Sung dynasty. His poem here is set at the festival of the tombs.

The poems here are Herbert A. Giles’s versions in Chinese Poetry in English Verse (1898), given with the poet’s name as spelled in that book.
