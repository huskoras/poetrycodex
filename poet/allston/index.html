American

Washington Allston

1779–1843

Washington Allston (1779–1843) was a South Carolina-born painter, the leading American Romantic painter of his day, who also published verse, including The Sylphs of the Seasons (1813).

Griswold included 13 of his poems in The Poets and Poetry of America (16th edition, Philadelphia: Parry & McMillan, 1855), the anthology from which the poems here are taken.
