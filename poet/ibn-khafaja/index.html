Ibn Khafaja

1058–1139

Ibn Khafaja (ابن خفاجة), Abu Ishaq Ibrahim ibn Abi al-Fath ibn Abd Allah ibn Khafaja, was born in 1058 at Alzira (Jazirat Shuqar) in the region of Valencia, in al-Andalus. He lived on his family estate and, unusually for an Andalusian court poet, held no office and was never a professional panegyrist; he visited governors and princes and praised them in verse, and he died in Alzira in 1139.

He is known above all as the poet of gardens, rivers, mountains and storms, whose elaborate and vivid images of the Andalusian landscape made him the most celebrated of its nature poets. He also wrote wine poems, love lyrics, elegies and a few panegyrics, and an old man’s poems of repentance and weariness; his Diwan was collected and printed in the modern era. The texts here are transcriptions of the Diwan from Arabic Wikisource, with the vocalisation of the source; the pieces of three or more lines are given.
