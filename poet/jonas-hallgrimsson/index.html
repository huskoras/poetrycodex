Jónas Hallgrímsson

1807–1845

Jónas Hallgrímsson was born on 16 November 1807 at Hraun in Öxnadalur, northern Iceland, and died in Copenhagen on 26 May 1845. He studied natural science at the University of Copenhagen, took part in surveys of Iceland's land and natural history, and was one of the founders of the journal Fjölnir (1835), which promoted Icelandic language, literature and nationhood.

His poems, among them Ísland, Gunnarshólmi, Jeg bið að heilsa and Ferðalok, made him one of the best-loved Icelandic poets; his birthday, 16 November, is kept as the Day of the Icelandic Language. The texts here are the original poems of Ljóðmæli (Copenhagen, 1847, edited by Brynjólfur Pjetursson and Konráð Gíslason), in the spelling of that edition (eptir, jeg, hjer); the second division of the book, his renderings of other poets, is not included.
