Auguste Brizeux

1803–1858

Auguste Brizeux (born in Lorient, Brittany, in 1803) was a Romantic poet who made his native province his chief theme. He went to Paris as a young man and moved among the writers of the Romantic generation, but he remained devoted to the Breton language and countryside, and his French verse is full of Breton names and customs.

His first and most loved book, Marie (1831), is a sequence of idylls and elegies in which the love of a Breton girl is bound up with memories of childhood and landscape; it was followed by La Fleur d’or (1841), Les Bretons (1845) and the Histoires poétiques (1855). He divided his later years between Paris and Brittany, and died at Montpellier in 1858.
