Victorian

Katharine Tynan Hinkson

1861–1931

Katharine Tynan Hinkson (dates as printed in Untermeyer's Modern British Poetry (1920): 1861–1931) was an Irish poet and novelist, born in Dublin. A friend of the young W. B. Yeats and part of the Irish literary revival, she wrote devotional and pastoral verse as well as a great many novels.

She married the barrister Henry Hinkson. Her poems here come from Untermeyer's Modern British Poetry (1920).
