Ancient Greek & Roman

Theognis of Megara

fl. 6th century BC

Theognis of Megara was an archaic Greek elegiac poet, traditionally placed in the sixth century BC. A body of about 1,400 lines of elegiac couplets, the Theognidea, survives under his name, though much of it is probably the work of other hands and later date.

The two epigrams here come from the Greek Anthology and are printed in J. W. Mackail's Select Epigrams (1890); the saying that it is best never to have been born is one of the most quoted sentiments of Greek poetry.
