Abram Joseph Ryan (Father Ryan)

1838–1886

Abram Joseph Ryan (1838–1886) was an American Catholic priest and poet, born in Maryland or Virginia and raised in Missouri. Ordained in the 1860s, he served as a chaplain to Confederate troops during the Civil War, and afterwards ministered in parishes in the South, notably in Mobile, Alabama.

Known as the 'Poet-Priest of the South', he wrote devotional verse as well as elegies for the Confederate cause, among them 'The Conquered Banner' and 'The Sword of Robert Lee'. His collected Poems: Patriotic, Religious, Miscellaneous appeared in 1880 and was reprinted in the 1890s.
