Tachihara Michizō 立原道造

1914–1939

Tachihara Michizō (1914–1939) was a Japanese poet born in Tokyo. He trained as an architect at the Imperial University of Tokyo and won prizes for his designs, while writing poetry that drew on classical Japanese sentiment and on Western lyric models.

His first collection, Wasuregusa ni yosu (1937), was privately printed and gathered sonatina-like sequences of delicate, elegiac love lyrics in the old kana orthography. He died of tuberculosis in 1939, aged twenty-four, and is remembered as one of the most beloved lyric poets of the early Shōwa period.
