Thucydides the Historian

date not given by Paton

Paton prints under the name of Thucydides the historian an epitaph ascribed to him. Paton's Index of Authors includes the poet among those of Book VII. Paton's index of authors gives no date for the poet.

This archive carries the 1 epigram ascribed to the poet in the second volume of W. R. Paton's prose translation (Book VII: 45).
