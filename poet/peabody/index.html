Josephine Preston Peabody

1874–1922

Josephine Preston Peabody (1874–1922) was an American poet and dramatist, born in New York City and educated in Boston and at Radcliffe College. She taught English at Wellesley College from 1901 to 1903, and later married Lionel Marks.

Her verse includes The Singing Leaves (1903) and The Singing Man (1911); her verse play The Piper won the Stratford-on-Avon prize in 1910.
