Metaphysical & Cavalier

George Sandys

1578–1644

George Sandys (1578–1644) was an English traveller, colonist and poet. In 1610 he journeyed through the Ottoman Empire, Egypt and the Holy Land, and published an account of it as A Relation of a Journey (1615), one of the most widely read travel books of its day. From 1621 he was in Virginia as treasurer of the Virginia Company, and there he worked on his English translation of Ovid's Metamorphoses, published in full in 1632.

Back in England he turned to sacred verse: A Paraphrase upon the Psalms of David (1636) and A Paraphrase upon the Divine Poems (1638), which added Job, Ecclesiastes, Lamentations and the scriptural songs, with musical settings by Henry Lawes. The 1638 volume is the source of the poems here, given in the original spelling.
