Francisco de Figueroa

c. 1536–c. 1617

Francisco de Figueroa, called "El Divino", was a Spanish poet born about 1536, probably at Alcalá de Henares, where he settled after his marriage in 1575 on his return from Italy. He had served as a soldier in Italy, and he died about 1617.

He was a Petrarchan poet of the school of Garcilaso de la Vega, and appears as Tirsi in Cervantes's pastoral novel La Galatea. An incomplete edition of his poems was printed at Lisbon in 1625; he seems to have abandoned poetry in later life.

Poems (2)

  2. Madrigal: Triste de mí que parto, mas no parto
