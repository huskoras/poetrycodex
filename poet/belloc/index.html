Hilaire Belloc

1870–1953

Hilaire Belloc (1870–1953) was a French-born English writer and Liberal MP, educated at Balliol College, Oxford, who wrote history, travel books, essays, novels and verse. He was a close friend of G. K. Chesterton, and a Catholic apologist.

His verse, from Verses and Sonnets (1896) and Verses (1910), ranges from serious lyrics of Sussex and of faith to comic and satirical ballads such as the “Dedicatory Ode” and “Lines to a Don.” The Cautionary Tales and Bad Child’s Book of Beasts are his best-known children’s verse.
