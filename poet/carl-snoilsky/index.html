Carl Snoilsky

1841–1903

Carl Snoilsky (1841–1903) was a Swedish count, diplomat and poet. He began as a civil servant and member of the Riksdag, and his early lyrics, published from the late 1860s, made him one of the most admired Swedish poets of his generation.

In 1879 he gave up his position and left Sweden for a long period abroad, and his later work turned to historical subjects and narrative verse. Svenska bilder (1894) gathers his poems on figures and scenes of Swedish history, from medieval kings to the Age of Liberty.
