Charles G. D. Roberts

1860–1943

Sir Charles George Douglas Roberts (1860–1943) was a Canadian poet and prose writer, born in Douglas, New Brunswick. He studied at the University of New Brunswick, taught and edited in Toronto and New York, and became famous for his animal stories as well as his poems.

His volumes of verse include Orion, and Other Poems (1880), Songs of the Common Day (1893) and New York Nocturnes (1898). Anthologists of his own time grouped him with the American poets.
