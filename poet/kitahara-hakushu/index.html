Kitahara Hakushū 北原白秋

1885–1942

北原白秋 (Kitahara Hakushū, 1885–1942), born Kitahara Ryūkichi in Yanagawa, Fukuoka Prefecture, was one of the most widely read Japanese poets of the Meiji, Taishō and Shōwa periods. He wrote tanka and free verse as well as children's songs, and his early books, among them the symbolist collection 邪宗門 (1909), made his name.

Here are two early collections as printed by Aozora Bunko, with the reading aids (ruby) and editorial marks removed: forty verse poems from 思ひ出 (1911), a book of memories of his boyhood in Yanagawa, in book order, with 糸車 (Itoguruma) placed last, and ten tanka from the opening sections of 桐の花 (1913), the first of them the best known, 病める児はハモニカを吹き. The prose pieces of both books, and a few poems whose text includes rare characters the archive cannot yet show, are left out.
