American

Eugene Field

1850–1895

Eugene Field (1850–1895) was an American journalist and poet, born in St. Louis. He wrote a widely read newspaper column, first in Denver and then for the Chicago Daily News, and became known above all for his verses about children.

His best-loved poems, among them “Wynken, Blynken and Nod,” “Little Boy Blue” and “The Sugar-Plum Tree,” were gathered in With Trumpet and Drum (1892) and Love-Songs of Childhood (1894). The poems here follow the combined Poems of Childhood issued by Charles Scribner’s Sons in 1904.
