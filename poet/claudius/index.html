Matthias Claudius

1740–1815

Matthias Claudius was a German poet and journalist, editor of the Wandsbecker Bote, whose plain, devout songs ("Der Mond ist aufgegangen", "Der Tod und das Mädchen") became part of German family life.

The English versions here are George MacDonald's, printed in Rampolli (1897).
