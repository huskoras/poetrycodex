Ancient Greek & Roman

Statius

c. AD 45 – c. 96

Publius Papinius Statius was a Roman poet who flourished in the late first century AD, under the Emperor Domitian.

He wrote a collection of occasional poems, the Silvae, the twelve-book epic Thebaid on the Seven against Thebes, and the unfinished Achilleid on the youth of Achilles; the Silvae are not included here.

Major Works (2)

  2. Thebaid — translated by J. H. Mozley (1928)
