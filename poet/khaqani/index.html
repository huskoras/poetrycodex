Khaqani

1121–1190

Khaqani (خاقانی شروانی, Afzal al-Din Badil ibn Ali Khaqani of Shirvan, 1121–1190; the dates are traditional and the year of his death is disputed) was a Persian court poet of the Shirvanshahs, born in Shirvan in the Caucasus and buried at Tabriz. He is counted among the great masters of the Persian qasida, admired for dense imagery, learned allusion and difficult, brilliant language, and was given the title Hassan al-Ajam.

This selection comes from his Divan as given on Ganjoor: the qasidas that have recitations or linked songs there, headed by the famous elegy on the ruined palace of the Sasanian kings at Mada'in (Ctesiphon), then the ghazals ranked in the same way. Each bayt stands on one line, the two hemistichs separated by a wide space. The Persian text is public domain.
