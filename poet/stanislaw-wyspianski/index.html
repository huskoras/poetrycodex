Stanisław Wyspiański

1869–1907

Stanisław Wyspiański (1869–1907) was a painter, dramatist, poet and designer of Kraków, the central figure of Young Poland (Młoda Polska). He studied in Kraków and Paris, designed the stained glass of the Franciscan church in Kraków and the interiors of theatres, and drew his subjects from Polish history, legend and the Greek tragedians.

His verse drama Wesele (The Wedding, 1901) takes its occasion from the real wedding of the poet Lucjan Rydel and a peasant bride at Bronowice near Kraków, and turns the feast into a vision of the national myth, as ghosts of Polish history appear to the guests. He died in Kraków at thirty-eight.
