Metaphysical & Cavalier

Thomas Jordan

c. 1612–1685

Thomas Jordan (c. 1612–1685) was an English actor, dramatist and poet. He began as a player in London before the theatres were closed in 1642, and published his verse in small, miscellaneous volumes through the Civil War, the Interregnum and the Restoration.

His Divine Raptures (1646) turns everyday sights — a weathercock, a windmill, a ship — into pious meditations; the later volumes gathered here, Divinity and Morality in Robes of Poetry and The Muses Melody, both printed undated, mix moral epigrams, elegies, acrostics and occasional verse with amorous and satirical pieces.
