American

Katharine Lee Bates

1859–1929

Katharine Lee Bates (1859–1929) was born at Falmouth, Massachusetts, graduated from Wellesley College, and became professor of English literature there. She also edited English classics.

Her verse includes The College Beautiful, and Other Poems (1887) and Sunshine, and Other Verses for Children (1890).
