American

Wallace Rice

1859–1939

Wallace Rice (1859–1939) was an American vexillographer.

Poems of American History gathers verse on events from the Norse voyages to the Spanish-American War, arranged by historical chapter. The poems here are “The First American Sailors”, “The Sudbury Fight”, “The Minute-Men of Northboro”, “Firstfruits in 1812” and “Defeat and Victory” among others; Stevenson's editorial headnotes are not reproduced.
