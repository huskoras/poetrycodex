Kazimierz Przerwa-Tetmajer

1865-1940

Kazimierz Przerwa-Tetmajer (1865-1940) was a Polish poet, prose writer and playwright, the best known lyric voice of Young Poland (Młoda Polska), the Polish modernist movement. Born at Ludźmierz in the Podhale region, at the foot of the Tatra mountains, he studied in Kraków and travelled in Italy, and he drew on the mountains and on Mediterranean antiquity alike.

His first series of Poezje appeared in 1891, and the collected selection Wybór poezji followed in 1897, containing many of his best-known lyrics of love, nature, weariness and doubt, among them "Koniec wieku XIX" and "Hymn do Nirwany". He published further series and later editions until the 1920s and died in Warsaw in 1940. The texts here come from the Wolne Lektury digital library, which names the printed edition behind each one; the 1897 Wybór poezji is the main source.
