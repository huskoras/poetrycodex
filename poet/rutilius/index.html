Rutilius Claudius Namatianus

fl. 416 AD

Rutilius Claudius Namatianus was a Gallic Roman aristocrat from the region of Toulouse. He served at Rome as master of the offices and then as prefect of the city in 414, when he was a pagan of the senatorial class writing under a Christian empire.

In 417 he left Rome to see to his estates in Gaul, which had been ravaged by the Goths, and wrote a poem in elegiac couplets, De reditu suo, about the sea voyage along the Tuscan coast. It opens with a famous hymn to Rome; the text breaks off in the second book.

Major Works (2)

  1. De reditu suo — Elegiac poem, c. 416
  2. The Home-Coming — translated by G. F. Savage-Armstrong (1907)
