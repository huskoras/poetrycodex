Ancient Greek & Roman

Moschus

fl. c. 150 BC

Moschus was a Greek bucolic poet who flourished in Syracuse in the second century BC.

Only a small collection of his works survives, the largest being the Europa; The Lament for Bion was formerly attributed to him but is now assigned to an unknown pupil of Bion.
