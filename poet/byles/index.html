Mather Byles

1707–1788

Mather Byles (1707–1788) was a Congregational minister in Boston and the best-known American poet of his generation. A nephew of Cotton Mather, he corresponded with Alexander Pope and others in England, and his Poems on Several Occasions was published in Boston in 1744.

Celebrated in Boston for his wit and his punning, he served the Hollis Street Church for decades. He remained loyal to the Crown during the Revolution and was dismissed from his pulpit in 1776.
