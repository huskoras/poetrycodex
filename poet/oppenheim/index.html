American

James Oppenheim

1882–1932

James Oppenheim (1882–1932) was an American poet and editor, born in St. Paul, Minnesota, who founded the magazine The Seven Arts in 1916.

His verse includes Songs for the New Age (1914), and his poem “Bread and Roses” became a slogan of the labour movement.
