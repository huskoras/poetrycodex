Salman of Sawa (Salman Savaji)

1300–1376

Salman of Sawa (Salman Savaji, c. 1300–1376) was a Persian poet born in Sawa, in what is now Iran, who served as court poet to the Jalayirid rulers Shaykh Hasan-i Buzurg (Amir Shaykh Hasan Ilkhani) and his son Sultan Uways, at Baghdad and Tabriz, and later their successors. He was admired in his own time as a master of the qasida and the ghazal.

His Divan holds ghazals, qasidas and other forms; he also wrote the romance Jamshid u Khurshid. The ghazals are polished, courtly lyrics in the manner of Sa'di and Khwaju, and later critics often place him among the poets who prepared the way for Hafiz. This selection gives the first fifty ghazals of the Divan in the order of the Ganjoor edition.
