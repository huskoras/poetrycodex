Lady John Scott

1810–1900

Alicia Ann Spottiswoode (1810–1900), who married Lord John Scott in 1836 and is known as Lady John Scott, was a Scottish songwriter and composer, born in Berwickshire. She is remembered for her version of 'Annie Laurie'.

Lyra Celtica prints her song 'Durisdeer'.
