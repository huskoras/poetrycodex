Micah Joseph Lebensohn

1828–1852

Micah Joseph Lebensohn (מיכה יוסף לבנזון, 1828–1852), known by the initials Mikhal, was born in Vilna, the son of the Hebrew poet Adam ha-Kohen Lebensohn. A leading younger voice of the Haskalah (the Hebrew Enlightenment), he wrote in a biblical Hebrew of unusual polish and died of consumption in Vilna at the age of twenty-four.

His one book published in his lifetime, Shirei Bat Tziyon (Songs of the Daughter of Zion, 1851), took subjects from the Bible (Solomon, Jael and Sisera, Moses on Mount Nebo) and also reworked European poems; a posthumous collection, Kinnor Bat Tziyon, followed in 1870. The poems here are in Hebrew with their vowel points, taken from the public-domain dump of Project Ben-Yehuda (benyehuda.org); the volunteers' notes, dedications and footnotes have been left out.
