Yitzhak Katzenelson

1886–1944

Yitzhak Katzenelson (יצחק קצנלסון, 1886–1944) was born near Minsk and lived most of his life in Łódź, where he taught Hebrew, wrote verse and plays in Hebrew and Yiddish, and published his early collections with the Hovevei Shira press.

He moved to Warsaw in 1939 and was deported in 1943 to the Vittel camp, then to Auschwitz, where he was killed in 1944. His Yiddish elegy of the Holocaust, written in Vittel, is his best-known work; the poems here are his early Hebrew lyrics.
