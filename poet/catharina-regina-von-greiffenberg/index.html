Catharina Regina von Greiffenberg

1633–1694

Catharina Regina von Greiffenberg (1633–1694) was an Austrian Protestant poet of the German Baroque. Born at Schloss Seisenegg in Lower Austria, she grew up in a Lutheran noble family at a time when Protestants in the Habsburg lands were under growing pressure, and later made her home in Nuremberg, where she died in 1694.

Her Geistliche Sonnette, Lieder und Gedichte appeared in Nuremberg in 1662 and brought her into the circle of the Nuremberg poets around Sigmund von Birken. The volume joins devotional songs to the sonnet cycle Kunst-Klang in dritthalb-hundert Sonneten oder Klinggedichten, in which she meditates on the Passion, the Holy Spirit, the Word of God and the seasons as signs of divine providence.

The poems here are taken from the 1662 edition as digitised in the TextGrid Digitale Bibliothek, with the original spelling kept.
