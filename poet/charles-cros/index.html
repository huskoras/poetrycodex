French

Charles Cros

1842–1888

Charles Cros (1842–1888), born at Fabrezan in the Aude, was a French poet and inventor. He moved in the literary circles of Paris in the 1870s, among the Parnassian poets and the cabarets of the Latin Quarter, and was a friend of Verlaine and Rimbaud.

In 1877 he described a method of recording and reproducing sound, a few months before Edison's phonograph, and he also worked on colour photography. His one collection of verse in his lifetime, Le Coffret de santal (first published 1873, enlarged 1879), joins love poems and sonnets to the comic, whimsical pieces such as Le Hareng saur.
