Yan Yanzhi

384–456

Yan Yanzhi (顏延之, courtesy name Yannian, AD 384–456) was a court official and poet of the Liu Song dynasty, born in Linyi in present-day Shandong. He served the Liu Song emperors in a series of posts, was a friend of the poet Tao Qian, and wrote the memorial essay for him.

He was ranked with Xie Lingyun as one of the two leading poets of his day, a pairing known as "Yan-Xie", and his dense, allusive, formal verse was set against Xie's landscape poetry. The poems here are given in the original Chinese.
