Bernardim Ribeiro

1482-1552

Bernardim Ribeiro (1482-1552) was a Portuguese writer of the court of Manuel I, born in the Alentejo and educated at Lisbon. He is the author of the prose romance Menina e Moça, whose melancholy sentiment of saudade made it one of the most distinctive books of the Portuguese Renaissance.

He also wrote five eclogues and a number of romances, which brought the bucolic mode into Portuguese verse together with the work of Sá de Miranda. Little is securely known of his life, and later tradition says that his mind was clouded by an unhappy love. His verse was printed in the sixteenth century after his death.
