Theophanes

date uncertain (Paton)

Theophanes is one of the Byzantine epigrammatists of the Greek Anthology. Paton’s Index of Authors gives his date as uncertain.

This archive carries the 2 epigrams ascribed to the poet in the fifth volume of W. R. Paton's prose translation (Loeb, 1918): Book XV: 14, 35.
