Kazim Khan Shaida

b. c. 1722–1727 (Raverty)

Kazim Khan, surnamed Shaida, was a great-grandson of Khushhal Khan, born in the five years after A.H. 1135 (A.D. 1722). Quarrelling with his elder brother Asad-ullah, who held the chieftainship, he left home as a young man, wandered in Kashmir, and settled at Rampur.

A disappointment in love led him to remain unmarried. His poems were gathered alphabetically into a volume in A.D. 1767, and Raverty describes the surviving copy as the only one; they are largely love ghazals in the Persian manner.
