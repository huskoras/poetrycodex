William Kenrick

1725?–1779

William Kenrick (1725?–1779) was an English novelist, playwright, translator and satirist who spent much of his career lampooning his fellow writers. He translated Rousseau's Emilius into English and wrote for the stage, and he later edited the London Review.

This selection is drawn from his verse book Poems; Ludicrous, Satirical, and Moral (1768). Alongside long philosophical epistles in couplets it includes fables, topical satires on the London theatres and their rivals, and prologues written for Drury Lane.
