Gil Vicente

c. 1465–c. 1536

Gil Vicente (c. 1465 – c. 1536) is counted the first great dramatist of Portugal. He was a court poet and playwright under King Manuel I and King João III, and the plays he staged for the royal family, the autos, farces and comedies, were written in Portuguese and in Castilian, sometimes in both within a single piece.

Woven through those plays are songs of a different kind: serranilhas, cantigas, vilancetes and ballads, which draw on the folk and courtly lyric of the Iberian peninsula and rank among its finest short poems. Many are sung by shepherds, sailors, angels and lovers, and stand free of the plots that hold them.

The selection given here is the one made by Aubrey F. G. Bell, Lyrics of Gil Vicente (Oxford: B. H. Blackwell, 1914): fifty-four songs, each set beside Bell's English version. The play each song comes from is named in its note.
