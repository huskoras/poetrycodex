José Joaquín de Mora

1783–1864

José Joaquín de Mora (Cádiz, 1783 – Madrid, 1864) was a Spanish poet, journalist, translator and critic. A liberal, he edited the Crónica científica y literaria in Madrid and, after the restoration of absolutism in 1823, went into exile in London.

He later lived in Argentina, Chile, Peru and Bolivia, working as a teacher and newspaper writer, and returned to Spain in the 1840s. His sonnet 'El Estío' appears in Menéndez y Pelayo's anthology of the hundred best lyric poems in Castilian.
