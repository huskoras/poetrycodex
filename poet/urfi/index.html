'Urfi of Shiraz

1556–1591

'Urfi of Shiraz (عرفی شیرازی, Jamal al-Din Muhammad, 1556-1591) was born in Shiraz and trained there in letters, calligraphy and music. While still young he sailed to India, where he joined the circle of Faizi and of Abu'l-Fath Gilani, was introduced to the Khan-i Khanan, 'Abd al-Rahim, and entered the service of the Emperor Akbar's court. He died at Lahore in 1591, aged about thirty-six.

One of the most admired poets of the Indian style (sabk-i hindi), 'Urfi is known for the daring conceits and proud, restless voice of his ghazals and for the weighty panegyric and devotional qasidas of his Divan. This selection takes thirty-five ghazals and ten qasidas, ranked by the attention readers have given them on Ganjoor, each given whole with one bayt to a line, the two hemistichs separated by a wide space. The Persian text is public domain.
