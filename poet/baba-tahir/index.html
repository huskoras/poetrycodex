Baba Tahir

c. 1000–after 1055

Baba Tahir (باباطاهر عریان, Baba Tahir ‘Uryan, ‘the Naked’) was a Persian mystic poet of the 11th century (fifth century AH), a contemporary of the Seljuk sultan Tughril. Little is firmly known of his life; later tradition makes him a wandering dervish from Hamadan, and he is dated here as Ganjoor gives him, c. 1000 to after 1055.

He is remembered for his do-beytis, short two-line poems in the dialect and manner of western Iran, in which longing for the beloved and for God, poverty, and the sorrows of exile are voiced with great plainness. They have long been sung and recited by Persian speakers. The poems here are taken from Ganjoor’s edition of the do-beytis, in Ganjoor order, without the sections marked as attributed.
