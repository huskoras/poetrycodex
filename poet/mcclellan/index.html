American

George Marion McClellan

1860–1934

George Marion McClellan (1860–1934) was a Black poet, minister and teacher from Tennessee, a graduate of Fisk University. His verse sets the landscapes of the South beside religious feeling and, at moments, the claims of his race.

He published Poems (1895) and The Path of Dreams (1916); both books were drawn on for James Weldon Johnson's anthology The Book of American Negro Poetry (1922). The Path of Dreams, printed in Louisville, gathers his nature lyrics, devotional pieces and a long narrative poem on the legend of Tannhauser.
