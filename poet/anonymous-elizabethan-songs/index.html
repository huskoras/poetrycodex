Anonymous (Elizabethan songs)

c. 1588–1630

Between 1588 and about 1630 English composers printed hundreds of songs for voices and lute: madrigals, ballets, airs and rounds by Byrd, Dowland, Morley, Wilbye, Jones, Ford, Ravenscroft and others. The words were usually printed without a poet's name, and the composer named on the title-page is not the author of the lyric.

This file gathers the lyrics of A. H. Bullen's Lyrics from the Song-Books of the Elizabethan Age (1887; second edition 1891), each with its song-book in the note, together with the few songs in his Lyrics from the Dramatists of the Elizabethan Age (1889) that belong to anonymous plays and masques. Lyrics that Bullen firmly gives to a named poet are not included here, and Thomas Campion's airs are on his own page.
