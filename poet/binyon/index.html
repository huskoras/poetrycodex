Laurence Binyon

1869–1943

Laurence Binyon (1869–1943) was an English poet, playwright and art historian, born in Lancaster and educated at St Paul’s School and Oxford. He worked at the British Museum, where he became Keeper of Prints and Drawings and a leading authority on Asian art.

He is best known for “For the Fallen,” published in The Times in September 1914, whose fourth stanza is read at Remembrance services. He also wrote odes, narrative poems and a verse translation of Dante’s Divine Comedy.
