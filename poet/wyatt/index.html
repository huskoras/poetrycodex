Tudor & Elizabethan

Sir Thomas Wyatt

1503–1542

Sir Thomas Wyatt was a Kentish courtier and diplomat under Henry VIII, credited with introducing the sonnet form into English poetry after his travels in Italy.

His lyrics of love and court intrigue circulated in manuscript during his lifetime and were first printed posthumously in Tottel’s Miscellany (1557).
