Micias

date uncertain

Micias is known only as the name under which a single epigram, on a wayside fountain, is preserved in the Greek Anthology. Nothing secure is recorded of his life, and his date is uncertain.

This archive carries one poem ascribed to him, in Sir Rennell Rodd's 1916 rendering from the Greek Anthology.
