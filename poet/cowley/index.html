Metaphysical & Cavalier

Abraham Cowley

1618–1667

Abraham Cowley (1618–1667) was among the most celebrated English poets of his century, a child prodigy admired for his metaphysical wit.

His 1656 <em>Poems</em> gathers odes, elegies, and the love sequence <em>The Mistress</em>.
