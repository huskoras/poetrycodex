Tudor & Elizabethan

Thomas Watson

c. 1555–1592

Thomas Watson was an Elizabethan poet and translator, a friend of Christopher Marlowe, who published the sonnet sequence Hekatompathia (1582) and the madrigals of The First Set of Italian Madrigals Englished (1590). Gilfillan gives his dates as 1560 to about 1592.

Gilfillan prints a short song, The Nymphs to Their May-Queen, and a sonnet.

Poems (2)

  1. The Nymphs to their May-Queen
  2. Sonnet: Actaeon lost, in middle of his sport
