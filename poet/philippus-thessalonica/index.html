Ancient Greek & Roman

Philippus of Thessalonica

fl. 1st century AD

Philippus of Thessalonica compiled the Garland that continued Meleager's, collecting the epigrams of poets of the Augustan age and after, and wrote epigrams of his own. Paton's index puts him in the second century AD; the editor follows the more usual first-century date.

This archive files under his name every epigram that Paton heads “Philippus”, including Book IV, 2, his proem to the Garland.
