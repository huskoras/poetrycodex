Romantic

William Blake

1757–1827

William Blake (1757–1827) was an English poet, painter, and printmaker, largely unrecognised in his lifetime and now seen as a seminal figure of the Romantic age.

He engraved and hand-coloured his own illuminated books, most famously Songs of Innocence and of Experience, where word and image are inseparable.
