Indian

Vemana

dates disputed (placed between the 14th and 17th centuries)

Vemana was a Telugu moralist and mystic poet whose short verses, ending in the signature ‘Vema’, are still widely quoted and revered in the folk tradition of Andhra. His dates are disputed, and scholars have placed him anywhere between the 14th and 17th centuries.

His verses teach plain living and yogic self-knowledge, and often mock caste pride, ritual and the claims of priests. C. P. Brown’s prose version, published at Madras in 1829, was the first printed translation of a Telugu classic into English.
