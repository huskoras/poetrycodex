Khwajah Muhammad Bangash

fl. late 17th century (Raverty)

Khwajah Muhammad belonged to the Bangash tribe of Afghans around Kohat and lived in the reign of Aurangzeb. Raverty says materials for his life are meagre: he lived as a poor darwesh of the Chishti order, made the pilgrimage to Mecca and Medina, and gave up poetry after his return.

His Diwan survives in a single copy known to Raverty. The poems are deeply tinged with Sufi mysticism, but some lament the loss of friends and the pain of separation.
