German

Stefan George

1868–1933

Stefan George (1868–1933) was a German poet, born in Büdesheim on the Rhine. After studies and travels in Paris, where he met Mallarmé and the Symbolists, he returned to Germany and founded the journal Blätter für die Kunst (1892), the centre of a circle of poets and scholars, the George-Kreis. He translated Baudelaire, Shakespeare and Dante into German.

Der siebente Ring (1907), his seventh book, gathers the Zeitgedichte, Gestalten, Gezeiten, Maximin, Traumdunkel, Lieder and Tafeln. Its centre is the cycle for Maximin, a boy George revered after his early death in 1904. George wrote in a strict, austere style, set the poems in his own lower-case spelling and punctuation, and left Germany for Switzerland in 1933, where he died. Here the first sixty poems of the book, in its order, are given as printed in 1907.
