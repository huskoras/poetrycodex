Metaphysical & Cavalier

John Fletcher

1576–1625

John Fletcher was an English dramatist who collaborated with Francis Beaumont and later with Shakespeare. Songs scattered through his plays are admired for their lyric grace.

Palgrave includes his song "Melancholy".
