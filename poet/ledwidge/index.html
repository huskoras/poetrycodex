Francis Ledwidge

1887–1917

Francis Ledwidge (1887–1917) was an Irish poet, the son of a farm labourer from Slane, County Meath, who worked as a farmhand, miner and road worker. Lord Dunsany read his early verse, encouraged him and arranged the publication of Songs of the Fields (1915).

He enlisted in the Royal Inniskilling Fusiliers, served at Gallipoli, in Serbia and in France, and wrote poems of the Irish countryside and of the war, including an elegy for Thomas MacDonagh. He was killed at Boezinge, near Ypres, on 31 July 1917.
