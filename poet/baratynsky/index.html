Russian & Slavic

Evgeny Baratynsky

1800–1844

Евгений Абрамович Баратынский (1800–1844), who signed his name Боратынский, was a Russian lyric poet, born at the family estate of Mara in Tambov province. Expelled from the Corps of Pages as a boy, he served in the ranks in Finland, where he wrote his early elegies, and he was a friend of Pushkin and Delvig. He later lived mostly in Moscow and on his estates, and he died in Naples in 1844.

This selection follows the three collections printed in his lifetime as they are grouped on Russian Wikisource: the Стихотворения of 1827, the Стихотворения of 1835 and Сумерки (1842). The cap reached here covers the first of them, the elegies, the miscellany and the epistles of 1827; a poem that appeared in both the 1827 and the 1835 volumes is given once, in its 1835 text. Where a pre-reform printing is held on Wikisource its spelling is kept; the remaining texts are in modern spelling.
