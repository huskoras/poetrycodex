Gustaf Fröding

1860–1911

Gustaf Fröding (1860–1911) was a Swedish poet born at Alster near Karlstad in Värmland. He studied at Uppsala, worked as a journalist in Karlstad, and published his first mature book, Guitar and Concertina (Gitarr och dragharmonika), in 1891. It joined dialect-flavoured Värmland ballads with melancholy lyrics and satire, and made him at once one of the leading voices of Swedish poetry in the 1890s.

He followed it with Nya dikter (1894), Stänk och flikar (1896) and Gralstänk (1898), moving between folk humour, biblical fantasy and metaphysical questioning. He suffered from severe mental illness and spent long periods in asylums; after 1898 he wrote little, and he died in Stockholm in 1911. This selection takes the whole of Gitarr och dragharmonika (1891) and the opening poems of Nya dikter (1894), in the order he published them, keeping the Värmland dialect spelling of the printed text.
