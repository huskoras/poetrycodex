Robert Emery

1794–1871

Robert Emery is represented here by verse printed in The Newcastle Song Book; or, Tyne-side Songster (Newcastle: W. & T. Fordyce, 1842), 19 pieces.

A writer of Tyneside songs in the local dialect; the spelling of the printed texts is kept as the books give it.
