Miyazawa Kenji

1896–1933

宮沢賢治 (Miyazawa Kenji, 1896–1933) was born in Hanamaki, in Iwate Prefecture in northern Japan, and died there of illness at the age of thirty-seven. He studied agriculture and forestry, taught at an agricultural school, and later worked to help local farmers, while writing poems and children's stories; he was a devout follower of the Nichiren school of Buddhism.

Here are the poems of 春と修羅 (Spring and Asura), the only book of poems he published in his lifetime, brought out in 1924 (the text is that of the facsimile of the first edition, without its prose preface), together with the notebook poem 〔雨ニモマケズ〕 (Ame ni mo makezu), found after his death. The text is that of Aozora Bunko, with the reading aids (ruby) removed.
