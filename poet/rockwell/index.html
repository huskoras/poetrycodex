American

J. O. Rockwell

1807–1831

James Otis Rockwell (1807–1831) was a Connecticut-born journalist and poet, apprenticed as a printer in Utica, who died young.

Griswold included 7 of his poems in The Poets and Poetry of America (16th edition, Philadelphia: Parry & McMillan, 1855), the anthology from which the poems here are taken.
