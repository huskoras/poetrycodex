Tudor & Elizabethan

Robert Southwell

1561–1595

Robert Southwell was an English Jesuit priest and poet who was arrested, tortured and executed for treason under Elizabeth I because of his underground Catholic ministry.

His devotional verse, most famously ‘The Burning Babe,’ was admired even by contemporaries hostile to his faith.
