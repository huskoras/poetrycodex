Edward Lovibond

1724–1775

Edward Lovibond was a gentleman-poet born at Hampton in Middlesex who contributed verse to the periodical The World and died in 1775.

Gilfillan prints his poem The Tears of Old May-Day, written on the change of the calendar in 1752.
