Ancient Near East

Anonymous Babylonian Poets

c. 1800–1000 BC

The Babylonian poets are unnamed scribes and singers who composed in Akkadian and wrote in cuneiform on clay tablets. Their works, among them the Epic of Gilgamish, survive in Old Babylonian, Hittite and Assyrian copies, the fullest being the seventh-century BC tablets from the royal library at Nineveh.

The Epic of Gilgamish is given here in R. Campbell Thompson's verse translation of 1928.
