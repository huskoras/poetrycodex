Francisco de Sá de Miranda

1481–1558

Francisco de Sá de Miranda was a Portuguese poet, born at Coimbra in 1481. He began as a court poet in the older manner of the Cancioneiro Geral, in which several of his short pieces appeared in 1516, and wrote in both Portuguese and Castilian. After a stay in Italy in the 1520s he came home with the new Italian forms.

He is remembered for bringing the sonnet, the terza rima and the verse epistle into Portuguese, and with them a more reflective, moral tone, and he withdrew from court to the north of Portugal, where he lived on his estate until his death in 1558. This selection gives three sonnets from the 1885 edition prepared by Carolina Michaëlis, two devotional pieces from the Parnaso mariano, and seven short court poems in their 1516 spelling.
