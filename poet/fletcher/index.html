American

John Gould Fletcher

1886–1950

John Gould Fletcher (1886–1950) was an American poet, born in Little Rock, Arkansas, who lived in London from 1909 to 1933.

A leading Imagist, he later won the Pulitzer Prize for his Selected Poems (1939).
