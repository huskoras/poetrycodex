Victorian

Francis Davis

1810–1885

Francis Davis (1810–1885), "The Belfast Man", was a weaver and poet born at Ballincollig, County Cork. He wrote his verses at the loom, contributed to The Nation, and published three volumes of verse.

The poems here are taken from H. Halliday Sparling's anthology Irish Minstrelsy (London: Walter Scott, 1888).
