American

Thomas Bailey Aldrich

1836–1907

Thomas Bailey Aldrich (1836–1907) was an American poet, novelist and editor, born in Portsmouth, New Hampshire. He edited The Atlantic Monthly from 1881 to 1890 and wrote the boyhood memoir The Story of a Bad Boy (1869).

In verse he was admired for small, polished lyrics and epigrams, which he refined steadily over his career.
