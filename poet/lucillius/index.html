Ancient Greek & Roman

Lucillius

1st century AD

Lucillius, a Greek epigrammatist of the first century AD, is one of the Anthology's satirical writers; not to be confused with the Roman satirist Lucilius.

Paton's first volume ascribes one epigram to him without qualification, a dedication in Book VI.
