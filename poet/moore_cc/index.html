American

Clement C. Moore

1779–1863

Clement C. Moore (1779–1863) was an American scholar of Hebrew and Greek, a professor at the General Theological Seminary in New York, and the author of A Visit from St. Nicholas.

Griswold included 2 of his poems in The Poets and Poetry of America (16th edition, Philadelphia: Parry & McMillan, 1855), the anthology from which the poems here are taken.
