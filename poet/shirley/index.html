Metaphysical & Cavalier

James Shirley

1596–1666

James Shirley was an English dramatist of the reign of Charles I, one of the last playwrights of the Jacobean and Caroline stage.

Palgrave includes two of his lyrics, "The Last Conqueror" and "Death the Leveller".
