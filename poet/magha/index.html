Indian

Magha

7th–8th century AD

Magha was a Sanskrit court poet of the seventh or eighth century AD, traditionally associated with Bhinmal (Shrimala) in Rajasthan. Little is known of his life beyond what his own poem says: its closing verses name his grandfather Suprabhadeva as a minister of King Varmalata.

His one surviving work is the Shishupalavadha, 'The Slaying of Shishupala', a mahakavya in twenty cantos that retells an episode of the Mahabharata, Krishna's killing of the king of Chedi, with lavish ornament and a famed command of metre and word-play. The Indian tradition ranks it among the great Sanskrit court epics. Here appears its first canto, in which the sage Narada brings Krishna the gods' summons.
