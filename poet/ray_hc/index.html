Henrietta Cordelia Ray

1852?–1916

Henrietta Cordelia Ray (1852?–1916) was an African American poet and teacher in New York City, the daughter of the abolitionist minister and newspaper editor Charles B. Ray and Charlotte Augusta Burroughs Ray. She taught in the city's public schools for many years and kept her home with her sister Florence.

Her ode on Abraham Lincoln was read at the unveiling of the Freedmen's Memorial in Washington in 1876, and she published Sonnets in 1893. Poems (1910), her collected volume, gathers her lyrics, ballads, sonnets and occasional verse on Nature, art, music, faith and the champions of freedom.
