Farrokhi Sistani

d. 1037/8

Farrokhi Sistani (فرخی سیستانی; Abu'l-Hasan Ali ibn Julugh, died 1037/8) came from Sistan, in eastern Iran, where he began as a poet in the service of local landowners and, by tradition, was poor and little noticed. He is counted among the leading court poets of the early Ghaznavid age, and is remembered for the lightness and clarity of his style.

His fortunes changed when he went to the court of the Chaghaniyan amirs, whose qasidas he wrote in praise of Abu'l-Muzaffar Ahmad of Chaghaniyan; these poems won him a place with Sultan Mahmud of Ghazna, whom he served and celebrated until the sultan's death. This selection gives two of his Chaghaniyan qasidas from the Divan as Ganjoor prints it: the great opening journey-poem (no. 171) and the long ode on the branding ground (no. 87).
