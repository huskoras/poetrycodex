Salvador Díaz Mirón

1853–1928

Salvador Díaz Mirón (1853–1928) was a Mexican poet born in the port of Veracruz. He began as a passionate Romantic, under the influence of Byron and Victor Hugo, and was also a journalist, teacher and deputy; a period in the Veracruz prison in the 1890s left its mark on several of his poems.

His proud, austere and sculpted later manner, collected in Lascas (1901), made him a precursor of modernismo in Spanish America. "A Gloria", with its famous opening "¡No intentes convencerme de torpeza", remains his best-known poem.
