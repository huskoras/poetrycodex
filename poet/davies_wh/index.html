Modern

W. H. Davies

1871–1940

William Henry Davies (1871–1940) was a Welsh poet and writer, born in Newport, Monmouthshire. He spent years as a tramp in America and Britain, losing a foot while trying to jump a freight train, and wrote of that life in The Autobiography of a Super-Tramp (1908).

His short, simple lyrics about birds, flowers, tramps and the poor made him one of the Georgian poets. “Leisure,” first published in Songs of Joy (1911), is his best-known poem; the other poems here are from Foliage (1913).
