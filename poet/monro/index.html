Modern

Harold Monro

1879–1932

Harold Monro (1879–1932) was a British poet and publisher, born in Brussels, who founded the Poetry Bookshop in London in 1913 and published the Georgian Poetry anthologies and many young poets there.

His own books include Strange Meetings (1917), from which the title piece here is drawn. The poems come from Untermeyer's Modern British Poetry (1920).
