Évariste de Parny

1753–1814

Évariste de Parny (1753–1814) was born on the island of Bourbon (now Réunion) in the Indian Ocean and educated in France. He served as an army officer and spent several years on Bourbon in the 1770s, where he wrote the love poems that made his name.

His Poésies érotiques first appeared in 1778 and were reprinted and enlarged many times; their light, sensuous, elegiac manner, addressed to a beloved called Éléonore, made him the most admired French love poet of his generation and a model for the Romantics who followed. He also wrote the Chansons madécasses (1787) and a mock-epic, La Guerre des dieux (1799), and was elected to the Académie française in 1803. This selection gives the pieces of the 1778 first edition in the order of the book.
