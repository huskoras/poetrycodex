Metaphysical & Cavalier

Richard Crashaw

1613–1649

Richard Crashaw (1613–1649) was an English metaphysical poet known for the ecstatic, ornate devotion of his sacred verse.

A convert to Catholicism, he collected his poems as Steps to the Temple (1646), consciously following George Herbert.
