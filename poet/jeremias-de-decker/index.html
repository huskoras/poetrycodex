Jeremias de Decker

1609–1666

Jeremias de Decker (1609–1666) was a Dutch poet of the Golden Age, born in Dordrecht and later resident in Amsterdam, where he died. He is remembered for his elegies, birthday and wedding songs, and for longer poems such as the satirical Lof der Geldzucht (Praise of Avarice) and the devotional Goede Vrydag (Good Friday).

The English renderings here come from John Bowring and Harry S. van Dyk's Batavian Anthology (1824), which presents him as a poet of intense, simple feeling. Rembrandt painted his portrait in the year of his death. The Dutch lines that Bowring prints above each translation are omitted.
