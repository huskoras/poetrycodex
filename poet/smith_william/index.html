Tudor & Elizabethan

William Smith

fl. 1596

William Smith (fl. 1596) was an Elizabethan poet of whom nothing certain is known beyond his one surviving work.

That work, Chloris, or the Complaint of the Passionate Despised Shepherd (1596), is a pastoral sonnet cycle in the Shakespearean rhyme scheme, opening with sonnets of homage to Spenser as the shepherd Colin Clout.
