Anonymous Korean Sijo

Joseon period

The anonymous sijo, the short three-line songs of Joseon Korea, are preserved in Kim Cheontaek's anthology 청구영언 (靑丘永言, Cheonggu yeongeon), compiled in 1728. They are set down without an author's name under the heading 無名氏 (the unnamed).

This selection is the anonymous section of the second mode, 이삭대엽 (二數大葉), arranged by theme: longing for the sovereign, exile, the life of retirement among rivers and mountains, farm life, drink, old age and the passing of time. The text is Middle Korean, mixing hangul (with archaic letters) and hanja.
