Igor Severyanin (Игорь Северянин)

1887–1941

Igor Severyanin (Игорь Северянин, born Igor Vasilyevich Lotaryov, 1887–1941) was a Russian poet of the Silver Age who gave himself the pen-name "Northerner" and founded Ego-Futurism, a short-lived Petersburg movement of 1911–12. His slim private pamphlets of 1904–1912 were followed by The Thunder-Seething Cup (Громокипящий кубок, 1913), which carried a preface by Fyodor Sologub, sold through many editions within a few years, and made him one of the best-known performing poets in Russia.

His verse is marked by a taste for champagne, motor-cars, aeroplanes and boudoirs, by musical forms (the rondel, the sestina, the nocturne, the "poeza") and by a stream of invented words, such as «грёзофарс» and «крылолёт», that he coined himself; the opening of Pineapples in Champagne (Ананасы в шампанском, 1915) became his signature. After the Revolution he settled in Estonia, where he lived until his death in Tallinn in 1941, publishing further collections and writing, in 1925, the poem «Классические розы» (Classical Roses). Spellings and coinages are given here as the sources print them.
