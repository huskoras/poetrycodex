Victorian

Edward Bulwer Lytton

1803–1873

Edward Bulwer-Lytton (1803–1873), created Baron Lytton in 1866, was an English novelist, dramatist, politician and poet. He is remembered chiefly for his novels, but he wrote verse throughout his career and gathered it, revised, in several editions of his Poetical Works.

The poems here are taken from the Routledge edition of 1860: the satire The New Timon, the narrative poems Milton, Constance and The Fairy Bride, the twelve-book romance King Arthur, the “Narrative Lyrics” and the short lyrics of Corn-Flowers and Earlier Poems. His plays, prose notes and pieces headed as translations or imitations are not included.
