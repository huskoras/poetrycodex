American

Micah P. Flint

c. 1807–1830

Micah P. Flint (c. 1807–1830) was a poet and lawyer, born in Lunenburg, Massachusetts, the son of the writer Timothy Flint, who grew up in the Mississippi valley and died young.

Griswold included 2 of his poems in The Poets and Poetry of America (16th edition, Philadelphia: Parry & McMillan, 1855), the anthology from which the poems here are taken.
