Restoration & Augustan

John Wilmot, Earl of Rochester

1647–1680

John Wilmot, second Earl of Rochester (1647–1680), was an English courtier and poet at the court of Charles II, and the most celebrated of the Restoration 'Court Wits'. Famed for his libertine life, he wrote satires, songs, lyrics and verse epistles, and died at thirty-three.

Texts and attributions follow The Works of the Right Honourable John, Earl of Rochester (London, 1718); some pieces in that collection are of disputed authorship.
