Ka Chia-yün

8th century AD

Ka Chia-yün is the spelling Giles uses for this poet of the 8th century AD. His poem here is a dawn poem of longing.

The poems here are Herbert A. Giles’s versions in Chinese Poetry in English Verse (1898), given with the poet’s name as spelled in that book.
