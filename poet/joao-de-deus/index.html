João de Deus

1830–1896

João de Deus Nogueira Ramos (1830–1896) was born at São Bartolomeu de Messines, in the Algarve, and studied law at the University of Coimbra, where he belonged to the circle of young Romantic poets and became known for his lyrics rather than for any legal career. He later lived in Lisbon, worked in journalism and published a primer for teaching reading, the Cartilha Maternal, which made him a national figure in education.

Flores do Campo (1868; second edition, Porto, 1876) gathered his best-loved poems: love lyrics of great simplicity, verses to friends and to the dead, religious pieces and the long, playful narrative poems that drew on popular speech. The texts here follow the lifetime second edition of 1876 in its original spelling. He died in Lisbon in 1896.
