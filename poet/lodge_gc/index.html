George Cabot Lodge

1873–1909

George Cabot Lodge (1873–1909) was an American poet, born in Boston. He studied at Harvard and at the University of Paris, and belonged at Harvard to a circle that included Trumbull Stickney and William Vaughn Moody.

His lyric and dramatic poems, in a classical manner, were collected in Poems and Dramas (1911), after his death in 1909.
