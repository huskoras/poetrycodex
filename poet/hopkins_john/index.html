Restoration & Augustan

John Hopkins

fl. 1700

John Hopkins (fl. 1700) was an English poet of the turn of the eighteenth century, remembered for a single large work, Amasia, or, The Works of the Muses, published in London in 1700 in three volumes. Little is recorded of his life, and the catalogue gives only a floruit.

Amasia gathers love poems and verse epistles addressed to a lady under that name, together with occasional pieces, Ovidian tales in the manner of the day, and poems to friends. He should not be confused with John Hopkins, the sixteenth-century psalmist, or with Gerard Manley Hopkins.
