James Gates Percival

1795–1856

James Gates Percival (1795–1856) was a Connecticut poet, physician and geologist, born in Berlin, Connecticut, and a graduate of Yale.

Griswold included 23 of his poems in The Poets and Poetry of America (16th edition, Philadelphia: Parry & McMillan, 1855), the anthology from which the poems here are taken.
