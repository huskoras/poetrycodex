Celtic

Conor O'Riordan

c. 1750

Conor O'Riordan is dated in Eleanor Hull's The Poem-Book of the Gael to about 1750, and is given there as the author of a hymn to the Virgin Mary.

The English version gathered here is the one printed in Hull's anthology (London, 1913), where no other translator is named.
