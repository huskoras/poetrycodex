P'an Yüeh

247–300

P'an Yüeh (潘岳, courtesy name An-jen 安仁, 247–300), often called P'an An, was a poet and official of the Western Chin (Jin) dynasty, born in Chung-mou in Honan. He served as a clerk under high ministers and as magistrate of Ho-yang and Huai county, and he was put to death with his family in the disorders of the War of the Eight Princes.

He is best remembered for the three 'Poems Mourning the Dead' (悼亡詩) for his wife, which made the title a standing term for elegies on a spouse, and for the autumn and retirement fu included in the Wenxuan anthology. The poems here are in the original Classical Chinese.
