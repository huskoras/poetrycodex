Antero de Quental

1842–1891

Antero Tarquínio de Quental (1842–1891) was born in Ponta Delgada, in the Azores, and studied law at the University of Coimbra. There he became the leading voice of the younger generation in the Coimbra Question of 1865, the quarrel with the older Romantic poets, and later helped to organise the Casino Lectures of 1871 in Lisbon, a landmark of the Generation of 1870. He was also active in the early Portuguese socialist movement.

His sonnets, written between about 1860 and 1884, move from youthful Romantic longing and Christian doubt to a stern, philosophical poetry of the Idea, the Unconscious, death and the divine. They were collected as Sonetos (1881) and, in their fullest form, in Os sonetos completos (1886), published with a preface by his friend Oliveira Martins. He died by his own hand in Ponta Delgada in 1891. The texts here follow the 1886 edition in its original spelling.
