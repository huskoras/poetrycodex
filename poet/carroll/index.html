Lewis Carroll

1832–1898

Lewis Carroll, the pen name of Charles Lutwidge Dodgson (1832–1898), was an Oxford mathematician best known for the Alice books, whose fondness for nonsense and wordplay also produced a distinctive body of comic and whimsical verse.

<em>The Hunting of the Snark</em> and the parodies collected in <em>Phantasmagoria</em> remain classics of English nonsense poetry.
