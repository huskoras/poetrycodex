Victorian

Charles Harpur

1813–1868

Charles Harpur (1813–1868) was an Australian-born poet, born at Windsor, New South Wales, who spent much of his life in the bush as a farmer and later a gold-fields official.

He published Thoughts: A Series of Sonnets (1845) and The Bushrangers (1853), and wrote under the influence of Wordsworth and Shelley; his best work is in longer poems.
