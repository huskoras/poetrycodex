Guerra Junqueiro

1850-1923

Abílio Manuel Guerra Junqueiro (1850-1923) was born in Freixo de Espada à Cinta, in the north-east of Portugal, and took a degree in law at the University of Coimbra. He served as a civil administrator and as a deputy, worked as a journalist, and became one of the best-known Portuguese poets of the late nineteenth century. He died in Lisbon in 1923.

His verse books include A Morte de D. João (1874), A Musa em Férias (1879), A Velhice do Padre Eterno (1885), Finis Patriae (1890), Os Simples (1892) and Pátria (1896). The texts here are taken from A Velhice do Padre Eterno and Os Simples, in the original spelling of the first editions as transcribed on Portuguese Wikisource.
