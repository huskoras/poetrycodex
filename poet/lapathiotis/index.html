Napoleon Lapathiotis

1888–1944

Napoleon Lapathiotis (1888–1944) was a Greek poet of Athens, whose short, musical lyrics of melancholy, solitude and desire made him one of the best-loved voices of the early twentieth-century Greek lyric tradition. He also wrote prose, and he translated from the French.

Several of his poems, among them «Πλατάνι», «Φθινόπωρο σ΄αγάπησα» and «Στη φυλακή με κλείσανε», were later set to music and became well known as songs. The texts here are taken from Greek Wikisource, with the spelling, accents and punctuation of the transcription kept as given, polytonic where the page is polytonic.
