Jan Neruda

1834–1891

Jan Neruda (1834–1891) was a Prague poet, journalist and feuilletonist, the chronicler of the Malá Strana district where he grew up and whose life he caught in his prose Tales of the Little Quarter. His newspaper columns, theatre criticism and travel writing made him one of the most widely read Czech writers of his generation.

This selection takes two books of his verse: Písně kosmické (Cosmic Songs, 1878), a cycle of short poems that looks at the earth, the moon and the stars with humour and a sceptical, modern eye; and Balady a romance (Ballads and Romances, 1883), in which he retells biblical, historical and folk subjects in plain, song-like language.
