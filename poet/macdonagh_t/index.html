Thomas MacDonagh

1878–1916

Thomas MacDonagh (1878–1916) was an Irish poet, playwright, critic and teacher, and a signatory of the Proclamation of the Irish Republic. He was executed after the Easter Rising on 3 May 1916.

Lyra Celtica (1924) prints a passage from his 'Litany of Beauty'.
