Chang Heng

78–139

Chang Heng (張衡, 78–139) was an Eastern Han statesman, astronomer, mathematician and inventor, born in Xi'e in Nanyang. He served as Grand Astrologer at the court of Luoyang and is remembered for his armillary sphere and for a seismoscope said to have registered distant earthquakes.

He was also one of the leading writers of the Han fu, the long descriptive rhapsody, with the Western Capital and Eastern Capital fu among his best known. Here are the short fu "Returning to the Fields", the "Four Sorrows", a love song in the voice of a young bride, and a dialogue with a skull that takes up the thought of Zhuangzi. The "Four Sorrows", with its repeated plea to a distant beloved, is often cited as an early finished seven-syllable poem.
