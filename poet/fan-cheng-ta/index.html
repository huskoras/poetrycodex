Fan Ch’eng-ta

1126–1193

Fan Ch’eng-ta (范成大; Fan Chengda; 1126–1193) was a writer of ci, the Chinese song lyrics composed to set tunes, in the Song dynasty. He is best known for his pastoral poetry.

The 3 ci here are from the chinese-poetry dataset of Zhu Zumou's 1924 anthology Three Hundred Song Lyrics (宋词三百首), in simplified characters, with the tune name as title.
