Metaphysical & Cavalier

William Barton

1598?–1678

William Barton (1598?–1678) was an English hymnodist and metrical psalmist. His versification of the Psalms was offered to Parliament as a replacement for the older version of Sternhold and Hopkins.

The Psalms here are the 1644 text of The Book of Psalms in Metre, with its original spelling. All 150 psalms are included, none omitted. Psalms made up of several parts, such as Psalm 119, are given as a single poem, and the printed musical notation, tune directions, argument and marginal notes are left out. A bullet (•) marks a letter that is illegible in the digitised source.
