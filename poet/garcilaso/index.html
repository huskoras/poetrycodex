Garcilaso de la Vega

c. 1501–1536

Garcilaso de la Vega was a Spanish soldier and poet, born at Toledo around 1501 into a noble family. He served Emperor Charles V in his campaigns, fought in the expedition to Tunis in 1535, and was fatally wounded in 1536 while leading an assault at Le Muy in Provence.

Often called the prince of Castilian poets, he brought the Italian forms of Petrarch and Sannazaro into Spanish verse: the sonnet, the hendecasyllabic line, the eclogue and the elegy. His poems were published after his death alongside those of his friend Juan Boscán. This archive holds J. H. Wiffen's English verse translation of his works, published in London in 1823.
