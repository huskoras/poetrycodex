Charlotte Grace O'Brien

1845–1909

Charlotte Grace O'Brien (1845–1909), daughter of William Smith O'Brien, was a novelist and poet who also devoted herself to the welfare of Irish emigrants.

The poems here are taken from H. Halliday Sparling's anthology Irish Minstrelsy (London: Walter Scott, 1888).
