Victorian

C. S. Calverley

1831–1884

Charles Stuart Calverley (22 December 1831 – 17 February 1884) was an English poet and wit, a Cambridge man often called the literary father of the 'university school of humour'.

He is remembered for parody and light verse, collected in Verses and Translations (1862) and Fly Leaves (1872), and for his translations, including of Theocritus.
