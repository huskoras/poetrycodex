French

Joséphin Soulary

1815–1891

Joséphin Soulary (1815–1891) was a French poet born and long resident in Lyon, where he worked as a civil servant and later as a librarian. He became known beyond Lyon with Les Sonnets humoristiques (1858), and is remembered above all as a sonneteer who revived the form's strict architecture in an age of freer verse.

His sonnets turn on a concentrated image or a sudden, ironic closing line, often on love, death and the small comedies of provincial life. Associated with the Parnassian circle, he contributed to Le Parnasse contemporain, and his work appears in Lemerre's Anthologie des poètes français contemporains (1887–88). The texts here follow the Lemerre editions of his Œuvres poétiques and the Parnasse contemporain volumes, in French.
