Victorian

Arthur O'Shaughnessy

1844–1881

Arthur O'Shaughnessy (1844–1881) was an English poet who worked as a herpetologist at the British Museum while publishing richly musical, melancholy verse.

His Ode, beginning "We are the music makers," is his most famous poem and a manifesto for the visionary power of artists.
