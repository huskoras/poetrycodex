Victorian

William Morris

1834–1896

William Morris (1834–1896) was an English designer, socialist, and poet, a central figure of the Pre-Raphaelite circle whose vast output includes the narrative cycle <em>The Earthly Paradise</em> and the Icelandic-inspired epic <em>Sigurd the Volsung</em>.

In later years his political convictions produced the direct, rallying verse of <em>Chants for Socialists</em> and <em>The Pilgrims of Hope</em>.
