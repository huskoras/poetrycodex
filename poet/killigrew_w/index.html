Sir William Killigrew

1606–1695

Sir William Killigrew (1606–1695), of Kempton Park, Middlesex, was an English court official who served under both Charles I and Charles II. He was the brother of the dramatist Thomas Killigrew, and Anthony van Dyck painted his portrait in 1638.

In old age he published Mid-night and Daily Thoughts, in Prose and Verse (1694), a book of short devotional meditations, each followed or replaced by a rhymed poem on a Christian theme: repentance, faith, time, the Christian festivals and the fear of death. The poems collected here are the verse of that book.
