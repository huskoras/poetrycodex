Edward Young

1683–1765

Edward Young (1683–1765) was born in Hampshire and educated at Oxford, where he became a fellow of All Souls. He made his name with a poem on the Last Judgment, a series of satires called The Love of Fame, and some tragedies, and took holy orders in middle age, becoming rector of Welwyn in Hertfordshire in 1730.

His blank-verse The Complaint, or Night Thoughts (1742–1745), a long meditation in nine Nights on life, death and immortality, was one of the most widely read poems in Europe in the later eighteenth century. William Blake made a famous series of designs for it.
