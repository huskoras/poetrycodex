Romantic

William Wordsworth

1770–1850

William Wordsworth (1770–1850) was an English Romantic poet who, with Coleridge, launched the movement in <em>Lyrical Ballads</em> (1798).

He made ordinary life and the natural world his subject, and served as Poet Laureate from 1843 until his death.
