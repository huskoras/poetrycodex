Victorian

Algernon Charles Swinburne

1837–1909

Algernon Charles Swinburne (1837–1909) was an English poet notorious in his own day for the sensuous, metrically dazzling, and often scandalous verse of <em>Poems and Ballads</em>.

An ardent republican, he also wrote fervent political verse cheering Italian unification and liberty, alongside a huge later output of odes, elegies, and sonnets.
