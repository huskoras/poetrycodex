Florbela Espanca

1894–1930

Florbela Espanca (1894–1930) was a Portuguese poet, born in Vila Viçosa in the Alentejo. She was among the first women to study law at the University of Lisbon, and her life was marked by three marriages and by a deep restlessness that runs through her work. She published her first book, Livro de Mágoas, in 1919, and Livro de Sóror Saudade followed in 1923.

Her poems are mostly sonnets, intimate and passionate, in which the speaker alternates between sorrow and exalted desire. She died at Matosinhos on her thirty-sixth birthday, 8 December 1930. Her last collection, Charneca em Flor, which she had prepared for the press, appeared in 1931; only the poems of these three books are gathered here.
