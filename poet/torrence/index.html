Ridgely Torrence

1875–1950

Ridgely Torrence (1875–1950, the dates this anthology prints) was an American poet and dramatist, born in Xenia, Ohio, who later served as poetry editor of The New Republic.

His Plays for a Negro Theater (1917) were an early attempt at serious drama for Black actors on the American stage.
