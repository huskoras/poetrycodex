Metaphysical & Cavalier

Henry Vaughan

1621–1695

Henry Vaughan (1621–1695) was a Welsh metaphysical poet and physician who styled himself ‘the Silurist.’

He is best known for the sacred poems of Silex Scintillans (1650), written under the deep influence of George Herbert.
