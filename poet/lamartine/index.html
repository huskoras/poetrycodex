French

Alphonse de Lamartine

1790–1869

Alphonse de Lamartine (1790–1869) was born in Mâcon and became the first great poet of French Romanticism with the Méditations poétiques (1820), whose lyrics of love, loss and faith, among them “Le Lac” and “L’Isolement”, made him famous overnight.

He followed them with the Nouvelles Méditations (1823) and the Harmonies poétiques et religieuses (1830), entered the Académie française in 1829, and later served as a deputy and, in 1848, as foreign minister of the French provisional government. The texts here are from French Wikisource: the first edition of the 1820 Méditations, and the poems of the Nouvelles Méditations and Harmonies as printed in the 1860 collected works.
