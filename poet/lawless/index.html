Victorian

Emily Lawless

1845–1913

The Hon. Emily Lawless (1845–1913) was an Irish novelist, poet and naturalist, born in County Kildare, daughter of Lord Cloncurry. She is best known for her novels Hurrish and Grania and for the poems of With the Wild Geese, and her love of the Burren and the Atlantic coast of the west of Ireland runs through her verse.

The poems here are taken from The Inalienable Heritage, and Other Poems, privately printed in 1914, the year after her death. Many were written during her last years of illness. Edith Sichel’s preface to the volume is not reproduced.
