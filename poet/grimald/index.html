Nicholas Grimald

1519–1562

Nicholas Grimald (1519–1562), also spelled Grimalde, was an English scholar, poet and playwright, a member of Christ Church, Oxford, and the author of the Latin play Christus Redivivus.

He was a leading contributor to Tottel’s Miscellany (1557), where his poems appeared with those of Surrey and Wyatt.
