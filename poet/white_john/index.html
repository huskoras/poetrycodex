Metaphysical & Cavalier

John White

1575–1648

John White (1575–1648) was an English Puritan clergyman, rector of Holy Trinity, Dorchester, in Dorset, where his long ministry earned him the nickname the ‘Patriarch of Dorchester’. He took a close interest in the colonisation of New England and was instrumental in organising the Dorchester Company and in the founding of the Massachusetts Bay Company.

His metrical version of the Psalms, made for congregational singing in common tunes, was left in the care of his son and printed posthumously in 1655 as Davids Psalms in Metre, with a preface by his successor Stanley Gower. The Psalms here are the 1655 text, with its original spelling. Fourteen psalms whose source pages are damaged or illegible are omitted.
