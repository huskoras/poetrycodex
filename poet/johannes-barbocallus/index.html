Johannes Barbocallus

6th century AD (Paton’s date)

Johannes Barbocallus is one of the epigrammatists whose short poems were gathered in the Greek Anthology. Paton's index of authors dates the poet to 6th century AD. Paton's index lists the poet among the writers of the Cycle of Agathias, compiled in the sixth century AD.

This archive carries the 1 epigram ascribed to the poet in the first volume of W. R. Paton's prose translation (Books I–VI) (Book VI: 55).
