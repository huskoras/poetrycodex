Metaphysical & Cavalier

John Cleveland

1613–1658

John Cleveland (1613–1658) was an English Royalist poet and satirist, a fellow of St John's College, Cambridge, who lost his post when the Civil War came and served the King's cause with his pen. He is best known for his sharp political satires against Scots, Parliament men and Puritans, and for love poems in the most extravagant 'strong-lined' conceited style.

Cleveland's verse was enormously popular in the 1650s and was reprinted many times, though the collections that carry his name also contain pieces by other hands. The text here follows George Saintsbury's Minor Poets of the Caroline Period, vol. III (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1921), using the poems he prints as Cleveland's and leaving out those he marks as disputed.
