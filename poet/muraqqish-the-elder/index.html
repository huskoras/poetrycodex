Muraqqish the Elder

6th century

Muraqqish the Elder belonged to the family of Sa'd ibn Malik of Qais ibn Tha'labah, of Bakr; he was uncle of Muraqqish the Younger. Lyall notes that the family fought in the War of al-Basus, and that he is chiefly of interest for his age.

Lyall remarks that the native critics speak slightingly of him, but that most of the pieces appear really old, especially Ode LIV.
