Sara Teasdale

1884–1933

Sara Teasdale (1884–1933) was an American lyric poet, born in St. Louis, Missouri, and later resident in New York City. Her early books, Sonnets to Duse (1907) and Helen of Troy and Other Poems (1911), were followed by Rivers to the Sea (1915) and Love Songs (1917), the latter of which won the first Columbia University Poetry Society Prize, a forerunner of the Pulitzer Prize for Poetry.

Her work is known for its compressed, song-like short lyrics of love, solitude, nature and the passing of time, in plain diction and tight rhyme. She died in New York in 1933.
