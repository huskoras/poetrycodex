American

Adelaide Crapsey

1878–1914

Adelaide Crapsey (1878–1914) was an American poet who taught at Smith College and died of tuberculosis at thirty-six.

She devised the cinquain, a five-line form of two, four, six, eight and two syllables, and her only book, Verse, appeared in 1915 after her death.
