John Todhunter

1839–1916

John Todhunter was born in Dublin, practised as a physician and later turned to literature, writing poems and verse plays. He settled in Bedford Park, London.

Yeats printed his poems "The Banshee" and "Aghadoe" in A Book of Irish Verse (1900).
