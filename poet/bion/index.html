Bion of Smyrna

fl. c. 100 BC

Bion of Phlossa, near Smyrna, was a Greek bucolic poet who flourished in Sicily in the late second and early first centuries BC.

Only one complete poem, The Lament for Adonis, and a few small fragments of his other poems survive; the fragments are preserved in quotations by Stobaeus and others.
