Romantic

Charles Lamb

1775–1834

Charles Lamb was an English essayist and critic, famous for the Essays of Elia, and a close friend of Samuel Taylor Coleridge. He also wrote a small body of verse.

Palgrave includes several of his short poems, among them "The Old Familiar Faces".
