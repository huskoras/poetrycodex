Anna de Noailles

1876–1933

Anna de Noailles (1876–1933) was a French poet, born in Paris as Anna de Brancovan, of Romanian and Greek descent. She became one of the most celebrated women writers of the Belle Époque and kept a famous salon.

Her first collection, Le Cœur innombrable (1901), brought her immediate fame; it was crowned by the Académie française. Its poems celebrate nature, the seasons, love and the fear of death. She died in Paris in 1933.

Texts are taken from the Wikisource transcription (CC BY-SA) of the 1901 Calmann Lévy edition; the original poems are in the public domain.
