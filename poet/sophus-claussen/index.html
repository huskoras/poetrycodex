Sophus Claussen

1865–1931

Sophus Claussen (1865–1931) was a Danish poet, born at Helletofte on the island of Langeland. He first appeared in print in 1887 and became one of the principal voices of Danish Symbolism in the 1890s, a poet of city streets, parks and half-light who spent long periods abroad, above all in France, and brought Symbolist habits of image and mood into Danish verse. He died at Gentofte in 1931.

This selection of fifty poems is taken from Kalliope, the Danish poetry archive, in the spelling and punctuation of the first editions, with capitalised nouns and the old aa: ten each from Pilefløjter (1899), Djævlerier (1904), Eroter og Fauner (1910), Danske Vers (1912) and Heroica (1925), in book order. Poems that reappear in a later book are filed under the first.
