Thomas Bastard

1565/6–1618

Thomas Bastard (1565/6–1618) was an English clergyman and epigrammatist, born in Blandford Forum, Dorset. He was educated at Winchester College and New College, Oxford, and later held church livings in Dorset, including Bere Regis.

His Chrestoleros: Seven Bookes of Epigrames (1598), dedicated to Lord Mountjoy, is a collection of short satirical and moral verses on lawyers, doctors, preachers, courtiers and fools, together with epitaphs and poems of praise. It is among the best-known English epigram books of the Elizabethan age.
