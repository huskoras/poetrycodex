Anna Wickham

1883–1947

Anna Wickham (1883–1947) was the pen name of Edith Alice Mary Harper, an English poet born in Wimbledon and brought up partly in Australia. She was a member of the circle of poets around the Poetry Bookshop in London.

She published The Contemplative Quarry (1915) and The Man with a Hammer (1916), terse, outspoken verse on marriage, womanhood and the poet's calling.
