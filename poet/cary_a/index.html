American

Alice Cary

1820–1871

Alice Cary (1820–1871), elder sister of the poet Phoebe Cary, was an American poet and novelist, born near Cincinnati, Ohio.

The poem here is taken from Edmund Clarence Stedman’s An American Anthology, 1787–1900 (Boston and New York: Houghton, Mifflin and Company, 1900).
