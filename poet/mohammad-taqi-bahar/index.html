Malek o-Sho'ara Bahar

1886–1951

Malek o-Sho'ara Bahar (ملک‌الشعرا بهار, Mohammad-Taqi Bahar, 1886–1951) was a Persian poet, scholar, journalist and politician, born at Mashhad. He inherited the title of poet laureate (malek o-sho'ara) from his father at the court of Muzaffar al-Din Shah, took the side of the Constitutionalists, sat several times in the Majlis, and was imprisoned and exiled more than once for his politics. He also taught Persian literature in Tehran and wrote the three-volume Sabk-shenasi on the history of Persian prose style. His verse joins the diction of the classical qasida to the public themes of his age: homeland, constitution and the failures of rulers.

This selection comes from the Divan as given on Ganjoor: qasidas and musammats ranked by the recitations and linked songs recorded there, beginning with the Damavandiyya ('O white demon, foot in chains'), with ties kept in Ganjoor's own order. Each bayt stands on one line, the two hemistichs separated by a wide space; titles give the first hemistich and Ganjoor's number. The Persian text is public domain.
