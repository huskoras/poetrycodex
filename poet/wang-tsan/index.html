Wang Can 王粲 (Wang Ts’an)

177–217

Wang Can (王粲, courtesy name Zhongxuan, 177–217) was born at Shanyang Gaoping in Shandong and counted as one of the Seven Masters of Jian'an. When Chang'an fell into chaos after the death of Dong Zhuo he fled south to Jingzhou, where he lived for some fifteen years under Liu Biao before entering the service of Cao Cao in 208.

He is best known for the three "Seven Sorrows" (七哀詩), which describe the starving roads of the ruined capital and the exile's nights on the Yangzi, for the five "Songs of Campaign" (從軍詩) written on Cao Cao's expeditions, and for the rhapsody "Ascending the Tower" (登樓賦). The texts here are those of Chinese Wikisource, mostly following the Wen xuan; some pages print the lines without commas, separated by a full-width space, and are kept as printed.
