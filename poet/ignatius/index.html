Ignatius

10th century AD (?) (Paton’s date)

Ignatius is one of the Byzantine ecclesiastical writers whose epitaphs and verses are preserved in the Greek Anthology. Paton’s Index of Authors dates him, with a query, to the 10th century AD.

This archive carries the 4 epigrams ascribed to the poet in the fifth volume of W. R. Paton's prose translation (Loeb, 1918): Book XV: 29, 30, 31, 39.
