Metaphysical & Cavalier

Sir John Denham

1615–1669

Sir John Denham (1615–1669) was an English poet best known for Cooper's Hill (1642), a landscape meditation that founded the English topographical poem.

His couplets were much admired by Dryden and Pope for their balance and control.
