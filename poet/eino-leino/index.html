Eino Leino

1878–1926

Eino Leino (born Armas Einar Leopold Lönnbohm, 1878–1926) was a Finnish poet, novelist, dramatist and journalist, one of the central figures of Finnish literature at the turn of the twentieth century. He came from Paltamo in Kainuu, the Kalevala country, and began publishing while still a student; his first collection of poems, Maaliskuun lauluja, appeared in 1896.

His Helkavirsiä (Whitsuntide Hymns, 1903 and 1916) recast the myths and folk meters of the Kalevala tradition in a new, modern voice, and are generally counted his greatest achievement. He also translated Dante and wrote prolifically for newspapers; he died in Tuusula in 1926. This selection gives the two series of Helkavirsiä and his early lyric book Maaliskuun lauluja, in Finnish.
