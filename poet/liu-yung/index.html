Liu Yung

c. 984–c. 1053

Liu Yung (柳永; Liu Yong; c. 984–c. 1053) was a writer of ci, the Chinese song lyrics composed to set tunes, in the Song dynasty. He was the first major poet to write the long form of ci (manci).

The 13 ci here are from the chinese-poetry dataset of Zhu Zumou's 1924 anthology Three Hundred Song Lyrics (宋词三百首), in simplified characters, with the tune name as title.
