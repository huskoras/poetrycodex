Mikołaj Sęp Szarzyński

c. 1550–1581

Mikołaj Sęp Szarzyński (c. 1550–1581) was a Polish poet of the late Renaissance, born into a noble family in the Ruthenian lands of the Commonwealth. He studied in Wittenberg and Leipzig, served at the court of King Stephen Báthory, and died young, apparently while holding an estate near Lwów. His religious sonnets and songs are usually counted among the first works of the Polish Baroque.

His verse was gathered after his death and printed in 1601 by his brother Jakub as Rytmy abo wiersze polskie. The collection holds six sonnets on the brevity of life and the war of the soul with the devil, the world and the flesh, paraphrases of psalms, songs, epitaphs and epigrams. The texts here follow Ignacy Chrzanowski's edition of 1903 (Kraków, Akademia Umiejętności), which prints the 1601 edition together with poems from a manuscript, in the old spelling.
