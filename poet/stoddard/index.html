Richard Henry Stoddard

1825–1903

Richard Henry Stoddard (1825–1903) was an American poet and critic, born at Hingham, Massachusetts. He worked in an iron foundry as a young man, taught himself to write, and later became literary editor of the New York Mail and Express.

He was a central figure of the New York literary circle of his day, alongside Bayard Taylor and Edmund Clarence Stedman, and his polished lyrics were widely anthologised.
