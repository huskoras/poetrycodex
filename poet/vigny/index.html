French

Alfred de Vigny

1797–1863

Alfred de Vigny (1797–1863) was a French poet, novelist and dramatist, born at Loches into an old military family and an officer in his youth. He is counted among the leading figures of French Romanticism.

His verse is gathered in two books, Poèmes antiques et modernes (1826; definitive text 1837) and Les Destinées, published after his death in 1864. This selection takes every poem from both collections.
