France Prešeren

1800–1849

France Prešeren (1800–1849), born at Vrba in Upper Carniola, is the great poet of the Slovene language. He studied law in Vienna, took his doctorate in 1828, and worked as a lawyer's clerk in Ljubljana before opening his own practice in Kranj in 1846. He died there on 8 February 1849.

Poezije (Poems), published at Ljubljana in 1847, is the only collection he brought out himself. It ranges from love lyrics, ballads and romances to satirical epigrams, gazelas and sonnets, among them the Sonetni venec, a crown of fifteen sonnets. Zdravljica, which the censor kept out of the book, was printed in 1848, and its seventh stanza has been the national anthem of Slovenia since 1991.
