Gustavo Adolfo Bécquer

1836–1870

Gustavo Adolfo Bécquer (1836–1870) was a Spanish poet and writer, born in Seville, and the leading voice of late Spanish Romanticism. He worked as a journalist and is also remembered for his prose Leyendas.

His poems, the Rimas, short and musical lyrics on love, poetry and loss, were gathered after his death and first published in 1871, a year after he died in Madrid. They became among the most loved and imitated verse in Spanish; the text here follows the 1885 Madrid edition of his collected works.
