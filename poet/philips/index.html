Metaphysical & Cavalier

Katherine Philips

1632–1664

Katherine Philips (1632–1664), called ‘the Matchless Orinda,’ was an English poet and translator.

She was admired above all for her poems of female friendship; her collected <em>Poems</em> appeared in 1664 and 1667.
