Victorian

Christina Rossetti

1830–1894

Christina Rossetti (1830–1894) was an English poet of devotional intensity and quiet formal mastery, sister of the painter-poet Dante Gabriel Rossetti.

Best known for ‘Goblin Market’ and the carol ‘In the Bleak Midwinter,’ she also wrote Sing-Song (1872), a book of nursery rhymes for children.
