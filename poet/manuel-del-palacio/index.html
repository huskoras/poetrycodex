Manuel del Palacio

1832–1906

Manuel del Palacio (Lérida, 1832 – Paris, 1906) was a Spanish poet, satirist and journalist. A native of Catalonia raised in Granada, he made his name in Madrid as a witty contributor to the satirical press and as a poet of easy, polished verse, and he later served the Spanish state in a diplomatic post in Paris, where he died.

His best-known book, Cien sonetos políticos, filosóficos y amorosos (1870), gathers love poems, meditations on age and death and sharp political satire of the revolutionary years after 1868. Menéndez Pelayo included his sonnet "Amor oculto" in his anthology Las cien mejores poesías líricas de la lengua castellana (1908).
