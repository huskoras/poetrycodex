Amado Nervo

1870–1919

Amado Nervo (Tepic, 1870 – Montevideo, 1919) was a Mexican poet, journalist and diplomat, one of the leading voices of Spanish-American Modernismo. He studied for the priesthood in Michoacán but abandoned it, and in the 1890s took up journalism in Mazatlán and Mexico City, writing for the Revista Azul and the Revista Moderna.

Místicas (1898), his first book of verse, is a sequence of poems in which religious faith, doubt and desire are set against one another in the manner of the Modernista poets. From 1905 he served in the Mexican diplomatic service in Madrid, and in 1918 he was sent as minister to Argentina and Uruguay; he died in Montevideo the following year.
