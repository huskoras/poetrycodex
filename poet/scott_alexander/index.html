Tudor & Elizabethan

Alexander Scott

c. 1520–c. 1583

Alexander Scott was a Scottish makar of the mid-sixteenth century, whose short love lyrics are preserved chiefly in the Bannatyne Manuscript. Eyre-Todd calls him the earliest of the more distinctly lyrical Scottish poets.

He also wrote a comic burlesque, "The Justing and Debait vp at the Drum". Little is securely known of his life.
