Laza Kostić

1841–1910

Laza Kostić (born in Sombor, 1841; died in Vienna, 1910) was a Serbian poet, dramatist, translator, aesthetician, lawyer and politician. He studied law in Pest, took part in the political life of the Serbs of Hungary, and translated several of Shakespeare's plays, among them Hamlet and King Lear, into Serbian. He also wrote the verse dramas Maksim Crnojević and Pera Segedinac.

He is counted among the greatest Serbian Romantics and as a forerunner of modern Serbian poetry. His lyric verse is daring in language and thought: 'Među javom i med snom' and the elegy for Kosta Ruvarac belong to its best-known pieces, and the love-prayer 'Santa Maria della Salute', printed in the 1909 edition of his Poems, is often named as the summit of his work.
