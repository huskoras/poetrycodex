Douglas Hyde

1860–1949

Douglas Hyde was born in Castlerea, County Roscommon, and was a scholar of Irish. He founded the Gaelic League in 1893 and later served as the first President of Ireland (1938–45).

His English versions of Irish folk songs, such as those in Love Songs of Connacht (1893), were widely admired.
