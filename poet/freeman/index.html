John Freeman

1885–1929

John Freeman (dates as printed in Untermeyer's Modern British Poetry (1920): 1885–1929) was an English poet who worked as an insurance executive while writing verse and criticism. He was associated with the Georgian poets; his book Stone Trees appeared in 1916.

The poem here comes from Untermeyer's Modern British Poetry (1920).
