Ts’en Ts’an

c. AD 750

Ts’en Ts’an (c. AD 750) was an intimate friend of Tu Fu and, Cranmer-Byng tells us, a censor under the Emperor Su Tsung who rose to be governor of Chia-chou. Most of his surviving verse takes the form of quatrains.

The poems here are L. Cranmer-Byng’s version in A Lute of Jade (1909), given with the poet’s name as spelled in those books.
