Nordic

Christian Winther

1796–1876

Christian Winther (Rasmus Villads Christian Ferdinand Winther) was born on 29 July 1796 at Fensmark in Zealand and died in Paris on 30 December 1876. He belongs to the Danish Golden Age, and is remembered above all as a lyric poet of love, the seasons and the Danish countryside, writing in clear, song-like stanzas.

His first book, Digte (1828), already contained the woodcut-like country idylls of Træsnit and the song “Flyv Fugl, flyv”. Later collections include Til Een (1843), the love cycle Da jeg var ung in Lyriske Digte (1849), and the long verse narrative Hjortens Flugt (1856). The poems here keep the spelling of the editions on Kalliope, and the four English versions come from A Book of Danish Verse (1922).
