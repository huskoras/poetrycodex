Cesário Verde

1855–1886

Cesário Verde was born in Lisbon in 1855 and died there in 1886, aged thirty-one, of tuberculosis. The son of a prosperous shopkeeper and farmer, he divided his time between the family's ironmongery business in the city and its country estate, and for a short while attended courses in the Curso Superior de Letras. He published poems in Lisbon and Porto newspapers and was largely unnoticed in his lifetime.

His verse turned from the romantic manner of the day toward the streets, markets, offices and suburbs of a modern city, seen with a painter's eye for colour and light, and with irony toward class and money. His friend Silva Pinto gathered his scattered poems in O Livro de Cesário Verde (1887), published after his death. Its long poem O Sentimento dum Ocidental, a night walk through Lisbon, is among the best known in Portuguese, and later poets, Fernando Pessoa among them, honoured him as a forerunner.
