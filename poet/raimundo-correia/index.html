Raimundo Correia

1859–1911

Raimundo da Mota de Azevedo Correia (1859–1911) was a Brazilian poet and judge, born at sea off the coast of Maranhão. With Olavo Bilac and Alberto de Oliveira he made up the Parnassian triad of Brazilian verse, and he was a founding member of the Brazilian Academy of Letters.

His books are Primeiros Sonhos (1879), Sinfonias (1883, with a preface by Machado de Assis), Versos e Versões (1887), Aleluias (1891) and the collected Poesias (1898). The sonnets "As Pombas" and "Mal secreto" are among the best-loved poems of the Parnassian school.
