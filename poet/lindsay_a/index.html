Lady Anne Lindsay

1750–1825

Lady Anne Lindsay, later Lady Anne Barnard, was a Scottish writer. She wrote the ballad "Auld Robin Gray" in 1771.

Palgrave includes "Auld Robin Gray", her best-known poem.
