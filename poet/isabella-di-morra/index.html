Isabella di Morra

c. 1520–1546

Isabella di Morra (c. 1520-1546) was an Italian poet of the Renaissance, born into a noble family at Favale (now Valsinni) in Basilicata, in the far south of the kingdom of Naples. She was educated in letters and wrote in the Petrarchan manner, but her poems are shaped by her isolation in a remote castle above the river Sinni, and by the exile of her father in France.

She was killed in 1546, reportedly by her brothers, who suspected a correspondence with a neighbouring nobleman. Thirteen poems survive; they were first printed in 1552 in the anthology edited by Lodovico Dolce and published by Giolito. The text here follows the Italian Wikisource transcription of the Rime.

Poems (13)

  1. I fieri assalti di crudel Fortuna
  2. Sacra Giunone, se i volgari amori
  3. D'un alto monte onde si scorge il mare
  4. Quanto pregiar ti puoi, Siri mio amato
  5. Non sol il ciel vi fu largo e cortese
  6. Fortuna che sollevi in alto stato
  7. Ecco ch'una altra volta, o valle inferna
  8. Torbido Siri, del mio mal superbo
  9. Se a la propinqua speme nuovo impaccio
  10. Scrissi con stile amaro, aspro e dolente
  11. Poscia che al bel desir troncate hai l'ale
  12. Signor, che insino a qui, tua gran mercede
  13. Quel che gli giorni a dietro
