Anne Killigrew

1660–1685

Anne Killigrew (1660–1685) was the daughter of Dr Henry Killigrew, Master of the Savoy and chaplain to the Duke of York. She became a maid of honour to Mary of Modena, Duchess of York, and was a painter as well as a poet.

She died of smallpox on 16 June 1685, aged about twenty-five. Her Poems were published posthumously in 1686 with an ode to her memory by John Dryden, which has kept her name alive; the verses collected here are her own.
