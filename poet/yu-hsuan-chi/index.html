Yü Hsüan-chi

c. AD 844–868

Yü Hsüan-chi (Yu Xuanji, 魚玄機, courtesy name Youwei, later Huilan) was a woman poet of the late T’ang dynasty, born in Chang’an around AD 844.

She became a concubine of the official Li Yi, and after being sent away entered a Daoist convent in Chang’an, where she exchanged poems with Wen Tingyun (Wen Fei-ch’ing) and others. Tradition says she was executed in about AD 868; some fifty of her poems survive.
