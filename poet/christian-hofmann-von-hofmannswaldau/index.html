Christian Hofmann von Hofmannswaldau

1617–1679

Christian Hofmann von Hofmannswaldau (1617–1679) was born in Breslau into a patrician family, studied at Danzig and Leiden, travelled in the Netherlands, England, France and Italy, and later served the city of Breslau as a councillor and president of the council. He is counted, with Daniel Casper von Lohenstein, among the leading poets of the Second Silesian School.

Most of his love poetry circulated in manuscript and was first printed after the author's death, notably in Benjamin Neukirch's anthology of 1695, Herrn von Hoffmannswaldau und anderer Deutschen auserlesene und bißher ungedruckte Gedichte. The poems here, taken from that anthology, are those signed C.H.v.H.; they include sonnets on the vanity of beauty, gallant lyrics to Flavia and other named beloveds, and epitaphs. The spelling and punctuation of the 1695 print are kept.
