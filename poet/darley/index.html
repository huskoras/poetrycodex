Romantic

George Darley

1795–1846

George Darley was born in Dublin and spent most of his working life in London, where he was a poet, mathematician and art and drama critic for The Athenaeum. His works include Sylvia; or, The May Queen (1827) and Nepenthe (1835).

He suffered from a severe stammer and wrote little for a wide audience in his lifetime.
