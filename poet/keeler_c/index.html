American

Charles Augustus Keeler

1871–1937

Charles Augustus Keeler (1871–1937) was an American naturalist, poet and writer, born in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, and educated at the University of California. He lived in Berkeley, California.

His verse volumes include A Light through the Storm (1894), The Promise of the Ages (1896), The Siege of the Golden City (1896) and A Season's Sowing (1899).
