George William Russell (A.E.)

1867–1935

George William Russell, who wrote under the initials A.E., was born in Lurgan and became a poet, painter, mystic and editor. He worked for the Irish Agricultural Organisation Society and edited The Irish Homestead.

He is not to be confused with the American poet of the same surname already in this archive. He died in 1935.
