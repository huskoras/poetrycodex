Thomas Erskine, Lord Erskine

1750–1823

Thomas Erskine, 1st Baron Erskine (1750–1823), was one of the most celebrated English advocates of his day and served briefly as Lord Chancellor in 1806–07.

The epigram here is the one James Parton printed under the name Erskine in his anthology of English humorous poetry.
