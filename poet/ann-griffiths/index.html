Ann Griffiths

1776–1805

Ann Griffiths (Ann Thomas, 1776–1805) was born at Dolwar Fach, a farm in the parish of Llanfihangel-yng-Ngwynfa in Montgomeryshire, the daughter of a farmer. She was brought up in the Church of England and, in 1796, after hearing the Methodist preacher Benjamin Jones at Pontrobert, she joined the Calvinistic Methodists. In October 1804 she married Thomas Griffiths, and she died in August 1805 at the age of twenty-nine, a few weeks after the birth of a daughter who did not survive her.

She did not publish her hymns. They were kept in memory and recited by her servant Ruth Evans, who afterwards married the preacher John Hughes of Pontrobert, and it was he who wrote them down. Some were first printed in 1806, and the whole of her surviving work was gathered in the edition of Owen M. Edwards (Cyfres y Fil, 1905), from which the texts here are taken, with its spelling kept. Her hymns are among the best-loved in Welsh.
