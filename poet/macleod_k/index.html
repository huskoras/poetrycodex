Kenneth Macleod

1871–1955

The Rev. Kenneth Macleod (1871–1955) was a Gaelic-speaking minister and writer from the Hebrides, best known for his collaboration with Marjory Kennedy-Fraser on the Songs of the Hebrides (1909 onward), for which he supplied English words to the island melodies.

Lyra Celtica (1924) prints three of his lyrics, 'Dance to your Shadow', 'Sea Longing' and 'The Reiving Ship'.
