Hayim Nahman Bialik

1873–1934

Hayim Nahman Bialik (ח. נ. ביאליק, 1873–1934) was born in Radi, Volhynia, and educated in the yeshiva of Volozhin before living in Odessa, where he became the leading poet of the Hebrew national revival. He later settled in Tel Aviv, where he died in 1934.

The poems here are in Hebrew with their vowel points, taken from the Dvir collected edition as digitised by the volunteers of Project Ben-Yehuda (benyehuda.org), whose public-domain dump supplies the text. They include his early lyrics, the poems of the Kishinev pogrom, and his late work.
