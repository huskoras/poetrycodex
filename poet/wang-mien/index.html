Wang Mien

1287–1359

Wang Mien (王冕, 1287–1359; also romanised Wang Mian), courtesy name Yuanzhang, was a painter and poet of the Yuan dynasty, born at Zhuji in Zhejiang. He came from a poor family and is said to have taught himself while herding cattle. He is remembered above all as a painter of plum blossom, and his ink plums and the poems he wrote on them are among the best-loved works of Yuan literati art.

His collected poems are known as the Zhuzhai ji (竹齋集). The best known of them, 'Mo mei' (Ink Plum), describes a plum tree beside the pool where he washed his inkstone, and has long been read as a statement of modest, unadorned integrity. The poems here are given in the original Chinese, from Wikisource; the longer ones follow the Siku quanshu text, which carries no punctuation, so they are set out in lines of five or seven characters.
