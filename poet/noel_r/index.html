Victorian

Roden Noel

1834–1894

Roden Berkeley Wriothesley Noel (1834–1894) was an English poet, a younger son of the first Earl of Gainsborough. His volumes include Beatrice and Other Poems (1868), The Red Flag and Other Poems (1872), A Little Child's Monument (1881) and Songs of the Heights and Deeps (1885).

Lyra Celtica gives him seven poems, among them 'The Swimmer', 'The Dance' and 'The Pity of It', and counts him among its Anglo-Celtic poets.
