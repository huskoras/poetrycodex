Paul Gérardy

1870–1933

Paul Gérardy (born Maldingen, then in the Kingdom of Prussia, 15 February 1870; died Brussels, 1 June 1933) was a writer and journalist who wrote in French.

Jethro Bithell's 1911 anthology classes him as a Walloon, "a Prussian Walloon", and prints seven of his short poems.
