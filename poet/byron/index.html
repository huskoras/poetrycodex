Lord Byron

1788–1824

Lord Byron (George Gordon Byron, 1788–1824) was an English Romantic poet and satirist, one of the most celebrated — and scandalous — figures of his age.

He died at Missolonghi while supporting the Greek war of independence, and gave his name to the brooding ‘Byronic hero.’
