Sarah Anne Curzon

1833–1898

Sarah Anne Curzon (1833–1898) was an English-born Canadian writer and advocate of women's suffrage, who lived in Toronto.

She wrote the poem and play Laura Secord, the Heroine of 1812 (1887). One of the poets chosen by W. D. Lighthall for Songs of the Great Dominion (London, 1889), an anthology of Canadian verse.
