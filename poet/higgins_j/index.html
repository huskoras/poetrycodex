Tudor & Elizabethan

John Higgins

fl. 1570–1602

John Higgins revised and extended A Mirror for Magistrates, the Tudor collection of verse “tragedies” in which fallen princes and nobles speak their own downfall. His First Part of the Mirror (1574, enlarged in later editions) carries the story back to the legendary kings of Britain and the Roman emperors, and begins with his own verse Induction.

The poems here are taken from Joseph Haslewood’s 1815 reprint, which follows the 1587 text of Part I collated with the editions of 1575 and 1610. Each legend is given with the Lenvoy that Higgins prints after it. One further legend, the death of Sir Nicholas Burdet, is credited to Higgins in the contents of Part III.
