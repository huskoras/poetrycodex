Victorian

E. Nesbit

1858–1924

Edith Nesbit (1858–1924), who published verse as E. Nesbit, is best remembered today as the author of children's classics such as Five Children and It (1902) and The Railway Children (1906). She was a founder member of the Fabian Society.

Her early poems were collected in Lays and Legends (1886; second series 1892). Two of her poems appear in Women Poets of the Victorian Era (1890).
