Joseph Addison

1672–1719

Joseph Addison (1672–1719) was an English essayist, poet and politician, educated at Charterhouse and Magdalen College, Oxford. With Richard Steele he wrote The Tatler and The Spectator, the periodical essays that made his prose a model of the polite style.

His verse includes The Campaign (1704), written in praise of Marlborough's victory at Blenheim, A Letter from Italy, An Account of the Greatest English Poets and a handful of hymns and odes first printed in The Spectator, among them 'The spacious firmament on high'. His translations of Ovid and Virgil, and his drama Cato, are not included here.
