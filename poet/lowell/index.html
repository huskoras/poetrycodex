American

James Russell Lowell

1819–1891

James Russell Lowell (1819–1891) was an American poet, critic, and diplomat from Cambridge, Massachusetts, who edited the Atlantic Monthly and later served as U.S. minister to Spain and Britain.

A member of the ‘Fireside Poets’, he wrote lyric and narrative verse — most famously ‘The Vision of Sir Launfal’ — alongside satirical and abolitionist poetry that engaged directly with the politics of his day.
