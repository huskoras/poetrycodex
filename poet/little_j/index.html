Romantic

Janet Little

1759–1813

Janet Little (1759–1813), known as 'the Scotch Milkmaid', was a Scottish poet who worked as a dairymaid and servant in the household of Flora, Countess of Loudoun, at Loudoun Castle in Ayrshire. Her Poetical Works were published by subscription at Ayr in 1792.

The volume mixes English and Scots verse: epistles, elegies, songs and tales, several addressed to friends in the Loudoun household, with an epistle to Robert Burns and a poem on her visit to him. The dedication and list of subscribers are omitted here.
