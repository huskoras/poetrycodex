A. Mary F. Robinson

1857–1944

Agnes Mary Frances Robinson (1857–1944) was an English poet and scholar who later wrote under the names Madame Darmesteter and Madame Duclaux after her two marriages, to James Darmesteter (1888) and Emile Duclaux (1901). She lived much in France.

Her early volumes include A Handful of Honeysuckle (1878), The Crowned Hippolytus (1881) and An Italian Garden (1886). Four of her poems appear in Women Poets of the Victorian Era (1890).
