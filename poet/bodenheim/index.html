American

Maxwell Bodenheim

1892–1954

Maxwell Bodenheim (1892–1954) was an American poet and novelist of the Greenwich Village bohemia, born in Hermanville, Mississippi.

His early books include Minna and Myself (1918) and Advice (1920).
