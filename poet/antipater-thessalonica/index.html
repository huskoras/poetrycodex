Ancient Greek & Roman

Antipater of Thessalonica

fl. c. 10 BC – AD 20

Antipater of Thessalonica wrote in the Augustan age and belongs to the Garland of Philippus. He addressed Roman patrons, among them Lucius Calpurnius Piso, in epigrams of many kinds.

Only epigrams headed “Antipater of Thessalonica” in Paton's volume are filed here.
