Celtic

Andreas Mac Marcuis

fl. 1607

Andreas Mac Marcuis is dated by Sigerson, in Bards of the Gael and Gall (1907), to 1607, the year of the departure of the Ulster earls O’Neill and O’Donnell from Ireland.

His lament for the flight of the earls is given here in Sigerson’s English version.
