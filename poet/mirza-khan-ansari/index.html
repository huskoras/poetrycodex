Mirza Khan Ansari

fl. 17th century (Raverty)

Mirza Khan Ansari was probably a descendant of Pir Roshan, founder of the Roshanian sect among the Eastern Afghans. Raverty reports that he began writing poetry in A.H. 1040 (about A.D. 1630), and that his odes were later gathered into a Diwan under his name.

Some claimed that his real name was Fath Khan of the Yusufzi tribe, but Raverty argues from old manuscripts that Mirza Khan Ansari is the name he bore. His poems are Sufi and mystical in tone, and the selection includes several built on the letters of the Arabic alphabet.
