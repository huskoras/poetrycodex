Anonymous Latin Hymns

c. 5th–15th century

Hymns of the Latin Church whose authorship is unknown, doubtful or disputed: breviary hymns, sequences and anonymous compositions of the early and later Middle Ages, together with a few whose authors are named in the source but are not otherwise represented in this archive.

They are given in the English of John Brownlie's Hymns of the Early Church (1896), which prints his source notes on the authorship of each, and in Catherine Winkworth's version of the Veni Sancte Spiritus from C. C. Nott's The Seven Great Hymns of the Mediaeval Church (1902). Each poem's note gives the Latin first line and what the headnote says about its author.

Poems (44)

  1. O day, the chief of days, whose light
  2. O Light that from the light wast born
  3. O Thou Eternal One in Three
  4. Now daylight floods the morning sky
  5. My heart goes forth in love to Thee
  6. Maker of the world, we pray
  7. Now sinks the glowing orb of day
  8. Thou who hast led our steps this day
  9. Christ, the light that shines eternal
  10. Dark night has drawn her curtain round
  11. See in the east the morn arise
  12. Now sinks the fiery orb of day
  13. To Thee, O Christ, our prayers shall rise
  14. When evening shades around us close
  15. Emmanuel, come! we call for Thee
  16. Zion is glad this glorious morn
  17. Wherefore in the lowly stall
  18. Thou our Redeemer art, O Christ
  19. Now let us tune our hearts to sing
  20. Hail, thou Blessed Cross, all hail!
  21. Veiled was the glory of Thy face
  22. Alleluia! Alleluia!
  23. Shout praises, ye heavens
  24. Burst are the iron gates of death
  25. Alleluia, hymn of sweetness
  26. Eternal King, enthroned on high
  27. To-day the lingering clouds are riven
  28. O Christ, who art ascended now
  29. Come, Thou Creator Spirit blest
  30. Holy Spirit, come with power
  31. O Holy Spirit, font of love
  32. O Thou Eternal One in Three (Trinity Sunday, Lauds)
  33. O Holy Father, gracious Thou and tender
  34. Be present, Holy Trinity
  35. Christian soldiers in the conflict!
  36. Hear us, O Christ, our King
  37. See, sweet soul, my sister dear
  38. O Food for pilgrims pining!
  39. Jesu, delight of every heart
  40. Lo, the day, the day approacheth
  41. O city girt with glory!
  42. O vision bright of heavenly peace
  43. Most firm the sure foundation stands
  44. Veni Sancte Spiritus (Winkworth)
