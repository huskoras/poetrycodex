Mordechai Zvi Mane

1859–1886

Mordechai Zvi Mane (מרדכי צבי מאנה, 1859–1886) was a Hebrew poet of the late Haskalah, born in Radoshkovichi (Radoshkowitz) in the Russian Empire. His poems are dated from Vilna, from his native town and from St Petersburg, and he was also a painter. He died at twenty-six in 1886.

The poems here are in Hebrew with their vowel points, from the Warsaw collected writings of 1913 (Tsentral), as digitised by the volunteers of Project Ben-Yehuda (benyehuda.org), whose public-domain dump supplies the text. They are lyrics of spring and the seasons, friendship, faith and the poet's illness and solitude.
