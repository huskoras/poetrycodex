Richard Henry Dana

1787–1879

Richard Henry Dana (1787–1879) was a Boston poet, critic and lawyer, a founder of the North American Review and the author of The Buccaneer (1827). He was the father of the author of Two Years Before the Mast.

Griswold included 6 of his poems in The Poets and Poetry of America (16th edition, Philadelphia: Parry & McMillan, 1855), the anthology from which the poems here are taken.

  4. The Little Beach-Bird
