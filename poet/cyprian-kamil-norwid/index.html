Cyprian Kamil Norwid

1821–1883

Cyprian Kamil Norwid (1821–1883) was a Polish poet, dramatist, painter and sculptor, born at Laskowo-Głuchy near Warsaw. He left Poland in 1842, lived in Italy, Paris, London and New York, and died in poverty in Paris in 1883, in a home for exiled Poles.

Largely unrecognised in his lifetime, he is now counted among the major Polish Romantic poets, known for the elegy Bema pamięci żałobny rapsod, the Chopin poem Fortepian Chopina and the short lyric Moja piosnka (II). This selection takes ten of his poems from the Wolne Lektury texts based on the 1934 Parnas Polski edition of his Dzieła, edited by Tadeusz Pini.
