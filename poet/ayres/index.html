Philip Ayres

1638–1712

Philip Ayres (1638–1712) was an English poet and translator, born at Cottingham and educated at Westminster and St John's College, Oxford. He became tutor in the family of Montague Garrard Drake of Amersham, Buckinghamshire, and lived with them until his death on 1 December 1712.

His chief work is Lyric Poems, Made in Imitation of the Italians (1687), a collection of sonnets, songs and original pieces together with many translations from Italian, Spanish, Latin and Greek poets. One copy of verses is addressed to his friend John Dryden.
