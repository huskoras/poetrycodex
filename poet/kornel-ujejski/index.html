Kornel Ujejski

1823–1897

Kornel Ujejski (1823–1897) was a Polish poet and publicist, born in Galicia, in the Austrian partition, in the county of Czortków. A friend of Juliusz Słowacki, he is often called the last great poet of Polish Romanticism, and he later sat as a deputy in the Austrian parliament in Vienna.

He is remembered above all for Z dymem pożarów, the Chorał (1846), a prayer of anguish and defiance written in the Galician disturbances of that year, which was set to music and became one of the best-loved patriotic hymns of the nation. This selection also takes from his Poezje (Leipzig, 1866) the biblical and patriotic lyrics of the Skargi Jeremiego cycle and other poems, and a few pieces from the Lutnia song-books of 1864–65 and from the periodical Życie (1898).
