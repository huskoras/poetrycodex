Castro Alves

1847–1871

Antônio Frederico de Castro Alves was born in Bahia on 14 March 1847 and died in Salvador on 6 July 1871, aged twenty-four. He studied law at Recife and São Paulo, and was known as the "poet of the slaves" for his verse against slavery, written in the grand, soaring manner of the Brazilian condoreiro school.

Espumas flutuantes, his only book printed in his lifetime, appeared in 1870; Os escravos, which holds "O navio negreiro" and "Vozes d'África", was published after his death. This selection takes the proofread poems of the 1913 printing of Espumas flutuantes from Portuguese Wikisource, with the two antislavery poems.
