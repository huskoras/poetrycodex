Victorian

William Watson

1858–1935

William Watson (1858–1935) was an English poet, born at Burley-in-Wharfedale, Yorkshire. He made his reputation in the 1890s with poems on public themes and with elegant, formal lyrics, and was often spoken of as a possible poet laureate.

He was knighted in 1917. His volumes include Wordsworth's Grave and Other Poems and The Hope of the World (1897). The poems here come from Untermeyer's Modern British Poetry (1920).
