Novalis (Friedrich von Hardenberg)

1772–1801

Friedrich von Hardenberg, who wrote as Novalis, was born in 1772 at Oberwiederstedt in Saxony and trained as a lawyer and mining administrator while moving in the Jena circle of early German Romanticism. The death in 1797 of his young betrothed, Sophie von Kühn, became the centre of his work.

His Hymns to the Night (1800) and Spiritual Songs (1802) turn that bereavement into a mystical devotion to night, death and Christ. He died of consumption in 1801, aged twenty-eight. The English versions here are George MacDonald's, printed in Rampolli (1897).
