Richard Watson Gilder

1844–1909

Richard Watson Gilder (1844–1909) was an American poet and editor, born at Bordentown, New Jersey. From 1881 until his death he edited The Century Magazine, one of the most influential American periodicals of the period.

His own poems, including the sonnet sequence The New Day (1875) and later lyrics, were admired for their delicacy and were frequently anthologised.
