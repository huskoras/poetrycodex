Dutch & Flemish

P.A. de Genestet

1829–1861

Petrus Augustus de Génestet (1829–1861) was a Dutch poet and Remonstrant minister, born in Amsterdam. He trained for the ministry in the Remonstrant Brotherhood, whose liberal, undogmatic theology shaped his work, and he served as a minister in Delft, where he settled in the 1850s. His verse joins an easy, humorous manner to sincere religious feeling, and it made him one of the most widely read Dutch poets of the mid-nineteenth century.

He died at Rozendaal in 1861, aged thirty-one. His poems were gathered after his death as De dichtwerken, edited by C. P. Tiele, who added a long biographical sketch. The poems here come from that collection, from the section Laatste der Eersten (1854–1860), reflective and devotional lyrics, and from the Leekedichtjes (1860), short satirical epigrams on the religious and intellectual quarrels of his day.
