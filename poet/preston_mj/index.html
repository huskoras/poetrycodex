American

Margaret Junkin Preston

1820–1897

Margaret Junkin Preston (1820–1897) was a Virginia poet and the sister-in-law of Stonewall Jackson, whose death she lamented in Under the Shade of the Trees.

The poems here are taken from American War Ballads and Lyrics, edited by George Cary Eggleston (New York and London: G. P. Putnam’s Sons, 1889), where it is printed under his or her name: Under the Shade of the Trees.
