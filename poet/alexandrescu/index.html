Romanian

Grigore Alexandrescu

1810–1885

Grigore Alexandrescu (1810–1885) was a Romanian poet, fabulist and satirist, born in Târgoviște and one of the leading writers of the Wallachian generation that preceded the revolution of 1848. He began as a clerk and soldier, and was imprisoned in 1840 for his part in the Filipescu conspiracy, then later held public office under the united principality.

He is best remembered for his meditations on ruined monasteries and the vanished glory of the Romanian princes, among them Umbra lui Mircea (The Shade of Mircea), written at Cozia, and for his satirical fables and epistles, which attacked the vices of the court and the new politics. His collected verse appeared as Meditații, elegii, epistole, satire și fabule in 1863.
