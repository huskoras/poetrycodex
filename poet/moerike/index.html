German

Eduard Mörike

1804–1875

Eduard Mörike was a Protestant pastor and, in his writing life, a poet and novelist of the Swabian school. His lyric poems are among the best loved in German, and many were set to music.

James Clarence Mangan (1803–1849) translated this poet in his German Anthology (1845); the poems here are his English versions, as reprinted in Poems, ed. John Mitchel (New York, 1859).

The German originals added here follow the order of his Gedichte in the 1867 edition (the last he saw through the press), from the TextGrid Digitale Bibliothek text, with the poems that also have Wikisource pages placed first.
