Ioannis Polemis

1862–1924

Ioannis Polemis (1862–1924) was a Greek poet, born in Athens. He belonged to the New Athenian School, the generation of poets who, from the 1880s, wrote in the living demotic language and in the clear, singing style of the folk song, in contrast with the learned manner of the earlier Romantic School.

He published several collections, among them Ερείπια, Αλάβαστρα, Κειμήλια, Χειμωνανθοί and Σπασμένα Μάρμαρα, and his lyrics, patriotic poems and love-songs were widely reprinted in school anthologies; some were set to music. Poems such as Η Παιδούλα, Το κρυφό σχολειό and Προς την σελήνην became familiar to generations of Greek readers.

The texts here are taken from the proofread transcriptions on Greek Wikisource, some in the polytonic spelling of their first printing (for example in the annual calendar of Konstantinos Skokos) and some in later monotonic form, with the spelling as the page gives it.
