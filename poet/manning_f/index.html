Modern

Frederic Manning

1882–1935

Frederic Manning was born in Sydney in 1882 and settled in England as a young man, where he was part of the literary circles of Ezra Pound and Edmund Gosse. He published Poems in 1910 and The Vigil of Brunhild before the war.

He enlisted in 1915 and served on the Somme, an experience that shaped Eidola (1917), a collection of war poems and lyrics, and the war novel The Middle Parts of Fortune. He died in 1935.
