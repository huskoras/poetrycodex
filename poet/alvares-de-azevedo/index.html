Álvares de Azevedo

1831-1852

Manuel Antônio Álvares de Azevedo (1831-1852) was a Brazilian Romantic poet, born in São Paulo, who studied law there and died at twenty from illness following an accident. He is a central figure of the Brazilian "ultra-Romantic" generation.

His one book of verse, Lira dos Vinte Anos, appeared posthumously in 1853; the poems here follow the arrangement of the 1862 Obras, in the original spelling of that edition.
