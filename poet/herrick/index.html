Metaphysical & Cavalier

Robert Herrick

1591–1674

Robert Herrick (1591–1674) was an English poet and clergyman, the most prolific of the Cavalier poets.

His single vast collection, <em>Hesperides</em> (1648), moves between pagan celebration, courtly love, and country ritual — over a thousand short poems in one book.
