Liu Tsung-yüan

T’ang dynasty

Liu Tsung-yüan is a Chinese poet named in Charles Budd’s Chinese Poems (1912). Budd, who spells the name Liu Tsong-yuen, places him in the T’ang dynasty and gives no further note for him.

The poems here are Charles Budd’s versions in Chinese Poems (1912), given under the poet’s name as Budd romanises it.
