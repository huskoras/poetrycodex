Dragotin Kette

1876–1899

Dragotin Kette (1876–1899) was a Slovene poet, born in Premantica in Carniola. With Josip Murn, Ivan Cankar and Oton Župančič he is counted among the founders of the Slovene Moderna, the modernist turn in Slovene literature at the end of the nineteenth century. His verse joins folk-song simplicity, impressionist moods and a gently ironic young voice, in lyrics, sonnet cycles and gazelles.

He studied in Ljubljana but never finished his schooling, living in poverty and writing for the journal Ljubljanski zvon. He died of tuberculosis at the age of twenty-three in the Cukrarna, the Ljubljana sugar-refinery building then used as a poorhouse and hospital. His Poezije appeared posthumously in Ljubljana in 1900.
