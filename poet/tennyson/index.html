Victorian

Alfred, Lord Tennyson

1809–1892

Alfred, Lord Tennyson (1809–1892) was Poet Laureate of Great Britain for over four decades and the most popular and representative poet of the Victorian age.

His work ranges from the intimate grief of In Memoriam A.H.H., written for a dead friend, to the martial narrative verse of Enoch Arden and the ballad-like Maud.

Major Works (6)

  1. Becket — Verse drama, 1884
  2. Harold: A Drama — Verse play, 1876
  3. Idylls of the King — Epic poem, 1859–1885
  4. Maud — Monodrama (narrative poem), 1855
  5. Queen Mary: A Drama — Verse play, 1875
  6. The Princess — Narrative poem, 1847
