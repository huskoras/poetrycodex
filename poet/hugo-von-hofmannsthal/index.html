Hugo von Hofmannsthal

1874–1929

Hugo von Hofmannsthal (1874–1929) was born in Vienna and wrote in German. A schoolboy prodigy, he published verse in his teens under the pseudonym Loris, and by his early twenties had written most of the lyric poems for which he is remembered; Stefan George’s circle printed several of them in the Blätter für die Kunst.

From about 1900 he turned from lyric poetry to the stage, writing verse dramas such as Der Tor und der Tod and, later, librettos for Richard Strauss, among them Der Rosenkavalier and Ariadne auf Naxos. He was a co-founder of the Salzburg Festival in 1920. The poems here follow the collected Gedichte published by the Insel-Verlag in Leipzig in 1922.
