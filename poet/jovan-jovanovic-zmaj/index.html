Jovan Jovanović Zmaj

1833–1904

Jovan Jovanović Zmaj (1833–1904), born in Novi Sad, was a Serbian poet, physician and translator, one of the most loved poets of the nineteenth century. He studied law and then medicine, practised as a doctor in Novi Sad and Pančevo, and took the pen name Zmaj ("the dragon") as a young poet.

His Đulići (1864), a cycle of short love lyrics in the manner of Heine and the folk song, made him famous; its elegiac sequel Đulići uveoci (1882) mourned his wife and children. He also wrote celebrated verse for children and political satire, and translated widely from Hungarian, German and Russian. Both Đulići cycles are given here in the original Cyrillic.
