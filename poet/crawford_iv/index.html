Isabella Valancy Crawford

1850–1887

Isabella Valancy Crawford (1850–1887) was born in Dublin and raised in Ontario, and supported herself and her family by writing poems and stories for periodicals.

Her only book in her lifetime was Old Spookses' Pass, Malcolm's Katie, and Other Poems (1884), which brought her little notice until after her death. One of the poets chosen by W. D. Lighthall for Songs of the Great Dominion (London, 1889), an anthology of Canadian verse.
