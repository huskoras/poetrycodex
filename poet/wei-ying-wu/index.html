Wei Ying-wu

8th century AD

Wei Ying-wu is dated by Giles to the 8th century AD, in the T’ang dynasty. Giles prints three of his poems, on spring and on absent friends.

The poems here are Herbert A. Giles’s versions in Chinese Poetry in English Verse (1898), given with the poet’s name as spelled in that book.
