Dafydd ap Gwilym

c. 1315/20–c. 1350/70

Dafydd ap Gwilym was a Welsh poet of the fourteenth century, a contemporary of Chaucer, and is generally counted the greatest of the medieval poets of Wales. Born in Ceredigion, he made the cywydd the leading Welsh metre and wrote lyrics of love, nature and wit.

Here are versions by several hands: Alfred Perceval Graves (A Celtic Psaltery, 1917), George Borrow (Welsh Poems and Ballads, 1915), and Arthur James Johnes, whose Translations into English Verse from the Poems of Davyth ap Gwilym (1834) supplies most of the poems.
