Delmira Agustini

1886–1914

Delmira Agustini (Montevideo, 1886 – 1914) was a Uruguayan poet, one of the principal voices of Spanish-American modernismo and among the first women to write of desire in it with open candour. She published El libro blanco in 1907, Cantos de la mañana in 1910 and Los cálices vacíos in 1913.

She married in 1913 and separated from her husband within weeks; she was shot dead by him in July 1914, and he then took his own life. A posthumous collection, Los astros del abismo, appeared in 1924. The poems here are those transcribed on Spanish Wikisource from her lifetime books and other early editions.
