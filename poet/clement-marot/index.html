French

Clément Marot

1496–1544

Clément Marot (1496–1544) was born at Cahors, the son of the poet Jean Marot, and rose from the household of Marguerite d'Angoulême, sister of Francis I, to become valet de chambre to the king. A court poet of wit and polish, he was imprisoned in 1526 and again for his Protestant sympathies, and spent his last years in exile at Geneva, Savoy and Turin, where he died.

L'Adolescence Clémentine, his first collection, was published in 1532 and later gathered into the collected Œuvres. It joins the older rhétoriqueur tradition to a lighter, more direct manner, and made the rondeau, the ballade, the epigram and the epistle models for French verse. The poems here follow the Lyon edition of 1547, in its original spelling, and are taken from the Wikisource transcription of that edition.
