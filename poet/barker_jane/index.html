Restoration & Augustan

Jane Barker

1652–1732

Jane Barker (1652–1732) was an English poet and novelist, born at Blatherwycke in Northamptonshire and later associated with Wilsthorpe in Lincolnshire. She was a Catholic and a Jacobite, and followed the exiled James II to the court at Saint-Germain in France after 1688, returning to England in the early eighteenth century.

Her first printed verse appeared as Part I of Poetical Recreations (1688), a miscellany of epistles, songs, odes and elegies, several of them addressed to friends and to her brother, with a second part by other hands. She later published further poems and the prose fictions Love Intrigues (1713), Exilius (1715) and A Patch-Work Screen for the Ladies (1723).
