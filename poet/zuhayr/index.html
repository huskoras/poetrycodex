Zuhayr ibn Abi Sulma

6th–7th century

Zuhayr ibn Abi Sulma (زهير بن أبي سلمى), of the tribe of Muzayna, was one of the great poets of pre-Islamic Arabia, a master of the polished, carefully revised ode. He is remembered above all as the poet of the peace between the tribes of 'Abs and Dhubyan, whom he praised for ending their long war. The dates traditionally given for his life are uncertain.

His ode beginning "A-min Umm Awfa dimnatun lam takallami" is one of the Mu'allaqat, and it opens this selection. It is followed by the shorter pieces of his diwan that run to three or more verses. The texts are from the Arabic Wikisource transcriptions and keep the vowel marks of that source.

  8. ألا أبلغ لديك بني تميم (Ala ablig ladayka bani Tamim)
  9. أمن آل ليلى عرفت الطلولا (A-min ali Layla arafta al-tulula)
  10. لمن طلل برامة لا يريم (Li-man talalun bi-Ramata la yarimu)

  13. لمن الديار غشيتها بالفدفد (Li-mani al-diyaru ghashituha bi-al-fadfadi)
  14. ألا ليت شعري: هل يرى الناس ما أرى (Ala layta shi'ri hal yara al-nasu ma ara)
  15. تعلم أن شر الناس حي (Ta'allam anna sharra al-nasi hayyun)
  16. أبلغ بني نوفل عني فقد بلغت (Ablig bani Nawfalin anni fa-qad balaghat)
  17. أبلغ لديك بني الصيداء كلهم (Ablig ladayka bani al-Saydai kullahum)
  18. ولا تكثر على ذي الضغن عتبا (Wa-la tukthir ala dhi al-dighni atban)
  19. إن الرزية لا رزية مثلها (Inna al-ruzzi'ata la ruzzi'ata mithluha)
  20. ألا أبلغ لديك بني سبيع (Ala ablig ladayka bani Subay'in)
  21. رأت رجلا لاقى من العيش غبطة (Ra'at rajulan laqa min al-aysh ghibtatan)
  22. لعمرك والخطوب مغيرات (La-amruka wa-al-khutubu mughayyiratun)
