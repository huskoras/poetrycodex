Alfred Perceval Graves

1846–1931

Alfred Perceval Graves was born in Dublin and worked as a school inspector, while writing songs and verse and collecting Irish and Welsh folk music. His song "Father O'Flynn" became widely popular.

He was the father of the poet Robert Graves and died in 1931.
